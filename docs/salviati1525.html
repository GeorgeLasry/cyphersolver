This result is George Lasry’s. This project did not re-solve the cipher: it checked one sample against the Gallica image and recorded what it found.

  • Batch A. Image checked: https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/btv1b90600674/f55.item

  • What we checked: f54r: interleaved clear words "Pure a me pare ... maxime. Stando ancora le cose ... come stanno hora" match his L13-15 excerpt; cipher sign after "pare" = H-10 = "che" per his key, consistent with "che l essere"; longer cipher run not decoded

05 Notes and corrections

The recipient is in the heading: the letter opens "Rdo Dno Lang. Ptri". The report says the recipient is not established.

How much is held here (reading audit, 22 October 2026). This repository holds only Lasry’s excerpt: three complete lines of f54r, which build an Italian sentence with three holes and carry his translation. That excerpt reads. His figure of 704 of 838 elements identified measures how much of the cipher his key gives a value to, not how much reads; his report says three of the eight pages are deciphered in part, and the rest of his decryption is not here to check. Only about a tenth of the letter is therefore held as a reading here.
