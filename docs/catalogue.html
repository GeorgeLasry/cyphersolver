About the catalogue

What is still in cipher in the digitised diplomatic volumes, and nobody has read. The first entries came from the French volumes on Gallica; the rest (52) from the DECODE database, every ciphertext record it marks non-decrypted or partly decrypted, grouped into letters and series and checked against this repository's own targets and Tomokiyo's list. A sweep of 22 Sept 2026 added candidates for new solves and outside reads: DECODE records never classified, letters Tomokiyo calls undeciphered while naming a key, Gallica notices and the printed calendars. On 14 Oct 2026 open items were pulled from Satoru’s Cryptoole index and checked against the repository. 84 entries are counted targets, 14 are noted only. Class A means siblings with decipherment in the same volume, B a partial key or known family in print, C no key and no sibling. Entries read, partly read, resolved or found already in print are taken out of the catalogue and written up; those attempted here without a reading stay, tagged attempted, open.

Scores are judgements from catalogue descriptions and the literature, not from the leaves: 31 of 98 entries have been viewed on the image, and each page carries the check that would confirm it is open. Data: catalogue.json · text: CATALOGUE.md · how to send a result: contribute.

How the scores were set

Importance asks what a full reading would add: 5 for a first-hand report of a major event by a principal witness (du Bellay in London 1529, Lanssac at the Polish election), 2 for routine business. Solvability asks whether the material online is enough: 5 when deciphered siblings of the same hand and year sit in the same volume, 1 for one short letter in an unknown language. Difficulty is the technical work at the keyboard: 1 for aligning a known sibling, 5 for a statistics-only attack on a large homophonic nomenclator. The default priority weights importance 0.4, solvability 0.35 and ease 0.25 (ease is 6 minus difficulty), rescaled to 0–10; change them under “Filter and reweight”. Every score is a judgement made before viewing the leaf, so treat the order as a work plan, not a finding.

Checked: catalogue descriptions and item numbering; presence or absence of a decipherment item in each volume; shelfmarks from the IIIF manifests. For the DECODE entries: the record metadata and notes, key records from the same archive series, overlap with this repository's targets and with Tomokiyo's list. Entries tagged rule-scored were scored by a script from DECODE's metadata and not yet reviewed by hand. Verify each entry on the image and in the literature before attacking it.
