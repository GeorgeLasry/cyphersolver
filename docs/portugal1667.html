03 The decipherment

f59r, cipher lines 9–15

prudencia. Com tudo me pareceo lembrarvos que com aquelle rei se deve trata[r] com todo o segredo por que os Olandezes se nam dem por ofendidos.

Translation. …prudence. Nevertheless, it seemed right to remind you that dealings with that king must be conducted with complete secrecy so that the Dutch do not take offence.

05 Our check

This result is George Lasry’s. This project did not re-solve the cipher: it checked one sample against the Gallica image and recorded what it found.

  • Batch B. Image checked: none (no Gallica link; Portugais 33 ark not resolvable offline)
  • Folio matches the report: unclear; cipher present: yes
  • What we checked: decoded from his f59r crop with his table: 70.f.x.40.50.26.82.35.ss.82.29.20.700.x.40.29.20.50 = p-r-u-d-e-n-c-i-a c-o-m t-u-d-o m-e; next row 70.ss.500.50.g.50.29.202.50.20.24.500.ss.f.x.29.62.28 = pareceo lembrarvos q = his excerpt
  • Verdict: confirmed (method read from existing decipherment, extent complete). The check is a sample, not a re-solution.
  • Notes: Sample decodes exactly, but folio not checked on Gallica. A historical decipherment exists on f60r and a copy on f119r, so the reading rests on it. Simple numeric/letter substitution.
