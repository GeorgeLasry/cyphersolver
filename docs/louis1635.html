03 The decipherment

f117r

Quand vous m’escriprez quelque chose d’importance souvenez vous que ce soit en chiffre. Car sy la despesche du xviiᵉ fust tombée entre les mains des Ennemis, ilz eussent appris [?] et en eussent tiré ?duantage.

Translation. When you write something important to me, remember to put it in cipher. For if the dispatch of the seventeenth had fallen into the enemies’ hands, they would have learned [?] and gained an [?] advantage from it.
