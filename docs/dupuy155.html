03 The decipherment

f41r, rows9-14

[ante]cessore et nel mio. Et se Vostra Altezza nel principio fusse contentata di por quel luogo in mano di una terza persona confidente anbe le parti, perche lo conservassi a che fusse stato legitimo et Catolico Re di Francia, come da Sua Maesta ella ne fu richiesta, che si saria rimediato a molti inconvenienti et non sariano nati li a[ccidenti].

Translation. ...my predecessor and in my own time. And if Your Highness had initially agreed to put that fortress in the hands of a third person trusted by both sides, to preserve it for whoever became the legitimate Catholic King of France, as His Majesty had requested, many difficulties could have been remedied and those incidents would not have arisen.

# BnF Dupuy 155, Francesco Guicciardini(?) to Ferdinando I de' Medici, 10 May 1597: English translation of Lasry's excerpt

Translation (Claude Opus 5.5, 23 Oct 2026) of `lasry_excerpt_reading.md`, **an excerpt only** of the letter, the passages George Lasry printed in his BnF cipher research report (f. 41r, rows 9-14); the rest of the letter is not in the repository. Same holes: [?] unread, [x] a word he supplied. His own translation is in `lasry_entry.md`.

## Translation

[pre]decessor and in mine. And if Your Highness had at the beginning been content to put that place in the hands of a third person trusted by both parties, so that he might keep it for whoever should be the legitimate and Catholic King of France, as His Majesty asked of you, many drawbacks would have been remedied and the in[cidents] would not have arisen.
