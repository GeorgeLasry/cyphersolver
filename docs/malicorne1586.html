Niort → Henri III · homophonic letter cipher with word signs · 21 April 1586 · read with known key, partial

Malicorne to Henri III, Niort, 21 April 1586

The governor of Poitou reports failed plots on Lusignan and a raid on two Protestant companies; one margin-confirmed sentence reads, and the rest of the decode is still being built.

Daniel Bourdeau

At a glanceRead with known key · partial · f. 163r A, 73% of words

The letter
Jean de Chourses, sieur de Malicorne, governor of Poitou, to Henri III, “A Nyort ce xxie avril 1586”, signed Malycorne: six pages mostly in cipher.
Where
Bibliothèque nationale de France, fr. 15572 ff. 162–164 (Gallica views 169–173). Not in DECODE or the catalogue.
The cipher
Invented signs and digits, several for each letter, with signs for de, la, le, que, sur, nous, et and for doubled letters, and nulls. Thirteen look-alike signs still carry two values.
How it was deciphered
Michael Brackx applied Tomokiyo's key table, corrected it, and checked the decode against the decipherment written in the margin of f. 163r at the time.
What it says
The rest “consiste en la creance du porteur et lesdicts articles”; two plots on Lusignan were discovered; Villeluisant's men cut two companies to pieces near Sanxay.
Still open
f. 162r and the second stretch of f. 163r give phrases between gaps; four pages await the contributor's aligned decode.
Credit
Transcription, corrected key and decode: Michael Brackx (@brackxm) with Claude Code, 22–23 October 2026 (issue #92). Key table: Satoshi Tomokiyo, Cryptiana.

The same volume holds Camus de Pontcarré's letters to Villeroy in another cipher (Camus 1586) and Matignon's and Mayenne's despatches (Mayenne–Forget), each with its own page.

01 The letter

f. 162r has four clear lines and 27 cipher lines; f. 162v 31 cipher lines; f. 163r is clear text with two cipher stretches (A, two lines; B, nine), both deciphered in the margin by a hand of the time; canvas 171 carries two clear letters with cipher blocks of 4 and 16 lines; canvas 172 has 31 and 24 cipher lines, then the clear close and the signature. The address leaf, “Au Roy”, is view 173.

02 f. 163r and its margin

Stretch A reads as one sentence, confirmed word for word by the margin (consiste en la creance du port[eu]r, & les articles); 73% of its words read as sense, the opening group and one word before porteur unread:

[?] consiste en la creance du [?] porteur et [l]esdicts articles.

[?] consists in the credence of the [?] bearer and the said articles.

Stretch B gives phrases between gaps (69%): les deulx entreprises que i'auois sur Lusignan, l'une … tramee aux charbonnieres, … donnant quelque argent … descouuertes, et fault que ie … ce peult aisement establir la charge en ce pais. The margin beside it is mostly illegible between argent and descouvertes.

03 f. 162r, decoded

The decode builds phrases, not sentences (62% of words read as sense, 65 holes): the failed enterprises against Lusignan, “le sieur de Uilleluisant et ceulx que ie … enuoies”, an attack in the bourg of Sanxay a league from Lusignan on two companies, “les taill[e]rent en piesses, prinrent les … capitaines … et l[e]urs enseignes … les amenerent ici”, with no loss but “l'enseigne du capit[a]ine Cossart et trois [?soldats]”. Most holes are letter strings that are not French, which points to look-alike signs still merged under one label rather than to a wrong key.

04 How it was done

  • Key. Tomokiyo's table, corrected: the hooked y is s (14 of 14 tokens; his table's t is probably a slip), the curly y p, the looped ꝺ n, 10 i, ρ p and h, and new word signs for nous and et. Three per-page keys were merged and pruned one value at a time, from 43 to 13 two-valued signs.
  • Against the margin. Stretch A 97.6% of the margin's letters matched in order, against 22–27% for shuffled keys; B lines 1–3 83.3%. For B lines 4–14 the first posted figure (72.8%) could not be reproduced; restated on the legible margin words against cipher rows 4–8 it is 79.3%, against 15–24%.
  • Departures from the raw decode. Six readings were chosen by hand on two-valued signs (Lusignan, ceulx, ici, Cossart, tramee, ce: 7 letters, under 1% of each page's letters) and three words of f. 163r B are taken from the margin where the decode is a letter or more off (i'auois, charbonnieres, descouuertes).
  • Language model. Corrected to Montaigne's Essais I–III after an Italian and a Middle English text were found in the first one: coverage 72.3% (76.5% as first posted), shuffled keys 32.5–47.7%.

05 What remains

  • f. 162v and the pages of views 171–172: the aligned decode (about 450 lines) and a legend tying each sign label to its shape have been asked of the contributor.
  • f. 162r and f. 163r B: thirteen labels still carry two values; their shapes could not be split at the resolution used.
  • f. 163r A: the opening group and the word before porteur.
