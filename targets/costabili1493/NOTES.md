# Beltrame Costabili to Ercole I d'Este, 1493: three clear letters

Status: not a cipher — catalogue correction, 20 October 2026.

Catalogue item 361 grouped these three records with the Costabili cipher letters of 1491–92.
Inspection of both photographs on each record shows ordinary Italian chancery writing,
including conventional abbreviations, throughout. No cipher passage was identified.
This is a classification correction, not a decipherment or a recovered key.

## Documents inspected

| Record | Date | Modena shelfmark | Images inspected |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1095 | 13 March 1493 | Ambasciatori Ungheria b. 2/22 no. 5 | I5642 recto, I5643 verso |
| R1096 | 22 April 1493 | Ambasciatori Ungheria b. 2/22 no. 6 | I5644 recto, I5645 verso |
| R1097 | 18 July 1493 | Ambasciatori Ungheria b. 2/22 no. 9 | I5646 recto, I5647 verso |

The versos are address/docket leaves. Each body is clear Italian, signed Beltrame de Costabili.
The headings and signatures use normal chancery contractions. The scans' date cards agree
with the dates in the letters. The recipient is Ercole I d'Este, as recorded by DECODE.

## Evidence and limits

- R1095 begins an ordinary report after the usual humble recommendation and directs the
  duke to reports carried by others. Its second paragraph refers to letters of the 11th
  and 12th and to a response to his consort. This is continuous prose, without substituted
  symbols or number groups.
- R1096 acknowledges a letter of the 11th and reports good bodily health of the duke's son;
  the closing names Strigonia and the date. No encrypted run interrupts the prose.
- R1097 refers to messages sent through the Venetian ambassador's household and Tadio de
  Lardi, then again reports the son's health. The dating clause reads 18 July 1493.

These are inspection notes, not a diplomatic edition. No complete transcription, new
historical interpretation, hidden-message test, or statistical cryptanalysis is claimed.
DECODE currently labels all three Partially decrypted; the proposed correction is record
type/labelling, not promotion to Decrypted. The correction is queued, not sent.
