## The record

Ff. 96–114, 17 images: "Post Scripta", dockets "…83". Squared grille sheets with dots, laid over a clear cover letter:
the dotted squares mark the letters of the secret text in the cover letter beneath. One cover letter shows through
under f.102 ("Die 4 od 5000 Cronen…"), but the cover letters themselves (ff. 115–118) are not among the DECODE images.
Without them the grilles give no text.

## 22 Oct 2026: already deciphered by Leighton (1974); the "grille" description was wrong

GitHub issue #88 (ry-xu) pointed to A. C. Leighton, "Eine neuentdeckte Chiffre und die erzbischöfliche Wahl zu Köln -
1583", *Zeitschrift für bayerische Landesgeschichte* 37 (1974) 828-844, free on the BSB scan bsb00009436 (IIIF images
152-168 = pp. 828-844; plates of f.96R on pp. 829 and 833, of the key strips on p. 832).

- **Same sheets.** Leighton's "Kasten schwarz 13478 (Alt 426/18)" ff. 96-115 is KAA 4591 ff. 96-115: squared paper with
  red lines, 66 x 43 squares; "Postscripta" on 96R, "Aliud Postscript" on 101V, "Presentatur 12 May ao 83 Monaci" on
  107V, "Presentatur 19 May 83 Monaci" on 115V; his plate of 96R is the DECODE sheet. His key ("Folio 49", four
  parchment strips) is KAA 4591 f. 49, DECODE R9304: the folio numbering is the same.
- **Our description was wrong.** These are not grilles over cover letters. The sheets are the cipher: a "thread" dot
  cipher read with a key strip laid along the sheet, three mixed 24-letter alphabets, and dots placed between letters
  standing for code words of an alphabetical name list on the strip (Kayser, Capitel, Brueder, Salentin, Sachsen...).
  A dotted stroke doubles a letter (COM-IS-ARY = commissary), ">" closes a section, a looped sign is E. L. (Euer Liebden).
  f. 115 is the last cipher leaf, not a cover letter. The text seen through f. 102 is not explained by Leighton.
- **The text.** Leighton deciphered all of ff. 96R-115V and prints the full German (pp. 836-838), normalised into
  words with code-list words in brackets: Duke Ernst of Bavaria (bishop of Freising, Hildesheim, Liège; elected
  archbishop of Cologne on 23 May 1583) to his brother Duke Wilhelm V. Postscripta dated "Datum (Cöln) den
  zwenundzwainzigisten tag april" 1583 (ff. 96R-101R); Aliud Postscriptum (101V-102R); a faulty trial of the opening
  (103V); a section presented 12 May (104R-107R); Postscripta presented 19 May (108R-115V). He checked ff. 96R-101R
  against a chancery decipherment in Kasten schwarz 3634 ff. 285-287a ("Extrait aus H. Ernst Ziffer 22 April 83") and
  104R-107R against Kasten schwarz 3636 f. 42. (Issue #88 says the key is in 3636 f. 42; that folio is the chancery
  decipherment, the key is f. 49.) He also read a further undated dot cipher with the same key, Kasten schwarz 3636
  ff. 43R-45 1/2 R (to Wilhelm, on Count Arnold of Manderscheid's vote), not on DECODE as far as checked.
- **Copyright.** The 1974 article is in copyright: profile `texts_withheld`, entries cite the page; quotes kept short.
  No dot transcription was ever made here, so there is nothing of ours to align with the printed text.
- Outcome: read from existing decipherment, class already solved; prior_solution in print, found after our attempt.
  Catalogue entry 161 narrowed to R9424.
