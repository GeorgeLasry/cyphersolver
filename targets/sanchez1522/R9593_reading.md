## Notes

- **Look-alikes.** Letters resting on the other member of a pair, counted on the reading: Lo/ϙ/q read s or t or l by
  sense in *aposta*, *escrito*, *testimonio*, *Griti* (×2), *postreramente*, *aventureros*, *pregunteles* (about 14);
  ꝑ read m (*Valcamonica*, *Geronimo*, *mensagero*, *Monza*) against ǂ read o (*ahora*, *cinco o*, *testimonio*) (about
  9); ꝫ read a, but s in *acostunbra* and c in *apercibidos* (3): about 26 of some 1,050 letters, 2.5%.
- **Code values used beyond the published key** (all logged in `R9593_reread_log.md`): `gim` = *suyos* (adopted: R9635's
  decipherment, and here four times); `fef` = *toda* (adopted: *todavia* here and in R9653, *todas estas* here); `ril`
  = *lansquenetes* (five times here, alphabetical slot between `rug` la and `pac` lo; sense only, flagged).
- **Open groups:** `xe`, `no` (as a word), `vub`, `lud`, `fug`, `la` (twice: *cargo [la] vuestra magestad*, *[la] donde*),
  `lif`, `ni`, `fu`, `sag`, `nif`, `ram`, `tag`, `paf` (as menda-, *mandado*?), `zom`, `fag`, `pil`, `dug`, `nad`, `mug`
  (*palabla?* does not fit), `tem`, `nog`, `do` (not *venecia* here), `ca`, `ce`.
- The second half of f. 27r (ll. 26-33) and f. 27v ll. 11-13 do not build sentences: the run of open groups and
  uncertain signs is too dense.
