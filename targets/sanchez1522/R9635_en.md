# R9635: Alonso Sánchez to Charles V, Venice, 16 September 1522 — English

Translation of `R9635_reading.md` (the contemporary decipherment on f. 22r, checked against the cipher). Same holes.

Seeing that letters from Your Majesty were so long [?already] in coming, and since nothing can be lost by it, I went
to the lords of this Republic and told them that they knew well that by the capitulation of the truce which they have
concluded with Your Majesty they were to pay, on the first of this month, another 20,000 ducats, besides the other
18,000 that they owe from last year; and that I asked them to be pleased to pay them and fulfil what they are bound to.
They answered me what they had answered before: that their lands should be restored to them, as is agreed in the said
[…] […] that the lands were in the power […] whoever [?it might be] […] Your Majesty had promised to restore them. I
told them that it [?should be enough for them] to hold back the first 18,000 ducats until the said lands were restored,
and that they should pay these 20,000 of now. They replied that they could not do it, because they had paid and were
paying every year more than six thousand ducats to their subjects for the said lands, which were worth more than
15,000 ducats a year; that on their being restored they would pay all the 20,000 ducats at once. I could not get them
off this, nor do I believe they will ever pay them unless the said lands are restored to them.

The lords of this Republic have [?had] these days letters from [?Menorami] of the 28th of last month from [?Lucerne],
and he writes that all their people [the Swiss] were holding that for now they would go to serve in France on the side
of Picardy, and that after this whole month no more campaigning could be done in that region, and they will come to
the enterprise of Italy. These lords also have letters from their ambassador in France of the 26th of last month; he
says that the King of France was going from Blois to Paris and from there to Picardy. This has been confirmed to me by
two ways.
