Alden Number one Dispatch received I
congratulate you upon the result of
your operations they have caused the
utmost satisfaction here I would not
undertake a dash at Saffron now
better devote everything towards turning it
first collecting a sufficient force near
Forts Henry & Donelson to make
success sure Either alvord or yourself
should soon go to the scene
of operations why not have alvord
take the line of Lonesome &
operate on Shylock while your troops
turn Saffron those two points gained
a combined movement on Torrent will
be next in order The bridges
at Tuscumbia & Decatur should at
all hazards be destroyed at once
Please number telegraphic dispatches & give
hour of transmittal Thank Bremen Foote
& their commands for me Andes
