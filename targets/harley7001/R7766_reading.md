*It is to be seriously considered what is to be done* in case a proclamation shall goe out against my [?patron] as
against the last [?Lord Keeper]. For as to the one side, I am of opinion they have not enough against him to
confiscate his fortune, and they would be [?willing] to take the advantage of his refusall to return upon the(ir)

bu[t] [?pressing] that [?Mr Forster] [?should] give in his [90]; but God knows why that will suit.
