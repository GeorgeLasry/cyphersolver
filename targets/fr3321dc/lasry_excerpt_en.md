# BnF Français 3321, f. 103r, letter signed DC, 20 May: English translation of Lasry's excerpt

Translation (Claude Opus 5.5, 23 Oct 2026) of `lasry_excerpt_reading.md`, **an excerpt only** of the letter, the passages George Lasry printed in his BnF cipher research report (f. 103r, first four lines); the rest of the letter is not in the repository. ## Translation

Affairs stand on such terms that the ruin of Épernon will infallibly follow from them, or the ruin of this state. For the union is so strong that it can better give the law than receive it. Tomorrow the pe[tition] is to leave; you will have the copy of it at once, as the rest is being sent to you.
