# Fernandez Duran to the marques de San Felipe, El Pardo, 30 November 1718 (ACA, Legacion en Genova y Turin)

One despatch in numerical cipher from Miguel Fernandez Duran, Secretario del Despacho de Guerra, to Vicente
Bacallar y Sanna, marques de San Felipe, Spain's envoy at Genoa, dated El Pardo 30 November 1718 and signed in
clear. Archivo de la Corona de Aragon, Diversos, Legacion en Genova y Turin, Cajon 1, leg. 22, doc. 67 (DECODE
record R10181, status Non-decrypted, access mode Public). The same fonds, Cajon 19, leg. 30, holds the legation's
cipher keys, nine of them catalogued on DECODE (R10189-R10197, 1711-1718).

What is here:

- `harvest/` -- two blind transcription passes of the cipher text (Sonnet subagents, one leaf per call), the
  reconciled ciphertext, and `keys_range.tsv`, the value ranges of the nine catalogued keys read from their images.
- `images/manifest.json` -- the DECODE file names, sizes and hashes of every image used. The images are not
  committed (DECODE holdings); they are served without login by the URL in the manifest.
- `NOTES.md` -- status, sources, what is established and what is inferred, the search log, the failure log.
