# Clairambault 460 — reciprocal alphabet: translation of the excerpt

George Lasry, report PDF `24_clairambault-460-reciprocal-alphabet.pdf`, "Excerpt and Translation".

## Translation 1 (p107 — complete cipher paragraph)

**as far as Neufchâtel** But instead of **making him cross the river, the Spaniards sent him six hundred horsemen** because, they say, he had little **cavalry, and they made him remain there**. This suggests that they **still intend to undertake a siege.**
