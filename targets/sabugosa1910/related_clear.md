# M14: clear texts that belong with the telegrams (context, not coded)

## m0001 (card, Paço das Necessidades) and, probably continued, m0002: a decode dated 22-5-10 whose telegram is not in the file

Pencil, with the decoder's question marks:

> 22-5-10. Entrevista deve realisar-se hoje Rainha Alexandra com carinhoso interesse convidou para jantar hoje
> Princeza ? sua Familia. Espero que isto seja significativo e que Princeza agradar a V. M. ElRei ? Alexandra tratar
> n'esse caso ? [m0002:] assumpto - V. M. ElRei escrever hontem V. M. Nada mais ha por emquanto - Partida
> terça-feira. Sabugosa

English: "22 May 1910. The interview should take place today. Queen Alexandra, with affectionate interest, has
invited the Princess [?] [and] her family to dinner today. I hope this is significant and that the Princess will
please His Majesty the King [?] Alexandra [to] handle in that case [?] the matter. His Majesty the King [to] write
yesterday [to] Your Majesty. Nothing more for the present. Departure Tuesday. Sabugosa."

That m0002 continues m0001 is our inference from the text (m0001 ends "Alexandra tratar", m0002 begins
"assumpto"). No coded telegram of 22 May is in M14.

## m0007: Queen Amélie's reply to Sabugosa, 21-5-10 (ink, clear)

> Ao C.de de Sabugosa. Agradeço telegrammas - Espero desejada entrevista se tenha realisado independente mesmo
> andamento negociações pois lembro impossibilidade voltar breve. A. 21-5-10

English: "To the Count of Sabugosa. I thank you for the telegrams. I hope the desired interview has taken place,
whatever the course of the negotiations, for I remind you that it is impossible to return soon. A[mélie].
21 May 1910."
