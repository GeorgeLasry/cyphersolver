# m0012-13: English translation

Queen Alexandra received me last night and said very kind words to me [?]. His Majesty the King ended by saying
[?va] [?] [?count] [?at ease]. The point is to get him married. I had no opportunity to see the Princess. Soveral
says [he] will go to handle the [?further] negotiation. Sabugosa.

Notes. From the decode of the time on m0005 and m0004, with the decoder's question marks kept as holes. "Him" is
presumably King Manuel II; the Princess is not named in the text. "va" stands out of alphabetical place (0539
belongs before "me" 2013), so the decoder's "va" is probably wrong.
