# m0012-13: English translation

Queen Alexandra received me last night and said very kind words to me [?]. His Majesty the King ended by saying
[?va] [?] [?count] [?at ease]: "I will see to getting him married." I had no opportunity to see the Princess. Soveral
says [he] will go to handle the [?further] negotiation. Sabugosa.

Notes. From the decode of the time on m0005 and m0004, with the decoder's question marks kept as holes. The
decoder wrote "tratar ei" (tratarei, "I will see to"), so the words "I will see to getting him married" are probably
the King's, at the end of what he said, not Sabugosa's comment. "Him" is presumably King Manuel II; the Princess is
not named in the text. "va" stands out of alphabetical place (0539 belongs before "me" 2013), so the decoder's "va"
is probably wrong.
