# m0010: English translation

Today the King intends to speak to the Queen so that Soveral may take up the matter with her. I hope to give news
tomorrow. Sabugosa.

Notes. From the decode of the time on the card m0008, in telegraphic Portuguese without articles. The King is George V (Edward VII died on 6 May and was buried on 20 May 1910); the Queen is probably
Queen Alexandra, as in the telegram of 24 May. Soveral is the Marquis of Soveral, Portuguese minister in London
[our identification]. "The matter" is the decoder's word; the telegram of 24 May (m0012-13) says what it is.
