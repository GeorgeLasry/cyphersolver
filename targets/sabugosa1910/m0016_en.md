# m0016: English translation

I thank Your Majesty with lively gratitude for the telegram, which has set my heart at rest. I am and always will be
the same. I am very glad of the good news and the demonstrations. I am writing. I kiss Your Majesty's hand. Sabugoza.

Notes. The coded words (from "veio" to "mesmo") come from the decode of the time on m0009; the rest is in clear on
the telegram. The code has the infinitive "ser" where the sense wants "sou" (I am). The year 1904 is from the
decode's date 24-4-04; the Naples stamp gives 24 April.
