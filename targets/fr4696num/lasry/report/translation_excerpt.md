# Français 4696 — numerical cipher, ff54r–55r: translation of the excerpt

George Lasry, report PDF `44_francais-4696-numerical-cipher-ff54r55r.pdf`, "Excerpt and Translation".

## Translation 1 (f55r, full cipher lines 1–6)

The more restrained Your Excellency is in writing to Italy, especially in a way that could become known, the better. Informing the Most Serene Duke of Mantua frequently, and by a secure route, of events in France will be exceedingly welcome to His Highness and will greatly benefit both him and Your Excellency.
