## Translation

Your Excellency will be more sober in writing to Italy, especially in a way that it could become so widely known. It will be better to advise the Most Serene Lord Duke of Mantua frequently, and by a safe way, of the events of France. It will be exceedingly welcome to His Highness, and will turn greatly to the account of him and of Your Excellency.
