Sir, Since I sent to you last we have receaved assurances out of Lancashire from all the considerable men in
that county that they are ready to ryse for us and doe only demand a party of <492 505> horse from hence to
countenance <7 42 68> theyre rysing, being confident to be able within <487> dayes after
they are up to engage with Cromwell without any other assistance <66 68>. Upon this <71> and <254> and some other
lords of great interest in this Kingdom have sent them back word that upon the first drawing together of our
army <479 506> of our best horse and as many good highland foot shall be sent under the command of <277> and
Buckingham. Buckingham in the meane tyme they have given Buckingham leave to rayse a troope of English horse,
which within <487> dayes will be neere <479 505> good men. I have chosen to goe this way into England, beleeving
I shall be able to doe the King more service <8> soe then if I went in desguise to stay any where about London.
This bearer Colonel Berkenhead will give you a particular account of the whole[?] businesse, and I shall desire
you to give entire credit to what he shall say, for hee has beene the mayne negotiator of the Lancashire
businesse <7 20>.
I am with great trust Your most affectionate Friend and Servant
<134 = Duke> <96 = Buckingham>
