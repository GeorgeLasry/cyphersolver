# Guido Rangone to Montmorency, Venice, 10 January 1530 (BnF fr. 3070 f. 81r): English translation

I wrote to you lately, Most Illustrious Lord, of a certain thing that happened to me, and I did not tell you more of
it at that time; now I will tell it as briefly as I can. Monsieur, he presses [me] to come into their service, o[?] my
[…] very ill satisfied with him; he has sent to tell it to Signor Cesare and to make him great offers. See, Your
Excellency, what treatment this is, the [?]icon[?]uorano having followed to attend to you. Now let us leave these
private matters of mine; in time I shall let Your Excellency know that I will not fail in my duty …
