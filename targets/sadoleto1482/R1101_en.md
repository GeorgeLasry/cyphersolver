# R1101, Nicolò Sadoleto to Ercole I d'Este, Pozsony, 15 July 1482: English translation of the collated cipher passages

### Page 2, the queen passage

… that the Queen herself is cross about something else, so that she does not care much about our business; although,
even if she did as much as was possible, she could not at present arrange things otherwise than as they stand, and
above all without that money; indeed she says that, even if she had been present and had done every effort for this …
[…] of an answer, that this one we have had through the […] if peace follows […] as she hopes, she will see to do and …

### Final page, opening

… to join and take up this enterprise, and not let this opportunity pass, which never was, nor perhaps will be, again;
and that he should do it for the love and goodwill, and for the very close affinity that he has with the whole League,
and for the honour and glory of His Majesty, and to humble [W01] once and for all, so that they would learn to stay
within their own bounds.
