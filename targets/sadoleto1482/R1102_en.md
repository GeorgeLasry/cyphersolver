# R1102, Nicolò Sadoleto to Ercole I d'Este, [Pozsony], 16 July 1482: English translation of the cipher extracts

### A-B. Page 2 foot and page 3 top

Being in Buda, about to leave to come to Pozsony, I learned in great secrecy from dom Francesco how [the Signoria of
Venice] had written to Francesco Quirino, who is here, to go to [the King of Hungary] and make him the offer that [the
Signoria of Venice] wished to have good friendship with him, and wished to give him Veglia, and wanted his son (he is a
bastard) as their captain of the fleet at sea, and wished to give him a hundred thousand ducats every year; but it
wished to be able to hire soldiers and [U20] draw men from this kingdom. And His [U03] had learned this by a subtle
way, and would not for anything in the world have me show that I knew anything of it.

… they could make him such a great offer that she does not know what [U06] …

### D. Page 3, the monetary passage

… ⟨in any case to dispose themselves⟩ to give this [King of Hungary] a hundred thousand ducats every year for six or
seven years, which would be this sum that he asks of them; and it would not be a great sum for the League, considering
the [?undoubted] victory that His M[ajesty] … [?prom…] ⟨is the way that there was never a better opportunity⟩ … I do not
know, however, whether His Majesty [?would attempt] … shows that he desires and has more need at this beginning. But
[U15] to the League [U16] to negotiate, and there is no time to be lost; and if they [?will] [?spend] money at this time
and make the good expense …
