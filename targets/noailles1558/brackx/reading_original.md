# noailles1558: reading in the writer's spelling, line by line

BnF fr. 3151 no. 33, François de Noailles, bishop of Dax, ambassador at Venice, to the Cardinal de Lorraine, Venice,
13 November 1558. Three cipher blocks inside a clear letter: A and B on f. 60r (Gallica btv1b9059865k, view 61), C on
f. 60v (view 62). Each block has a contemporary decipherment in the margin (the gloss); on f. 60r the binding hides
the first letters of every gloss line, on f. 60v the gloss is in the outer margin and nearly complete.

**What it rests on.** Each line is the decode of the same line of `transcription_f60r_f60v.txt` with `key_r5.txt`
(`decode_tvd_r5.txt`). The spelling is the decode's (`u` for *v*, `i` for *j*, no accents). Word division, capitals and
punctuation are editorial. A word broken at a line end ends with `-`.

**Notation** (as in the earlier write-ups):
- plain words: carried by firm values (two or more independent words, or one word and the gloss);
- `[x]`: a letter or word supplied (nothing in the cipher carries it);
- `{?x}`: a tentative value (one context only); inside a word only the tentative stretch is braced;
- `[?sign]`, `[?2 signs]`: signs with no value; `[?5 letters]` decoded letters that make no word, with the letters
  given after a colon where they help.

## Block A (f. 60r), after the clear "... particulier [?3 signs]"; then clear "De sorte monseigneur que ..."

```
A1  [po]ur le maulvais ordre et pauure-
A2  te{?} u peu {?v} conduitte qu'il donne a se-
A3  s affaires [?3 signs] a
```

Gloss: *…le maulvaise / …e pauur[eté] / [cond]uitte q[u'il] donne / … a / …affaires*.

## Block B (f. 60r), after the clear "... pardeça,"; then clear "estant allé accompaigné ..."

```
B1  [?5 signs: gloss …e Seigneur] entreprand, {?monseigneur},
B2  sur luy et le [?sign]l COMME [?sign] qui e{?st} le iour
B3  [de] la Toussaint z son{?t}
```

Gloss: *…e seigneur / [entrep]rend sur luy c… / …me a dit / de la Toussai[n]ct / …*. *COMME* is written in clear inside
the block. The gloss puts *seigneur* before *entreprend*, so the five large signs that open B1 carry it (probably
*le Grand Seigneur*, the Sultan, as subject of *entreprend sur luy*); the sign after *entreprand* has nothing in the
gloss and is read as a vocative.

## Block C (f. 60v), after the clear "... au dommaige"; then clear "tant en Hongrie qu'[en] Italie"

```
C0  mais ie d-
C1  oubt {?pour} le singulier plaisir
C2  [a] prouffitt qu'ilz ont [?8 signs: d a t · u · u l]
C3  es riche{?s}ses ilz ne viennent
C4  [?8 signs: e r o · a r d] ny plus hardiz ny [?2 signs]
C5  [?3 signs: t h ·]ritailles qu'ilz ont [?sign]e aux ault-
C6  [r]es [?4 letters: o u e r] des passees TANT
```

Gloss: *Mais je me doubte / pour le singulier / [pl]aisir a prouffit / ilz ont d? commun… / …[ri]chesse ilz ne / …
p[ou]r a hazard / plus hardys n'y / … ravitaillez quelz / … osté aux aultres / …[ouu]ertes passer tant / [en]
Hongrie*. Lines 9–10 suggest *qu'ilz ont {ost}ée aux aultres* (the open sign in C5 carries a macron, as an
abbreviation would) and *ouuer[t]es* for C6's *o u e r*; both are left open in the reading.

## Open

- **A3** `E zB O k`: four signs after *affaires*, not in the gloss.
- **B1** `GS ? H2 X I`: five large signs before *entreprand*; their gloss is under the binding.
- **C2** *d a t · u · u l* (gloss *ilz ont d? commun…*; `Ob` may be an ink blot, `ag` a clear correction).
- **C4** *e r o · a r d* (gloss *… pour a hazard*).
- **C4–C5** *· · t h ·* before *…ritailles* (gloss *ravitailler*).
- **C6** *o u e r* (gloss *pourront passer*).
