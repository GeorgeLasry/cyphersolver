# Cod. Pal. germ. 597: the three sign alphabets (copied from NOTES.md)

Copied verbatim from NOTES.md (sections "Key A", "Key B", "Set C"). Recovered here from the Heidelberg images by
aligning the signs with Wattenbach's 1869 plaintexts (keys A, B) and the 2014 catalogue's reading of f. 93r (set C).

Grades: a–l high (clear on the image and checked). m–ch lower: the strike is heaviest there and
several of those signs are read from their shape, not checked in running text.

## Set C — f. 1r lines 1–2, ff. 5v–6r, 44v, 93r

- f. 93r: ✱ i, T n, ∴ d, ⊡ (one dot) e, filled box m, 2 c, ▪ a, ⊏ h.
- f. 1r line 1: s = 中 (box on a stem), t = ⊡̈ (box with two dots); grade C for s and t (one word each).

It shares a, b, c, d, e with key A and differs for i, n (T is w in key A). The f. 6r glossary uses
these signs and a few dotted-box variants not yet assigned.
