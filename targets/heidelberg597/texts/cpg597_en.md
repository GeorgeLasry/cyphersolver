# Cod. Pal. germ. 597: the deciphered passages: English translation

Translation (Claude Opus 5.5, 23 Oct 2026) of `texts/cpg597_decryption.md`, **excerpts only**: the passages whose
plaintext the repository holds (Wattenbach 1869 for f. 1r and f. 6v; the 2014 Heidelberg catalogue for f. 93r; this
project's check words). Wattenbach's plaintext of f. 5v is not in the repository; the recipe cipher, f. 9r ll. 7-12,
the f. 6r glossary and f. 44v are unread. Words in [brackets] are as the catalogue brackets them. Early New High
German.

## Translation

- f. 1r (Wattenbach): *This is an ABC, and we have made it new ourselves / abcdefghiklmnopqrſstuwschch and I / have
  therefore set it all one after another, so that one should not understand that it is an alphabet.*
- f. 6v (Wattenbach): *In the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Ghost, I, a guilty man, thus begin
  this book.*
- f. 93r (catalogue 2014): *Note: we have tried that [in the sign] of the [Virgin]*
- f. 11r line 4 and the 10v heading (key A): **niklas gank?icz** (a name; a modern pencil gloss on 11r reads
  "= Niklas Gankwiz")
