# “San Francisco” table: English translation

Working translations from the Wenmi site; […] = passage not translated for want of a sure reading.

## 0128 | 18 September 1914 | San Francisco → Sunwen c/o Toyama ; signé Linsun [sixth] | JACAR B03050087500, PDF page 17

My funds are exhausted while travelling; send two hundred US dollars urgently. On the [15th], ten thousand yuan were sent to Japan: have you received them? Reply on this too.

## 0274 | 4 October 1915 | San Francisco → “Sunwen”, Tokyo | JACAR B03050087700, PDF page 52

The five-yuan [bonds] sell easily; send many quickly.

## 0436 | 24 March 1916 | San Francisco → “Dr Sunwen”, Tokyo (11 words, received on the evening of the 24th) | JACAR B03050088200, PDF page 54

Bought two aircraft, price 40,000 in gold. Deng returns first. Send the bonds and merit medals quickly. — Sen (Lin Sen).

## 0630b | April 1916 | San Francisco (186) → Dr Sunwen 109 Harajuku (15 words) | JACAR B03050088600, PDF page 26

Telegram received; twenty thousand remitted […]. […] returns on the same ship as Deng. The aircraft can […] in two months. Bonds […] received. Lin Sen. Exact day unreadable.

## 0624b | 22 April 1916 | Tokyo (中山) → Youngchina San Francisco (34 words, 2 sheets) | JACAR B03050088600, PDF page 20

The situation worsens daily. Yuan […] independence to counter the revolutionaries […]. Unless I enter the country myself, I fear our party cannot build a strong force. I therefore decide to return on the 27th. Send the aircraft and comrades back quickly, and remit spare funds urgently. Xie Chi will act for the Tokyo headquarters; Xie Xinzhun will head the Yokohama liaison office. I shall telegraph after arriving. Wen. Some opening words remain uncertain.
