Arrived safely in Shanghai? […] the Jiangmen letter was received, but the reply was lost (?). [Wang] Zhaoming means to find Du Shi to help draft the bonds; ask brother [Liao] Zhongkai whether he can see to it. — The 3rd.

## 0022b | 4 May 1916 | Tokyo → Shanghai (Junzaburo) | JACAR B03050089000, PDF page 17 | sent 5.40 pm

Shao Zifeng telegraphs: at Huairen, Wang Huanxia on 28 April led [the troops of] two districts, [Huairen] and […], five hundred [men], who declared themselves independent (獨立); but their forces having been defeated (?), he plans to move men and arms along the Yalu to the Andong concession, recruit several thousand more and await orders. Move them on to another province; reply quickly,” etc. Instructions requested: reply. Because of the Sakamoto affair, [I] am taking the Kasuga-maru instead; a friendly gathering has been set for the […]. — Chi.

## 0024 | 4 May 1916 | Tokyo → Shanghai (Junzaburo) | JACAR B03050089000, PDF page 19 | sent 9.10 pm

There are 6,000 in cash: should they be remitted to Shanghai? […] The Japanese Fukaya (深谷) came to ask whether the unification [proposed by] brother […] and Nakasuga (中須賀) […] can be carried out. Reply. — Chi. The 4th.

## 0031b | 5 May 1916 | Shanghai → Tokyo (Linwailuk) | JACAR B03050089000, PDF page 26 | sent 7.20 pm

On the 3rd […] arrived in Shanghai; the representatives Ma and others may come to Shanghai. Also prepare banknotes quickly: 20,000 of 100 yuan, 80,000 of 10 yuan, and 10,000 each of 1 and 5 yuan, to be handed to the Shun’yō-maru (春洋丸) and carried to Hong Kong; the 耻義會 society (?) is to pass them on to Mei Qiaolin (梅喬林). As for [those in charge of] accounts and notes, I leave it to you, brother. — Wen (Sun Yat-sen).

## 0030a | 6 May 1916 | Tokyo → Shanghai (Junzaburo) | JACAR B03050089000, PDF page 25 | sent 6.35 pm

Yang Bing forwards a telegram from Tianjin: the students who have arrived number about a hundred (?); funds are needed most urgently. Awaiting your reply. — The 6th.

## 0031a | 6 May 1916 | Shanghai → Tokyo (Linwailuk) | JACAR B03050089000, PDF page 26 | sent 4.15 pm

Reply to Shao: the place is not ready; slow down recruitment; keep the arms at Andong until the time comes to use them. The 6,000 in funds go through the Yokohama Specie Bank (正金). The bearer cheques are being sent (?).

## 0034 | 8 May 1916 | Tokyo → Shanghai (Junzaburo) | JACAR B03050089000, PDF page 29 | sent 6.20 pm

Last night, a telegram from Juesheng (Ju Zheng): on the 4th [we] took Zhoucun (周村); our army attacked the city of Weixian the same day (?); on the 5th [we] took its East Gate; at noon a slight pull-back; in the evening [we] decided to surround [the city] and occupied the nearby villages; morale is excellent. Forwarding this. […] Shao Zifeng's [army] […] is now on its way to Shandong. Yang Bing has also had a telegram from Tianjin: […] funds; please act quickly. — Chi. The 8th.

## 0035 | 8 May 1916 | Shanghai → Tokyo (Linwailuk) | JACAR B03050089000, PDF page 30 | sent 9.20 pm

The students already arrived in Tianjin: please allot funds to keep them, pending the Jinan order […]. Yang Bing should stop recruiting. Shao's affair cannot be handled for now; once the headquarters' document has come, if it is enough, there is no need to remit the sixty (?). — The 8th.

## 0042a | 10 May 1916 | Tokyo → Shanghai (Junzaburo) | JACAR B03050089000, PDF page 37 | sent 5.10 pm

Keqiang (Huang Xing) arrived yesterday; he is avoiding visitors, I have not seen him yet. […] Mengshuo (孟碩) took the same ship to Shanghai. Please launch the action with Ju Zheng (居正). It is essential to telegraph the Shandong news to San Francisco (金山). — Chi. The 10th.

## 0054a | 15 May 1916 | Shanghai → Tokyo (Linwailuk) | JACAR B03050089000, PDF page 49 | sent 5 pm

Get notes of 10 and 100 yuan made quickly, 20,000 of each, with the special seal for Fujian, to be handed to Chen Qun (陳羣) for Huang Zhanyun (黃展雲). Funds here are exhausted; it is hard to raise more. Please decide and reply everywhere.

## 0056 | 15 May 1916 | Tokyo → Shanghai (Junzaburo) | JACAR B03050089000, PDF page 51 | sent 10.50 am

Saw Keqiang (Huang Xing) today: he strongly advises that Sir [Sun]'s journey to Shanghai be put off. He also says that a bureau chief (局長) of the General Staff stated: in Shandong the revolutionary army has too few troops; if they are not increased and it comes to peace talks, his country's diplomacy will find it very hard to act, and he means to slow down his help. [Keqiang] replied that the troops are loyal and will never make a compromise peace, and asked for active help. The other then agreed, and [Keqiang] telegraphs specially to ask that the troops be expanded urgently. Further, Xi Zhengming asks for funds by urgent telegram; extract of the original: the cadets of the [Baoding] military school who have come to Tianjin and signed a pledge (誓約) already exceed a hundred; their representatives pledge obedience; I have told [those in] the school to refuse. Outsiders are spending large sums to buy them over. [I have] fixed […] five, issued certificates and assigned [the men]; send ten thousand yuan quickly. The Model Regiment (模範團) […], Bao's and the other troops really can do something,” etc. Tianjin should now be the target. — Chi. The 15th.

## 0059a | 17 May 1916 | Tokyo → Shanghai (Junzaburo) | JACAR B03050089000, PDF page 54 | sent 2.30 pm

As for the 3,000 in funds: may 2,000 be given to Xi Zhengming and 500 to Zhao Jinqing? Reply. — Chi (Xie Chi). The 17th.

## 0061 | 17 May 1916 | Tokyo → Shanghai (Junzaburo) | JACAR B03050089100 | sent 8.10 pm

This afternoon Honjō forwarded the telegram of the 11th from Zhao Jinqing (Tianjin), as follows: “Zhang Yunchou […] leaves for Shanghai on the 19th (?). More than thirty students have arrived at my place; the Baoding (?) school plans to disperse them on the 17th. The students [will go] to the nine northern provinces; as we want to gather them in advance, 4,500 yuan are urgently needed. Yan Xishan and Jin Yong are secretly at odds, and [one of them] leans towards our party; the bandits have already moved. Kong Geng favours a gradual approach, Wang and the others a quick one, but all keep to the plan. May they be allowed to rise at once? Remit funds to Tianjin quickly.” Furthermore, Ozaki has returned to the capital without aircraft. Zhongmin (Xia Zhongmin) says the Master spoke of two aircraft sent by the Japanese: where are they? Otherwise they will have to be bought. — The 17th.

## 0069 | 20 May 1916 | Tokyo → Shanghai (Junzaburo) | JACAR B03050089100

Miyazaki […] has met Yamamoto […]o (山本□夫); he says the German (?) rifles are ready: does our party decide to buy them? […] reply. Shandong arms […]; moreover, Yingshi (Chen Qimei) is dead: we should telegraph overseas. The Master must be strictly on guard: we fear a disaster that would shake the foundations. The papers report that Ding […] was at his side and was wounded: is it true? Reply in detail. — Chi (presumably Xie Chi, 謝持). The 20th.

## 0071 | 21 May 1916 | Tokyo → Shanghai (Junzaburo) | JACAR B03050089100 | sent 5.40 pm

In Tianjin, Xi Zhengming plans to use students and keeps asking for large sums: the matter is urgent. Send funds and dispatch military men to carry out the agreed method; there must be no further delay. Please decide and reply quickly. — Chi. The 21st.

## 0075 | 23 May 1916 | Tokyo → Shanghai (Junzaburo) | JACAR B03050089100 | sent 1.20 pm

Wu Fei (?) has returned to Shanghai; he lives at 26 Avenue Joffre (霞飛路), in the French Concession. His letter says: “An order has been placed with Ōkura & Co. in Dalian for 300 Type 30 rifles and 100 Browning pistols; under the contract, payment is due two months after delivery; a deposit of 2,000 taels has been paid, which headquarters can draw on. But one must first give notice by letter.” Please send someone to deal with it on the spot. But it is not known who put up the deposit. — Chi. The 23rd.

## 0087 | 25 May 1916 | Tokyo → Shanghai (Junzaburo) | JACAR B03050089100 | sent 5 pm

The students' affair: the day before yesterday, following your instructions, I telegraphed Tianjin to stop. Now [I am told] to put it on hold: is that right? Cancel the previous order; reply quickly. After welcoming Keqiang (Huang Xing) on the 27th, I return on the 28th. — Chi. The 25th.

## 0092 | 26 May 1916 | Tokyo → Shanghai (Junzaburo) | JACAR B03050089100 | sent 10.25 am

Keqiang (Huang Xing) says that, in the Foreign Ministry's view, the Master should delay going to Qingdao. For the arms matter, a telegram from Aoki to the General Staff would carry more weight, etc. I have already passed on the circular telegram […] to him and urged him to telegraph in detail, because the telegrams he relays through the Minguo newspaper office get no reply. — [Chi]. The 26th.

## 0130a | 31 May 1916 | Tokyo → Shanghai (Junzaburo) | JACAR B03050089200, PDF page 13 | sent 1.30 pm

The American Chinese staying in Yokohama number about sixty; they want to go and serve at Weixian (濰縣): what should be done? Reply. We hear that the American branch [of the party] has a great deal of funds on deposit. Please press it by telegram.

## 0132 | 31 May 1916 | Tokyo → Shanghai (Junzaburo) | JACAR B03050089200, PDF page 15 | sent 10.30 am

About the military notes (軍用票): how should we proceed? Reply quickly. — Chi. The 31st.

## 0131a | 1 June 1916 | Tokyo → Shanghai (Junzaburo) | JACAR B03050089200, PDF page 14 | sent 11.35 am

For the bonds sent to Lufang (魯芳), proceed as planned. About the military notes, Juesheng (Ju Zheng) telegraphs: if Tokyo cannot meet the emergency, he will have them printed himself, etc. — Chi.

## 0263 | 15 June 1916 | Tokyo → Shanghai (Junzaburo) | JACAR B03050089400, PDF page 53 | sent 1.45 pm

Telegram received. The inconvertible notes: may 'provisional note' be added on the face? Should they be signed? If so, who would be best to sign? But Sir [Sun]'s signature might cause problems. May I accompany the notes to Shandong (魯)? [Wang] Zhaoming (兆銘), as well as Kayano (萱野) together with Sakamoto (坂本), take two (?) aircraft to […]. The flying school is entrusted for the time being to Tachibana (立花); among the students, half have no hope of success. Sakamoto, after consultation, asks for them to be replaced; reply quickly. […]

## 0328 | 21 June 1916 | Shanghai → Tokyo (Linwailuk) | JACAR B03050089800, PDF page 53 | sent 8.7 pm

Yokohama Specie Bank (正金): have the two […] thousand yuan of June been received? Shanghai is short of money; […] cable Tokyo to raise funds. The military notes should bear the signature of the person in charge of issuing them. Noted that Sakamoto’s (坂本) flying school is closing.
