# “Manila” table: English translation

Working translations from the Wenmi site; […] = passage not translated for want of a sure reading.

## 0164 | 6 January 1915 | Tokyo (頭山滿) → “Konglipo”, Manila | JACAR B03050087600, PDF page 2 | sent 3.40 pm

There is nothing of the sort. — Wen, replying.

## 0430a | 23 March 1916 | Manila → “Rosamonde”, Tokyo | JACAR B03050088200, PDF page 48

Tan Gen has arrived in Manila; he is taking aeroplanes to support Long Jiguang and leaves Manila tomorrow. What should be done? Reply urgently. — Ying.

## 0470 | 24 March 1916 | Tokyo → Manila (Konglipo) | JACAR B03050088300 | sent 8.45 pm

Bacon (倍根) […] has not written a receipt: it is feared that this has no value in law. There is no need to sue him; we can only [press for payment (?)] on simple common sense. See how he replies; we will then find other means.

## 0445 | 27 March 1916 | Tokyo → Manila (Konglipo) | JACAR B03050088300 | sent 11.50 am

The Uncle telegraphs that Fujian still needs funds: raise some and remit them to Japan, for transfer to Shanghai; also have Iloilo (怡朗) informed. — Wen (Sun Yat-sen).

## 0461b | 27 March 1916 | Manila → Tokyo (Rosamonde) | JACAR B03050088300

Who is using our code to telegraph from Shanghai? The instruction to hand 50,000 to Yamada (山田) in Shanghai is suspicious. From now on, use the code left by Zhantang (Hu Hanmin). — Ying.

## 0478a | 31 March 1916 | Manila → Tokyo (Rosamonde) | JACAR B03050088300

Please draw 4,000 yuan at the HSBC. Reply. — Ying.

## 0566 | 12 April 1916 | Manila (65, compac) → Rosamonde (10 words) | JACAR B03050088500, PDF page 16

[…] Telegram: southern Fujian will soon declare independence. Please […] provide assistance. […] Instructions requested.

## 0016 | 3 May 1916 | Manila → Tokyo (Tōyama) | JACAR B03050089000, PDF page 11

Funds remitted to Hong Kong; no bonds are left; please […] 5,000 yuan in notes; the certificates and the promotion and merit medals, send them quickly. I expect to return home in the next few days. — Cheng (誠), with respects.
