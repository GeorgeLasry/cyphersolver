# Swatow telegram, 3 April 1916: English translation

Chaozhou city has declared independence under Mo Qingyu. Our army too has recovered Swatow. Afterwards Mo came with a
large body of troops and ordered us out of the garrison headquarters. Our army's strength is weak; for the time being we
are holding on with the support of 翼□. Wenhui returns to [?Hong Kong] … [last word garbled]. (Tanaka)
