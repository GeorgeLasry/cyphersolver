# R1854 image 4: Francesco de' Cattanei to Gianfrancesco Gonzaga, Rome, 13 February 1430: English translation

After the Florentine's departure I went to our lord [the Pope] [?...] fully about the coming of the Lord Marquis's
servant, who has not yet come. He answered: I shall do it gladly. The Magnificent Lord Carlo told me that if [?I
believed] the business [?...] did not have a place [?...] [?I would seek] a kinship with King Louis, and perhaps obtain
[?...] the petition [?...] Florence. I shall press for the bull and send it by [?Trrusius]. Giovanni de [?Orlandis] had a
galliot at sea, which took a Catalan ship and brought itself into the harbour of Gaeta; [?two] Catalan galliots came
there, recovered the ship with its goods and captives, and took away with them [?also] the galliot of the said Giovanni;
he loses the value of five thousand ducats. The King of Castile presses the King of Aragon hard; it is believed [?...]
will make him lose his state [?...]. [?Berardello], who had come here for those lords of Rimini to make an offer, et
cetera, left discontented and could obtain nothing. The Lord Archbishop of Patras, with our lord's licence, wished to
give the temporal lordship to the lordship of the [?Venetians], and they would defend it against the infidels. Our lord
had it proposed to the cardinals; they answered that it is better to lose it than to hand it to the Venetians, because
once [lost] it will be recovered; but if the Venetians had it, it would never be possible to recover it. Niccolò da
Tolentino's chancellor left here with his business well settled; hence it is believed that Niccolò must soon withdraw
from Bertinoro. Bonifacio de [?Meana] [?...] came here on the business of the lord abbot, his brother; our lord restored
the abbey [to him] and freed the said abbot entirely from all [charges] and freed him from excommunication. But the said
Bonifacio left here with a great company without taking leave of his host; it is presumed this must be because some
dispute has arisen between those of [?Canedulum] and them. Six citizens of Bologna are expected here, namely two of the
greater, two of the middling, and two of the lesser [orders]. ⟨Rome, 13 February 1430.⟩
