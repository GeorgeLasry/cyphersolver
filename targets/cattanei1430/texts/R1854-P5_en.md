# R1854 image 5: Francesco de' Cattanei to Gianfrancesco Gonzaga, Rome, 21 February 1430 (verso): English translation

Lord Astorgio and Lord Bartolomeo, ambassadors of the lords of Rimini, appeared on Monday before the commissaries and
produced a mandate and certain authenticated copies of bulls. The said commissaries admitted them "if and in so far as"
[i.e. with reservation]. I believe it will help little. ⟨I have no money for expenses. Rome, 21 February 1430.⟩
