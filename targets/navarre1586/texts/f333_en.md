## Translation

The Duke of Mayenne's army is to … besiege Castillon … Montauban was nearly surprised through the treason and
conspiracy of the Sieur de Claux … Drake has wrought havoc among the Spaniards. He has ruined and razed the island of
Santo Domingo. He has gone to Cartagena and strikes terror into all the Spaniards. [162 = the King of Navarre] greatly
desires to have news of [204], of [189] [190] ⟨[…] and their advice on the road he is to take, and how⟩. He awaits
with good devotion what will be sent him in order to carry it out. [167] to assure that, as soon as ⟨the reiters
march, he will mount his horse; [166] and his brother are of good will; the levy must be hastened and set marching⟩ as
soon as can be. The King of Navarre has sent, as you know, the Sieur de Vesin de la Marsillière to Berne with the
ambassadors of the four cantons of Switzerland.
