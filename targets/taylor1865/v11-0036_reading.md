# Surget to Levy, Meridian, 6 April 1865: the telegram as sentences

Words that were in cipher are in capitals. The telegram is in English, so no translation is given. Abbreviations
are written out; the decryption against the transcription is in v11-0036_decryption.txt.

## Reading

Lieutenant Colonel William M. Levy, District Headquarters, Mobile.

SEE THAT SAM DUNCAN RETURNS PROMPTLY ON TIME. You will understand it is TO HIS ADVANTAGE TO DO SO. Think the enemy
have crossed river at Selma and going east, but have nothing definite.

E. Surget, Assistant Adjutant General.

## Notes

- Printed in clear in the Official Records, Ser. I vol. 49 pt. 2 (1897), p. 1209, with the same words: "See that
  Sam. Duncan returns promptly on time. You will understand it is to his advantage to do so. Think the enemy have
  crossed river at Selma and going east, but have nothing definite."
- Sam Duncan is not identified here.
