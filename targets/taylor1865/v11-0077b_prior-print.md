# 20 April 1865: Taylor to Secretary of War — equipment and movements

NARA vol. 11 frame 0077, manuscript p. 149, second telegram.
Prior plaintext located in OR I/49/2 p. 1255:

Meridian, April 20, 1865.
Hon. Secretary of War, through Major-General Cobb, Columbus or Macon, Ga.:

Have completed equipment of Mobile garrison for the field. No indication of
any decided movement from Mobile nor from Mississippi or Tennessee Rivers.
Former very high, all its lowlands being under water.

R. Taylor, Lieutenant-General.

The manuscript has cipher for EQUIPMENT OF MOBILE GARRISON FOR THE FIELD,
NO INDICATION OF, and DECIDED MOVEMENT FROM MOBILE NOR FROM (ANY is clear).
The beginning GEGMGQX... agrees with COME RETRIBUTION. The flowing capitals
have NOT yet been fully transcribed/aligned; this is a prior-print pointer,
not a completed new manuscript reading. Keep this separate from the repeated
14 April dispatch that ends above it on the same page.
