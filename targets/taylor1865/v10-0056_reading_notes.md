# 18 January 1865: Taylor to Davis — provisional reading

NARA vol. 10, frame 0056, manuscript p. 107. Known key COMPLETE VICTORY
applied; uncertain proposed words marked [?]. Cipher words capitalized.

I have received orders to ASSUME COMMAND OF ARMY OF TENN. The condition of
THIS ARMY RENDERS THE ASSIGNMENT [?UNFORTUNATE] for the cause. The Army
and the Public UNANIMOUSLY [?DESIRE] GENL JOHNSTON. THIS ALONE [?WILL]
RESTORE [?CONFIDENCE]. My deliberate judgement is that JOHNSTON [?SHOULD]
be sent at once. [?THE] SAFETY OF [?THE] COUNTRY demands it.
[?GENL] BEAUREGARD [?IN] CAROLINA and JOHNSTON HERE [?WILL] [?RESTORE]
confidence in people army. I can do [?MORE] IN TRANS MISS EITHER IN
[?CROSSING] [?TROOPS] or creating diversions, than elsewhere, but am
[?CONTENT] to serve in a subordinate capacity IN ANY FIELD you may direct.
My profound convictions of our necessities impel me to express these opinions.

Respectfully, R. Taylor, Lieut Genl.

counted. It is a PARTIAL reading; it is not a completed new decipherment.
