# 20 April 1865: Taylor to the Secretary of War — request for policy

NARA vol. 11, frames 0076–0077, manuscript pp. 148–149.
Existing decipherment: OR Series I, vol. XLIX, part II, printed p. 1255.
Key COME RETRIBUTION, offset 0; key continues over cipher letters across the
page boundary. Clear prose does not consume key letters.

Meridian, April 20, 1865.
Hon. Secretary of War, through Major-General Cobb, Columbus or Macon, Ga.:

Following dispatch to the Government was sent 14th. Having received no
reply, it is repeated:

Your cipher of 7th relative to Georgia received last night. Instructions
had been sent Brigadier-General Adams to unite his infantry with General
Cobb, at Columbus, for defense of that place, as we could not expect to hold
Montgomery. MOBILE WAS EVACUATED BY GEN MAURY ON NIGHT OF ELEVENTH before
I could reach him from Cahaba River. Will send full report as soon as
possible. Am fitting up transportation for MOBILE GARRISON. Can unite
everything with Cobb, in Georgia, or cover supply region of Alabama and
Mississippi and preserve communication with Mississippi River. ENEMY HAS
POWER TO OCCUPY country south and east of Alabama River, AS HIS LARGE
FORCE ENGAGED AT MOBILE IS NOW LIBERATED. Decision should be had at once
as to which of the courses to adopt. Ignorant of the policy of the Government,
I cannot decide.

R. Taylor, Lieutenant-General.

This is a full reading backed by an earlier printed decipherment. All 27
lexical cipher words / 124 letters have external plaintext; 114/124 candidate
manuscript letters agree directly. Ten glyph differences remain explicit in
the alignment. The manuscript's fused HASPOWER and divided ENGA/GED are
normalized here. GEN is kept as the cipher abbreviation. Spelling and
punctuation in clear prose are lightly normalized. The printed page number
was checked in OCR; the scan-page image is not independently verified.

This telegram repeats one of 14 April. The two dates must not be counted as
two independently transcribed ciphertext witnesses. The following 20 April
telegram on p. 149 is a separate document and remains to be aligned.
