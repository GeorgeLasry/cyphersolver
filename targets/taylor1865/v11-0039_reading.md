# Surget to Taylor, Meridian, 8 April 1865: the telegram as sentences

Words that were in cipher are in capitals. The telegram is in English, so no translation is given. Abbreviations
are written out; the decryption against the transcription is in v11-0039_decryption.txt.

## Reading

Lieutenant General R. Taylor, Demopolis, Alabama.

NORTHERN PAPERS JUST RECEIVED by General Hodge SAY RICHMOND FELL BY ASSAULT ON THIRD INSTANT. He will send them
here.

E. Surget, Assistant Adjutant General.

## Notes

- Printed in clear in the Official Records, Ser. I vol. 49 pt. 2 (1897), p. 1216, with the same words; the print
  has "on 3d instant" for THIRD INST.
- Richmond was evacuated on 2-3 April 1865 and occupied on 3 April; "by assault" is the Northern papers' account.
