Meridian March 30th 1865
Lieut Genl Taylor
Selma
Have sent Genls Lee & Pillow's despatches to be repeated
Genl Dana stated in Vicksburgh day before yesterday that
Sherman had been wounded in North Carolina, Thomas suc-
-ceeding him, To alleviate sufferings of sick have directed
Genl Hodge to permit Federal ambulances to ply between
Big Black and Jackson so long as they bring our own sick
and comply with such restrictions as he may adopt.
War Dept ought to be again recommended to - TSXMVZX -
- TWM - QTBHF - have no official - EVMVXIL - ROB
CNAH - UKA - NYK - LBJ - PBYBBG - HPTUX
[p. 41]
- YMF - entirely for the position
E Surget
AAG
