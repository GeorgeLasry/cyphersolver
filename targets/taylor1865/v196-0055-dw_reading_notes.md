# 9 March 1865: Bullock to D. W. Adams — keyword-change instruction

Meridian, March 9, 1865.
Brigadier-General D. W. Adams, Montevallo or Selma, Alabama:

In sending CIPHER DISPATCHES after this date use the FOLLOWING KEY WORD,
“COME RETRIBUTION.” Acknowledge receipt.

By order, W. F. Bullock Jr., Assistant Adjutant-General.

NARA RG 109, ch. II vol. 196, frame 0055, manuscript p. 105, third telegram
on the right-hand page. Cipher words capitalized; punctuation normalized.
Encrypted with COMPLETE VICTORY at zero offset, key advancing only over cipher
letters. All seven lexical cipher words / 47 letters are covered. One N/M
glyph remains ambiguous (NXM/MXM, KEY); 46/47 candidate letters agree directly.
The annotated sibling to Wirt Adams immediately above supports this reading.

Method: known key applied, checked against the adjacent annotated sibling.
The two telegrams repeat the same instruction; do not count them as independent
cryptanalytic recoveries. The original ink copies are distinct documents to
different recipients and both need to be counted for document coverage.
This is a dated local instruction, not evidence of a simultaneous army-wide
key change or proof of the day the new key was first used elsewhere.
