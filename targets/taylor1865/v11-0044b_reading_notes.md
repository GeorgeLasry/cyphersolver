# 10 April 1865: Surget to Maury — troops and stores

NARA vol. 11, frame 0044, manuscript p. 84, second telegram.
Existing decipherment: Official Records, Series I, vol. XLIX, part II,
printed p. 1223. Key COME RETRIBUTION, running over cipher letters, offset 0.

Meridian, April 10, 1865.
Major-General Maury, Mobile, Ala.:

From my knowledge of General Taylor's plans, TROOPS AND STORES MUST BE
MOVED IN THIS DIRECTION. Considerable force accumulating at Vicksburg
WILL MOVE WESTWARD, PROBABLY BY END OF THE WEEK.

E. Surget, Assistant Adjutant-General.

The text is available in full in the earlier printed edition: all 18 lexical
cipher words / 79 cipher letters have external plaintext. The manuscript
candidate stream agrees at 74/79 letters; five ambiguous readings remain in
the diplomatic transcription. This is existing decipherment checked against
ciphertext, not a new unpublished plaintext or a ciphertext-only solution.
