# 9 March 1865: Bullock to D. W. Adams — keyword-change instruction

Meridian, March 9, 1865.
Brigadier-General D. W. Adams, Montevallo or Selma, Alabama:

In sending CIPHER DISPATCHES after this date use the FOLLOWING KEY WORD,
“COME RETRIBUTION.” Acknowledge receipt.

By order, W. F. Bullock Jr., Assistant Adjutant-General.
