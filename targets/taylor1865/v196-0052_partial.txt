NARA vol.196 fr.0052, MS p.99, Taylor to Col. George Wm. Brent, Augusta,
Meridian March 8th 1865. IMAGE-CHECKED PARTIAL, not a completed telegram.

Clear opening: Genl Maury reports.
Opening cipher candidates: GBQQP EN CMBNM BKRPHK XYSNJIOX
COME RETRIBUTION -> ENEMY AU LEATT TWENTY THOUSAND
With N/M ambiguities in EN/EM and CMBNM/CMBMM:
ENEMY AT LEAST TWENTY THOUSAND.
26 cipher letters; 24 candidates agree directly. M has sometimes been split
into NI by OCR, especially in THOUSAND's cipher XYSNJIOX.

The middle cipher passages have not been fully transcribed or aligned.
Clear concluding phrase: ... had commenced ...
Final cipher: DSOZVH ZKWA AQFFL
At key offset 4, COME RETRIBUTION -> MOVING FROM NORTH, exactly.
This final offset was tested locally; continuity through the middle is NOT
established, so do not claim a full telegram reading or a complete alignment.

Date, addressee, opening and final cipher were checked on the full image and
fresh crop. This is partial image evidence of COME RETRIBUTION in a different
channel on 8 March, before the 9 March instructions to the two Adamses.
It does not supply a general first-use date.
