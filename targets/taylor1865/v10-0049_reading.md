# 9 January 1865: Taylor to President Davis

I have just returned from a visit to General Hood and his army. The army
NEEDS REST, CONSOLIDATION, AND REORGANIZATION. Not a day should be lost in
effecting these latter. IF MOVED IN ITS PRESENT CONDITION, IT WILL PROVE
UTTERLY WORTHLESS; this applies to both infantry and cavalry. Full powers
should be immediately given to the commander. For prudential reasons I use
one of the former key-words instead of the last.

R. Taylor, Lieutenant-General.
