NARA RG 109 ch. II vol. 196, frame 0055, manuscript p. 105.
Second telegram on right-hand page.

Meridian March 9th 1865
Brig Genl Wirt Adams
Jackson Miss

01 In sending - EWBWPV - WMN -
02 XCMQYCU - after this date use the - TAAWSPMIO -
03 NXM - NMTR -
04 "ODXI - KIOZKUIKGQB" - acknowledge receipt
05 By order
06 W F Bullock Jr AAG
07 Appd W F B Jr AAG

Pencil additions, recorded separately from the ink text:
Above EWBWPV: cipher
Above WMN: dis-
Above XCMQYCU: -patches
Above TAAWSPMIO: following
Above NXM: key
Above NMTR: word
Above ODXI KIOZKUIKGQB: Come Retribution

The pencil hand is not dated; do not call it a contemporary decipherment
without further evidence. Candidate NXM at line 03 reads LEY under the key;
MXM reads KEY, as explicitly supplied in pencil. N/M forms remain ambiguous.
All other 46 ink-candidate letters agree directly with COMPLETE VICTORY.
