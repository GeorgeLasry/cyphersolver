# Surget to Taylor, Meridian, 30 March 1865: the telegram as sentences

Words that were in cipher are in capitals. The telegram is in English, so no translation is given. Abbreviations
are written out; the decryption against the transcription is in v11-0022_decryption.txt.

## Reading

Lieutenant General Taylor, Selma.

Have sent Generals Lee and Pillow's despatches to be repeated. General Dana stated in Vicksburg day before yesterday
that Sherman had been wounded in North Carolina, Thomas succeeding him. To alleviate sufferings of sick have
directed General Hodge to permit Federal ambulances to ply between Big Black and Jackson so long as they bring our
own sick and comply with such restrictions as he may adopt. War Department ought to be again recommended to
RELIEVE COLONEL WATTS. Have no official CHARGES AGAINST HIM, BUT HIS HABITS UNFIT HIM entirely for the position.

E. Surget, Assistant Adjutant General.

## Notes

- Colonel Watts is probably Lt. Col. N. G. Watts, the Confederate agent for the exchange of prisoners. The first
  telegram on the same page (p. 40, to Brig. Gen. Hodge at Jackson) begins "Inform Col Watts that in consequence of
  enemy's movement in Ala Genl Pillow has stopped shipment of Prisoners to Vicksburgh". The identification was not
  checked further.
- The report that Sherman had been wounded was false.
