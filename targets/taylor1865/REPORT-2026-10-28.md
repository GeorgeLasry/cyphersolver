Contribution by Bryan Payne ([bdpayne](https://github.com/bdpayne)), with
OpenAI Codex (GPT-6), 28 October 2026. Builds on WAINUT's 27 October work;
all earlier transcriptions, alignments and credits are preserved.

**Status: in progress.** This contribution covers six further telegrams:
five have full readings supported by existing plaintext or an annotated sibling;
one remains provisional and is classified **decoded, not read**. Other passages
are still untranscribed or unresolved. This does not complete the three volumes.

## Principal finding: a dated local keyword-change order

Two telegrams on NARA vol.196 frame0055, manuscript p.105, are dated **9 March
1865**, from Meridian, signed by W.F. Bullock Jr., AAG, "By order." They tell
**Wirt Adams at Jackson** and **D.W. Adams at Montevallo or Selma**:

> In sending cipher dispatches after this date use the following key word,
> “Come Retribution.” Acknowledge receipt.

The orders themselves are encrypted with **COMPLETE VICTORY**. Each contains
47 cipher letters; 46 candidate glyphs agree directly, with one N/M ambiguity
in NXM/MXM, deciphering as LEY/KEY. The Wirt Adams copy has a complete pencilled
interlinear decipherment, including COME RETRIBUTION. Its writer and date are
unknown: this is existing adjacent plaintext, not a newly broken unread message.

A partial image check supports **COME RETRIBUTION in Taylor's Brent channel on
8 March**, before these Adams orders. The opening gives ENEMY AT LEAST TWENTY
THOUSAND with two N/M ambiguities; a closing passage gives MOVING FROM NORTH at
a locally tested key offset. The middle remains untranscribed, and full-stream
continuity is not claimed. The evidence establishes a recipient-specific
instruction issued on 9 March, not a simultaneous Confederacy-wide switch or
an earliest-use date.

Full evidence, exact image link and limits:
[KEY-CHANGE-1865-03-09.md](KEY-CHANGE-1865-03-09.md).

These counts measure candidate-glyph agreement, not the share of coherent

letters are separate columns; no manuscript letter is silently replaced.

The **9 January** telegram warns that moving Hood's army without rest,
consolidation and reorganization would render it worthless. Its printed text
explicitly says Taylor is using a former keyword for prudential reasons.
Matching the plaintext to the image yields THENAVYOURPRIDE. This is distinct
from the historically reported OUR NAVY OUR PRIDE; its broader novelty is
unestablished. The 51/55 agreement after position39 is a retrospective
partition check after transcription/key refinement, not an independent blind
holdout. See [continuation_key.md](continuation_key.md).

The **10 April** telegram discusses moving troops/stores toward headquarters
and a force assembling at Vicksburg. The **20 April** message reports Mobile's
evacuation and asks Richmond to choose between joining Cobb in Georgia and
protecting the Alabama/Mississippi supply region. It repeats a dispatch of
14 April; those dates are not two independently transcribed cipher witnesses.

The **18 January** text advocates Johnston's command and offers subordinate
service, but fifteen lexical corrections remain unconfirmed. They are marked
[?] in the reading and excluded from completed-reading claims.

## Validation and limits

`python3 targets/taylor1865/continue_analysis.py` reproduces six alignments,
key-offset comparisons, random-key comparisons for 9 January, and an arithmetic
round-trip control. It is an alignment tool, not a ciphertext-only solver;
no language model was trained or synthetic solver-recovery claim made.

`python3 docs/_check_profile.py taylor1865` validates the structured profile.
The overall fraction_read is intentionally unknown because no exhaustive
inventory exists. Per-reading measures and marked holes are recorded in
`*_read_measure.txt`; the original four profile text entries are preserved.

The repository's reading diagnostic was also run. Cipher/alignment tables are
naturally flagged as nonprose, and short orders' headings, names and abbreviations
lower its dictionary score. Source-backed sentences were reviewed as sentences
by Codex, recorded as a model review rather than independent human review.
The provisional 18 January item is conservatively decoded/not read.
