# 9 March 1865: Bullock to Wirt Adams — keyword-change instruction

Meridian, March 9, 1865.
Brigadier-General Wirt Adams, Jackson, Mississippi:

In sending CIPHER DISPATCHES after this date use the FOLLOWING KEY WORD,
“COME RETRIBUTION.” Acknowledge receipt.

By order, W. F. Bullock Jr., Assistant Adjutant-General.

NARA RG 109, ch. II vol. 196, frame 0055, manuscript p. 105, second telegram
on the right-hand page. Cipher words are capitalized; spelling and punctuation
are normalized. The phrase is encrypted with COMPLETE VICTORY, starting at key
offset zero and advancing through cipher letters only, despite intervening
clear prose.

All seven lexical cipher words / 47 letters have plaintext: a pencilled
interlinear decipherment supplies every cipher word on this copy. Its date
and writer are unknown. One ink glyph (N/M in NXM/MXM, KEY) remains ambiguous;
46/47 candidate letters agree without reconstruction. This is an existing
adjacent decipherment verified with a known key, not an unread plaintext
newly broken here. No matching printed version was found in the bounded
normalized OCR search of OR I/49/1 or I/49/2 for “retribution,” “key-word,”
“keyword,” or “after this date use.” OCR search is not proof of nonpublication.

Historical finding: this office issued the new-key instruction to Wirt Adams
on 9 March, effective in the order's words “after this date.” It does not
establish when Adams received it or prove a global Confederate switch date.
