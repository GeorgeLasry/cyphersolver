NARA RG 109 ch. II vol. 11, frame 0076 p. 148, continuing frame 0077 p. 149.
Working transcription; candidate cipher glyphs retained.

Meridian April 20th 1865
Hon Sec of War
Thro Maj Genl Cobb, Columbus or Macon Ga
Following dispatch to the Government was sent on 14th -
having received no reply it is repeated.
"Your cipher of 7th relative to Georgia received last
night - Instructions had been sent to Brig Genl Adams
to unite his Infantry with Genl Cobb at Columbus for
defence of that place as we could not expect to hold Montgo-
mery - OCNMCI - PRA - FPTKINVSP - FP - KXB - UBOKG
- CA - PWSLK - SY - VTZPXVHU - before I could reach
[frame 0077, p. 149]
him from Cahaba river - Will send full report as soon as
possible - Am fitting up transportation for OCNMCI -
ZRZSCIWB - Can unite everything with Cobb in Georgia
or cover supply region of Ala and Miss and preserve
communication with Mississippi River, RPSYC -
YEIGWXYK - BC - BEQGIP - Country south & East of Ala
river,
EI - YQT - FTZUR - HCDGV - IGXI HYW - IH -
LQPUFV - ML - EWX - FAJSKCHQH - Decision should
be had at once as to which of the Courses to adopt.
Ignorant of the policy of the Government - I cannot decide.
R Taylor, Lieut Genl
Official, W F Bullock Jr, AAG

Candidate letters are provisional. KXB/E; VTZPXVHU/F; ZRZSCIWB/L;
YEIGWXYK/L; BEQGIP/T; EI/L; LQPUFV/ZQPUPV; FAJSKCHQH/FBJSECHQH
are checked against external plaintext in the alignment, without silently
replacing uncertain manuscript glyphs. Cipher HASPOWER is fused in the
manuscript; ENGA/GED is divided there. The printed edition uses Cahawba
and "General Maury on night of 11th"; those variants do not alter the message.
