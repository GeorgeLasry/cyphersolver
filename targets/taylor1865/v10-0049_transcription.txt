NARA RG 109, ch. II vol. 10, frame 0049, manuscript p. 92.
Meridian Jany 9 1865. To his Excellency President Davis, Richmond Va.
Working visual transcription; uncertain glyphs retained as candidate letters below.

I have just returned
from a visit to Genl Hood and his Army. The
Army GLIQS - MCGN - TDEARPBKECIJL -
OHV - GVWUKTVM-MAOCCH - Not a day should be
lost in effecting these latter. - ZU - DWYIW - PR - VTN -
NEVJTEB - FSCKMGIJL - WN - NXCT - SV-
HCI - HTOCFFP - LTZWLELWF - This
applies to both Infantry and Cavalry. Full
powers should immediately be given to the Com-
mander. For prudential reasons I use one of
former key words instead of the last.
Respectfully, R. Taylor, Lieut Genl.

Glyph qualifications (not independent of the printed plaintext):
MCGN: M has multiple strokes; the initial OCR/transcription read IVCEN.
TDEARPBKECIJL: C/G uncertain at letter 10.
OHV: V/U uncertain at letter 3.
GVWUKTVM-MAOCCH: V/U at letter 7, C/G at letter 12.
VTN: last letter is overwritten; read as N provisionally.
NEVJTEB: E/F and V/Y uncertain at letters 2 and 3.
FSCKMGIJL: C/G uncertain at letter 3.
LTZWLELWF: T/F uncertain at letter 2.

there is re-enciphered from the printed text, NOT a claim of exact manuscript letters.
