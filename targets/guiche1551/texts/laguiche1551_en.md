# La Guiche to Montmorency, Rome, 22 Nov 1551 (BnF fr. 3138 no. 22, f. 60v): English translation

Translation (Claude Opus 5.5, 23 Oct 2026) of `texts/laguiche1551_reading.md`: the deciphered cipher runs (in ⟨ ⟩;
key recovered ciphertext-only) with the clear words round them as far as the repository has them. Same holes: `[…]`
clear words not transcribed or unread signs, `[::]` the undeciphered four-dot code sign, `[?word]` doubtful.

## Translation

*Struck through by the writer:* ⟨[…] Die[go] […] to put in prison in this [?town] […] a [?Sienese] […] believe
th[at] […] informed the⟩

⟨trial, which I have found means to see⟩, in order to inform the said Lord Cardinal of its contents, so that on that
basis he may lay such foundation as he shall think fit, ⟨in the negotiation you have heard of.⟩ And also to tell the
said Lord Cardinal the remarks which [?Mignet] made to ⟨the ambassador of Siena who was here, who has left to return
to Siena with [Dom Diego] […]⟩. And since the said Lord Cardinal has begun to write to you of this affair, I shall
refer to what he will write to you of it. I hear that ⟨the Count of Santa Fiora is ill satisfied with [::], and that
he would easily come over to the King's service⟩ […] of the Seance […] ⟨the service⟩ […]. For the said person who was
here negotiating ⟨with me the business of the Lord Barnabé Adorno⟩ […] the answer that […] […] ⟨inform you of the
service the King could have from the said Lord Barnabé, and also of the conditions he would ask.⟩
