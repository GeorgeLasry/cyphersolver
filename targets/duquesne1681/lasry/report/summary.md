# NAF 22736 — Duquesne, ff137r–138r and144v

Source: George Lasry, report PDF `46_naf-22736-duquesne-ff137r138r-and144v.pdf` (received 2026-10-24), page 1.

**Contents:** Duquesne discusses negotiations with the Tripolins, the return of enslaved French subjects and captured ships, vessels suitable for use as fire-ships, treaty provisions and naval affairs. The later letter states the obligation to retain the position until express orders arrive and the hope of ending the campaign advantageously.
