# R923, ff. 233-234: from St Germains, [29] Sept [1645]

Header: one letter and a postscript on the second leaf (image 2), which breaks off after "give him".

## Reading

St Germains, Sept [29].

[529] my {dispatch} of the last week to the King and to [120], there is more to add now. I write only to wrpte, and
to tell your [sic] the King will receive more from the Queen this [?post]; {she} had the meagrim today, which is a very
just eocuse. Our {design} for the [424] advances well, and again the Duke of Lorrain puts us in some little remote
hopes, but that ought not to be built upon yet. {Do} I beleive some forrain [?succour] is so like to be had that I
already apprelend the comeing togethere of our forces for the releef of [85], least that draw on a battail, which I
think ought as much to be avoyded as the releif of [85] endeavoured. The releif of the place will give us that which
wee only ought now to look for after so many disasters, the advantage of winter without any furthere ill effects of
{them}; and the baitail will take that from us if wee lost it. I am going to Fountainbleau in this instant and have
no time to tell you why until next week. I am entirely yours.

Pray return me word whether the [257] [300] had ever any order to cea[60] the person of [148], for Porter haih told us
hear, and to his father also, that it was {design}[ed] by {them} to be donne. If there were no [489] order nor no caws
for it, as I suppose there could be non[e], it wnll be necessary to take from him the jealowsies [of] his father upon
this ocation; on naeds give him [...]

## Notes

- "from the Queen this post": the transcription has 126 (the King); on the image the last figure is a 7 written over a
  6, i.e. 127, the Queen (Lasry: "the King (Queen?)").
- "to tell your": 554 (your) on the image, its 4 written over a 5 (555, you); the writer's correction, kept.
- "some forrain [?succour]": the figures are 341 (for) r a 382 (in) 497, i.e. "forrain" (foreign), checked on the
  image; Lasry read "Lorrain support". 497 sits in the S block of the codebook.
- [85]: a two-figure number with no value, twice, each time after "the releef of"; a place (perhaps Chester, whose
  relief was attempted in late September 1645), not identified.
- "cea[60]": 60 has no value in the key (seize?).
