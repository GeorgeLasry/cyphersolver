# “Laisaosang” table: English translation

Working translations from the Wenmi site; […] = passage not translated for want of a sure reading.

## 0104a | 28 June 1914 | Tokyo Aoicho → Laisao Sung, 70 Broadway Terrace, Shanghai ; signé 頭山満 | JACAR B03050087400, PDF page 45

A letter will be sent to Gao tomorrow; three hundred yuan are to be passed on for your family expenses. On receipt, come to Tokyo. “Receipt” includes a proposed reading.

## 0182 | 31 January 1915 | Tokyo (頭山滿) → “Laisaosang”, Shanghai | JACAR B03050087600, PDF page 20 | sent 6 pm

Pass on to Mr Tominaga: once his mission is done, he must wait in Shanghai for orders; on no account return to Japan. Because the government …, if he comes back he will not be able to leave again. — Wen

## 0185 | February 1915 | Tokyo (頭山滿) → “Laisaosang”, Shanghai | JACAR B03050087600, PDF page 23 | sent 6 pm

What day do you leave for Hong Kong? Reply.

## 0186 | 2 February 1915 | Shanghai → “Rosamonde”, Tokyo | JACAR B03050087600, PDF page 24 | sent 3.42 pm

These days … very strict, so I cannot take an ordinary boat; I must wait for the 7th to be able to go.
