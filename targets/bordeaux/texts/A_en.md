# Bordeaux to [Brienne], London, 30 May 1653 NS (BL Add MS 4200 ff. 88-90, DECODE R8390): English translation

[clear] This will show you that the news of my return to France is not true.

[cipher] Not that, if our affairs were more flourishing, we would not have reason to show some dissatisfaction at the
reception given to the deputies of Bordeaux. They have been heard by two commissioners of the Council, and the audience
I had asked of the General was given me only after theirs. [..]; they [..] their proposals {332} to the {396‾} [..]
[?separate] {344}, and I believe that they deliberate on them as well as on mine, although after the resolutions taken in
the [?Parliament] nothing remained but to sign the treaties on which we had agreed. I am warned from several quarters that
they mean to help Bordeaux indirectly. That is why, if you have not yet offered your mediation, it could be deferred
until I see what the conduct of this State will be. In my last discourse I did not fail to assure them that His Majesty
[?would] employ himself in it with affection; but if they take measures with Bordeaux, they will not have much confidence
in our goodwill. The fear that, if [?France] finds itself purged of seditions, it may favour the party of {25‾}, and the
refuge that has been given to {28‾}, are capable of [?making] the General take a resolution contrary to our interests.
If the {43‾} send deputies here to treat of [30'], as is being published, he will [35'] even more. Not that his own
interest ought not to oblige him to seek our alliance; but he believes he will [68'] in time even when he has delivered
Bordeaux from the misery in which it finds itself. In a few days I shall be informed of it with more certainty.

[clear] There is no news here of the fleet since it appeared off Scotland ... The government is still in the same state;
but it is believed that within [..] Cromwell will have a body composed of seven score persons chosen by the army and
approved by the provinces. ... The Swedish resident has not yet been able to [get] an answer to his offer, nor the
Portuguese a resolution on his peace treaty. ... De Bordeaux. London, 30 May 1653 [N.S.]
