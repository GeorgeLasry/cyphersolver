# Vestigia 267, Maffeo Trivilense to Ludovico Sforza, Vienna, 2 Apr 1490: English translation

Translated from `V267_reading.md` (Claude Opus 5.5, 23 Oct 2026): the clear opening and close of the letter and the
cipher as given by its contemporary clear extract (Vestigia 268), which the key decode of the cipher confirms.

## Translation

The Turk's ambassador is still here; this Most Serene Lord King has heard him only once. The prelates and some
barons have been with him many times since, and as far as I can understand, among other things he is also treating
with him of a prolongation of the peace. What more I can learn I shall report to Your Lordship, who I wish to be
certain that I shall not fail to investigate. I commend myself to Your Excellency. Vienna, 2 April 1490.
