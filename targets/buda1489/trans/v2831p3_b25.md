## Pass 2 with the original key R5411 (23 Oct 2026)

The Milan chancery's key "Cum Mapheo Triviliensi" (ASMi Sforzesco cart. 1597 no. 111, DECODE R5411; transcription

Sign decisions from the key:
- **x̄ (x with a bar above) = Ma**, distinct from the crossed x̸ = et. The key writes Ma as x with an o-stroke above; on
  this page the stroke is a short bar, and the sign stands where "ma" is wanted at every occurrence:

  ro-**ma** = **Roma** (L11, L17 twice, L19). Adopted: it holds at every occurrence. (L22 "x̄ o-bar // g r" = ma tena
  follows.)

  Regina". M: = Re de Hungaria confirmed (L13, L20, L27, L31).

- **Ra = madonna Bianca** (Bianca Maria Sforza, the bride promised to John Corvinus): L28 "la [R a]".
- **+ = Episcopus Varadinensis**, not a null (the key's nulls are ∸ ÷ w = ∩∩ B b-tail): L24 "lo [+] de le" = lo
  Vescovo Varadino.
- **ƀ (b crossed at the top) = fa**, distinct from b̶ (stroke below left) = ca: L02 "forse lo **fa** con arte"; L22
  "in **fa**vore del re Ferdinando" (ƀ 6 E q^e). Look-alike b-forms: counted.
