# DECODE R1098 - Maffeo da Treviglio to Ludovico Sforza, Buda 12 January 1490: reading of the ten cipher lines (v3)

Rests on: the 18 Sept 2026 reading (`r1098_reading.md`, lines 1-4 and 7-8 unchanged) and a re-read on 23 Oct 2026 of
lines 5-10 from native crops of IMG_R1098_I5648_P1 with the original key "Cum Mapheo Triviliensi" (ASMi Sforzesco
cart. 1597 no. 111, DECODE R5411, `key_R5411.md`), found by the b25 re-read and not available on 18 Sept. Sign
corrections: `trans/r1098p1_v3_corrections.tsv`. The clear p. 2 of the same letter says independently that the legate,
the other ambassadors and Maffeo decided to leave on the 15th by the road to Vienna (ll. 7-10 repeat it). New values
used, all from R5411: n with a long crossbar = tt ("tutti"), ɒ = l ("lochi"), ƀ = fa ("facto"), 7 =
f ("edificare"), ʒ = r and δ = s ("presto"), n# = ss ("andassimo", "spassando"), ꝛ = e ("fece"). Holes: [?word]
partly carried, [word] supplied, [...] unread signs.

Checked against the contemporary decipherment printed by Nagy & Nyáry, MDE IV (1878) no. 91, pp. 161-162 ("Extractus
Ziffri Maphei Triviliensis. 12. Januarii 1490"; main-checkout `mde4_no91.txt` and NOTES, print search of 9 Oct 2026,
which quotes it for the lines this re-read changes): l. 5 "divertira a tutti li luoghi ove ha fatta edificare"; l. 8
"siamo"; l. 9 "Sua Maiesta mi fece" and "andassimo"; l. 10 "perche Lei voleva andar spassando". The sign re-read agrees
with each of these. Where the print fills a gap the reading follows it and says so: l. 9 G (б, the nomenclator's Sua
Maiestas) = Sua Maiesta, "mi" from the print; l. 10 ẋ = perche from the print (no key value), "voleva" (the figures give
vole-c-a). The two signs after "ove ha facto" (÷ and an o+ form; the key's o+ is Imperatore) have no word in the print
and stay [...]. The cipher's last words, "non sono piu lon[...]", are not in the printed extract.

## Reading

Questo Ser.mo Re partite de qua ali 8 del presente per andare ad Vienna, non ta[nto] per che la speranza de la dieta
lo tiri, quanto per proprio desiderio de andarli; et che sia el vero, questi prelati mi hano dicto che in questo
camino divertira ad tutti li lochi ove ha facto [...] edificare, che sono molti, et non si condura ad Vienna piu [...]
presto che in 20 o 25 di. Lo legato et li altri oratori hano, et io con loro havemo, deliberato partire ali
15 de questo, et siamo rimasti dreto, perche Sua Maiesta mi fece dire che andassimo recta via ad Vienna, perche lei
voleva andare spassando. Non sono piu [...].

## English

This Most Serene King left here on the 8th of this month to go to Vienna, not so much because the hope of the Diet
draws him as from his own wish to go there; and in proof, these prelates have told me that on this journey he will
turn aside to all the places where he has had building done, which are many, and will not reach Vienna sooner than in
20 or 25 days. The legate and the other ambassadors have, and I with them have, decided to leave on the 15th of this
month, and we have stayed behind because His Majesty had me told that we should go straight to Vienna, since he wished
to travel at leisure. They are no more [...].
