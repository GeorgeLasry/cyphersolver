# The original key: "Cum Mapheo Triviliensi", ASMi Carteggio Sforzesco cart. 1597 no. 111 (DECODE R5411)

Found 23 Oct 2026 (re-read batch b25) by reading the heading of every cart. 1597 key record on DECODE (134 records,
PDFs already downloaded for it1583; contact sheets by research/reading_audit/b25_keysheets.py). The DECODE record has no
date, author or transcription ("1450 - 1555"); the heading "Cum Mapheo Triviliensi" and the nomenclator (Rex
Hungariae, Regina Hungariae, Jo. Corvinus, Ludovicus dux Bari, Dux Mediolani, Episcopus Varadinensis, Madonna Bianca)
make it the Milan chancery's copy of Maffeo da Treviglio's key of 1489-90. Every value Somogyi (2016) reconstructed and
key.md lists can be checked against it. The leaf is torn at the upper right: the T, U/V, X, Y, Z, & columns of the
alphabet are lost except their first signs. Image: DECODE IMG_R5411_I30419_P.pdf (native 1758 x 2397 jpeg), ASMi,
not public domain; kept in the session scratchpad only.

A second key headed "Cum Mafeo Triviliensi" is cart. 1597 no. 107 (DECODE R5407): a different alphabet (A = ⊥ ...);
probably a later cipher of Maffeo's (1490-92: R1158? R1152?). Not used here.

Glyph names follow key.md where the shape is the same; doubtful shapes in [ ].

## Against key.md and the 2026 readings

- Confirmed: a = r/i/f; c = s; d = x; e = 4, //; f = 7; g = 8; h = 9; i = n; l = d; m = a; n = g, v; o = ε, 3;
  p = c; q = ); r = z-forms (the key's ʒ); s = δ (key.md ð); t = p̸; u = 6; doubles nᵉ = ll, n# / n-crossed-4 = ss,
  n-long-crossbar = tt; syllables mᵃ sa, mᵉ se, mᵒ si, mᵘ so, qᵉ re, qˢ ro, Lᵃ ta, L. te, d: fi, x' do, bᵒ no;
  nulls w and ÷; M: = Ser.mo Re de Hungaria; Ra = madonna Bianca; + = vescovo varadino.
- Corrected: the o and o-bar signs are **b**, not t (key.md 58); the 2026 forks had found "unbarred o = b" from
  ambasiatore, comproba, deliberata. a+ is **Sua Dominatio** and a# **Vostra Dominatio** (key.md had a+ = Ex.tia
  Vostra from context). Plain b with a top stroke is **co**, b̶ **ca**, b. **ce**.
- New: the word signs the forks could not place: ɑ· / Q. = Ill.mo Duca de Milano; o+ = Imperatore (the "δ+" of
  p. 2 l. 20?); ŧ. = Papa; Et = Sua Santita; G = Sua Maesta; M̄ = Zuan Corvino; M+ = Regina; B is a null.
  "qˢ x̄" = ro + [x with an o-stroke above] = **Ro-ma** if the second sign is the key's Ma (to be checked on the
  plate: x̄ = et elsewhere).

Note (b25 p. 3 fork, 23 Oct 2026): on V2831 p. 3 the Ma sign is written as x with a short bar above (x̄), not an
o-stroke; it is distinct from the crossed x̸ = et and gives "Roma" (qˢ x̄, four times), "affirmatione" (L14) and "ma"
(L12, L22). The sign at p. 3 L18 is a# (two crossings) = Vostra Dominatio. ƀ (crossed at the top) = fa is confirmed by
"fa con arte" (L02) and "favore" (L22); + = Ep. Varadinensis at L24. Plain q (not in this key outside the syllable rows)
reads e at every occurrence on p. 3.

Note (b25 fork, p. 2 ll. 17-30, 23 Oct 2026): checked against the image for Q., a#, a+, Papa, Bi m:, Fa, Fe, Ri, Ma (xo), B null, the a-forms (r, dotted 1, f) and the n-forms; no misreading found. On the letter the o-form also stands for **t** (intercepte, state, contentione, constantemente, ducati, arte on p. 2) besides b (habia); the torn T column probably held an o-form, so 'o = b' alone does not hold across the letter.
