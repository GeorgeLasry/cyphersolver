## System

Telegrams sent without encipherment, directly in the Chinese standard telegraph code (four-digit groups), 1913-1914 and June 1916. Telegrams from the same file sent without encipherment, directly in Chinese telegraph code: Li Yuanhong’s reply to Huang Xing (14 June), Huang Xing’s message to Tan Renfeng about Li Yuanhong, a telegram of Huang Xing about Shaanxi, one to Mukden, and a short message signed 亞; then, in the next volume (June–November 1916), the Shanghai notables’ appeal to Huang Xing (25 June), his salute to the rallied fleet (27 June), messages signed 羣 and 鍾 (July–August) and the telegrams sent to Shanghai after his death (November).

Key file: `(none: plain standard telegraph code)` (none). Each pair of syllables (four letters) gives one
four-digit standard telegraph code (電報新編), with no additive (here the digits are written directly).
