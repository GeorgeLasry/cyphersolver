# Rajan & Sivanantham 2025, Indus Signs and Graffiti Marks of Tamil Nadu: A Morphological Study

TN Dept of Archaeology Publication 357 (2025), ISBN 978-81-977842-5-5, 145 pp. Tamil Digital Library TVA_BOK_0065889.
PDF and text extract kept locally in this folder (git-ignored). Read 26 Oct 2026. Page numbers = printed pages.

- **Method:** shape matching only ("morphological", p. 37), against Mahadevan 1977 (p. 35 footnote). Their numbering:
  42 base signs, 544 variants, 1,521 composites (single marks combining base signs) (p. 29). No matching criteria or
  chance control. Plates 1-100 (pp. 41-140) pair an Indus sign with a graffito by image only.
- **No concordance table.** p. 35 lists 27 graffiti signs called exact parallels and 22 Mahadevan numbers, unpaired;
  the pairing is in tngraffiti.in's API (data/graffiti/graffiti_indus_map.tsv). Many comparisons are with Indus pottery
  graffiti, not seal signs.
- **Chronology:** no graffiti-bearing layer is dated; no counts by period. Table 2 (pp. 27-28): 20 early-iron dates
  (1030-3345 BCE) at other sites; the authors themselves flag the 4th-millennium dates (p. 28); one Mayiladumparai pair
  with the same BP age is calibrated to two different dates (p. 27). Graffiti dates must come from site reports.
- **Sequences:** graffiti read left to right (p. 18). Qualitative claims only: jar 16.0 and ladder 23.0 mostly initial
  in composites; wavy line 34.0 never alone, "as in the Indus script" (p. 37). No sequence of separate signs is
  compared with Indus texts.
- **Statistics:** Table 3 (pp. 29-32), 140 sites, 15,184 sherds reported; Table 4 (p. 33) type counts per base sign
  (some rows do not add up). "Nearly 60%" of base signs have Indus parallels (p. 35), without a method.
- **Conclusions:** the shared shapes "were not merely accidental" (p. 37), hedged as "at the embryonic stage" (p. 35).

Tested here: set 635 (graffiti pairs through their parallels: not Indus-like), set 637 (Indus-parallel graffiti not
deeper within sites). Set 638: the positional claims on the Indus side (jar = line-final ending, leftmost; ladder 6 of 15 initial, p = 0.06;
wavy line never alone but untestable). Set 639: the chance rate. Against 90-sign samples, Indus gives 5-6 exact parallels of 42, unrelated scripts 2-11;
at the loose level (exact + near) Indus 57%, Cypro-Minoan 64%: the book's ~60% is what simple marks find in any list.
