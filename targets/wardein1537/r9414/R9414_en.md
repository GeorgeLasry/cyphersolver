me, and how my gracious prince and lord dispatched me, I leave aside for this time. Almighty God […] has granted me by

Princely Grace will learn herewith. Gracious lord, these […] have come here: the embassy of the Pasha of Greek
Belgrade and of the Pasha of Bosnia. I have heard their errand [?…]: the lords have dispatched them […] to tell him
[…] that the Turkish emperor had ordered their lords that they, each with […] horses, should be armed in all haste,
and as soon as his beloved brother should summon them […], then march at once and do all that he …

(P2.20-P2.37) … [because] of it he will suffer great damage in his hereditary lands. Your Princely Grace will know
without doubt how to keep good watch over your lands and people and to load no cost upon yourselves […] [?…] Your
Princely Grace with […] bear; may be the […] is not well content that both your Princely Graces write him nothing at
all; his […] […] me holds […] nothing has come; I give reasons as I can. For my gracious lord, Your Princely Grace's
brother, has to this day neither ordered nor written me anything [?accordingly, since I such] so great danger myself,
I beg Your Princely Grace in all most humble trust that you will speak with your brother, my gracious [?prince] and
lord, that His Princely Grace will consider that I have stayed on as an old servant of His Princely Grace's lord and
father, and such great dang[er] …

(P3.10-P3.38) … the advance, that they may […], and serve Your Princely Graces as a faithful countryman and subject,

[?…] Your Princely Grace so […] […] […]; so I am driven to it that I must serve beside the Turkish dogs, so that I do
not know what can be done at this time in haste. The Turk is arming himself all the while […] for such an expedition;
so your graces will all …

(P4.02-P4.20) … say with good ground: even if […] a hundred thousand men, what he means to do: his intention is to win

(P4.36-P5.04) Your Princely Grace will [to] this […], who belongs to Hans Schwab of Kraków [?…] [?give], for the

commend myself herewith to Your Princely Graces. Given at Wardein […] the fourteenth day of March in the year
