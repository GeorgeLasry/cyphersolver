# AGS Estado leg. 1394 no. 247, García de Toledo to Philip II, Messina, 16 July 1565: English translation

## f. 1r

I brought Your Majesty such *bad news* by the last courier that it has not seemed right to me to hold this one back, so
that it may *partly* improve it. The *six hundred soldiers* that Don Juan de Cardona put *ashore*, God was pleased *to
guide them* so well that all *entered the Borgo safely*.

This man who has come out tells me that the *Grand Master's* joy was so great, *embracing* the men without *being able*
to speak, that *he wept for pleasure*; and he also says that the next day the same men went out to fall on the Turks and
*killed* more than two hundred, although *these sorties* I *wanted* him *by no means* to *make*, and I have *written* it
to him in *many letters* with great urgency.

From the *Grand Master* I have no *letter*, at which I am *astonished*, but I do not *take* it as a *bad* sign that he
forgets me, although I do not believe it will be *two or three days* before it comes; and according to what *he writes I
shall see to provide* whatever *can* be done for him.

Being without *galleys* *destroys* me, because some *effects* could be *achieved* against their *vessels* that come and
go; but with *those* that are here *occupied* in *gathering* the men, it is *not* possible *to provide for everything*. If
*those of Spain* came they would give me *much help*.

## f. 1v

The galleys *that were to enter by* the harbour *came within four* miles of it, in *very* good *order* and with great
*determination*; and that *night*, which will have been *Monday*, an *hour* before *dawn*, coming on *Tuesday*, the
*barqueta* that I *had* sent *to advise* the *Grand Master* of the *signals* *entered safely*, by which he had *word* of
what he *was to do*; and the *galleys* coming back on *Tuesday* night to within another *four* miles of the *harbour*,
the *Grand Master* made them *the* most *clear* *signals* by *fire* that they should not *enter* — and so says *Monsieur
de Lenni* and all the rest; and within the *appointed hour* he repeated *them* a *second* time, and the *galleys* *turned*
back out to *sea* so as *not* to be *discovered*, and have *arrived* here this *morning*.

Don Juan Çanoguera, who *was guiding them*, *did* it very *well*, and so did all the others. And this man does not know
*why* the *Grand Master* did not *want* to accept *these galleys*; although it might be because *the entry seemed* to him
very *difficult*, I do not fail to take it as a good *sign*.

All *our men* say that, as the *Grand Master* finished making the *signals*, they saw and *heard* a *great quantity* of
arquebus fire, and that *they* judged that it was a *salute* to *show us* that *our* men were *inside*.

This man who has come *left the Borgo* on the same Monday, the nineteenth, and *brings* no news of *the barqueta* that
*entered*, because it will not yet have *arrived*; which [barqueta] put *the man* I *sent* where I ordered him, and from
there

## f. 2r

he was to *swim* to *the Borgo*, and so he *arrived safe*, as is clearly seen from *the signals*; and the *city* made the
*same fire*, which is also an *indication* that the man I *sent* by that *side* with the *duplicate* *arrived*.

The *times* are such and the *needs* so great that even if *one had* much money I *would not dare to part* with a *real*
to pay *debts*; and although what Your Majesty orders concerning Juan Andrea is most just, little *can be done* with him,
but what *can* will not *fail* to be done as Your Majesty orders, for indeed I desire to give him every satisfaction, and
he alone will be the one at this time who *will collect from me* any quantity.

It is necessary to *set* hand at once *to begin to fortify*, against all that *could happen*, the *front* that *Syracuse*
has *on the mainland*, and to *raise* a cavalier *on the sea* side that *corresponds* — it is certainly *shoals* where it
*could* be *assaulted*. It is also necessary *to begin*, at the end of *October*, *the fortification of Augusta*, so that
the *occasion* should not show *the enemy* the second jump. For these things *money* is needed, and in *this* kingdom *there
is nothing* but *infinite debts*.

Let Your Majesty order a reckoning to be made of the *Italians* who are to be *raised*, and of the *Spaniards* who are to
be paid, and of the pay *of the ships, which every month comes to forty thousand escudos*, besides two *months' pay* owed
them; and let account be taken also of the *biscuit* that *must* be made anew, for although the galleys' share is later
deducted, still this *money* comes out of what Your Majesty has sent me. *Five hundred quintals* of artillery by the
weight

## f. 2v

*of Spain* have been bought, and much *cordage and timber* for the *netting*; and although this *cordage and timber* can
serve for *the galleys* that are to be built, and what is *metal is money* that is not *lost*, still it costs a good
deal. *Equipment* is also being made for the *eight galleys* that are coming from *Spain*, and other expenses for *casks,
barrels, morions and corselets* that cannot be avoided.

And if this is reckoned up, and the *pay* of the *infantry* is added, counting also the expense of the *eleven galleys*
that are being newly *armed*, Your Majesty will see that the provision made *is not enough* for *so many* things, nor *can*
I *sustain* with it the *expenses* that Your Majesty notes in *your letter*, all the more *if this* were drawn out at all.
And of the *forty-eight thousand ducats* that came for the *galleys*, *half* was already *eaten*, and another good part *is
owed to* the men. And *considering* this, I beg Your Majesty to order *provision* as shall seem best for your service; and
in *spending them* the care that Your Majesty orders will be taken.

This man who has *come out* says that all the *Turks* who are *ashore*, besides the *bastions* where they have their
*batteries*, are all *surrounded* by *trenches* seven, eight and ten *palms high*, both on the side of *our men* and on the
*side* of the *open country*; and that when *he left* there were no *others* without them except *one thousand five
hundred Turks* who were *at the water*. He confirms that they *had* lost *many men*, and that in the *camp sicknesses were
running* of which *they were dying*.

From those *reports* Your Majesty will see *the ships* that the *vessel* that *was going* with *wine* to the *Goleta*
found off *Cape Bon*. I hold it for certain that *they are those of Algiers*, who *are coming to join the armada*; and this
I believe because I do not know that any number *of them* has *left* *Malta*, for this man *says* that no *ship* had
*detached* *from the armada* when *he left*, nor does the *governor* of the *city* *write* it, as Your Majesty will see by
his original letter, which will go with this.

Whose life may Our Lord keep for as many years as his servants and vassals desire and Christendom needs. *From Messina*,
16 July 1565.

## Postscript (entirely in the clear)

I have already written to Your Majesty in other letters how well the Count of Modica serves you, and at his own cost;
and the Admiral his father having now written to him of the death of the Duchess his mother, and calling him to go to
Spain, although this matters much to him, he has not wanted to do it, nor to leave this kingdom until he sees the
outcome of things here. It has seemed to me very just to inform Your Majesty of it, so that you may understand his will
and the service he does Your Majesty in it.
