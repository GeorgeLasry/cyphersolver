# R9956, Francisco de los Cobos to his nephew Juan Vázquez, Brussels, 11 January 1531 (English)

## Translation

Very noble sir and nephew. Benavides, who left fourteen days ago today, will by now have arrived there; with him an answer went to everything that had been written from there up to then, except what the courier brought who arrived at the time of his departure. Four days ago the courier came whom the archdeacon of Segovia dispatched about the regimiento (council office) of Toledo, and the Emperor was very pleased with Her Majesty's letter and to hear of her health and of the Prince our lord and of the lady Infanta.

{As this merchants' courier is being dispatched, which I was [?told of] at the moment, I write this at once so that the Empress may know of the Emperor's health and of affairs here, until a courier is sent with the answer to what has been [?written] from there, which is in hand. The Emperor is not writing because there is no time, for he did not know of this courier's departure until that very hour. He is very well, [?thanks] be to our Lord, and is beginning to attend to the affairs of this country, where there is much to do. Monsieur de Praet, as was written by Benavides, has left for France. On the council (of the Church) no decision has yet been taken on the answer to be given to the Protonotary Gambara, in order to wait for the opinion of the King of the Romans and to see what Monsieur de Praet finds in the King of France. All of Italy is at peace; and although in Siena, where there has been some disturbance between the exiles, who a few days ago were restored to the city by an agreement and settlement made through His Majesty's hand and authority, and those who were inside, His Majesty has provided what is needed so that the city may be quiet and at rest and live peaceably. The Spanish infantry has been and is still in that district, and has been and is very necessary to settle the affairs of the city. The Pope is in everything of the mind that has been written at other times. Of the Turk there are reports of various kinds: some that he is making great preparations to come into Christendom this year, and they even say by Hungary and Italy; others that he will not come this year; so that there is no certainty about it, although by more channels it is affirmed that he will come. [?I] know nothing certain. I will give an account of all this to Her Majesty, kissing her hands for me.}

And excuse me to all those gentlemen that I do not write to any of them. May our Lord keep your very noble person and house as you desire. From Brussels, 11 January 1531. February [...]. At your service, Cobos, comendador mayor.

Address: To the very noble sir my nephew Juan Vázquez, secretary of His Majesty.
