# R9767: Alonso Sánchez to Charles V, Venice, 16 August 1523 — English

Translation of `R9767_reading.md` (cipher from the contemporary decipherment on f. 567r, checked against the figures).
Same holes.

Since Your Majesty will see all that is happening from the letter that the Protonotary Caracciolo and I are writing
together, I will say nothing in this one except that I received Your Majesty's letter of the 15th of last month; and as
for keeping this new Doge in hand, I will do what I can, although, to tell Your Majesty the truth, one can see in him
that he is no bad Frenchman; but I hope in God that he will be able to do little harm, although it is well all the same
to keep him in hand.

Your Majesty will see by the capitulation that no cause was expressed why those of this Republic give the money to Your
Majesty; on the contrary, [?several] times in negotiating with them we told them that Your Majesty asked it because he
gave them a perpetual peace and for no other cause, showing always that neither through the Empire nor through the
House of Austria were they to be given any more right than they had; and so Your Majesty will see it in all our
writings, as it seemed to us that this would be of more service to Your Majesty.

Since it seemed the same to us as Your Majesty writes, we did not wish to bind the lord Infante to the defensive
league, since these [lords] would not bind themselves against the Turk. The matter of navigation for the subjects of the
archduchy of Austria could not be achieved with these [lords]; they will remain as hitherto. May Our Lord prosper and
guard the imperial life and estates of Your Majesty with the fulfilment of the monarchy. From Venice, 16 August 1523.

(The new Doge is Andrea Gritti, elected 20 May 1523; the capitulation is the league of 29 July 1523.)
