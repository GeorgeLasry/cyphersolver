# The Rosamonde telegrams: Brice Dudout's readings

Copied 22 Oct 2026 verbatim from NOTES.md ("The telegrams"), which quotes his edition at <https://bdudout.github.io/wenmi/en/rosamonde.html> (CC BY 4.0). His cipher transcriptions of the forms are not published; the three checked here are in `ciphertext_checked.txt` and `texts/check_decryption.txt`.

## The telegrams

Readings are Brice Dudout's, quoted from his page. Square brackets: letters the form gives differently or leaves out
(he checked them on the images on 15 Oct 2026; mostly n/v, i/r, y/h, g/p, a/u, e/c cursive confusions); "…" not
deciphered.
