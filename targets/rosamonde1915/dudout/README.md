# Rosamonde, May 1916: the forms transcribed and the confusions tested letter by letter

Answer to your Verification 3 (23 Oct 2026): "What would lift it: the 1916 forms transcribed from the JACAR images and
the confusions tested letter by letter against the key". Brice Dudout, 28 Oct 2026.

- `transcription.txt`: the seven forms of B03050089000, transcribed **blind** by Brice Dudout from the images on 15 Oct
  2026, before the key was known (lot B1), then confirmed non-blind the same day (R18).
- `decryption.txt`: each cipher word with what the key gives, unchanged.
- `emendations.tsv`: every letter where the reading departs from the key, aligned automatically (difflib) between the
  decryption and the established text: the letter on the form, what the key gives, the letter read, the cipher letter
  that would give it, and the pair. Most frequent pairs of cipher letters (on the form / needed): n/v ×23, i/r ×18, r/i ×7, y/h ×6, t/l ×3, a/u ×3, s/c ×3, k/z ×3, l/r ×2, g/b ×2.
  These are the copyists' cursive confusions already seen on the 1915 forms (n/v, i/r, e/c, a/u, g/p, y/h).
- `reading.md`: the established text, as on the site (https://bdudout.github.io/wenmi/en/rosamonde.html).

The address words ("rosamonde", "inuzuka", "porthos") are in clear on the forms and agree with the Chinese-code
telegrams of the same volume (the iron trunk, Inuzuka). Whether this lifts the 17% emendation rate above your bar is
for you to judge; the table makes each emendation checkable on the image.
