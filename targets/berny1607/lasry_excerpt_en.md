# BnF Français 3516, f. 132r, Brulart de Berny to Pierre Jeannin, 26 July 1607: English translation of Lasry's excerpt

Translation (Claude Opus 5.5, 23 Oct 2026) of `lasry_excerpt_reading.md`, **an excerpt only** of the letter, the passages George Lasry printed in his BnF cipher research report (f. 132r, cipher lines 7-9); the rest of the letter is not in the repository. ## Translation

No foundation but a few reports that reach them from the place where you are, where there is no doubt a good deal of corruption. I shall not fail, if anything wor[thy] presents itself…
