# NAF 11355, June 1859: George Lasry's decipherment, the three excerpts of his report, English translation

f. 19r. I am much pained, in the midst of my labours, to have to answer all the foolish suppositions of diplomacy. I
maintain the Pope in Rome with my army. I do not meddle at all with the rest of his States.

f. 35r. Impossible to give details of yesterday's battle. Every[one] was dead tired. The enemy withdrew last night. I do
not know the exact number of guns, flags and prisoners. Last night I slept in the room where the Emperor of Austria
breakfasted yesterday morning. Niel is appointed Marshal. The battle will take the name of Solferino.

f. 27r. I am sorry to have caused you pain; it was? not my intention. Do not worry about the little one, he is doing
wonderfully. I am writing today by the post.
