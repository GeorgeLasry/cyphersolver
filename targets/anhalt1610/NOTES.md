# Johann von Alairt to Prince Johann Georg I of Anhalt-Dessau, Prague 1610-11: upside-down German, and an undeciphered slip of November 1611

Status: in progress (the Nov 1611 slip is the open item; the 1610 letters were deciphered at the time).

Catalogue no. 597. Checked image by image by Michael Brackx (@brackxm, issue #128, 27 Oct 2026), with Claude Code.
Three of his images were checked here the same day (Claude Code, Claude Opus 5.5).

## The files

Landesarchiv Sachsen-Anhalt, Z 44 (Abteilung Dessau) A 9a Ic: reports of the Anhalt-Dessau agents at the imperial
court in Prague (Johann Loew, "Johann von Alairt", Jacob Quelwitz) to Prince Johann Georg I and his secretary Peter
Meyer, 1604-11. Digitised: Nr. 1 Bd. I-III, Nr. 2 Bd. I-IV, Nr. 3, Nr. 4 Bd. I, Nr. 5, Nr. 6.

Images, numbered by file:
`https://recherche.landesarchiv.sachsen-anhalt.de/images//Z_44/A_9a_Ic_<volume>/max/Z_44__A_9a_Ic_<volume>__NNNN.jpg`,
`<volume>` e.g. `Nr_2_Bd_I`. Local copies of the three checked images are in `img/` (git-ignored).

## The two keys (unused)

- **Nr. 2 Bd. I, image 6**: *Cifra Serenissimi Principis ac Domini Domini Joannis Georgii Principis Anhalt*. A sign
  alphabet (one sign per letter a-z) and a nomenclator numbered 10-70 (*Imperium Romanum* 10, *Caesar* 11, the
  electors 12-17, *Matthias* 19, imperial councillors 28-33, *Papa* 38, *Jesuitae* 40, nations 43-51, *Liga* 55,
  *Bellum* 56, *Pax* 57, *Principes Anhaltini* 64, *Legati* 70, added later). Checked here.
- **Nr. 2 Bd. II, image 184**: a slip nomenclator of signs for *Barbitius*, *Judaeus*, *Religio*, *Jesuitae*,
  *Fratres Caesaris*, *Einsidell*, *Gödelman*, *Bavarus*, *Insidiae*, *Liga, foedus*, *Protestantes*. It settles
  table 37 = *Iudaeus* and 68 = *Einsidell* (Brackx).
- **No letter uses either key.** Brackx searched Nr. 1 Bd. I-III, Nr. 2 Bd. I-II, Nr. 3 and Nr. 4 Bd. I (about
  2,280 images) on contact sheets with zooms: no passage in the signs or the numbers. A single sign inside a line could
  have been missed. Not searched: Nr. 2 Bd. III-IV (accounts, 50 images), Nr. 5-6 (newsletters), Nr. 1 Bd. III
  image 80 (would not load).

## Alairt's cipher: German written upside down

German in Latin script with every letter turned top to bottom: b/p, d/q, n/u, m/w trade places; *h* looks like *y*,
*a* like *v*, *e* like *o*, *i* like *X*, *l* like *j* (Brackx). Reflecting a line top to bottom makes it readable.

Letters with cipher, all from Prague, 1610, each with a decipherment in a second hand in Kurrent above every cipher
line except the formulas (salutation, close, signature):

| volume | images | date | cipher |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nr. 1 Bd. II | 152-153 | 5 Apr 1610 | short runs in plain text, darker ink |
| Nr. 1 Bd. II | 177 | 19 Apr 1610 | a block and about 12 lines |
| Nr. 1 Bd. II | 197 | 4 May 1610 | about 9 lines |
| Nr. 1 Bd. II | 241-242 | 18 May 1610 | the whole letter (checked here) |
| Nr. 1 Bd. III | 41-42 | May 1610 | most of 1½ pages, unsigned, his hand |
| Nr. 1 Bd. III | 154 | July 1610 | a whole page |
| Nr. 1 Bd. III | 263 | Aug 1610 | about 5 lines, dark ink |

Brackx's readings of the 18 May letter: opening *Edler und vester ... seyn meyne gantz willig dienste und alles
guets ...*; date line *geben den achtzehenden Maij Anno ... hundert zehen* (gloss: *... Anno Sechs hundert Zehen*);
unglossed signature *Johann von Alairt*.

Check here (27 Oct 2026): image 241 flipped top to bottom; the pale cipher lines come out as Latin-script German with
*o* for *e* (*... der ... auf der ...*), the dark gloss above each.

## The open item: the November 1611 slip

**Nr. 4 Bd. I, image 400** (foliated 383): a small slip mounted on the leaf, eight lines all in cipher, no gloss;
docket *praes. Dessau den 23. Novemb. 1611*. Unsigned; filed among Alairt's Prague reports of November 1611. Same
system.

Line 1, as written: `Wxll qoa kvyſoa poXXyt weaqou, qvff qoa yoatz` (transcribed here from the image, provisional);
read *Will der Kayser bericht werden, dass der Hertz-* (Brackx; agreed here). Line 2 continues *-og von
Braun[schweig] ... zu ihm erfordert ...* (Brackx). The rest is read only in part (*gantz*, *Ihr Mayt.*, last line
*haben.*). If *Kayser* and *Braunschweig* hold, the slip reports on Rudolf II and Duke Heinrich Julius of Brunswick,
then at the imperial court.
