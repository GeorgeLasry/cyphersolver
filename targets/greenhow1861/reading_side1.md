# Greenhow, letter in cipher on mourning paper, side 1: the reading as sentences

English, so no translation. Spelling as written (buisy, panick, beleive, McLeland). Words in clear on the leaf are in
italics. Marks: [...] = lost with the paper; [x] = letters supplied where the paper is lost or a sign is missing;
[?x] = a conjecture or an uncertain sign.
