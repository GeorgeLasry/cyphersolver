# Each sign is written as a neutral shape label, not as a letter (the labels and their key values are in
# key_1864.md and decode.py). Read on enlarged crops of image 1 by a second agent, independently of the first
# reading; the two agree except where noted in NOTES.md.
#   |   word gap or dot          ~   paper loss (one or more signs)
#   =x  word written in clear    +   word runs on to the next line
#   ?   after a label: uncertain sign
