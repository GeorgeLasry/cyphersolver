# fr. 15571 f. 176r (lower part) and f. 176v: the contemporary decipherment of the cipher on f. 177r-v

Forget to Villeroy, 31 Dec 1585. Ark `btv1b90618802`: f. 176r = canvas 184 right, ll. 1-11 below the marginal
note *Mons. du Mayne* (the twenty lines above it are another passage and are not transcribed here); f. 176v =
canvas 185 left, ll. 12-22 and the signature. Read from the full-size openapi.bnf.fr IIIF image, 22 Oct 2026.
- Line breaks are the manuscript's; the line numbers are added. Spelling is the copy's; abbreviations are
  expanded in square brackets (p[ou]r, n[ost]re, M[onsieu]r, S[ieu]r, led[it]); accents are not added except é
  in past participles; punctuation is sparse in the copy and is kept sparse.
- `(?)` marks a doubtful word; `[…]` an unread word. There are no interlinear additions on these lines.
- Underlinings: the copyist (or a later reader) underlined names and some words (*Pons*, *Taillebourg*,
  *Charante*, *trante mil*, *Matignon*, *Turenne*, *Bourdeaux*, *Gascongne*, *Mr du Mayne*); not marked below.

Notes on doubtful places
- l. 2 *on(?)*: a short word of two letters with a cross-stroke after *Indubitablement*; *l'on* (as in l. 1)
  is possible if the l is merged with the preceding flourish.
- l. 6 *desp[en]ce(?)*: written *desp* + a superscript; *despence* or *despense*.
- l. 7 *Guyenne(?)*: *Guy* then an ending written over or struck through; the sense is certain.
- l. 13 *soudain[ement](?)*: *soudain* with a final flourish, the same stroke that ends *Indubitablem[en]t* in
  l. 2; read as the -ment abbreviation, but plain *soudain* is possible.
- l. 17 *l'en(?)*: *l* + the form this hand uses for *en* (as in *en la Jouissance*, l. 4, and *s'y en Iroit*,
  l. 8); *luy* is the other possible reading.
- l. 18 *autres*: the *au* is enclosed in a loop, perhaps a correction.
- The right margin of f. 176r shows ink at the line ends of ll. 9-11 that belongs to the facing leaf, not to
  this text.
