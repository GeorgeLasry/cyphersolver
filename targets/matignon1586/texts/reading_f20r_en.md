# f. 20r, English (from the clear copy at the end of f. 19v)

> Translation by Michael Brackx (issue #87, 23 Oct 2026), with the same holes as the copy.

The remains of the regiments of [?Sacremore], of [?Vic], of [?] [?] and of la Roche [?Montoisy], and that of the
Swiss of Colonel Heid. We have fixed a day to meet again on the [?6th] of next month, resolved not to stop at any
enterprise unless it is [?very] easy, since [?it] would be a shame for the negligence. Several of those on this side
would like to draw me into the Limousin and the Quercy, but I see that this would make me spend too much time, having
also learnt from some intercepted letters of the vicomte de Turenne to the King of Navarre that they are about to come
to terms to get the garrison out of Tulle, for four or five thousand écus that the people of the said town ask; which
they will do all the sooner when they know I am approaching, so that I hold it certain that it will be abandoned, and
that Lamorez, who is inside, will withdraw to the said King of Navarre.

Endorsed: Monsieur du Mayne, the [?6th] of January 1586.
