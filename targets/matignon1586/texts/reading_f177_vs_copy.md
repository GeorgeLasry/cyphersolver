## 3. Where the copy itself is uncertain

- c. 2 *on(?)* for the cipher's *l'on*: a two-letter word with a cross-stroke; *l'on* is possible if the l is
  merged with the flourish of *Indubitablement*.
- c. 6 *desp[en]ce(?)*: *desp* + a superscript; the cipher's *despence* fits.
- c. 7 *Guyenne(?)*: the ending is written over or struck; the cipher has *Guiene*.
- c. 13 *soudain[ement](?)*: a final flourish read as the -ment abbreviation (the same stroke as in
  *Indubitablem[en]t*, c. 2); the cipher's figures give *soudainement*.
- c. 17 *l'en(?)*: the hand's form for *en* after *l*; *luy* is the other possible reading. The cipher's
  figures (Cl B v = l e n) decide for *l'en*.
- c. 18 *autres*: the *au* is enclosed in a loop, perhaps a correction.
