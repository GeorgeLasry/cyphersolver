# ff. 123r-v, Mayenne to Henri III, Marmande, March 1586: the reading

fr. 15572 f. 123r (Gallica canvas 129, right page) and f. 123v (canvas 130, left page). The despatch opens in clear,
then runs in Cipher-1 for 37 lines on f. 123r and 41 lines on f. 123v; it goes on over ff. 124r-v, which are read
in a later pass. No clear copy is known.

What was done, 22 Oct 2026: Michael Brackx's transcription was decoded with the merged key and then read sentence by
sentence against each figure's letter set. Every line of both pages was cropped from the full-resolution image
(openapi.bnf.fr IIIF) and the breaks were checked there. Brackx's f. 123r stops one line short: the last manuscript
line of the recto (l. 37) was transcribed here from the image. A second pass re-checked f. 123r line by line,
figure by figure, against an exemplar set cut from this hand (straightened line strips with every figure numbered;
crops kept outside the repository). The label changes, the corrected figures, the leaf key and the passes are listed
in the files named at the end. A third pass built an atlas of the hand (every figure of ff. 123r-124v cut and
labelled from the reading) and tested its shape classes on the open words; it adds *[?à satisfaire]* on l. 7 and
changes no key value.

Marks: a word in square brackets with a question mark is carried only in part by its figures; the ellipsis in
brackets is a run of figures that does not form a word; a number in brackets is a code group not identified; [UNK]
is a figure not in the key. Where a manuscript line breaks inside a word, the line marker stands before the word.
Accents and punctuation are modern; the spelling is what the figures give.

## The clear opening (f. 123r, five lines)

Sire, la dernière que j'ay [?escript] à Vostre Majesté est du huict[iesm]e de ce moys, et suis tousjours attendant
la responce des miennes des cinq[iesm]e, sept[iesm]e et dix[iesm]e de ces moys, estant estonné de me veoir réduict à
n'avoir de voz nouvelles que une foys par moys, comme je n'en ay pas eu tout cest hiver davantaige. Par toutes les
miennes depuis six sepmaines ou deux moys j'ay tousjours [?dépeint] […]

[The clear text was read from the image for context only. The last clear word, after *tousjours*, is written *d·*
then a p with a stroke, a dot, two minims and a hooked final stroke: *dépeint* fits it (*depuis*, written the same
way on the line, has no dot after the p); it stays marked. After a filler stroke the line ends with four signs that
are not in Brackx's file: a figure like *tt* joined to a C, a triple bar, and a crossed loop. They stand where the
sense wants *à Vostre Majesté* before cipher l. 1 *[UNK] la nécessité*, but no value is read from them.]

## Notes on the reading

f. 123r (second pass, figure by figure; changes in `brackx2026/corrections/f123r_v2.tsv`):
- l. 1: [UNK] is the first cipher figure, a cross with an o at each end of its bar; not in the key. *[?nécessité]*:
  the figures give *necesiti* (one s; the last figure is the ε-figure, i). *[?prévois]*: between *prevoi* and the s
  stands a double upright with a looped foot (label `Hl`), not the plain double cross that is c in *nécessité*,
  *clameurs*, *aucun*, *encores*; its value is not known.
- l. 2: *[?retrouverions]*: the figures give *retrouerions* (one u/v figure). Code 16 stands where *quand* (or *si*)
  is wanted here, at l. 8 (*[16] nous serions tous ensemble*) and at l. 36 (*[16] tous moiens me deffaillent*);
  read as a code, not adopted.
- l. 3: *[?reiondroient]* (*reiondroiet*, the n of *-ent* missing; the o is a slanted ff, not Hc); *[?trouve]*
  (*troue*: one u/v figure).
- l. 4: the six figures after *l'avois* are x, a long s (S, p/s: on the image it is the S of *plus*, not a
  virgule), e, t, the V with an underbar, the dotted v and B: *a p r e u n e*, no word.
- l. 6–7: *[?guerres]*: g u e rr s, no final e; the five figures after *que l'on* (Ca box ff dd m: *a u o s t*) do
  not make a word. l. 7–8: the figure Brackx writes oo2 (two o joined by a long bar) is the barred double o, ff, of
  *affaires* and *deffaillent* on f. 123v; with it the run after *remis* reads *a s a t ff a i r e e s a t ff r e a*.
  Third pass: the first nine figures are read *[?à satisfaire]* (*a sat·ffaire*: every figure carried, the *is* of
  *satisfaire* not written); the last eight (*e s a t ff r e a*) still do not form.
- l. 11: *[?optenir]*: *pour opt[e]nir*, one e short; the r of *pour* is a small hooked c.
- l. 12: *[?pacience]*: *p a c i e c e*, the n missing (the double cross is c, as everywhere on the page); the
  ε-figure after it is i, *i vivre* (*y vivre*).
- l. 12–13 *[?proteste]*: *protesse*, the small s-shaped figure where t is wanted; l. 14 *[?tresorier]*: the figure
  before the last *er* is the open C with an r inside (l), as in *qu'à l'argent* l. 33, not i; l. 15 *[?mareschal]*:
  *mar c a l*; l. 18 *[?forces]*, *[?lesquels]*: one e short each.
- ff = o on this leaf (as well as n): *retrouverions*, *reiondroient*, *tous ensemble*, *comptant*, *pouoir* (l. 25),
  *monstres*, *fournir* (l. 35).
- l. 21 *[?sortir]*: the crossed-stem figure (u) where s is wanted. *Toute l'[66] a esté*: the group is two 6 written
  together like 16 and 25 (Brackx: 6 B), a code whose value is not known (*armée*, *infanterie* fit the sense).
- l. 22 *[?harasée]*: a 4 with a looped stem (q4), not the crossed q, where h is wanted. l. 22–23: *maladie, […] si
  mal vestue*: the five figures before *si* are the heavy curled C (Cv, s/x; Brackx Cl), B, Ze, dd, m: *s e t s t*,
  no word; the C before *o* (*si*) is the same Cv.
- l. 23–24 *m'[?ont] [?tous]*: *mo t tos*, the figures checked (E, m, crossed q, dd): n and u are not written.
- l. 25–26 *[?monstres]*: after *deu-* stand the open C (l), dd, then *mon* + Cv t r dd V e t: *deul s monstr s
  et*, one figure too many and one e short. *[?protestent]*: *protetent* with a struck figure before the last t.
  *qu'il n'[…] [?recevront]*: v, 6, ff (*n i/n/s n/o*), then *r e c e r o n t*, one u/v figure short.
- l. 27–28 *[?gendarmes]*: *gend a m e s* (the first figure of l. 28 is an open a, not the cross), one r short;
  *[?veulent]* (*velent*); l. 30 *[?importuns]* (*impotus*); l. 31 *qui [?est]* (*quiet*); l. 31–32 *[?peult]*
  (*pelt*); l. 33 *n'[?en]* (*n i n*).
- l. 33 *qu'à l'argent comptant*: Brackx's *s y* is one group, the s-shaped 5 and a 9: code 59 = *argent*.
- l. 35–36 *[?incommodités] [?m'arrivent]*: the q with a barred foot is read as t from these two contexts only.
  *ensemble*: the last figure is the t-cross (e), not the 4.
- l. 36–37 *[?deffaillent]*: *d ff a i l e n t* (the first figure of l. 37 is the barred double o, ff). Codes 101
  and 102: two troop names, probably.

f. 123v:
- l. 6 *non [?plus]*: the figure for p is the barred o (a/m/i); l. 9–10 *[?deperir]*: the figures give *deper* + *i*
  + a cross; l. 10 *[?entretenement]*: the figures give *entretinemint* (an ε-figure twice where e is wanted).
- l. 11 and l. 38: one figure, a barred o crossed by a looped f-shaped stroke with a small o, stands where *Vostre
  Majesté* is wanted in both places (*proposé à [ ] que je puis*, *recourir à [ ] et vous depescher*); read as a
  code group from context, not from the key.
- l. 13–14 the two figure-groups after *et que* (*f o n t e o a s r e n t* by the sets) do not make words.
- l. 19 *contraints* is written twice (a slip of the encipherer).
- l. 22 *[?recouvrement]*: *le e e courement*, the third figure gives no r; l. 23 *[?difficile]*: *d i a i n/i s c i l*.
- l. 27 *[?promises]*: the second figure is x (e/a), not r. l. 31 *je [?ne] pensois*: the figures give *ient*.
- l. 36 *[?costé]*: *c o s t r s e*, two figures too many. l. 39 *[?humblement]*: the first figure is the long s/p
  figure, not h.
- l. 41 *et si […] desire*: one large curled figure, not identified (perhaps a code for the King).
