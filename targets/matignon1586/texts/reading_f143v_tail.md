# f. 143v, ll. 26–35, Mayenne to Henri III, April 1586: the reading after the copy's break

fr. 15572 f. 143v, Gallica canvas 151, left page. The contemporary clear copy on f. 155r (`reading_f143.md`) ends at
the foot of that page on *où il eust …*. Those words fall in manuscript l. 26 of the cipher, at the figures
`… 12 x g z Ze` (*il eust*). This file reads the cipher from there to its end (l. 26, from code 62, to l. 35).

What was done, 22 Oct 2026: the figures (Brackx's transcription) were read against the letter sets of the merged
key, and each line was cropped from the full-resolution image (openapi.bnf.fr IIIF) and checked. The copy does not
stop at the break. It runs on over the page to the head of **f. 155v (canvas 163, left page)**, seven lines and a
docket. This continuation had not been transcribed and is now in `brackx2026/copies/f155v_f143_tail.txt`. The cipher
reading below was made from the figures. The copy was then used as a check, and the figures carry every word of it.
The cipher has one word more (*si*, l. 27). Ten label changes, including one untranscribed line (l. 35), are in
`brackx2026/corrections/f143v.tsv`. The corrected figures are in `brackx2026/corrections/f143v_cipher.txt`.

Marks: `[?word]` = carried only in part by its figures; `[…]` = figures that do not form a word. Accents and
punctuation are modern. The spelling is what the figures give. English: `reading_f143v_tail_en.md`.

## The cipher, from the copy's break

… ayant de luy ceste obligation de ne porter les armes de six mois pour son party, où il eust [l. 26] peu nuire pour
beaucoup [?davantaige], [l. 27] et si j'ay grande espérance de l'en retirer du [l. 28] tout. Et ce faisant, de
pouvoir [?tirer] de bons services, [l. 29] et par son moyen et exemple retirer d'aultres [l. 30] hommes et places de
conséquence.

C'est se qui m'a [l. 31] meu d'entrer plus volontiers en ce traicté.

[l. 32] Sur quoy Vostre Majesté me fera, s'il luy plaist, entendre son [l. 33] intention, ayant le tout excepté la
somme dont [l. 34] je me suis engaigé de ma parole, remis soubz [l. 35] le bon plaisir de […].

## Notes on the words

- *peu* is code 62 (*peut/peu*). *beaucoup* is code 61, which the file had as a bare 6 (see corrections).
- *davantaige* ("more"): w ao g X v Ze o B y + = d-a-u-a-n-t-a-i-g-?. The last figure is a plain cross. Elsewhere on this
  leaf a plain cross stands for r (*tire[r]*, l. 28), and the key gives it m/s/i. The copy has *davantage*. All
  letters but the last are carried, hence the `[?]`.
- *et si j'ay*: x m q o ei ao B = e-t-s-i-j-a-y. The copy has *Et j'ay*: the copyist dropped *si*.
- *[?tirer]*: Ze o L h + = t-i-r-e-? with the same plain cross as l. 26 (copy *tirer*).
- *services*: y q h L g o | dd t dd = s-?-e-r-v-i-c-e-s. The q is one figure too many (q = s/o/u): a slip of the
  encipherer, or a figure that has been misread. The o was missing from the transcription.
- *hommes*, *somme*: the x with a bar over it is one figure for *mm* (f 7 x̄ t dd = h-o-mm-e-s;
  q 7 x̄ t = s-o-mm-e). Brackx labels it plain x.
- *C'est se qui*: f x z m | z x 13. The z figure (ꝺ) is s, as in *conséquence* on the line above, so the
  figures spell *se* for *ce* (copy *ce*).
- *Sur quoy Vostre Majesté*: z g L 15 68. These are codes 15 = *quoy* and 68 = *Vostre Majesté*; the file had
  them as x and 6 8.
- *soubz le bon*: z 7 g λ q | n t λ 7 v. Brackx labels the λ figure lam (= b) in *de bons* l. 28 but X (= a) here.
  l. 35 was not in the transcription.
- *de […]*: the last four figures of the cipher, `fig o gg T`. The first is a long stroke looped at both ends and
  crossed by a bar, a figure not seen elsewhere on these leaves. Where they stand, the copy has *Vostre Ma.té*, but the
  figures do not spell it, and Vostre Majesté is code 68 two lines above. They are left open. After them, on the same
  line, the hand writes *me.* in ordinary letters and then goes on in clear.

## Remaining holes

Four: the closing figures (l. 35), and three single letters carried by a figure whose value does not fit (the plain
cross in *davantaig[e]* and *tire[r]*, the extra q in *s[q]ervices*). Every other word is carried by its figures,
and the copy on f. 155v agrees with all of them.

## The clear text that follows (ll. 35–39, outside the cipher)

Read from the image only and not checked against another witness; `(?)` = doubtful.

[l. 35] *me.* Hier(?) Mons.r le Mar.al faict [l. 36] sortir ceux qui estoient dedans lad. place de Castets, et
faict desja(?) […] commancer [l. 37] à la razer(?), à quoy ceux du païs contribuent si volontiers que ce sera bien
tost faict. [l. 38] J'attendz icy dans deux jours led. s.r Mar.al avec toutte sa(?) trouppe(?). Et ay cependant
[l. 39] commancé ce jourd'huy de faire investir ceste place, dont les ennemis sont fort b[…] …

The page ends there. The f. 155v copy does not carry these lines: after the cipher's last words it gives only the
docket, *M.r du Mayne du x(?)me avril 1586*, so the despatch is from April 1586. The day is not certain: it may be *xme* (10th).
