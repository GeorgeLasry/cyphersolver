# ff. 124r–v, Mayenne to Henri III, Marmande, March 1586: the reading

fr. 15572 ff. 124r–124v, Gallica canvases 130 (right page) and 131 (left page). These two pages end the despatch
that opens in clear on f. 123r. f. 124r has 41 lines of Cipher-1 and f. 124v has 38. A clear postscript, the
closing and the dating line follow. The writer is the Duc de Mayenne with the army in Guyenne. He writes about
holding the army together, his money, the help of the Maréchal de Matignon, Gourgues and Forget, and the siege he
plans at Sainte-Bazeille.

What was done, 22 Oct 2026: Michael Brackx's transcription was decoded with the merged key. It was then read
sentence by sentence against each figure's letter set, and every line was checked on the full-resolution image.
Sixty-five labels were corrected and seven letter values were added for this leaf. A line-by-line forced alignment
of the reading onto the corrected figures was used as a check. The files named at the end list the corrections,
the leaf key and the passes. The beginning of the sentence on l. 1 is on f. 123v and is not read here.
Second pass on f. 124r (22 Oct 2026): every line was cut again along its own slope and compared figure by
figure with the labels; ten more label changes (`brackx2026/corrections/f124r_v2.tsv`) give *armée*, *sans
cela*, *je sai qu'elles*, *sur les lieus*, *nous a doné* and a candidate *[?faisant]* after code 60. f. 124v
is unchanged. Third pass (22 Oct 2026): an atlas of this hand (every figure of ff. 123r-124v cut and labelled from
the reading) shows the crossed oval and the tall crossed d, both labelled tm or th, as two shapes, mostly m and s;
l. 22 now reads *[?mieulx] estant* where the crossed oval had been taken as s (*si elles tant*). No key value changed.

Line numbers: a word divided between two lines stands after the marker of the line on which it ends.
Marks: a word in square brackets with a question mark is carried only in part by its figures. The ellipsis in
brackets is a run of figures that does not form a word. A bare number in brackets is a code group with no known
value: 60, 67 and `me` here; 68 is *Vostre Majesté* and 76 is *le Roy de Navarre*. Accents and punctuation are
modern. The spelling is what the figures give.

## What stays open

- **Codes with no value:** 60 (twice: f. 124r l. 4 and f. 124v l. 9, "ordre [60] faire monstre", where *de* or
  *pour* would fit), 67 (twice: "avec quelque [67]", "pour nostre [67]"; a word like *troupe* fits both), `me`
  (f. 124v l. 38).
- **Figures that do not make the word** (each one was checked on the image): l. 4 the word after code 60 (*[?faisant]*: the first figure is the f of f. 110, not yet seen elsewhere on this leaf);
  l. 7–8 the word after *que le*, which goes over a three-figure interlinear insertion whose place is not certain;
  l. 11 *[?affin]* (crossed 4, slashed q, dotted cross: not the letters *a ff*); l. 12 *[?Quercy]* (q u e/r c i,
  one figure short); l. 13–14 *[?deffraier]* and *[?délivrance]* (the A figure gives l, not n); l. 15 the four
  figures before *vostre*; l. 21 *[?préjudiciables]* (no r and only one i in the figures); l. 23 the crossed-d figure after *lieus*; l. 18 the figure after *qui*, which is a blot; l. 25 the end of the line; l. 37 the eleven figures before *ai aussi*; l. 41 the two words
  around *province*. On f. 124v: l. 2 *[?combien]* (a y where b is needed); l. 6 the verb after *luy en*; l. 12
  *[?uniesme]*; l. 18 the place-name (*[?Castels]*) and the words before *Pasques*; l. 23 *[?mandé]* and
  *[?seré]*; l. 30 the river (*d o r d …*); l. 37 the participle before *audict siège*.
- **The clear end** is in a small cursive hand, and two words in it and the day of the month were not read from
  the image at this resolution.
