# f. 110, Mayenne to Villeroy, Villeneuve-d'Agenais, March 1586: the reading

fr. 15572 f. 110, Gallica canvas 116, right page: a small slip, 38 lines wholly in cipher in a small, faded hand,
dated at the foot "De Bellebourg … jour de Mars 1586" in clear (but see the note on the dateline below). Mayenne
writes to Villeroy, the King's secretary of state, about the army he commands in Guyenne against the King of
Navarre: no money, the promised funds not sent, the clergy's money held back, and his reputation at stake if the
army is left to fall apart. No clear copy of this leaf is known.

What was done, 22 Oct 2026. Pass 1: Michael Brackx's transcription was decoded with the merged key and read sentence
by sentence against each figure's letter set; nine lines were checked on the image and ten labels corrected
(`brackx2026/corrections/f110.tsv`). Pass 2: the whole leaf was re-transcribed figure by figure from full-resolution,
straightened line crops (openapi.bnf.fr IIIF; gallica.bnf.fr/iiif answers 403) against an exemplar set cut from this
hand, every one of the 38 lines (`brackx2026/corrections/f110_v2_cipher.txt`, 2,121 figures; 123 changes against
Brackx in `brackx2026/corrections/f110_v2.tsv`), then re-decoded and read again. The leaf is **read in part**: the
frame of the letter and most of its argument hold; lines 1-2, 10 and 29 and a few words elsewhere stay open (see the
log).

**The dateline.** On the full-resolution crop the clear words after the last cipher group read *De Villeneufve
d'Agenoys ce … jour de Mars 1586*, not "De Bellebourg". Recorded here, not yet carried into NOTES or the profile.

Marks: a word in square brackets with a question mark is carried only in part by its figures; the ellipsis in
brackets is a run of figures that does not form a word; [45], [47] are code groups not identified (47 = *tous/tout*
where the context settles it). Accents and punctuation are modern; the spelling is what the figures give.
