# fr. 15571 f. 177r-v, Forget to Villeroy, 31 Dec 1585: the reading

fr. 15571 f. 177, Gallica canvas 185 right (recto, 29 cipher lines) and canvas 186 left (verso, 11 cipher lines,
then clear text). The archivist's heading: *Lettre de Mr Forget a Mr de Villeroy sur les desseins d'assieger Pons ou
Taillebourg, mais le Vicomte de Turenne ayant paru en campagne avec gens et canons, on a changé d'advis*.

What was done, 22 Oct 2026: Michael Brackx's transcription was decoded with the merged key and read sentence by
sentence against each figure's letter set. Every break was cropped from the full-resolution image. Twelve labels were
changed (one added, one code merged); the leaf key adds seven values. **A contemporary clear copy of this cipher was
found on the preceding leaf**, f. 176r (canvas 184 right, from the marginal note *Mons. du Mayne* to the foot) and
f. 176v (canvas 185 left, signed *Mr Forget*). The copy was read from the image and used to choose between the
letters a figure allows and to check the reading; it supplies no word that the figures do not carry. Where the figures
and the copy disagree, the reading follows the figures and marks the word.

Marks: a word in square brackets with a question mark is carried only in part by its figures; `[…]` is a figure that
gives no letter of the word; `[cs]` is a figure left unread. The barred double g (`gg`), the `me` sign and one barred
infinity sign are nulls on this leaf (the copy has nothing in their place, at seven places) and are left out of the
text. Code 73 reads *le Roy* and code 64 *quelque*, both from the copy. Accents and punctuation are modern; the
spelling is what the figures give.

## The clear opening (not re-read in full)

Four lines in clear above the cipher, in Forget's hand, partly legible: *Depuis Mr d'Aumont … Limozin …* and
*… persuader avec de nouvelles raisons de tenter …*. They are not part of this reading.

## The cipher

[l. 1] … assiegeant Pons et Tailebourg, que l'on [l. 2] disoit que l'on pouvoit faire tout ensemb[l. 3]le, et que
indubitablement l'on les empo[l. 4]rteroit; et que ce eust esté aux ennemis, [l. 5] specialement pour Tailebourg,
oster un [l. 6] grand revenu qu'ils tirent du passaige [l. 7] de la Charante, où ils levent de [l. 8] grandes
impositions, et remettre le Roy en la [l. 9] jouissance des tailes de tout ce pais, [l. 10] que l'on dict monter
beaucoup.

Que oultre cela le pais [l. 11] nous offroit trant mil escus pour aider [l. 12] à la despence du siege.

Et y estans [l. 13] desja si disposés, que nous consentions que, [l. 14] s'il venoit advis de quelque [?remuement] en
Guie[l. 15]ne, que Monsieur le mareschal de Matignon [l. 16] s'y en iroit avec quelque peu de [?forces] s'y
[l. 17] [?oposer], et preparer aussi ce qui seroit [l. 18] necssaire [?pour] y recevoir ceste armée [l. 19] à la
primevere; cependant que nous conti[l. 20]nurions [?ce siège].

Mais comme nous estions pour [l. 21] clore ce pacquet, le dict sieur de Ma[l. 22]tignon a eu advis de Bourdeaux que
le vicomte de Turenne [cs] [l. 23] estoit paru en campaigne avec forces [l. 24] et canons.

Cela nous a soudainement faict [l. 25] changer nostre premier advis, parce qu'il ne veult [l. 26] point partir qu'il
n'emene avec lui [?la moitié] de [l. 27] l'armée; de sorte que nostre dicte depesche a [l. 28] esté convertie en celle
que vous avés icy.

Voil[l. 29]a comment noz opinions sont erantes.

Je [v. l. 1] pensois trouver icy le dict sieur de [v. l. 2] [?Matignon] tout resolu de ce que [?nous] devions
[v. l. 3] faire.

Mais je vous [?confesse] que je l'en [v. l. 4] ay veu aussi incertain que les autres, comme à [v. l. 5] la verité il
y a prou de raisons de part [v. l. 6] et d'autre jusques icy.

Nous sommes [v. l. 7] tous resolus à la Gascongne, et croy que [v. l. 8] la responce de nostre depesche s'y
trouver[v. l. 9]a […] conforme.

Mais toutesfois crains, pour le che[v. l. 10]min que Monsieur du Mayne doibt tenir, que nous n'y employons
[v. l. 11] beaucoup plus de tems que nous ne [?desirons].

## What stays open

- [?pais] (r. l. 9): the first figure is read as p after correction; confirmed by the clear copy f. 176r, which has *ce pays* (its next instance, l. 10, is *le pays*).
- [?remuement] (r. l. 14): *r e m u e d e n t*: the infinity figure (d) stands where m is needed. Copy: *remuement*.
- [?forces] (r. l. 16): the figure 6 (i/n/s) stands for e.
- [?oposer] (r. l. 16-17): the large C at the end of l. 16 (Cl = l) stands for p.
- [?pour] (r. l. 18): *p o u* then a small c (p/l/s) for r.
- [?ce siège] (r. l. 20): *c e s i e* then U and 6, which give no *g e*. Copy: *ce siege* (f. 176r), which agrees with the figures' *ce*.
- [?la moitié] (r. l. 26): *l a m [o] t i e*, one figure short (no o). Copy: *la moictié*.
- [cs] (r. l. 22): one figure after code 84, not read (the copy has *estoit paru*).
- [?Matignon] (v. l. 2): the third vowel is the figure 7 (o): *Matognon*, a writer's slip.
- [?nous] (v. l. 2): the code is 26 (*vous*) on the image; the copy has *nous*, and only *nous* fits *devions*.
- [?confesse] (v. l. 3): *c o n f* + two figures (ao, B) + *ss e*: one figure too many.
- je l'[…] ay veu (v. l. 3-4): after *l* the figures give *e n*; the clear copy has *l'en(?)*, which the figures decide: *je l'en ay veu*.
- trouvera […] conforme (v. l. 9): one figure U between the two words, no letter fits.
- [?desirons] (v. l. 11): *d e s o n s*, two letters missing at the end of the cipher, before the clear text.

## Checked against the clear copy (22 Oct 2026)

The contemporary decipherment on fr. 15571 f. 176r-v (transcribed in `brackx2026/copies/f176_f177.txt`; full
comparison in `reading_f177_vs_copy.md`) runs the whole cipher. It confirms 73 = *le Roy* and 64 = *quelque* here,
the nulls, *ce pais* and *je l'en ay veu*. It gives a word for every remaining [?…]; the figures carry most of them
only in part (one or two figures short or misread), so they stay marked here, and the copy is the reading for them.
The cipher has *dicte* in "nostre dicte depesche" (r. l. 27) where the copy omits it.
