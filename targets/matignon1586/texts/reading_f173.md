# f. 173, [Forget] to Henri III, 1586: the reading

fr. 15572 f. 173, Gallica canvas 180, right page. The letter opens in clear and goes on in Cipher-1 for
thirty-three lines, on Mayenne's want of money and the failure of credit in the town. The writer is the man Mayenne
sent back to the town for the second time. No clear copy of this leaf is known.

What was done, 22 Oct 2026: the transcription by Michael Brackx was decoded with the merged key and then read
sentence by sentence. Every break was cropped from the full-resolution image and checked there. Fourteen labels were
corrected and three letter values added for this leaf; the corrections, the corrected figures, the leaf key and the
passes are listed in the files named at the end.

Marks: a word in square brackets with a question mark is carried only in part by its figures; the ellipsis in
brackets is a run of figures that does not form a word; 44 is the stop code. Accents and punctuation are modern;
the spelling is what the figures give.

## The clear opening

Sire, Vostre Majesté a esté suffisamment advertie par les deux dernières depesches de Monsieur du Mayne
