# m0001: English translation

I again congratulate Your Majesty on the warm welcome received there, which has made the happiest impression among
us. Everything continues without news. I am writing to Windsor. I kiss Your Majesty's hands. Wenceslau de Lima.

Notes. From the pencil decode of the time above the groups; in the reading [ ] marks our addition and [?] a group not read or doubtful. Only "Tudo continua sem novidade. Escrevo Windsor" is coded (four groups); the rest is in clear on the form. The King
was in Madrid on the first leg of his state visit of November 1909 (Madrid, Paris, London); Windsor is a later stop.
The first figure of 05764 and the fourth of 80054 are not sure; the glosses are clear.
