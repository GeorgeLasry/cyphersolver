# m0018: English translation

Since our welcome in France has been so affectionate, it seems right to make the [dinner?] toast warmer. In the first
section, after "sympathy", add "and friendship"; in the second, after "you have done", say "for the welcome the French
nation gives us in its great and beautiful capital, its intellectual Paris". In the third section, after "between
Portugal and France", insert "and also for the closer tightening of their relations". If the President of the
Republic's toast contains anything special, make the due allusion in the reply, [?] discreet and prudent. I kiss Your
Majesty's hands. Wenceslau de Lima.
