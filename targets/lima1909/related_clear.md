# M09: the clear telegram of the unit (context, not coded)

## m0003, continued on m0004: Venceslau de Lima to King Manuel II, Lisbon -> Madrid (Palacio Real), 9 Nov 1909

Spanish palace form "Real Palacio", by hand: Despacho de Lisboa, Núm. 12725; Expedido el 9 á las 6.40 t[arde];
Recibido el 9 de N[oviem]bre á las 10.35 t [?]. Address: Sua Magestade el Rei. Real Palacio Madrid. No code groups.

> Muito agradeço telegramma de Vossa Magestade a quem muito respeitosamente felicito pela forma por que viagem vai
> correndo e acolhimento ahi recebido. Aqui tudo bem. Logo voltarei a telegraphar. Beijo [m0004:] as mãos de Vossa
> Majestade. Wenceslau de Lima.

English: "Many thanks for Your Majesty's telegram. I respectfully congratulate you on how the journey is going and on
the welcome received there. All well here. I will telegraph again soon. I kiss Your Majesty's hands. Wenceslau de
Lima."

The two Madrid forms (this one and m0001) are the start of the King's state visit of November 1909; the coded
telegrams to Paris (m0005-m0020) are from its end.
