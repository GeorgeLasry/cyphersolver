# m0014: English translation

I remind Your Majesty that, since the President of the Republic has given the Grand Cross of the Legion of Honour to
Bocage, it matters to be friendly to Briand and leave no bad impression; above all there must be no inequality with
what was done in England and Spain. I kiss Your Majesty's hands. Wenceslau de Lima.

Notes. From the pencil decode of the time above the groups; in the reading [ ] marks our addition and [?] a group not read or doubtful. Bocage is presumably Carlos du Bocage, Portuguese foreign minister in 1909, and Briand the French premier [our
identifications, not checked]. The point is a Portuguese decoration for Briand in return. 93186 is glossed
"G.C. Legião" (the first pass had "a decoration, gloss unclear").
