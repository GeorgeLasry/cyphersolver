# m0020: English translation

Confidential. It seems best that Your Majesty go only to [the theatre of] São Carlos on the night of the day of
arrival. [Someone] told me whether [...] I can count on that [the clause is not clear]. It also seems better that Your
Majesty hold the reception at the station, where a suitably prepared room will be ready. That will make the reception
easier and better and will free the arrival arrangements in the preparation. I await Your Majesty's decision. I
earnestly ask Your Majesty for an answer, because the arrangements have to be ordered. I kiss Your Majesty's hands.
Wenceslau de Lima.
