# m0005: English translation

Confidential. A stop at Bussaco would have been awkward, so I insist on the advantage of coming straight through,
leaving there [Paris] by the night express. Vasconcellos Porto is pressing for the answer, which I earnestly ask of Your
Majesty. I kiss Your Majesty's hands. Wenceslau de [Lima].

Notes. From the pencil decode of the time above the groups; in the reading [ ] marks our addition and [?] a group not read or doubtful. "teve" (had) is the gloss; the sense is conditional. Vasconcellos Porto is presumably the minister in charge of the
King's return journey [our identification, not checked].
