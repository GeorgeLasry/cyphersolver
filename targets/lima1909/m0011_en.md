# m0011: English translation

In agreement [?] with Porto and Serpa we settled that the best course would be to take there [in Paris] the [?] night
[train] at 7.40 pm, in full comfort, as well as or better than the Sud-Express, and come as far as Medina [del Campo],
where a special train would wait that would reach Lisbon at 11 in the morning, or later if preferred. This would cut
out the stop on the way at Luso, which is awkward and makes a bad impression. The service is better arranged in the
way I indicate. [...] comfortable at Hen[daye?] [...] already at 8-something, and at Medina for the special train at
about 7 in the evening. Awaiting [the orders of] Your Majesty, so as to give the orders, which are urgent. W. de Lima.
