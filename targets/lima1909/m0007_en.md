# m0007: English translation

I thank Your Majesty for your telegram and for agreeing to the proposals. Arrival time in Lisbon a quarter past one
in the afternoon. Any earlier would be awkward, because the train would pass Pampilhosa and Coimbra too early; any
later would make the journey needlessly slow. I should be most grateful if Your Majesty would tell me whether you will
receive greetings on entering Portugal at Vilar Formoso, where they very much wish to offer them. After that there
will be none, because it will be night, so that you can rest as far as Pampilhosa, which you reach at 8 in the
morning. Lunch between Santarém and Lisbon, from half past [?] to a quarter past one. I kiss Your Majesty's hands.
Wenceslau de Lima.

Notes. From the pencil decode of the time above the groups; in the reading [ ] marks our addition and [?] a group not read or doubtful. Mostly in clear. 50667 is circled on the form; the receiver wrote "uma palavra indecifravel" (one undecipherable
word), so the hole is his as well as ours. The decoder wrote "tornaria" over 58538 28787; 28787 is "-ria" also in
"chega-ria" (m0011).
