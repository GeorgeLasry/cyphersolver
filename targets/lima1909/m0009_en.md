# m0009: English translation

The lack of an answer to Saturday's telegram about the return trains is causing the greatest trouble. I remind you
that the day after tomorrow is the day of Your Majesty's departure, and nothing is fixed for want of that instruction,
which I ask for most urgently. Vasconcellos Porto has telegraphed [two groups unread]. [We are working] on the
assumption of the Northern express, but Your Majesty's decision is missing. I kiss Your Majesty's hands. Wenceslau de
Lima.

Notes. From the pencil decode of the time above the groups; in the reading [ ] marks our addition and [?] a group not read or doubtful. "Saturday's telegram" is m0011 of 27 Nov 1909, a Saturday. The glosses over 18703 and 10586 are too faint to read
("comp..são", "Th.." [?]). The office noted that a correction would follow for a missing word; the word count is one
short.
