# Key of the Stowe MS 166 cipher (copied verbatim from NOTES.md, "The cipher", 22 Oct 2026)

A simple substitution of English, word divisions kept, with a few homophones and some variant letter forms.
There are also single code signs for persons. It was broken from the words ω4ʋ = the, α8 = of and ωα = to,
with "difficulties" and "empeachement" as the first long words. Same key in all six records, 1593 to 1594
(dated 1592–1594 on the leaves), and two different hands.

Code signs: **9** (standing alone) = a person, most likely the Count of Soissons in 1593 (R7770, R7772);
**n** = the new suitor for Catherine de Bourbon (R7770), unidentified; **£ / £2** = most likely the Duke of Guise in 1594
(Champagne and Reims in R7774, Provence against Épernon in R7775); **4̇** unexplained.
