# fr. 3625 no. 55, f. 66r — Laurière (La Verrière) to the Duke of Nevers, Châlons, 9 July 1593: the cipher runs

Written 23 Oct 2026 (Claude Opus 5.5, verification 4) from `../reading_3625_55.txt` (setsunaatto's transcription and
decipherment with Nevers key no. 57, issue 13, 29 Sept 2026; lines 1-2 checked group for group against our own
`../ct_3625_55.txt`) and the table in NOTES.md. The letter is in clear French with eight cipher runs; the clear text is
not transcribed in full, so each run is given with the clear words that lead into it (from the transcription's run
headings and NOTES), in roman, and the deciphered run follows. Spelling is the key's letters (*atandre*, *prandre*,
*anpescheman*); name signs are written out (the crossed X = le Roy, XX = le Pape, the Lorraine, Gondi and Tuscany
signs). "come" over 347 is the gloss on the leaf. Marks: `[x]` a letter missing in the cipher itself, `[…]` clear text
not transcribed.

## Reading

[…] pour vous faire resouvenir come vous estes necessaire pres du Roy, afin qu'il ne retarde d'ung jour son
assemblee, ce que ses ennemys luy donne[r]ont tout l'anpescheman[t] qu'ilz pourront pour luy faire prandre autre
dessein.

Revenant a mon voiage, le duc de Lorraine montre desir de traiter, mais necessaire avant que le Roy soit catholique.

[…] quoy fait une election […] ce moquans de cela.

[…] s'il veult atandre la volonte du Pape, non aussitost qu'il sera catholique, qui la traitera.

[…] et en donne sa parolle le cardinal de Gondy pour le grand duc de Toscane.
