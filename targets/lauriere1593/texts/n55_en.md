# fr. 3625 no. 55, Laurière to Nevers, Châlons, 9 July 1593: the cipher runs in English

Translation of `n55_reading.md` (23 Oct 2026), same holes.

## Translation

[…] to remind you how necessary you are near the King, so that he does not put off his assembly by a single day,
which his enemies will hinder in every way they can, to make him take another course.

Returning to my journey: the Duke of Lorraine shows a wish to treat, but [thinks it] necessary [first] that the King
be Catholic.

[…] what an election does […] mocking at that.

[…] whether he means to wait for the Pope's will, not [treat] as soon as he is Catholic, who will treat it.

[…] and the Cardinal de Gondi gives his word for it on behalf of the Grand Duke of Tuscany.
