# Re-reading log, Cotton Caligula C II f. 74r (23 Oct 2026, Claude Opus 5.5; REREAD_BRIEF, batch b31)

## Starting point

Reading audit (22 Oct): "decoded, not read": `f74r_decryption.md`, a line-by-line reading with holes or unkeyed signs in
ll. 1, 4-6, 8-10, 12-17.

## 1. Is there a text already?

No. Tomokiyo (cryptiana `mary.htm`) lists the letter as undeciphered and left a partial overlay
(`img/mary_CottonCaligulaCII_f74.png`). CSP Scotland iii (Boyd 1903, archive.org `calendarstatepa02boydgoog`, OCR
searched for Calig. C II, Scrope, Talbot, harquebus, "of this instant") calendars the sibling Mary-Norfolk ciphers of 1570
(no. 520 and the letters appended to Cecil's extracts, "cipher, with decipher of the same") but not this letter; no
decipherment of f. 74 was found. Not a DECODE record. The BL images (bl.digirati.io IIIF, 2023 stitch in the main
checkout `img/f74r_text.jpg`) are the only source; f. 74v (`img/f74v_s4.jpg`) was seen in the earlier session.

## 2. Sign-by-sign re-read

All 23 lines transcribed as sign tokens on the slope-following strips (`view/L01a..L23d.jpg`, stacked views and
enlargements in the scratchpad `reread/n74/`): `f74r_signs.tsv` (tokens, decode, reading), corrections with their evidence
in `f74r_corrections.tsv` (20 rows). Main corrections: l. 1 *[g]entle* was an emendation, the sign is 4 (l): [?lentle];
l. 7 the supplied *be not [lost]* withdrawn, the signs carry *demen*; *B[ishop of Ross]* withdrawn (one φ, no Ross sign);
l. 8 the supplied *[to]* is an unkeyed sign {o°}; l. 10 *be[gan]* is *begen* + 3 blotted signs; l. 12 the supplied
*[It]* dropped; l. 13 *the veyt* is *that veyt*; l. 15 *ve have som[e]* not carried (no *have* sign), the run after *ve*
unread; l. 20 *[lie?]* withdrawn.

## 3. Key

No value adopted. Every unkeyed sign occurs once on the page (⊕ l. 2, ⊔ l. 4, ∨ l. 6, ⊘ and o° l. 8, rʒ l. 9, ø l. 13,
⊗ l. 17, three in l. 1) or in the tail-dash family {a—} l. 5, {o—} l. 11, {Ш—} l. 15 (letter + long dash: probably
nomenclator words; *our {o—} Murray* = "enemy" is sense-only, flagged, not adopted). ff. 66, 69, 75 not searched for them.
