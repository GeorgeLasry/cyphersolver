# Matheo de Segura to the Constable of Castile, Madrid, 8 August 1596 (BnF espagnol 336 no. 99): English translation of the cipher passages

## A — f. 196v

… for they will not grant a favour but only strict justice, with which I would be content if it is kept. **To Ponte, I
truly believe,** friendship is owed, and I do not know whether they agree, **whether he and Escudero agree**, nor whether
there is any cause of clash between them, for if there were, it would cost both of them their money; I would not want
Your Excellency's to diminish for this reason, and as for the others, let each one's luck help him; and so I shall go
back to Toledo to finish off these matters, and I shall report what there is.

## B — f. 197r

A very weighty councillor has told me that they have held a meeting here about the comet that has appeared these days,
and **they say that the world will end or the king will die around September. He seems to be very near it. I believe in
God.**
