## Translation

The pasha of Nándorfehérvár [Belgrade] received Your Majesty's gift kindly, and had it praised; whereupon he said
that, if Your Majesty would send six or seven thousand troops against the Serbs, he assures Your Majesty that he
would help Your Majesty's troops with troops, powder and grain as well.

[A short, hard-to-read sentence about the kuruc envoys.] Having arrived here, I received Your Majesty's, my Lord's,
letter. I went and asked that the deputation [obtain an audience / be heard more fully] before the grand vizier,
which he promised to do. [His intermediary] sent word that there would have been an audience, but the Hungarian […].
