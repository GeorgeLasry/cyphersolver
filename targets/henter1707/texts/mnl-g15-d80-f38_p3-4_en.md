# Hentér to Rákóczi II, 8 July 1707, paragraphs 3-4 (English)

Translation of `mnl-g15-d80-f38_p3-4_reading.md`, same holes.

## Translation

And he sent word that we would have had our audience, but a disturbance broke out among the Janissaries.

Wanting to change the chief [?officials], [they say] we should wait until matters are settled.

In the course of time we again solicited the audience twice, and he sent word that they should have patience; they
will be called to present the letters.

Lately, too, I am hopeful about the liberation of Your Highness's poor captive, my [?lord].

For which may God bless Your Highness; and I [?for my part] remain to the end of my life Your Highness's humble,
faithful servant,

Mihály Hentér.
