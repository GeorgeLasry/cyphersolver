# Childs residue, the nine messages read in full (German ADFGVX radio traffic, November 1918): English translation

p. 100. No interference from the enemy. At midday 2 enemy divisions on the march towards Belgrade.

p. 105. Germania, base (Etappe) Constantinople. For Mediterranean Division, re tel. no. 62. Div. telegram no. 58
done. 32141. Q4 [...] of 4 November dealt with. Admiralty Staff (Admiralstab). 32398. B.

p. 109. O.K.M. evening report. S4V4. Quartering of the last elements completed. 1 further DU. Div 5 on the march
towards Belgrade KAVV. 2. Otherwise no events. ASO. K511.

p. 132. For EILVESE. Repeat telegram from fourth period in fifth. Prayer order 5 min. VVV.

p. 146. Radio station Kertsch [Kerch] is operating. 1F. Call signs Richard Emil Karl. Notify the radio stations of
that area. Chief of signals 4B. 7834.

p. 171. In the Ukraine and Poland rouble rates rising sharply as a result of the break between Germany and the
Soviet government and the expectation of the restoration of Russia by Germany and the Entente.

p. 176. Breakthrough prepared. Whether the direction of the breakthrough will be to the north or north-east cannot
yet be judged.

p. 187 (part 1). Intercept Russian and Polish army traffic in full. Send anything important, especially from Polish
traffic, enciphered by radio via the OHL [Supreme Army Command] station.

p. 187 (part 2, AFAFF). As far as the teleprinter connection is not working. The rest in writing. To the chief.
