## What the document is
Three small NARA RG 393 series of Union Army cipher telegrams:
- Middle Department (Baltimore), "Press Copies of Cipher Telegrams Sent", Jan-Jun 1865, naId 4486363, 0.5 in.
- Dept of the Gulf / Fifth Military District (New Orleans), "Telegrams Sent in Cipher", Dec 1866-Apr 1867, naId 5081118, 2 in. (scope note: "These records are in cipher").
- Fifth Military District, Office of Civil Affairs, "Cipher Telegrams Sent and Received", Feb-Jun 1868, naId 5131597, 1 in.
Correspondents: department/district commanders (Hancock, Sheridan, ...) and the War Department and posts. Language English (expected).
