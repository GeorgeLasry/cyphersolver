# HStAM 4 f Dänemark Nr. 125, ff. 2-4 (HCPortal 494), Hamburg 4/14 May 1672: English translation of the cipher items in their clauses

Translation (Claude Opus 5.5, 23 Oct 2026) of `HCP494_reading.md`, **an excerpt**: the ciphered items with the clauses
round them; the clear German letter round them is not transcribed in the repository ("..." marks it). Resolved codes
are in {braces} as in the reading; `[?Ahlefeldt]` (code 625) and `[?Brunswick]` (602) are doubtful; `[?]` five signs
that make no word. The middle sentences mix German, French and Latin as the letter does.

## Translation

But at the time of {Blumenthal}'s presence ... [it] would have given a perpetual misunderstanding between {the King
of Denmark} and {the Elector of Brandenburg} ... and [?Ahlefeldt] has done signal services in this ... that he has
received from {the Elector of Brandenburg} ... the portrait set with diamonds. Although in {Berlin} they stretch the
bow of ceremonies far too high ... that no one goes to {the Elector of Brandenburg} with any other character than
Minister of the King. {Blumenthal}'s despatch has still been favourable ... {Holland} ... if {the States General}
... and pays {the King of Denmark} his due respect. That {France} has concluded with {Sweden} and passed {Denmark}
by gives annoyance. [?Ahlefeldt] will, after the lapse of some weeks, go to {Berlin} upon this and bring the work to
a conclusion, so that {the Elector of Brandenburg} be drawn into the Danish-Brunswick alliance.

Totally disgusted, and [?] in {Holstein}, now as well as in {Denmark}, nobility, burghers and peasants are now
everywhere combined in complaints.

If formerly he was not ... an insidious enemy to {the Swede}: God grant that all may turn out well. [He] was also at
Hamburg, and the same has [made] a close {alliance} between {Duke} [?of Brunswick] and {the King of Denmark} ...
