# No. 17, Smith to Cecil, Bourbon-l'Archambault, 22 March 1566 — English

[?Not] that crown (the Scottish one; they look for a bigger). (That) young [?King] and Queen (Darnley and Mary). Intelligence
and practices in England. Foreign realms.

The Pope's and the King of Spain's [?hands] are in that (dish). French; French; that.

That had both one dispatch. Have seen. Carried, and one […] brought the. A […] secret dispatch of. Nor. From Scotland.
[?Go] unsearched.

The Ambassadors of Spain and Scotland and the Cardinal of Lorraine (consult together) overmuch; the Bishop of Glasgow
expects to be made a cardinal; popery; (not only in) Scotland but also (in) England.

Which (letters) long [?came] out of Scotland. A fat Scottish woman; she was married (to a captain); she is the carrier of
(letters) out of Scotland to the […] (Lady) Lennox. From her; the Queen's […]; her to; be discovered, and […] practices.
