# No. 20, Smith to Cecil, Poissy, 20 October 1562 — English

Newhaven (Le Havre) ⟨is not as strong as people think; if it were summer, they would try to drive our men out of it
with a⟩ "garden of chamillion" (Smith's veiled phrase, meaning not settled) on the hill on this side. ⟨Those who know the place will understand
what I mean. And the works begun there by⟩ the French ⟨are not finished.⟩
