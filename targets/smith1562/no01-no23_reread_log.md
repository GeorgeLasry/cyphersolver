# Re-reading log, Smith letters nos. 01, 03-06, 08, 09, 11, 13, 15-20, 22, 23 (22 Oct 2026, Claude Opus 5.5; REREAD_BRIEF, batch b03)

## Starting point

Reading audit (22 Oct): seventeen of the 23 letters "decoded, not read" (passage tables of isolated names, code items and
fragments in decipher_A/B/D/E.md), nos. 01 and 04 not transcribed. Add MS 4136 is Patrick Forbes's deciphering file:
for every letter he copied only the ciphered stretches, numbered (1), (2) ...; the clear text of the letters is not in
the manuscript.

## Sources used

- Images: DECODE R9248, R9250, R9252, R9253, R9254 (already in the main checkout, targets/smith1562/decode/, git-ignored)
  and R9236 (fetched 22 Oct with the project cookie, targets/bordeaux/decode/cookie.txt; kept in the session scratchpad
  only). Key: R9261 p. 1 (Add MS 4136 f. 179), re-read in three crops for the word column.
- Forbes, *A Full View* vol. ii (1741), archive.org item `AFullViewofthePublicTransactionsintheReignofQ.Elizabeth`
  (Google scan; leaves 151, 155, 353-355, 416-425, 450-452 fetched as page images; the OCR in forbes2.txt garbles some
  lines). **Forbes sets every deciphered word in italic**, so his print gives both the deciphered passages and the clear
  sentences around them: this is what turns the Forbes letters into readings.
- CSP Foreign vi-viii (csp/*.html, already in the main checkout): calendar summaries of the 1563-1566 letters, from the
  originals "deciphered". Used for the setting only; their wording is a paraphrase and is not put into the readings.
- No other print of the 1563-66 letters was found (web search for the Arles 24 Nov 1564 text; Forbes ends July 1563).

## Key observations (hold across the documents)

- M̈ = my in no. 03 (print), m̈ba = married in no. 17; in no. 08 M̈ is my or marry by context; m̄ = marriage (nos. 11,
  13, 15); the key's "M̄ more" is a different mark. Key sheet values used (checked on R9261 p. 1): dotted sign = secret,
  Γ̇ can, Γ̈ carry, Γ̣ came, :Γ captain, Γ+ϑ crown, k̈ king, ƙ kindred, .ꝏ overmuch, Ṗ privy, ·P practises, ꟿ she,
  :ꝏ woman, the C-family who / will / upon / whom (C̄ = whom), .ẏ young.
- ·∂ = sea in "by sea" (nos. 11, 15), a code word not on the key sheet.
- ‖ (double bar) = i on ff. 140 and 159 (it, Warwick, given, is, necessarie, I); the line-initial sign before 4 on l. 3 of
  no. 22 is read ll (la Rochelle), as decipher_E noted for the key's double-bar row.
- the dotted sign = secret (key; no. 19 "secretary"); the C-family = who/will/upon/whom (key); .ſ = shall; Γ̣ = came, Γ̈ = carry, Γϑ = crown; k̈ = king;
  .ꝏ = overmuch; Ṗ = privy; ꟿ = she (all as on the key).
- Forbes's copying slips: dots dropped from 5./9./10. (r, s), 3 for m (chamillion), 4 for e (speake, whose, carte), :b where the sense wants "sent" (no. 22).
