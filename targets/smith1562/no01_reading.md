# No. 01, Sir Thomas Smith to the Queen, Chartres, 17 January 1562/3

⟨The 14th cam a secretary with lettres⟩ from the Cardinal of Lorraine.

⟨I do take yt marvelous necessary, that⟩ some man were sent to be with the Admirall who now is chief on the other
side; that your Majesty may be as well advertised what is done there, as you may be what is done here. Of things
done on that side ⟨we can lerne els but only such as yt pleaseth them here to brute abrode.⟩ And he wold be one who
hath some familiaritie, acquaintance, and credit with them; ⟨and⟩ I think Mr Middlemore were a very ⟨handsom and fit
man.⟩ As for help, if your Majestie be not minded to revoke me, ⟨I do not dowte but to do well enough.⟩

⟨I may be deceived; but by all the means, wais, and intelligence which I can learne, they myende⟩ to com to no
agreement nor apointment with your Majesty: ⟨they will rather⟩ agree to all the Admiral's demands. ⟨As for religion,
they make no difficultie here in wordes.⟩ For the offices; the Constable ⟨shall greatly helpe to yt: who will begyn and
doth now, as men say,⟩ suspect the greatnes of the Duke of Guise, and consider the ruyne of the house of
Chastillon, being his nerest kindred in France, next his owne children.

⟨Yet methincks I do se,⟩ that if the Admiral do holde out a while, or give them again but a doubtful battle as the
last was, ⟨necessitie will compell them to com to accorde.⟩ ⟨What consideration⟩ the Admiral and his sequele will
have of your Majestie, ⟨I am not able to say; with whom I never had conference nor acquaintance:⟩ but if one were
with him from your Majestie as your minister, ⟨I wold thincke, it wold stand your Highnes to so good purpose, as none
could be more.⟩

⟨Marry, for all that I cannot yet se or lerne, best it were for your Majeste to trust unto your own strength, and so
provide, as that⟩ which you wold do were done with all spede; to lose no tyme, so long as you may have help of the
Frenche of that faction which be now at your devotion; to use both their countenaunce and help, while time may serve
thereto: making of them as confederates ⟨who be sone wery of the travaile, where all the profit is not thers.⟩
