## What the records are

Add MS 4136 is **Patrick Forbes's own deciphering file** for *A Full View of the Public Transactions in the Reign of
Q. Elizabeth* (2 vols, 1740-41). Forbes copied only the ciphered stretches of each despatch, numbered them (1), (2), …,
and filed the keys he used at ff.177-190 (DECODE key records R9260, R9261, R9262). DECODE lists the six Smith
records as "Non-decrypted"; they were in fact deciphered at the time (the SP 70 originals are "deciphered" in CSP
Foreign) and the 1562/3 ones were printed in full by Forbes.

**The key is on DECODE: R9261 p.1 = Add MS 4136 f.179, "Sr Thomas Smith's Cipher".** It is a homophonic alphabet
(symbols for a-m, digits 2-10 for n-p and, with a dot or colon, for r/s and t/u/y), a word list of Latin letters
distinguished by dots, colons and bars, about 30 name symbols, and a column of "Nothings" (nulls): Garnesy, London,
Lyn, Boston, Poole, Dover, Hastings, and, bycause, don, for, yf, Hul, Calais, Jersey, Andwerpe, Lyn, Sandwich,
Dunkirke, by, do. The clear English place names in the passages are these nulls. Summary in KEY.md.
(R9261 p.2 is Sir James Croft's cipher; R9262 is Throckmorton's third cipher.)

## Key additions from the letters
- "enos" = Ambassador (code, not null; gloss in the 20 Nov margin, and the key's top line "enos Ambassador").
- M̅/M̈ = marriage/marry; B: = Bishop; C̄ = Congregation; J̣ = is/are; ! = in/into; :F = French.
- {{, ſſ, ꟿ = st; ʋ = l; looped kite = w; the flag sign = Archduke Charles/the Emperor.
- An England-sign variant = Earl (before Murray, Lennox, Warwick).
- Forbes's copying slips: single dot for the colon (v), 4 for e, ⊔ for π.

## Prior art / contamination
- Deciphered at the time (CSP Foreign: "portions in cipher, deciphered"). The 1562/3 letters were printed in full
  by Forbes 1740-41 from the originals. The 1563/4-1566 letters are only summarised in CSP Foreign vols 6-8
  (1869-71); the verbatim ciphered text of those passages was not in print as far as found here.
- Our readings were made from the glyphs with the key on R9261; Forbes and CSP were used as confirmation cribs.
