# No. 08, Sir Thomas Smith to Cecil, Valence, 1 September 1564

Scotland; […]; Scotland. The amity; England; Scotland.

The Lord of Murray; such lords and great men; be the great favourers of religion there.

To do [?some] great enterprise that way. The Scots […]; of the religion; for [?my] […] Lord of Murray.

The Earl of Huntly died, and his sons were taken.

Bishops here; the Pope's counsel; be as busy as may be.

The Lord of Lethington; is at Bruges; the Bishop of Glasgow; pension; […] Lord of Lethington, of the French King; of
the King of Spain.

The Queen of Scots; to [?marry] Lord Robert. England and Scotland. […] to the old Pope. England and Scotland.

[?My] Lord of Murray. Of the Queen of Spain. The King of Spain to the Queen of Scots.
