# No. 17, Sir Thomas Smith to Cecil, Bourbon-l'Archambault, 22 March 1565/6

[?Not] that crown. Young [?King] and Queen. Intelligence and practices in England. Foreign realms.

The Pope and the King of Spain['s] [?hands] be in that. French; French; that.

That had both one dispatch. Have seen. Carried and one […] brought the. A […] privy dispatch of. Nor. From Scotland.
[?Go] unsearched.

The Ambassadors of Spain and Scotland and the Cardinal of Lorraine. Overmuch; Bishop of Glasgow looks to be a
cardinal; popery; Scotland but also England.

Which long [?came] out of Scotland. A fat Scottish woman; she was married; she is the conveyer of; out of Scotland to the
[…] Lennox. From her; the Queen's […]; her to; be discovered and […] practices.
