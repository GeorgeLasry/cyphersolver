# No. 01, Smith to Queen Elizabeth, Chartres, 17 January 1563 — English

⟨On the 14th a secretary came with letters⟩ from the Cardinal of Lorraine.

⟨I think it most necessary that⟩ someone be sent to stay with the Admiral (Coligny), who is now the leader on the
other side, so that your Majesty may be as well informed of what is done there as of what is done here. Of what is
done on that side ⟨we learn nothing except what they are pleased to spread abroad here.⟩ He should be someone who has
some familiarity, acquaintance and credit with them; ⟨and⟩ I think Mr Middlemore would be a very ⟨suitable and fit
man.⟩ As for help, if your Majesty does not intend to recall me, ⟨I have no doubt I shall manage well enough.⟩

⟨I may be deceived, but by every means and source I can find, they intend⟩ to come to no agreement or settlement with
your Majesty: ⟨they would rather⟩ agree to all the Admiral's demands. ⟨As for religion, they make no difficulty here
in words.⟩ As for the offices, the Constable (Montmorency) ⟨will greatly help: he will begin, and already does, as
men say, to⟩ suspect the greatness of the Duke of Guise and to weigh the ruin of the house of Châtillon, his nearest
kin in France after his own children.

⟨Yet I think I see⟩ that if the Admiral holds out a while, or gives them another doubtful battle like the last
(Dreux), ⟨necessity will force them to come to terms.⟩ ⟨What regard⟩ the Admiral and his followers will have for your
Majesty ⟨I cannot say, having never met or known him;⟩ but if someone were with him from your Majesty as your
minister, ⟨I think it would serve your Highness as well as anything could.⟩

⟨Indeed, from all I can see or learn, it would be best for your Majesty to trust to your own strength, and so to
provide that⟩ what you mean to do is done with all speed; to lose no time while you can have the help of the French of
that faction who are now at your devotion; to use both their countenance and their help while the time allows:
treating them as confederates ⟨who soon tire of the labour when all the profit is not theirs.⟩
