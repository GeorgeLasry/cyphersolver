# No. 22, Smith to Queen Elizabeth, 4 November 1563 — English

De Montaigne, of whom I [?do] speak, whose map of La Rochelle I have [?sent] herewith; and under another pretext I have
given him letters to my Lord Robert (Dudley) and Mr Secretary (Cecil), to whom I wrote from Rouen about the same
matter. The other letters he carried himself; and in the undertaking I wrote of, I think he can succeed; but this, at
this time, is most necessary.
