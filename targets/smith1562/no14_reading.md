# No. 14, Sir Thomas Smith to Cecil, Arles, 24 November 1564

## Reading

The Cardinal of Lorraine and the Prince of Condé have had conferens and familiar banketting togither at Soissons and
at Nanteuil le chasteau, which was the late [?Cardinal] of Burbons, and all to […] […]. This is much noted here.

They of Guise do complaine that all letters that come from the Cardinal, and the […], be [?opened] and […],
principally at Paris and Lyons. This at this […]; and the Embassatour of Scotland and the Cardinal and Madame de Guise
be daily in great dispeches to and from Scotland and to and from the Cardinal of Lorraine, and all by […].

This bearer Hume is altogether a Lidington, whom you shall find double, I am afraid, or rather triple, having […] and
pencions of all three princes.
