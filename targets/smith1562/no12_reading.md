# No. 12, Sir Thomas Smith to Cecil, Arles, 20 November 1564

## Reading

This [bearer] Adam Hume, I p[erceive] by Mr Alingtons letter which you sent me, was well rewarded. What he deserved I
can not tell; it was he, at the [?least] he was one of them, which with lies and forged invencions to let the peace at
the making thereof made Sir Nicholas almost or altogether mad. [He] [?lay] all this tyme [with] the Ambassador of
Scotland, [?who] […] suspecteth him to be sent from the Protestants of Scotland. The Ambassador is wholly for the
Papistes, and […] glad to be rid of him.

I […] [?understand] now this by Hume['s] small errand […] at I [?marvel]. [?Probably] he had […] [?warned] […]
[?procured] […] of him.
