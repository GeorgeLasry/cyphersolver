# No. 19, Sir Thomas Smith to Cecil, Paris, 15 October 1562

⟨You perceive, what Sir Nicholas doth think of their Ambassador in England: and I am certainly advertised by divers⟩
Scots, ⟨that there be divers sent already, and more to be sent, to move⟩ the Queen of Scots to break with you, and to
make war ⟨out of hand; and that the matter is now in hand and in traffic,⟩ that the hostages which you have shall be
convoyed ⟨off into⟩ Flanders. ⟨For the first matter were appointed, and be going or gone,⟩ secretary Rollet and Monsieur
Vyllemort.
