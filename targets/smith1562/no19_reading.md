# No. 19, Sir Thomas Smith to Cecil, Paris, 15 October 1562

⟨Yow perceive, what Sir Nicholas doth thynk of ther Ambassador in England: and I am certaynlie advertised by diverse⟩
Scots, ⟨that there be divers sent allredie, and more to be sent, to move⟩ the Queen of Scots to break with you, and to
make war ⟨out of hand; and that the mater is now in hand and in traffic,⟩ that the hostages which you have shall be
convoyed ⟨of into⟩ Flanders. ⟨For the furst mater were apoyntid, and be goeng or gone,⟩ secretary Rollet and Monsieur
Vyllemort.
