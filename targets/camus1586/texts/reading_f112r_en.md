# f. 112r (canvas 118 right), cipher block at the head: English

please write to me about the affair of the relative, [?and] make me a separate packet, for otherwise the [?oath-bound one] does not scruple to look at the letters, so curious is he; and if you please, let it be ciphered according to the memorandum I leave you, to which you will please add, for the name of him who carries the letter to the relative, these two words: the brother-in-law or the bearer; and for me: the servant or the obliged one.
