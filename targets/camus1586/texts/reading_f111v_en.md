# f. 111v (canvas 118 left), cipher block at the foot: English

I wrote to the bearer by a man sent expressly, and while awaiting the answer; but since the relative is no longer in this country, I find myself much at a loss how to govern myself, for the said bearer can no longer help me, nor even get me seen.
