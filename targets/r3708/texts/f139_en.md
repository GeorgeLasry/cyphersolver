# BnF fr. 3976 f. 139, letter from Paris, 9 June 1588: English translation

> It is a true thing that the prévôt des marchands has gone back to put himself in the Bastille. I have not been able
> to discover truly that any **levy of money** is being made. But I have certainly been assured, notably by 38
> [Garrault], that **notaries** are seeking **money at interest**, for which **Nilly, Marcel, the prévôt des marchands
> and the échevins** stand surety. But many have made difficulty about lending any, and for my part I am astonished
> that **Marcel is** mixed up **in it**. They say that 107 [the Grand Écuyer] is insinuating himself into the good
> graces of 9 [Guise] and presses him. His brother has also left 10 [Épernon]. But for the haste I am in, I would have
> given you a short, succinct account of what was said in the town assembly of the captains, of which I wrote to you
> before, although I think that 79 will have been able to tell you at length, for it was already known when he left.
> It is held for certain that 11 [the King] grants 9 **the lieutenant-generalship**, and that by this means we shall
> have 98 [peace]. 38 has several pieces of advice to give 24 [Nevers] when he is near 11, both for the general and for
> his own particular, of which he does not inform him now because it would be useless while he is absent. This 9
> June. [monogram]
> I have enclosed a packet for Monsieur de Lorme, physician. The said 38 will not stir.
