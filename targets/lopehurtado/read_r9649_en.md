# R9649: Lope Hurtado de Mendoza (Rome) to Charles V, 9 November 1522: English translation

Translation (Claude Opus 5.5, 23 Oct 2026) of the "Running reading" in `read_r9649.md`: the letter as enciphered,
clear text with three cipher runs (in **bold**), read in full and checked word for word against the contemporary
clear version on f. 268r. No holes. Spanish.

## Translation

His Beatitude has commanded me to write to Your Majesty on his behalf that **the Cardinal of Santa Cruz is pressing
him to death over Ostia**, [claiming it] as he needs it, being the most senior in the College; [and] that he would
like Your Majesty to write to His Holiness that he should order a strong guard put **in the fortress of Ostia, and
not give it to anyone, because he would not want to give it to him, nor to have him aggrieved.**

At this moment I have learned **that the licentiate Bernardino** has come here; he embarked at Barcelona on the 5th
of last month. They tell me he is hidden in the house of a brother of his. I am informing the Duke at once, to see
what he thinks. Let Your Majesty write what you command. May Our Lord keep and increase Your Majesty's most royal
person and estate. From Rome, the 9th of November [1522].
