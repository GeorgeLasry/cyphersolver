# R9652: Lope Hurtado de Mendoza (Rome) to Charles V, November 1522: English translation

Translation (Claude Opus 5.5, 23 Oct 2026) of the text in `read_r9652.md`. This letter is written entirely in the
clear (RAH Salazar 9/26 ff. 295-296); there is no cipher on the leaf. Same marks: `[x]` letters or a word expanded or
supplied by the transcriber. Spanish.

## Translation

I wrote to Your Majesty on the [x] of this month how the **licentiate Bernardino** had come here. Then I went to the
Duke, and spoke to His Holiness, and he told me he would order justice to be done on him, because he was a great
scoundrel.

The Duke came back here at once; he begged His Holiness to be pleased to let him have him arrested. The Pope answered
him that, if he was inside Rome, he would order it to be done, and if he was outside, that he should do as seemed good
to him. The Duke has learned that he is in the city, in his brother's house; he has sent to beg His Holiness to be
pleased that he may have him arrested there one night. The Pope has answered me that here it would not be well, but
that we should put in a petition that His Holiness order justice to be done.

Let Your Majesty write to the Duke what is to be done in this; and it would be well to send an information of his
guilt, so as to proceed against him here as far as possible, to get him out of the Pope['s hands], so that Your
Majesty may order justice to be done. And it seems to me that I ought to inform Your Majesty, so that you may send
orders for what may be for your service. At this hour I am writing the Pope's answer to the Duke, so that he may see
what ought to be done.

I have informed His Holiness of **the news of Rhodes** which the Viceroy writes; I send Your Majesty with the present
the copy of the [Viceroy's] letter and of the news.
