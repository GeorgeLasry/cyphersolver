# R9646 — Lope Hurtado to Charles V, Rome, [November 1522] — English

Translation of `R9646_reading.md`, with the same holes. Passages in cipher in the original are in *italics*.

S. C. Majesty,

On the third of this month I wrote to Your Majesty *that the Pope had come to terms with the Duke of Ferrara*, and the
capitulation has now come signed; the Pope has ordered that I be given a copy of it to send to Your Majesty, and so that
the Duke of Sessa may see the form to be kept with the Duke *of Ferrara*.

The Duke ordered me to speak to the Pope *about the [?tenth]* that Your Majesty asks for; I found His Holiness well
disposed, but he answered me that *he was afraid that there might have to be […] in […]*; he had written of it to Your
Majesty by Rodrigo Niño, and we should wait for what you answered. I spoke to the Datary, the Archbishop and the Auditor
of the Chamber, who are the ones who deal with these matters. The Archbishop has advised me that it will be done; let
Your Majesty answer the Duke at once what you command, and whether it would be well *to offer […] to the Pope for his
needs, for the […], for I believe that for money he [?will do] all […]*; of everything I am informing the Duke.

Eight days ago a courier arrived here coming from Portugal; he passed through Valladolid; he brought the Pope letters of
the 17th of last month from Don Bernardino Pimentel, and they all came in cipher, and of these and of those he has
written to His Holiness up to now since he left Spain they do not know what they say, because Theodoric has lost the
