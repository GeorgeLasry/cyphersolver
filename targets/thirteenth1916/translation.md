# “Thirteenth” table: English translation

Working translations from the Wenmi site; […] = passage not translated for want of a sure reading.

## 0307 | 28 February 1916 | Tokyo → “Junzaburo”, Shanghai (indicator “Thirteenth”, table k13; “bearer” in clear) | JACAR B03050088000, PDF page 28

Zhongyuan (Deng Keng) is returning on the Iyo-maru. Please have 10,000 yuan ready to buy bearer notes (“bearer”, in clear) of the Yokohama Specie Bank, to be handed over as soon as the ship reaches Shanghai; and tell Ruwei (Xu Chongzhi) to go to the ship to confer and inspect the items. — Wen. The 28th.

## 0348 | 12 March 1916 | Tokyo → “Junzaburo”, Shanghai | JACAR B03050088100, PDF page 13

Telegram received. Within a day or two I shall wire the full amount, 200,000, for Jiangsu and Zhejiang (?) and the Second Squadron. Set to work quickly.

## 0382 | 16 March 1916 | Tokyo → “Junzaburo”, Shanghai | JACAR B03050088100, PDF page 47

Today 110,000 yen were wired through the Yokohama Specie Bank (正金) to Chen Qimei. Please allocate […] 10,000 Shanghai dollars. Act with caution in everything and aim at complete success. — Wen. The 16th.
