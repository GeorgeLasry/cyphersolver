# R9412, an agent at Ofen (Buda) to a duke of Bavaria, 8 Feb [1534]: English translation

Translated from `R9412_reading.md` (Claude Opus 5.5, 23 Oct 2026): German cipher, read with the R9411 key refitted on
German; no contemporary decipherment of this letter. The Latin passages in capitals are clear on the page and are
given here in English in SMALL CAPITALS style (upper case). Holes as in the reading: `[..]` not read; a word in [ ]
supplied from sense; `[?word]` a conjecture. "Your Grace" renders *Eurn G.* The passages the reading marks as not
building sentences (TERTIO, and the part on the plans against Transylvania) are translated word for word as far as
they go, and read as broken in English too.
