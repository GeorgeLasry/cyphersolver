# R100 — English (Cardinal Alessandrino to the nuncio, cipher of spring 1569)

From the way His Majesty's ministers proceed one can see that they intend to deceive Your Lordship in that matter of
the Inquisition of Catalonia, and they prevent those people from coming to you for absolution. Here the Ambassador is
now pressing His Holiness that this case be referred to His Majesty in Spain; so, without waiting for any resolution
from here other than the first, Your Lordship is to put the brief into execution when the parties ask you for it.
