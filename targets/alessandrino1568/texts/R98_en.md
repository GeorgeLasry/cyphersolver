# R98 — English (Cardinal Alessandrino to the nuncio, cipher, 1568)

Same holes as R98_reading.md. Modern spelling; codes as names.

Although the orders which the King wrote in recent days that he had sent to his ministers in Italy, so that they
should be provided in case the Huguenots of France wished to turn towards these parts, were good and suited to the
need, nevertheless, because such provisions are often delayed by the ministers themselves wanting to wait for new
commissions from the King and new limited orders, whereby harm is done or at least the enterprise is put off while
these commissions are awaited, Your Lordship will therefore press His Majesty to give express and limited orders to
his ministers in Italy now, so that if the case should ever come — which God forbid — His Holiness [?could] make use
of them in time and place, for the public good [?and] for the security of Italy and of His Majesty's states […].
