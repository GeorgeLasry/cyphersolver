I wrote to [4050] [?of Alessandrino] two months ago, on the last day of November, that he should ask the King in His
Holiness's name for the nomination to the church of Monreale in the person of Don [?Francisco] de Reinoso, private
steward of [2010], and also the consent to be able to transfer the pensions that he had laid on the archdeaconry of
Toledo onto the same church, informing me [?also] of the truth […]; of […] that, as I have had no [9070] so far, His
Holiness wished me to give Your Lordship the same commission, so that you do not fail to speak of it with the King
[…] first you can have a good occasion, pressing him for the speedy dispatch, so that the matter does not drag on
for ever, and then to let me know […] his decision. […] the cause of […] […] the trade from there His Holiness will
[…] satisfy, even if it is pursued by those means that seem best to the King.
