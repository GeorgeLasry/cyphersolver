# R102 — English (Cardinal Alessandrino to the nuncio, cipher, summer 1569)

Same holes as R102_reading.md. Modern spelling; codes as names.

In the public letter I have written more mildly of Cardinal [Espinosa] than His Holiness has in truth spoken of him
and of his bad conduct; but I want you to know, for your own information, that His Holiness ordered me to let Your
Lordship know that the Cardinal fails in many things to do the office he should with the King in the service of this
Holy See, so that His Holiness has judged him unworthy of the dignity he holds, and has said at times that he [?did
not] regret having made him cardinal. But if one could hope for amendment in him, it would be well to let him know it;
but in this Your Lordship will act as you think fits the time and the persons, leaving it to your [?own] prudence.
