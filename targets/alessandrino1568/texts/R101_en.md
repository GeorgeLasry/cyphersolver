# R101 — English (Cardinal Alessandrino to the nuncio, cipher of summer 1569)

For Your Lordship's instruction, [you will know?] that His Holiness [recently?] accepted the hackney and the customary
moneys paid on St Peter's day, but with a protest by the fisc, to which His Holiness adheres — that is, that he has
not accepted it as a census but as the fruits of that kingdom, a direct fief of this Holy See — and claims the
devolution [of the kingdom] on many heads of the investiture of Julius II and Julius III, of holy memory. [9090] the
King or his ministers by his order [have] stirred up a manifest schism in that kingdom, as anyone free of passion can
make out, taking away obedience to the Apostolic See, so that neither the bishops nor their vicars can have notaries
to serve them, nor executors who, for the most part, in doing their office are not imprisoned by the royal ministers;
and […] from this […] a doctor from [?Sora], of decrepit age, was left to die in prison. If such is His Majesty's
mind, as his ministers say, His Holiness will have patience and will pray the Lord God to free His holy Church from
such tribulation; but let the King beware of the judgement of God, who is indeed wont to permit His Church to be
troubled, but in the end whoever troubles it will not go unpunished; and whoever is disobedient to God and to His
vicar, it will be no wonder if he loses the obedience of his own subjects. Therefore, if the King does not resolve to
remedy it soon, His Holiness will be obliged to take some measure there [6020] [8020] of Your Lordship.
