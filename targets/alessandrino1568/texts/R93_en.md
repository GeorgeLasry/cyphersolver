# R93 — English (Cardinal Alessandrino to the nuncio, cipher, 1568)

Same holes as R93_reading.md. Modern spelling; codes as names.

His Holiness is asked here by some friends of the crown of France to use his good offices with the Emperor so that he
be pleased to give one of his daughters as wife to the King of France; His Holiness has not wished to set this
negotiation moving without knowing the King's mind in this matter. Your Lordship will therefore find it out in the
way that seems most suitable and fitting to you, assuring him that His Holiness will never say a word of it before he
knows what his intention is, expressly; and once you have learned it […], you will let me know at the first
opportunity.
