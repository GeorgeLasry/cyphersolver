# R95 — English (Cardinal Alessandrino to the nuncio, cipher of early 1568)

Answering your letters of last month more particularly, I tell you that, were what the French Ambassador has said not
true — about being able to raise troops in the State of Milan in case it were attacked from here — it would give the
King just cause to give up his protection and even to become indignant with that Ambassador, who spreads such
falsehoods. Now as to the [08.: the Crusade?], His Holiness did not say it […] so that those who gave the alms should
not gain the indulgence, but so that Christian discipline should not slacken in Spain, as has happened elsewhere; but
His Holiness is content to grant him a new concession of it in such manner as his conscience allows, at the great
instance made [an] by […], who in the office he [?performed] on this matter did not fail to say that the King would
become estranged from this Holy See; but that, if that happened, His Holiness would grieve more for the loss the King
would make of his soul than for any other human consideration. And in this same sense Your Lordship is to speak
whenever you hear the subject raised.
