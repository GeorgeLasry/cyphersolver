# R96 — English (Cardinal Alessandrino to the nuncio, cipher of Rome, 19 May 1568)

Same holes as R96_reading.md. Modern spelling; codes as names.

His Holiness waits to hear some answer from Your Lordship on the particular of the marriage between the Emperor and
the King of France, about which I wrote to you in the last few days so that you might learn the Emperor's view of it,
His Holiness judging that if by chance it were concluded, one could easily hope for some good union or league among
these three chief princes of Christendom. You will let the King know as soon as possible that in this Holy Office
Scipione [?Cuttinardo] has been charged as a heretic, who, it is understood here, aspires to be Regent of the
Collateral [Council of Naples]; and as the news is of great importance, as you see, do not fail to tell it to the
King at once in His Holiness's name.

If in the execution of that order against the Hermits of St Jerome there should be opposition or any difficulty, once
Your Lordship has made the proper inquiries you must [give] me notice of everything, so that from here provision can
be made for [?whatever] will be needed in this matter.
