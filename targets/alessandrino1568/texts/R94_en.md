# R94 — English (Cardinal Alessandrino to the nuncio, cipher of [7 November 1567])

Taking a good occasion, and invited also by the talk His Majesty sometimes has with Your Lordship on these matters of
jurisdiction, His Holiness wishes you to tell him freely that His Holiness does not intend to revoke his ancient
privileges, but rather to remove the abuses that have grown up in them through the fault of his ministers, who, for
their own private interest more than for anything else, have tried to enlarge them and to use them to the detriment
of that authority which granted His Majesty's predecessors the graces and privileges they allege. But this will have
to be said at a suitable moment, as I tell you, […] His Majesty begins […] freely, and is out of the trouble of the
funerals; adding further that, should His Majesty be resolved, through his ministers, to strip this Holy See in fact
of its authority in some cases, His Holiness — not with temporal forces but with those the Lord God has granted him —
will put up every strong and lively resistance, even to the shedding of blood.
