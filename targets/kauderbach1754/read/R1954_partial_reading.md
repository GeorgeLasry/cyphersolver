## Notes

- *l'inpossible* (f. 1, figures 69 27 11 73 24 36 31 07): *in* is the code 27; read on the image (the September transcription
  had 25).
- *Yorcke* (13 73 92 43 76): Colonel Joseph Yorke, British minister at The Hague; the clear N. 21 writes *York*.
- *satisfaction*: the figures give *satisfac-c-c-tion* (43 three times), a scribal repetition.
- *ne peut […] refuser*: the figures first give a false start, *refu-l-pas-e-cou-tre-*, then *refuser ce secours*.
- *et l'etat*: the figures give *et et l'etat* (26 twice).
- *[?interet]*, *[?interets]*: the figures give *l ce re t* / *ce re t s* (69 21 03 29): code 21 (*ce*) stands where *inte*
  is wanted, twice; not adopted as a key change (21 = *ce* reads in some twenty other places).
- *[?jetter]*: the figures give *ie sa te r* (06 49 71 92); the image shows 4 where *jetter* needs 2.
- *[?au]jourdhui*: the figures give 07 (*le*) where *au* (04) is wanted.
