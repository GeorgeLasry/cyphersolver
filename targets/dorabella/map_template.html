It behaves like English letters

On the statistics no key can change, Dorabella is a typical sample of English letters, and of Elgar's own letters (0.89). Speech sounds 0.002, music 0.03.

R25 · Two simple generators0.01

But not English under any key

English under a random key misses the flips (11 against 27). A writer who just flips often misses the repeated pairs. Both fail.

R26 · Combined0.71

English under a flip-heavy key fits

A key that puts common neighbours on opposite sides of the dial reproduces all six statistics at once. Under such a key a clean transcription would solve about 98% of the time. This one does not.
