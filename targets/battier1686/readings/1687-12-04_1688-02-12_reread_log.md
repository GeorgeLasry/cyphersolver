## 2. Re-read on the scans and the key

Every cipher line of 92L, 91R (doubtful groups), 99R and 100R cut at native resolution and compared group by group with
`tokens/s0*.txt`; every doubtful group looked up on the key scans (NA 1.10.29 inv. 1209) with
`research/reading_audit/b17_keylook.py` (key/colindex.txt gives scan, column and row). Findings, all logged in
`readings/1687-12-04_corrections.tsv` and `readings/1688-02-12_corrections.tsv`:

- 92L 9?9 = 12:908 Turcken (struck middle digit = block 12; key 908 turken): "als de vrede met de Turcken sich gesloten wort".
- 92L 352 carries the backslash mark: 3:352 = by ("by consequentie").
- 92L 403 = 11:403 Secreet fits "dat men alles voor [?seker] [?secreet] houdt, op dat de Fransche soo veel minder gewaar
  worden"; the block-11 stroke is on the group before it, so both stay [?].
- 100R 4ø (r) = 8:48 minste ("sonder het minste fundament"); 455 with the c/ mark = 14:455 voordragen; the struck
  ꝕ7ø = 11:678 soo ("soo dat schynt").
- Two cipher runs were missing from the token files: 99R last line = "Gravinne van Soissons" (letters), and 100L line 3
  (11:784 de Coninginne and four groups without sense).
- The "3-shaped" mark is the e-mark (block 6): 99R "374 [e] = 6:374 in" confirms it. Not applied to the earlier
  "4:696 en (mark 3-shaped)" readings, which are kept as the decoder had them (en fits every place; 6:696 not checked).
- Battier's ø is 8 in most groups (2ø = 28, 74ø = 748, 33ø = 338) but the frequent 9ø (de) is 4:90: the struck o is
  read 0 or 8 by the key word that fits, as the earlier transcription did.

Failures (left as numbers in the readings): 92L 889 and the letters a-u-t-o-r-i-s-g before "soon"; 91R 11:466 and the
end of the Novelli sentence (14:6ø, 1:894); 99R L07 431 374 137 512 and L08 604 (the last campaign? 'groote' expected);
100R L04 the struck group after Madrid and 59ø ø39; L07 the struck group after gesoubconneert; 100L 867 826 296 765.
