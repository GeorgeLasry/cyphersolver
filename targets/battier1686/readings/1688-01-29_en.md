# Battier to Fagel, Madrid, 29 January 1688 (end of the letter): English translation

⟨Of the government of the Netherlands there is now very little talk, and the friends of the Marquis of Gastañaga hope
that His Excellency will be continued for three more years. But since by this post the Elector Palatine offers Mr
Novelli the ministry at this court, and says he will send letters of credence to His Honour by the next post, it is easy
to presume that⟩ the Grand Master's pretension is not yet entirely [?], which I shall surely learn from him ⟨when his
letters of credence and his instructions come. Meanwhile the⟩ guard Don Marcos de Velasco has also [?] [?], and offers
[?] rixdollars for it; ⟨likewise yet another gentleman presents himself⟩ who is willing to give a [?] sum. ⟨But before he
makes any effective suit, they will not tell me his name ...⟩
