# Battier to Fagel, Madrid, 13 March 1687, second letter (scan 55R)

## Translation

[...] the Empress says that it matters little whether Spain, which is in any case in so poor a state, [888] itself
with it or not; but for the sake of the consequence Spain will not let itself be brought to it.
