# Battier to Fagel, Madrid, 8 April 1688 (scans 107R-109): English translation

⟨Since the last post ... the Bishop of Breslau and the High Marshal sent to Vienna ... and by this last post⟩ the Grand
Master has ordered his agent to assure the old Queen ⟨that in 14 days without fail the Imperial letters would be here ...
I fear that our slowness will give⟩ Oropesa ⟨time to get ahead of us.⟩ Melgar is still soliciting, and so ⟨Leganés ...⟩

⟨Count Cifuentes, to whom⟩ only so as to be able to make Altamira viceroy of [?] ⟨they had given the viceroyalty of Peru
... Don Gaspar de Silva, Viceroy of Mexico⟩ — Spanish thrift now shows itself here clearly. ⟨Portocarrero has cut His
Majesty short by 60,000 pieces of eight ... No money will be remitted to the Netherlands⟩ as long as Castañaga remains
governor; ⟨at least this is the opinion of everyone ...⟩

⟨Moreover, last Monday⟩ Holstein ⟨formed yet another project and⟩ handed it to Oropesa, ⟨[saying] that with the revenues
of the Netherlands together with the 75,000 pieces of eight a month ... 30,000 men could be maintained ... or something
better would now have to be found for the Master of the Horse.⟩ Meanwhile a sworn party is forming against the same: the
Constable ⟨of Castile⟩, [?Osuna] and [?Monterrey] are brewing something together. ⟨... having fallen into words, the same
threatened⟩ to go and complain to Oropesa, at which Monterrey became so angry that he could hardly get out of his house.
Monterrey would now also lightly wish to be President of Italy, or, as the Flanders presidency is more profitable [that
of Flanders]. For he sees well that if the Grand Master or a creature of Oropesa comes to Flanders, the Netherlands
consents, as happened in Grana's time, will not pass through his hands much. ⟨I remain with respect ...⟩
