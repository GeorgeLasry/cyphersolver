NA 3.01.18 inv. 401, scan 67 right page (67R): Pieter Battier to Gaspar Fagel, Madrid, June 1687. The letter runs over
scans 66R-68L and is signed on 68L "Madrid den 19 [or 10] Juny 1687". Cipher lines 1-3 of 67R, 45 tokens.
Contributed by WAINUT. Tokens: tokens_s067R_L01-03.txt; table with grades: s067R_L01-03.tsv; decode: check_67R.py.
Scan: https://www.nationaalarchief.nl/onderzoeken/archief/3.01.18/invnr/401/file/NL-HaNA_3.01.18_401_0067
No interlinear, marginal or separate decipherment was found on 66R, 67R or 68L.
[ ] = clear text; ( ) = open or uncertain group, see the table.

Key decode, as check_67R.py prints it:

L01: soo seer veel klaghte n te hem jnkomen als of r anders niet dae
L02: de als geduyrigh edespences en argh noch soigneuslyke
L03: borse te maken tot total e ruïne van de Spaansche Nederlanden

With the clear text around it and editorial spacing:

[Daer is geen apparentie, dat de Hr. Gastañaga geremoveert worden sal, eer syn tyt uyt is, soo hy geen considerabele
faute begaet, offe een ander groot accident daer tusschen komt, niet tegenstaende]
soo seer veel(?) klaghten te hem inkomen(?), als of 'r anders niet daede, als geduyrighe despences en (885) noch
soigneuslyke borse te maken tot total(?)e ruïne van de Spaansche Nederlanden;
[men kan hem sonder waer anders te placeren niet wel wechjagen]

English gist: "There is no likelihood that Gastañaga will be removed before his time is up, unless he commits a
considerable fault or some other great accident intervenes, notwithstanding that so very many complaints come in
against him, as if he did nothing else than constantly run up expenses and (885) also carefully fill his purse, to the
total ruin of the Spanish Netherlands; one cannot well chase him away without placing him somewhere else."

Open:
- 885 carries a single dot, which is none of the fifteen marks. 1:885 = argh, 10:885 = regulier (key s12L1, s55L2).
  Neither gives clear sense here; the group is left open.
- 13:171 veel (faint strike on the last digit), 6:490 jnkomen (e-mark; 4:490 = Wismar) and 12:784 total (no strike
  seen beyond the struck-o 8) are graded M.
- "te hem" may stand for "tegen hem".
