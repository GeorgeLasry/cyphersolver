# Clairambault 1225: translation of the excerpt

George Lasry, report PDF `28_clairambault-1225.pdf`, "Excerpt and Translation".

## Translation 1 (f58v)

Qualities one could wish for. She will complete her twenty-second year on 25 October next, having been born in the same month in 1692. She is well formed and blonde, with a round face, fine and fairly regular features, and a fine figure, though of medium size; much intelligence and a very modest manner.
