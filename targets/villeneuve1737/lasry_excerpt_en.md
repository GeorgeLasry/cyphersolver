## Translation

I have shown Kerim Bey here as much kindness as I could; and although I have not been able to have any conference with him, I have reason to be content with the favourable dispositions he has shown towards our nation to the persons who have gone to speak to him on my behalf. You must continue to cultivate his friendship and show him the satisfaction I have at the marks he has given me of his.

… in future a consul at Isfahan. The report you make to us of what you have been able to discover of this prince's dispositions on this subject must be accompanied by an exact and circumstantial account of the state of the affairs of Persia, and even by some reflections on the turn you can foresee they will take, so that the steps that [the] King might take on this occasion should not compromise us.

Before your departure from here I made known to you that it would suit the interest of France to re-establish the trade we formerly had in Persia. This idea, which then came only from me, seems to me to have been well received.
