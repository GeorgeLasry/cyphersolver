# Français 22225: translation of the excerpt

George Lasry, report PDF `05_francais-22225.pdf`, "Excerpt and Translation".

## Translation 1 (f145v — beginning of the excerpt f146r — cipher continuation and cleartext)

**I have shown Kerim Bey as much favour as I could. Although I have been unable to confer with him, I have reason to be pleased with the favourable disposition toward our nation that he showed to the people who spoke to him on my behalf.** You must continue to cultivate his friendship and tell him how pleased I am with the signs he has given me of his own.

## Translation 2 (f145v)

[…] in future? a consul at Isfahan. Your report on what you have been able to discover about this prince’s intentions on that subject must be accompanied by an accurate? and detailed? account of the state of affairs in Persia, and some? reflections? on the course you can? foresee they will take, so that any steps the King might take on this occasion do not compromise us.

## Translation 3 (f143v — five completecipherlines)

**Before you left here I made [known] to you that it would be in France’s interest to re-establish the trade we formerly had in Persia. This idea, which then came only from me, seems to have been welcomed.**
