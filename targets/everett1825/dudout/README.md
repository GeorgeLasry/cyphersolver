# The eight enciphered pages of Everett's "Notes" (catalogue 638), Brice Dudout

Contributed 28 Oct 2026. Write-up: https://bdudout.github.io/europe-chiffree/en/documents.html#everett-pages
History article: https://bdudout.github.io/europe-chiffree/en/madrid-1825.html

The NARA duplicate of the *Notes of a conversation with Mr Zea* (M31 reel 27) carries cipher on eight pages besides
the "six lines" printed by Manning: the end of p. 1, the foot of p. 2, two blocks on p. 3, pp. 4 and 5 entirely in
code, the top of p. 6 (images 0112-0115), and the end of p. 17 and p. 18 (images 0120-0121, also read in this
folder by WAINUT as passages B and C, made the same day and independently).

1,127 groups, 1,111 read (0.986); 0.929 without the readings from sense [x]; 16 groups unresolved. Every page is above 0.96. Daniel Bourdeau’s table is applied as it stands, with an overlay of values proper to Everett, each justified (place in the alphabetical block and sense) and recurring with the same meaning in independent contexts: 727 = rather (×3), 1201 = g (×5), 557 = thro (×2), 948 = Russia (×4)… Limits. The held-out validation is that of the first part (despatch of 8 September 1825: 0.947); no complete period decoding exists for these pages. There is only a partial pencil gloss: “Decyphered” at the foot of p. 4, and on p. 5 five words in another hand (“besides these” over 1327 1392, “des” over 829, “because” over 1315, “the” over 213, “1535?” over 1355). It agrees with our reading.

The jqa table (`../../jqa/code_table.tsv`) is applied as it stands; values proper to Everett are in `overlay.tsv`,
each graded and justified. Per page in `texts/`: transcription, group-by-group decryption, established text with
the plain sense (the text is English, so no translation).

None of these pages was printed in 1826 (American State Papers, Foreign Relations V no. 418, p. 795) nor by
Manning (vol. III doc. 1134), whose extract begins and ends where the cipher does.

Total: 1127 groups, 1111 read.
