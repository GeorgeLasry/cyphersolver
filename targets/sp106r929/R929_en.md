# R929, SP 106/10 f. 247: Paris, 26 January [1646], to "Sir"

## Reading

Paris, January 26. Sir, the [?Queen] has this week received your letters of the 26th of December and of the 4th of
January, and [?gives] you thanks for the continuation of your care in [?giving] her weekly reports of [566] the state of
the King's affairs. However bad it may be, I hope it is not so desperate as not to be able to hold out without further
losses until our efforts here for his [?Majesty]'s relief can be brought to effect. For that, as also concerning the
embassy and the marriage of [?Port]ugal, I refer you to the King's [?letter]s, and remain, Sir, your most [554] [620]
humble servant, [316] [440] [236].
