THE SHEET
- The memorandum is written in paragraphs, each opening with an underlined catchword: "E disse di tutto
  ... dovessi scrivere", "di papa Felice", "Or tochiudendo(?) dicto etc.", "di buona voglia", "Nel
  polizolo dicto segue" (= "in the said slip there follows"), "Anchora disse", "antiveduto questo".
  The underlined words are the clear words of the ciphered text; the words after them are the
  decipherment of the cipher that follows them. Paragraphs 1-4 belong to a letter that is not on this
  sheet; the last three ("Nel polizolo dicto segue ...", "Anchora disse ...", "antiveduto questo ...")
  are the decipherment of the slip below.
- The slip mixes clear cursive and cipher. Its clear words "Anchora disse" (L3) and "e stato antiveduto
  questo" (L5) are the memorandum's last two catchwords.

- L6 "q U": the q and u signs occur only here.
