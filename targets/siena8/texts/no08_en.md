# Siena, Concistoro 2308 fasc. 2 no. 8 (DECODE R4797), c. 1440-41: English translation of the cipher runs

… to [make] chapters and a league between the [Duke of Milan] and the Marquis of Ferrara, to make …

… the [Venetians] and their league …

The [Duke of Milan] had given Madonna Bianca to messer Leonello, the Marquis's son …

… and it cannot fail that in this you will hear of very great deeds.
