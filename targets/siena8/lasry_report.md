# George Lasry, "The cipher documents of Concistoro 2308, fasc. 2" (17 Oct 2026)

Consolidated report sent by email to Daniel Bourdeau, 17 October 2026. The report gives this piece no section of
its own and no appendix part; it lists it on its contents page only. Quoted verbatim:

Contents page (p. 3), heading **H. Pieces with an interlinear or separate plaintext**:

> • No. 8 = R4797 — c. 400 symbols — unsolved as of 17 October 2026 (no interlinear plaintext; a relation to the clear memorandum above the cipher is possible but speculative)

Heading H groups nos. 9, 13/16, 10, 7 and 8 by the kind of plaintext they carry. The contents page also says "Pieces under one heading share a cipher key"; for nos. 7 and 8 that cannot hold: the gloss of no. 7 gives 7 = l, + = o, 2 = e, the decipherment of no. 8 gives 7 = d, + = c, 2 = a (this project, 18 Oct 2026). On 18 October 2026 this project aligned the slip with the memorandum sign by sign: the memorandum's last paragraph is its decipherment (see NOTES.md).
