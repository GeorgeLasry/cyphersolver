# Français 14765 — p351: translation of the excerpt

George Lasry, report PDF `06_francais-14765-p351.pdf`, "Excerpt and Translation".

## Translation 1 (p351)

Be assured that you will have succeeded in your undertakings, and that you will be happy if you make no bad use of it.

## Translation 2 (p351)

Those fortunate enough to discover this secret will be happy, provided they are good Christians and follow what comes next.
