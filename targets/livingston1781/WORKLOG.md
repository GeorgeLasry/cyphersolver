# Worklog: livingston1781 (catalogue 636)

- 2026-10-27 literature: Founders Jay/01-02-02-0272 read. The "passage" is the last line, an addition of groups 600-605 to the cipher, printed as figures; n. 7 calls it undecipherable ("syllables ending in r").
- 2026-10-27 literature: editorial note Jay/01-02-02-0003. Thomson code = Weber WE007, Jay's copy at NNC; lost copy went with Palfrey. Weber 1979 not consulted (not online).
- 2026-10-27 access: Columbia DLC Jay Papers, ldpd:50144 (Jay's copy of the Thomson code), stitched from IIIF level-0 tiles.
- 2026-10-27 reading: ldpd:50144 p. 2 has handwritten additions "be 600, ce 601, de 602, fe 603, ge 604, he 605" (+ le, me, ne, pe, three names). Hypothesis: these are the 28 Nov addition, so 226 = e, not r.
- 2026-10-27 key test (failed): the addition numbers looked up in WE007 give twelve/into/el/en/sha/their + edge. Nonsense, so the line is not in WE007.
- 2026-10-27 key test (failed): ldpd:50124, "key to cipher used in Livingston's letter of this date", is a one-part word code; 69 apparent, 226 destroy. Not this key.
- 2026-10-27 crib: ldpd:32488, a 19th-c. copy of the 20 Oct passage with figures under the words. Lost-code values not 203, will 175, for 235, his 139 etc. These differ from WE007, so the lost code is a renumbered copy of the same form.
- 2026-10-27 literature: Founders Jay/01-02-02-0344 (9 May 1782). Livingston says letter no. 2 (by Lafayette) is in the Palfrey cipher, and that his letters note "a slight alteration in Mr. Thompson's cypher".
- 2026-10-27 access: all six Columbia records of the 28 Nov letter (ldpd:6328, 11572, 50588, 35012, 71956, 87671) stitched; contact sheet made.
- 2026-10-27 reading: draft (ldpd:11572 p. 3) has the addition in column, each line glossed Be, Ce (clear), then four smudged glosses legible as De, Fe, Ge, he. Matches Jay's key sheet.
- 2026-10-27 transcription: ALS (ldpd:6328 p. 7) addition line, 17 cipher tokens; "to your cypher" interlined over 398, 264, 23.473.370. The WE007 copies carry no addition line.
- 2026-10-27 verification: three witnesses agree. The printed Thomson form lacks bare be/ce/de/fe/ge/he but has ar/er/ir/or/ur, which supports e over r. Reading 17/17 tokens.
