# R196, Acciaiuoli to the Secretariat of State, Lisbon, 26 Sept 1758

The public talk about the cause of the King's illness goes on; nobody doubts it any more, and everyone is persuaded of
it on hearing that His Majesty still stays in bed all day. Many indeed see him, but nobody sees the arm, which is
bandaged. Everyone now believes that His Majesty was not coming from the Marchioness of Távora, but from another place,
as I reported. It is said that the arm will remain impaired, but two surgeons see him. This is the common talk.
