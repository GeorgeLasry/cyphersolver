# R198 (117/5), Acciaiuoli to the Secretariat of State, Lisbon, f. 104r, 31 Oct [1758]

In the letters in clear I say what the Ministers and the Gentlemen of the Chamber tell us; but it is held that the
King did not have erysipelas, but an abscess of matter, which made the wound that causes much concern, and the fever
came as a consequence. The King is in great apprehension, and others who see him have been noticed to be in low
spirits. The belief that the illness is worse than is said is, one must confess, universal; but for certain I have
nothing more.
