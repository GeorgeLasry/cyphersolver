# R197 (117/4), Acciaiuoli to the Secretariat of State, Lisbon, f. 103r, 10 Oct [1758]

The King has got up, but never [goes out] all day. Most people hold that the arm is badly damaged, and that at the
least it will remain permanently impaired.
