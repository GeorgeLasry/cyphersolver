# R199 (117/6), Acciaiuoli to the Secretariat of State, Lisbon, f. 106r, 24 Oct [1758]

Although the King is said to be much better, it is held that the arm will certainly remain impaired. A confidant of
his, speaking with the King about the death of the Queen [of Spain], the King said to him: she was a good sister, and
I am sure that the King will marry my daughter Maria Francisca, and that he promised it to the Queen before she died.
What the Spanish Minister shows is the very opposite, so I cannot judge whether it is true or a flattering hope with
which he and his Ministers keep him calm. That the King said this in confidence is quite true; for all the rest I
cannot commit myself.
