## Joined reading (French)
Les conseillers de la [ville de] Barcelonne ayant ⟨p⟩ pris soing de faire preparer un l⟨ϭ⟩ a M. de Marca, ont esté ⟨22⟩ de choisir la maison d'un amy de M. le Mareschal de La Motte, qui a estimé ⟨23̂⟩ par ce moien estre in[formé ?] ponctuellement de tout ce que ledit sieur Marca fe[ra] ou dira, de ce que contiend[ront] les despesches qu'il rece[vra] de la Cour ou qu'il y envoy[e]r[a] ; dont [on] a estimé devoir inform[er] ledit S[ieur] Marca, affin qu'il prenne garde et ne se la[isse] pas persua[d]er par ⟨Ꝺ 22⟩. ## English gist (ours, not a translation of record)
The councillors of Barcelona, in having lodgings prepared for M. de Marca, chose the house of a friend of Marshal de La Motte, who reckoned that in this way he would be informed in detail of everything Marca does or says and of the contents of the despatches he receives from, or sends to, the Court. Marca has therefore been warned to be on his guard and not to let himself be talked round. The man who handles Barcelona's affairs here [at Court] has complained several times that packets addressed to him by the Catalans, and others, are being opened at [?] de La Motte's, so as to discover what is written from here and to take away the freedom to report [?] what happens in Catalonia. The Queen [Regent] earnestly wishes Marca to watch this and report what he finds; above all he must stop it continuing, if indeed it has been happening.
