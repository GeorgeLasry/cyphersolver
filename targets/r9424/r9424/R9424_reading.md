## Notes

- `[...]` before geistlichen: two signs (looped d, tailed ʒ) that the key does not value; `nprse`: n, ρ before "als"
  unexplained. l. 21 `izriafzia/kz` (a name or title, letters f-r-a-u-n-?-d/r-u-nn-e-r) and l. 22 `eo` after "ghen"
  (Cöln expected) are unread. f. 275r `zodDcw3` is glossed "Aichstet" in the other hand but does not decode to it.
- The contemporary glosses on f. 275r agree with the key where they stand: der, thumbrobst, Wirtzburg, Hildesheim,
  "den nonnen haben" (the cipher gives namen).
- "Si." on f. 275r was transcribed `Sl` as a cipher sign; on the image it is the clear sigil "Si." of l. 14.
