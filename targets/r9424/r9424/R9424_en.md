# R9424, a letter to Duke Wilhelm V of Bavaria, 12 June 1583 (English)

f. 274r, ll. 8-30

... that I find both the spiritual affairs and the conduct of the war in great confusion. Between Ho., Fe. and Ni.
there is much dissembling and delay; none will give way to another, each wants to rule the field for himself. All
three of them understand the business too little. (At court Si. alone governs, to the great indignation of the people
of both Cologne and Liège.) As yet there are very few men of Cologne at court, but some notables who have been summoned
are expected daily. In short, I find the greatest want of good, loyal people. There is a suspicion that they mean to
let Susanna come down here; [...] of Freising is to escort her down with his [?suffragan], but I do not know whether
she is to be taken to [...] or to Liège. [#] should consider how this might properly be prevented, for it would give
great scandal in these parts, and many well-meaning people, as well as those of Ca., would be estranged. If it were
possible to keep [your?] [#] from these and similar things, into which he has been led by bad people, with tact and
gentleness, then one could wish this see no other lord.

f. 275r (cipher words inside clear lines)

... with the cathedral provostship of Würzburg [...] ... to resign it to his son Philipp ... then Si. at Freising would
take most of the revenues from it, and Si. would only have the name ... Hildesheim.
