- Textual records, loose sheets, paper, container 100; National Archives at College Park, Textual Reference
  (Archives2reference@nara.gov). Access and use unrestricted. Extent and scope note not given.

  RG 107 War Department telegrams, the Eckert ledgers at the Huntington, the Confederate Vigenère messages and keys,

  unknown.

  photograph units, 41248 "Equipment (Civil War)" (naId 23891132) and 41241 "Event - WW I and Civil War" (naId
