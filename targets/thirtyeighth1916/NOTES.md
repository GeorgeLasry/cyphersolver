## The documents

Copies made by the Communications Bureau of the Ministry of Communications for the Foreign Ministry (Gaimushō
1.6.1.4-2-1-2, 特定人ノ発受郵便物取調), JACAR. The copies carry no decipherment.

- 0042b | 11 May 1916 | Tokyo → Shanghai (Junzaburo) | JACAR B03050089000, PDF page 37
- 0045a | 12 May 1916 | Tokyo → Shanghai (Junzaburo) | JACAR B03050089000, PDF page 40 | sent 5.20 pm

## System

Tokyo → “Junzaburo”, 11–12 May 1916; clear indicator “Thirtyeight”. Table recovered in October 2026.

Key file: `key.tsv` (observed cells (50)). Each pair of syllables (four letters) gives one
four-digit standard telegraph code (電報新編), with no additive.

## Reading

40 of 40 codes give a character. `transcription.txt` (letters by code), `decryption.txt` (code by code,
with status and emendations), `reading.md` (Chinese text, doubtful codes in {braces}), `translation.md` (English).
Doubtful letters were re-read on the scans by Brice Dudout in blind panels before publication.
