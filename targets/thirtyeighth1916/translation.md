# “Thirtyeight” table: English translation

Working translations from the Wenmi site; […] = passage not translated for want of a sure reading.

## 0042b | 11 May 1916 | Tokyo → Shanghai (Junzaburo) | JACAR B03050089000, PDF page 37

I hear that Zhang Ji (張繼) has returned to Japan; that is very surprising — is it true?

## 0045a | 12 May 1916 | Tokyo → Shanghai (Junzaburo) | JACAR B03050089000, PDF page 40 | sent 5.20 pm

The trunk has been handed to Inuzuka (犬塚). On the 15th [I] come by a French ship. For any urgent matter, telegraph as follows: “Mrs …, steamer Porthos”. Please send two people to meet [me]: there is a lot of luggage.
