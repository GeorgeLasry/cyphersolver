# Körler's Vienna reports 1674-75 and the 1635 Lower Rhine letter, Fürstbistum Münster (Unsolved Catalogue no. 609)

Status: no write-up. Blocked on access: B 001 Nr. 3009 and Nr. 673 are not digitised.

Nothing below rests on a sight of the ciphertext. The two digitised Münster key volumes named in the catalogue
entry were downloaded and inventoried; neither carries a key dated to either target.

## The documents

- **A. LAV NRW W, B 001 (Fürstbistum Münster, Landesarchiv - Akten) Nr. 3009** (old sign. 338 - 33), 1675.
  "Berichte des Jesuitenpaters Theodor Körler aus Wien über die Recherchen in der kaiserlichen Registratur
  bezüglich der Belehnung des Johann von Büren mit Haus Davensberg (Text zum Teil chiffriert)". Filed under
  14.6.14 Haus Davensberg. A fief-records search in the imperial registry, so the ciphered parts are probably
  names and sums, not war news. DDB id BX66KFGZFY5RNRPORTK6NPRW3GD4QHKY.
- **B. B 001 Nr. 673** (206 - 10), 1635. "Streitigkeiten zwischen den Eingesessenen der Kirchspiele Rhede und
  Brünen wegen des Torfstichs im Venn"; "Enthält auch: Zum Teil chiffrierter Brief mit Angaben über den Kauf von
  Munition, das Steueraufkommen aus Dingden und Rhede und das Kriegsgeschehen am Niederrhein." One letter, writer
  and addressee not given, under Bishop Ferdinand of Bavaria, not Galen. DDB id FZIOD6ZQENJ2ILFZ4WJ3EDCCC7ZWOOS7.
- The two items share nothing but the fonds; they were grouped by the Archivportal-D sweep. Neither key below
  applies to B (1635).

## Siblings found (DDB search "Körler", 28 Oct 2026)

- **B 001 Nr. 7801** (12 - 12 Band 3), 1674, 31 Bl.: "Berichte des Jesuitenpaters Theodorus Körler (alias
  Dorotheus Diethrichs) aus Wien an Fürstbischof Christoph Bernhard von Galen über die Verhandlungen ... wegen eines
  Bündnisses mit dem Kaiser"; also his reports to the secretary Heinrich Bruchhausen. The main body of Körler's
  Vienna correspondence, under a cover name, and the likeliest place for more cipher and any decipherment. Not
  digitised. Should be ordered with A.
- B 001 Nr. 5372 (1674): Körler's account "zurzeit im Auftrag des Landesherrn in Wien". Not digitised; no cipher
  mentioned.

## Key material inventoried

**W 005 (Manuskripte VII) Nr. 499 a**, "Notiz- und Formelbuch eines Münsterischen Kabinett-Sekretärs", 1659,
41 images. Several keys, none dated after 1659 on the pages seen:

- f. 1 (img 002): letters A-Z to two-digit numbers (A 30, B 32 ... Z 45) and a syllabary of CV pairs to numbers.
- f. 4 (img 005): a numbered code list ("Mons. V."): 3 Pabst, 4 Röm. Kayser, 5 Spanien, 6 Frankreich, 7 Pohlen,
  8 Engelland, 9 Schweden, 10 Dennemarck, 11 Türckischer Kayser, 12 Respublica Veneta, 13 Erzherzog Leopold ...;
  up to 670 admiralitet, 671 mare balticum, 672 ministri. Northern War vocabulary, ca. 1658-59.
- f. 5-6 (img 007): two letter-symbol alphabets, one headed "Ziffer ... Thumbeshausen"(?), one struck through and
  rewritten.
- f. 16 (img 017): letters to numbers (A 3, B 7, C 9, D 40, E 4 ...) and a symbol table: "Ziffern Ihrer hochfl.
  Gn. zu Münster und Ihrer Excellenz Herrn Volmar[?] ... Montecuccoli". A key between Galen and imperial ministers,
  so a Vienna-side key, but of 1658-59 (Volmar died 1662).
- f. 24, 31 (img 025, 032): a homophonic "Clavis positiva" / "Clavis resolutiva" with numbers 1-134, and a code
  list ending ~686-687 (Zusammenkunft, Zerrüttung).
- Not yet read page by page: imgs 009-016, 018-024, 026-031, 033-041 (more lists, accounts, Soldlisten, drafts).

**W 005 Nr. 475**, titulary ca. 1668-78, 1689, 67 images. Key on imgs 058-060: "Chiffre mit Herrn Canzlern ...",
vowels A 10-14, E 21-25 ... with a code list (157 Herzog von Wolfenbüttel, 169 Venedig, 106 Königin in Schweden,
173 Dalberg[?], 178 Herzog Rudolf[?], 189 Engelland ...), dated "24 Feb. 1689". Fifteen years after A; not
expected to fit, but should be tried if A's numbers look like it.

## Prior art (checked 28 Oct 2026)

- Repo: no folder; no mention of 3009, 673, 7801 or Körler outside the catalogue row.
- **Erich Hüttenheim, *Die Geheimschriften des Fürstbistums Münster unter Christoph Bernhard von Galen
  1650-1678*, Münster: Aschendorff 1974** (Schriften der Historischen Kommission Westfalens 9). A monograph on
  exactly this chancery's ciphers. It very likely reconstructs or prints the key Körler used in 1674-75 and may
  cite B 001 Nr. 3009/7801 directly. Not online; must be seen before any attack. It does not cover B (1635).
- Web search (Körler + Galen + Chiffre; Volmar/Montecuccoli key): nothing beyond Hüttenheim.
