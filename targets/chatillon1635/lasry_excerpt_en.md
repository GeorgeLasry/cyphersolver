# BnF Français 3758, Châtillon to Servien, 3 August 1635: English translation of Lasry's excerpt

Translation (Claude Opus 5.5, 23 Oct 2026) of `lasry_excerpt_reading.md`, **an excerpt only** of the letter, the passages George Lasry printed in his BnF cipher research report (f. 127v, cipher continuation); the rest of the letter is not in the repository. Same holes: [?] unread, [x] a word he supplied. His own translation is in `lasry_entry.md`.

## Translation

This consideration may prevent them from committing themselves so far; but I answer for it to you that we are playing no trick this time, but are crossing the Rhine for our greater safety, so as to have this great river between the enemy and us.
