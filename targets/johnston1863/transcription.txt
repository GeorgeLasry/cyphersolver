# Johnston to Seddon, Jackson, 16 June 1863: the telegram as written (NARA naId 3854726, image 1)
# https://catalog.archives.gov/id/3854726  (images 1-3; image 3 is the "Translation of Cypher")
# Clear text as written, line by line. The five cipher words are in capitals, as on the leaf.
# [?] = uncertain reading. The cipher letters were read on the native page image of the NARA PDF (2510 x 2400 px).

By Telegraph                                        (130)
                       Jackson 16th June 1863
Hon J A Seddon,
          Genl Bragg[s?] informs me
that a Telegram from Louisville of
the 10th says that Part of the 9th
23d Corps' have been sent to reinforce
Grant. will not this enable us to
UNIEK - CXNKVNED. for this
Genl Bragg & should JJTRRK -
Over ISLX - KFYHJEER -
                       J E Johnston
                            Genl
53/514

# Stamp "WAR RECORDS COPIED" on the leaf. Docket (image 2): "1705. Genl Johnston, Jackson Miss. June 16. 1863."
# The "&" after "Bragg" is written as a cross-like sign; the 1864 print has "General Bragg's command should".

# Cipher words only (31 letters):
UNIEK CXNKVNED JJTRRK ISLX KFYHJEER

# Sign notes
# ISLX     third letter is a script capital L (bottom loop, top stroke to the right), not D: the catalogue's "ISDX" is a misreading.
# KFYHJEER second letter is a crossed "7" form, a script F; a script T is possible but less likely (T fails under the key).
# UNIEK    fourth letter is E-like with a top hook; a hasty C cannot be excluded [?] (C would match the key).
