# Johnston to Seddon, Jackson, 16 June 1863: the telegram as sentences

Words that were in cipher are in capitals. The telegram is in English, so no translation is given. Notes on "9th, 23d"
and on the sign after "Bragg" are in NOTES.md.

## Reading

General Bragg informs me that a telegram from Louisville of the 10th says that part of the 9th, 23d Corps have been
sent to reinforce Grant. Will not this enable us to INVADE KENTUCKY? For this General Bragg & should EXTEND over EAST
TENNESSEE.

J. E. Johnston, General.
