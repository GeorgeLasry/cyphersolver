# Jafet to Climent, 15 Sep 1400: English of the printed fragment

English of the one deciphered sentence held in texts/jafet1400_decryption.md (Puig y Puig 1920, p. 466 n. 2); the rest of Puig's printed passage is not in the repository.

Some of [Climent's] letters "were intercepted today and recovered" (sunt intercepte hodie et recuperate).
