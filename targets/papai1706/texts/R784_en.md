# R784, unsigned report to Ferenc Rákóczi II, [1707]: English

Translation of `R784_reading.md` (23 Oct 2026), same holes.

## Translation

The ambassador of France reported [?…] that with no arguments can he bring the Sublime Porte to war; he has approached
the men by another way, which was of much use in the last war too, [?…] there are already five hundred; [?all] the
grandees, who have sworn together that they will by all means bring it about that the Turkish Emperor must resolve on
war, since the whole Turkish nation is inclined to it. If they can reach five or six thousand, the matter will soon
come out. As it has not yet come to its turn, I asked the ambassador whether anything could be hoped from it. He said:
I certainly do not spend in vain, and it costs me much money. This, however, he reported secretly at Arad: as I see,
gracious lord, France still has in mind to turn this Turkey wholly against the German, from which not profit but harm
would follow for the Hungarian nation. The ambassador has also written to the Tatar Khan and sent him the arguments
with which he approaches the Sublime Porte, since he wrote to [?…]: one must absolutely make war in some direction.
These matters being worth considering, I wished to let Your Highness know in haste, knowing that if now [?…] Muscovy
and Poland do not second it, the German [?…] will overrun Hungary entirely; then perhaps he will not even wish to treat
with Your Highness.
