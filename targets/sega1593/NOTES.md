# Cardinal Sega's legation, Paris 1593: BnF fr. 3984 nos. 6 and 8 (catalogue no. 20)

Status: in progress (waits on a comparison of Brice Dudout's readings with Tomokiyo, Cryptologia 43(1) 2019)

Split 20 Oct 2026 from the old `targets/sega1593/`, whose work (17 Sept 2026) was on the Baudouin-Desportes letters
ff. 186 and 189. That folder is now `targets/desportes1593/`; those letters were deciphered by Paolo Rosson

## The items (BnF fr. 3984, Gallica btv1b9060633d)

- **no. 8, f. 22r-v, Paris, 15 May 1593.** A homophonic substitution with a small nomenclator: Z separates words,
  and D, N and X are nulls. It reports on the Suresnes conference.
- **no. 6, ff. 12-17, the Curia's instruction to Sega (duplicate of 14 May 1593), c. 14,500 figures.** A different
  cipher: the figures 3 and 8 are nulls, letters are pairs of figures, and nomenclator numbers are framed by nulls
  (426 Navarra, 017 Infanta). It argues against a new excommunication and against the Infanta's claim under the Salic
  law, and it fears a "finta conversione".

## Outside work

- **Brice Dudout** (contributors.json `dudout`): candidate readings, still partial, at
  https://bdudout.github.io/europe-chiffree/en/sega-1593.html (CC BY 4.0). His second review of 20 Oct puts the
  reading breaks under 0.5 % of figures.
- **Prior work:** Tomokiyo, "Identifying Italian ciphers from continuous-figure ciphertexts (1593)", *Cryptologia*
  43(1), 2019, 23-46, doi:10.1080/01611194.2018.1503207. It gives the segmentation and a preliminary decipherment of
  three 1593 continuous-figure texts, probably including no. 6. Neither Brice nor this project has read it yet.
