# “Hong Kong” substitution (1914): English translation

Working translations from the Wenmi site; […] = passage not translated for want of a sure reading.

## 0064 | 11 March 1914 | Hongkong → Toyama, 26 Reinanzaka, Tokyo | JACAR B03050087400, PDF page 5

A few thousand are still lacking; then we can act. Please remit quickly to […] Connaught Road, [Kwong] Hing Cheong, for Song. Address partly uncertain.

## 0067b | 13 March 1914 | Tokyo → Kwongkingchong, Enock Road, Hongkong ; signé 頭山満 | JACAR B03050087400, PDF page 8

I have telegraphed Lu Wenhui about the loan. Send someone to my elder brother’s house to ask Lu. I shall do my utmost to raise pay and ammunition. Wen replies.
