# A115, 16 June 1942: Bletchley Park's decrypt: English translation

Translation (Claude Opus 5.5, 23 Oct 2026) of the German text of Bletchley Park's decrypt quoted in
`texts/A115_decryption.md` (entry 21 of the day's German police decrypts). No holes. "Rf SS" is Reichsführer-SS
(Himmler); "Chef Orpo" the Chief of the Order Police.

## Translation

To the Reichsführer-SS and the Chief of the Order Police. On the Bobruisk-Mogilev road, partisan fighting: 16 men of
Police Battalion 51 fell. The village of Borki, in which arms and ammunition were found, was razed to the ground. The
inhabitants liquidated. From the Higher SS and Police Leader Russia Centre.
