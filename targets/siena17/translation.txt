# No. 17 (R4805): George Lasry's translation, verbatim from his report of 17 Oct 2026 (pp. 25-26).
# Line breaks follow the PDF text layer, not the manuscript.

1528. Most magnificent and generous Knight etc. Since Your Lordship is absent from the magistracy of the
Balìa, we communicate to you some important matters of the government, so that you may understand the
things that will occur and have the inside knowledge. First: here, among many and almost publicly, [it is said]
that the Mustetto(?) has(?) certain promises of I know not what bishoprics for the nephews; I do not know
whether it is true. Second: the Cardinal of Naples has(?) given and gives [his hand] in the affairs of
Pitigliano, because he has kinship there; and it has also been a matter of the Countess, when they were
young: it seems that he made love with her. Third: the Otto(?), wishing to have it, [in order to] constrain(?)
[it] ..., let no victuals go there, nor let [anyone] be allowed to deal with our contado.
I speak briefly and in substance because the cipher wants it so. Messer Bartolomeo Lamilucci(?) [Landucci?],
who was secretary of the Pratica, [is there] and will be able to read it easily. From Sarteano(?), on the 25th(?)
of August 1528. Most devoted Bartolomeo Tantucci. [Endorsement:] This cipher is that of the Balìa.
Address
To the magnificent and generous knight messer Antonio del Vescovo, most worthy rector of the cathedral
church. Cipher.
