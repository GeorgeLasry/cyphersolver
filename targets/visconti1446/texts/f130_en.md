# BnF italien 1583 f. 130 -- Filippo Maria Visconti to Francesco Sforza, Milan 25 Nov 1446 (cipher slip): English

The Duke writes that he has dispatched the enclosed brief addressed to the Magnificent Messer Federico (probably
Federico da Montefeltro, Count of Urbino) and has received Sforza's brief of 14 November, brought by Perino da
Cotignola, which pleased him greatly. He warns that his affairs are at a point that calls for provision and action,
and presses Sforza to act. To Sforza's report of Cosimo de' Medici's good disposition he answers that he is sure
Cosimo has always been well disposed; men of this world look only to the present and not to the future. He begs
Sforza to come very quickly ("prestisimo venire"): Sforza may dispose of the men-at-arms and of the Duke's own
person as he likes, and when Cosimo sees this he will be better disposed still. He sets out the choice between
staying "over there" (de là) and coming "over here" (dezà) and asks to be told at once which Sforza decides, "o de
atendere de là, de venire dezà", so that the Duke can make his own decisions. The letter is the Duke's call to
Sforza to leave the Marche and come over to Lombardy, four days before f. 136 (29 Nov), which reports Zohane del
Maino sent with full powers.
