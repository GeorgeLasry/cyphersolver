# BnF italien 1583 ff. 110, 154, 158 and 112: key identification only (worker D, 29 Oct 2026)

Status: in progress. Not yet transcribed beyond what is given here. All three cipher slips are in key **R5323**.
No clear text of any of them is known; any later reading will be an independent decipherment.

## f. 110, Milan 1 Nov 1446 (DECODE R7902)

- P1 is the recto (folio number 110, pencil "1446 1° Nov"): about 40 cipher lines, full width. **P2 is the verso
  continued in cipher**, not a docket: about 14 dark cipher lines at the top; the faint rest of P2 is show-through of
  the recto (mirror image). So the letter runs to about 54 lines, the longest of the set.
- Key R5323. The first line is the salutation, in Latin and in cipher:
  `ħ̄ ffp ɱo gtt L ꝛo | ‖ bı ƀ̿ | b | ꞡ ∂e b | υ par 9 b̄ | ħ ƀ̄ 2p ∂+ ÷`
  = "Il[l]ustris gener et fili noster carissime" (ꝛo = is here; b = et; υ par 9 b̄ = n-os-t-er; ħ ƀ̄ 2p ∂+ ÷ =
  c-ar-is-im-e). The clear letter f. 112 (below) opens with the same words.

## f. 154, Milan 12 Dec 1446 (DECODE R7914)

- **P2 is the recto** (folio number 154 at top right; about 40 lines); **P1 is the verso** (stamp; 14 cipher lines at
  the top, show-through below). The letter starts on P2 and ends on P1.
- Key R5323. P1 line 14: `oto | ꝯ ħ- -o | c bo ⊢ p+ ʒ̶ b | ꝯ ħ ⊢ | ffp | +o 8 ...`
  = "{null} Data Mediolani die {null} du[odecimo] ..." (ꝯ = the looped d; ⊢ = i in Mediolani). The next line runs
  "-ia Anglus [Dux Mediolani] et cetera Papie Ang[lerieque] ...", with the name sign looped δ∞ for Dux Mediolani.
- P2 line 1 starts `[..] 8 ꝗ bı gtt ≐ ·‖· ...` (first signs damaged at the mount edge).

## f. 158, Cusago 31 Dec 1446 (DECODE R7915)

- One image, 13 cipher lines; a pencil "4758" (?) at the end of L1 is a modern mark.
- Key R5323. L1 opens with the same salutation as f. 110 (`... gtt L ꝗ ‖ bı b 5 ≙ ∂e b ꝯ par 9 b ħ ħ̄ 2p ∂+ ÷` =
  "[Illus]tris gener et fili noster carissime"). L2 has `-o N` = "a [Roma]" (N = Roma, the copy's name sign).
- L12 has the date: `... ꝯ ħ- -o [..] +o ⊢ ‖ x∞ 9 ∂+ x +o b3 ƀ° ꝑ+ L ...` = "Data [Cusagi?] die ultimo Decembris"
  (x∞ = ul, 9 = t, ∂+ = im: "ultimo"; +o b3 ƀ° = d-ec-em). L13 is the intitulatio ("[Fi]lipus Maria Anglus Dux
  Mediolani et cetera Papie Anglerieque Comes ac Ianue Dominus"), Dux spelled `ꝯ ϕ` (looped d + ux).

## f. 112, Milan 7 Nov 1446 (DECODE R7903): not a cipher

- A clear letter in Italian, one paragraph of about ten lines in a chancery hand on a slip; archivist's "(42.)
  1446. 7 Nov." Opening: "Illustris gener et fili carissime. In questa hora siamo avisati per lettere ... del passato
  da Capua come la mayesta del Re de Ragona era partita ... per venire in qua ..." (King Alfonso of Aragon leaving
  Capua to come north). End: "Datum ... die vij novembris 1446, hora 22. Dupplicata a di ... de novembre."
  Mazzatinti is right not to mark it as cipher. Not deciphered here (nothing to decipher); the clear text is not
  transcribed in full.
