# Key additions and corrections from the slips (worker A: ff. 8, 5, 15, 23 of BnF ital. 1584)

Against the key copies as described in `../key.md`. Evidence is given as folio/line. ff. 5, 8, 23 (and 24) are in key
**R5323**; **f. 15 is in the Duke's other key, R5324** (cart. 1597 no. 24), not R5323.

## A. R5323 (ff. 5, 8, 23), calibrated on the translations f. 6 and f. 9

1. **ʂ (S with a descending hook) = the conjunction e/et.** About twenty times on ff. 5 and 8, always where the
   translation has "e"/"ꝛ". Not in the key copy. S without the hook is f (f. 8 L4 "in-f-or-m-a", f. 5 L6 "f-ar-e"),
   but on f. 5 a plain-looking S also stands for "e" (L1, L2, L5): the hook is not always drawn.
2. **‖3 / ‖1 = "vui/vuy" (you).** Code group, 7 times on f. 8, 3 on f. 5, 2 on f. 23, always "vuy" in the translations
   (f. 9 l. 8 "ad nuy" is the translator's slip). Not among the copy's names or word signs; possibly one of the copy's
   unlabelled name signs, or dropped by the copyist.
3. **Name signs identified** (looped δ + suffix): **ꝺ## (looped δ with tt) = Marchese di Mantova** (f. 5 L6, translation
   "el Marchexe de Mantoa"; the copy has looped δtt with a blank label); **ꝺ7 = Veneziani** (f. 5 L6, L10, as the copy);
   **ꝺo / ꝺa (looped δ∞) = Duca di Milano**, used as "Dux Mediolani" in the ciphered title (f. 8 L16, f. 5 L14).
4. **The slips end with the Duke's title in cipher**, after the date in words: "Filipus Maria Anglus DUX MEDIOLANI
   etcetera, Papie Anglerieque comes ac Ianue dominus" (f. 8 L15-16, f. 5 L14-15; f. 15 the same in R5324). It uses
   ꝶ = ip (Fil-ip-us; not in the copy's table), ∂e = il, ɱ̊o = us, 5̿/5̄ = ar (M-ar-ia), ‖ = g (An-g-lus). After the title
   come nulls and a short group still unread (f. 8: ÷ ♀ b ħ ŧ̶ q; f. 5: ÷ σ- b 5̄ ·‖· ϵ β ꭓ 8 ƀ).
5. **"etcetera" is spelled with syllable signs**: S/ʂ ħ ƀ ƀ σ- = et-c-et-er-a (f. 5 L9, L15; f. 8 L16; f. 23 L7); f. 5 L6
   has the order ƀ̄ ħ ʂ b σ-. So **b̄/ƀ = et** as well as er (f. 5 L1 "l-et-er-e").
6. **δ̄ (barred δ) = ic** (f. 8 L9 "a(m)ico", "inimico", L10, L13 "amico"). The copy gives ic = b3 and δ only under n; the
   slips also use plain ∂ = ic (f. 5 L2, L3 "d-ic-to", L9 "d-ic-emo"; f. 23 L2, L7).
7. **ẋ ("n/x with cross above") = ut** (f. 5 L7 "sap-ut-o", "pos-ut-o", "t-ut-o"; f. 8 L6 "t-ut-o"; f. 23 L3, L6
   "hav-ut-o", L8 "ven-ut-o"). This is the copy's ut sign.
8. **x- = uc** (f. 5 L11 "ind-uc-to"), as the copy.
9. **ꝓ / ꝑ (p with loop) = o** (f. 8 L1 "confortiamo", L8 "uno"); ꝑ with the bar through the stem = od (f. 8 L2 "m-od-o");
   ꝑ = ot (f. 5 L13 "p-ot-eremo") as the copy; po = of (f. 23 L7 "of-esa") as the copy.
10. **5̄ (5 with one top bar) serves ar, i, as and l**: ar (pare, mandare, apparira, faremo, sara, fara), i (fidati, habia,
    intentione, intendiati, habiamo, Abiate), as (f. 5 L7 "p-as-ato", L14 "quarant-as-ette"; f. 23 L1 "p-as-ato"), l
    (f. 5 L6 "pig-l-a", L14 "mi-l-e"). The copy lists barred 5 under i and ar, and as = plain 5.
11. **5̿ (5 with double top bar) = l**, also **er** (f. 8 L11 "s-er-emo", L13 "s-er-a") and **ar** (f. 8 L16, f. 5 L8
    "f-ar-emo", L14 "ianu-ar-ii"). The copy has l = barred 5 with flourish; the er/ar uses are not in the copy. At this
    resolution 5̄ and 5̿ cannot always be told apart: treat the barred-5 family as one homograph group.
12. **♀ (Venus) is a and b**: a in forma, informa, apparira, alcuna, za; b in "ben" (f. 8 L11, L14) and "breve" (f. 23 L1).
    ♀ with a tail (♀-) = n (f. 8 L4 "nui", L8 "mandarne", L9; f. 5 L7 "nui"). **♁ = b** (f. 5 L3 "ben"), as the copy.
13. **-q (the copy's am) also seems to stand for "ad"** in "adeso" (f. 5 L8, L11) [?]; otherwise -q = am (habiamo,
    faciamo, siamo, solamente) as the copy.
14. **b plain = i; b- (b with tail) = at; b+ = eg; b° = em; bı = en; bo = ed; bʒ = et/ec/ev** (f. 8 L13 "perf-et-to"; f. 5
    L9 "s-ec-uro", L5 "pers-ev-erare"; f. 23 L1 "r-ec-ev-emo", "br-ev-e"). In "deli" the slips write +o b 5̄ (f. 8 L2),
    ꝯ b 5̄ (f. 5 L4) and +o ƀ b (f. 5 L6), so b/ƀ = el there.
15. **8 = u/v; 8̶ = al; ƀ = v, u, er, el, es, et** by context; ƀ̿ (double bar) = v (f. 8 L5 "via", L14) and eb (f. 5 L6
    "d-eb-iamo", L12 "d-eb-ito"), the copy's eb.
16. **ro = ir** (f. 8 L4 "apar-ir-a"; L2 "ten-ir-e"; f. 5 L5 "aven-ir-e"; f. 23 L4 "c-ir-ca"). Copy: ir = "w (ꝛo)".
17. **5° = az** (f. 8 L7 "f-az-a", L10 "m-az-ore"), as the copy.
18. **4 = z** (f. 8 L12 "de za"; f. 23 L1 "Valen-z-a"), as the copy.
19. **‖ without dots = e** (f. 8 L6 "sap-er-e"; f. 5 L10 "lui e"); with dots it is the copy's ·‖·. ‖ alone is also g
    (f. 5 L8 "g-li", title "An-g-lus"), and ## (tt) = g (f. 5 L3 "rin-g-ratiamo", L7 "g-li"), as the copy.
20. **q⁝ = quanto** (f. 5 L2, L3, L4), as the copy; g' with a long flourish = qua (f. 8 L15, f. 5 L14 "qua-trocento").
21. **Insertions with a caret** by the cipher clerk: f. 8 L5 "ꝗo" (per) above the line; f. 5 L1 "+o" above "havemo".
22. **Nulls used**: +, oto, π, ϕ, ffp. (all in the copy's list).
23. **Plaintext habits** (useful for the untranslated slips): Lombard 2nd plural -ati/-iti (voglati, posiati, sapiati,
    intendiati, habiati, haviti, diciti, deciti, faciti), single consonants (steso, aparira, afare, fuse, quelo, pasato,
    posuto, adeso, ofesa), mazore, noma "only", Pedro for Piero, "et cetera" written out, the date in words, the title
    at the end. The translators normalise these (-ate, tenere, magiore, fa for fara, che for chi).

## B. R5324 (f. 15), calibrated on the clear text f. 16

24. The R5324 copy's right-hand word block is written cipher above plain: **cim = per** (f. 15 L1, L5, L9, L10; also
    inside a word, "ado-cim-are" = adoperare, L9), **cet = come** (L6, L11), **cib = ciò**, **bb = ss** (L5 "precessori",
    "cossi", L9 "appresso"), **pp = mm** (L11 "co-mm-issone"), **fo = di** (L13 "di-e"). The left block is plain above
    cipher: **cul = de, com = in** (also inside words: "r-in-gratiando", "in-tendera", "in-tegramente"), **∵ = con** (also
    inside words: "se-con-do", "con-seglii"), **caz = che, cam = da, ɔ- = qua, -c = que, ggf = ff** (L4 "a-ff-ectione").
25. **Code words on f. 15 not in the R5324 copy**: **cab = quello** (L1, L10, L11; f. 16 "quello"), **ba = Conte** (L10,
    L11; f. 16 "del Conte", "con esso Conte"), **cel = se** (L6 "come se disponemo", L11 "se intendera").
26. Copy names confirmed: cima = lettere, fun = date (also "Dat." L13), cos = havemo, fen = cosa, ceb = questo,
    alma = Papa (copy "alna"), adiutor = Re de Ragona, auene = Ambassadore, atno = Dux Mediolani, ∵ with two dots = vuy.
27. Letter homophones confirmed on the slip: a δ ꞡ c ∴(..); b σ-; c ♀ δ; d ɔ ♀(ꝗ); e a b ħ L; f ʃ S; g ⊢ ⊣; h =;
    i 4 − 2 3; l 5 1 ∞ ħ; m 7 8; n 9 6(δ~); o y r(ɼ) ꝺ ʒ; p x ʒf; r p ꝺ̸ q; s ∴ ≡ ꝑ, and ♀̲ = s in "commi-s-s-ione",
    "di-s-ponemo" [?]; t ß ff; u/v m ♀ gf o+; z ꝺf (L4 "grande-z-elo", L9 "Tre-z-o"). **ꝺ is used for a, c and o**,
    and ꝺ̸ (δ with a stroke) for r: one shape family, four values.
28. **gf (written ʒꝑ / ꞡꝑ) = u** (L1 "a undece", L3 "vostri", L8 "Pusterla", L10 "tutto").
29. **4 (drawn like ♀̲) = i** at line ends (L1 "scriviti", L3 "recordi", L5 "cossi", L10 "necessario", L12 "diriti"); the
    et sign is q with a crossed tail (q̲). The two are easily confused.
30. Nulls used: + be :n f fos, the blot ■, ":" alone (L8, L11) and probably ".." (L6).
31. The ciphered title at the end (L14-15) in R5324: "Fili[..]pus Maria Anglus, atno (Dux Mediolani), etcetera, Papie
    Anglerieque comes ac Ianue dominus". Between the date and the title four signs among nulls (1, ♀̲, ♀̲, 7) are unread
    (perhaps the year); after the title seven signs are unread.
