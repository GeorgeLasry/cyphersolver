# Key additions and corrections from the slips (worker C: ff. 23-24, 72, 83, 94, 102 of BnF italien 1584)

Against `../key.md` and `key_additions_A.md` (A) / `key_additions_B.md` (B). Evidence as folio/line.

## ff. 23 / 24 (Milan 5 Feb 1447, key R5323): one letter enciphered twice

1. **f. 24 is a second encipherment of f. 23, not a copy of the cipher**: same plaintext to the date, homophones chosen
   afresh almost sign by sign (f. 23 L1 `g L b- ÷` / f. 24 L1 `ʃ L b- ÷` "frate"; "Cremona" `o## L b° pı ♀` / `o## L b° pı
   σ-`). The pair works as a calibration set for the homophone families: wherever one slip has a clear sign the other's
   variant is fixed.
2. **σ- (o with tail) = n as well as a** (key: n has an "o with tail"): f. 23 L1 "queli n-ostri", f. 24 L6/L10 "non n-e",
   f. 23 L12 "n-ostro". Read by sense only.
3. **ħ = l in f. 24** throughout (L8 "in l-o", L12 "a l-i", L14 "l-audiamo", L15 "l-i nostri", L17 "g-l-i"), and = c
   elsewhere ("c-os", "s-c-rivere"). In f. 23 the same: L9 "che-l", L11 "s-c-rivere". Key gives ħ only for c.
4. **## (double-barred tt) = g, f and h**: g in "gli" (f. 24 L17, L18; f. 23 L12 `## ħ ⊢`), f in "forma" (f. 24 L7; key
   f = blot-tt), h in "havere", "habia" (f. 24 L7 `## b= ħ̄ ·‖·`, L19; key h = #).
5. **b- = ef** in "ne f-aciti" (f. 24 L8 `υ ƀ- 5̄ fa`), as the key's ef (b-); elsewhere b- = at (frate, dato, stato).
6. **ꝶ = ip** in "scr-ip-to" (f. 23 L11, f. 24 L15), as A found in "Fil-ip-us".
7. **6 (small) = n / con**: f. 24 L10 "n-e" (6 Ɛ), L16 "Vis-con-te" (ƀ̄ ƀ̄ 6), L17 "con-tenti" (6 gtt bı g); the key's con
   is 6 with a stroke (6̶), used for "con" and "com-preso" (f. 24 L7).
8. **‖ + digit-like stroke = VUY**: f. 24 L8 has ‖5̄ and ‖+ (as A's ‖3 / ‖1). 
9. **Lombard preterite 1st plural in -esemo**: "recevesemo" (f. 23 L1 r-ec-ev-**Ɛ**-em-o; f. 24 L1 ... **ƀ̄** ...),
   "respondesemo" (f. 23 L12-13, f. 24 L17), "sapesemo" (L13 / L18). A spelling to expect on the other slips.
10. **"cride" (proclamations)**: `o## L ꝯo ⊢` / `o## ꝯ ꝺo ÷` (f. 23 L14, f. 24 L19); "crida" `ħ ꝯ ꝯo -o` / `ħ ∂ ꝯo -o`.
11. **Nulls seen**: π, oto, ffp, + (all in the key list), **ɯ** (u-u, key null) f. 24 L10, ϕ f. 24 L21, f. 23 L16;
    ♀ alone as a null [?] (f. 24 L2, L20; the key's null list has ♀). After the date of both slips a run of nulls mixed
    with signs not yet read (f. 23 L15: `ffp ı π oto q oto ‖ g + π >`; f. 24 L20: `π ! ffp 4 + π ♀ 4 3° -o > oto`).
12. **Post-date groups differ**: f. 23 L15 after the nulls reads tentatively `+o ẋ 5̄ ꝯ b- ♀ +o b ·‖·` = "duplicata die"
    [?], then a dated group [..] and the ducal title; f. 24 L21 has `ϕ ɔ bo b p+ ʒ̶ b` = "Mediolani" followed by 28 signs
    that are not the title (unread). If "duplicata" is right, f. 23 is the copy sent later (cf. f. 29 "duplicata die
    vigesimo quarto").
13. **Unresolved recurring group**: "que[?] tendia" (f. 23 L5 `g' 9 bı ꝯ b ♀`, f. 24 L6 `g' 9 bı ꝺ b σ-`) after "non ne
    pare necessario"; g' is the key's qua/que sign.

## ff. 83, 94, 102 (Milan 11, 18, 23 Mar 1447, key R5323), calibrated on the clear texts

14. **ꭓ with a cross = ub** (the key's ub, "crossed xo"): f. 94 L1 "U-b-aldini" `ꭓ 8 +o ∂÷ ħ` = ub-al-d-in-i.
    **q-like 4 = z** and **= p**: f. 94 L1 "z-onto" (`q pı gtt ꭓ`), f. 102 L7 "non p-osa" (`4 par -o`). The key's z is
    "4"; on the slips it is drawn like ♀/q with a cross bar and is easily taken for q (p).
15. **The closing group.** After the ducal title most slips end with a short run of signs that is not text in the key:
    f. 8 `÷ ♀ b ħ ŧ̶ q`; f. 5 `÷ σ- b 5̄ ·‖· ϵ β ꭓ 8 ƀ`; f. 23 `σ- ÷ b β y ♀ ## ⊢ 5̄ 5̄ ‖·‖· -c ϵ`; f. 83 `b 5̄ σ- ÷ β ϵ ꭓ ·‖·
    ⊢ ♀ -o`; f. 94 `σ- ÷ ⊢ ħ o+ ħ ħ ꝑ L q- ♀`. They share σ-/÷/b/β/ϵ/♀ and length 6-12; the 18th-c. copies (ff. 82-85,
    92-93) leave them out. A check-group or the secretary's mark; unread. f. 102 (no title) and f. 24 have a dated group
    there instead.
16. **The pre-title filler** is the same on several slips: f. 23 L15 `ffp ı π oto q oto ‖ g + π >`, f. 83 L36 `π ı ffp +
    q oto π q >`, f. 24 L20 `π ! ffp 4 + π ♀ 4 3° -o > oto`: key nulls (π, oto, ffp, +, 3°) mixed with ı, q, >, 4 that are
    therefore nulls too.
17. **"Filip-L-us"**: the title is spelled `g ∂e ꝶ L ɱ̊o` on ff. 23, 83 and 94 (and f. 8 has `ꝯe ꝶ ɱ̊o` without L).
    L (r in the key) is either a null or a second p ("Filippus"). Left as {null?}.
18. **Year**: f. 94 has the year in words (`b ɔ ꝺe 5̄ ÷ g' ꝑ g L p3 bı gtt y g' ꝑ ꝯ ʒ̶ gtt 5̄ 9 ·‖·` = "mile quatrocento
    quarantasete", with ꝑ after each qua as a null [?]); f. 83 has **no year** (die undecimo martii, then nulls), and its
    18th-c. copy adds "1447" in figures.
19. **Dates in words**: "die decimo octavo marti(i)" f. 94 L19 (`+o bʒ ∂+ ꭓ p3 9 b= β ɔ ħ gtt ⊢`); "vigesimo tertio" f. 102
    L7 (`8 ∂ı ƀ̄ ∂+ ꭓ gtt b o## ⊢ ꭓ`); "undecimo" f. 83 L36 (`ɱ ꝯ bʒ ∂+ y`). **∂ı (∂ with hook) = ig** (vigesimo,
    inteligentia f. 83 L3, Signori f. 83 L20), as the key's ig.
20. **Name signs in use**: looped δo = **Papa** (f. 102 L1 "el novo Papa"), looped δ÷ = **Re de Ragona** (f. 102 L3, L4),
    as the R5323 copy; f. 102 also spells "papa" out (`∵ ft σ-`, L3, L5).
21. **pı = on and = op** (f. 83 L2 "op-timamente", the key's op); **ꝑ= (p/x with =) = ul** in "ultimo" (f. 83 L1, the key's
    ul "x="); **ft = ap** in "a la p-arte" (`8̶ ft 5̿ gtt ·‖·`, f. 83 L2, L20) and "P-ap-ie" (f. 83, f. 94).
22. **5 (unbarred) = et** in running text (f. 83 L2 "etcetera" `5 ħ ƀ ƀ σ-`, L3 "et havemo", "et per"), as worker B found.
23. **## = h and g** again (f. 83 L3 "havemo" `## b= b° β`; f. 83 L36, f. 94 L21 "An-g-lus"/"An-g-lerie").
24. **Words the copyist changed**, useful as evidence that the 18th-c. copies were made from the cipher: f. 94 cipher
    "Ma[ff]eo" (`c ħ̊ ÷ β`) / copy "Mageo"; cipher "zonto **qui**" / copy "zonto"; cipher "mandasiti" / copy "mandasti";
    f. 83 cipher no year / copy "1447".
