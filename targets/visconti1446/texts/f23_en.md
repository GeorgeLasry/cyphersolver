# BnF italien 1584 f. 23: cipher slip, Filippo Maria Visconti to Francesco Sforza, Milan 5 Feb 1447: English

Yesterday Fra Benedetto da Valenza brought your brief (dated the 23rd [?] of last month) answering the briefs we sent by him.
We answer briefly that we are not only sure but most sure of everything you write about your good will and your wish to
please us, in this as in all else; we think you have understood our mind and intention towards you. You mention a report
from Cremona that we had a proclamation (crida) made that no harm be done to Cremona and the Cremonese: this is not true;
we not only did not have it made, it never entered our mind to do it; we know clearly it was done maliciously to put
suspicion into our enemies ... we strive to hold the state with all our power. We much approve [?] the course you have
taken of writing to Cremona that they should attack our men, as you write. It is true that when Pietro Visconti, our
commissary at Pizzighettone [?], wrote to us about what Foschino [Attendolo] had asked of him, we answered that we were
content that he should do it, but secretly, so that it should not appear that we knew anything; but that we had the
proclamations made, or anything else, is not true, as said. Milan, 5 February [1447]. Then (f. 23 only) a dated
'duplicata' note [?] and the Duke's title in cipher; f. 24 ends with a different, unread group after the date.
