# BnF italien 1584 f. 83: cipher slip, Filippo Maria Visconti to Francesco Sforza, Milan 11 Mar 1447 (SAMPLE LINES): English

By Gasparro degli Ubaldini we received your brief of the last of last month. On the Dauphin: we understand each other
perfectly; Tommaso da Bologna, our man, should have concluded and sealed with him on the first of this month; we wrote
that he should conclude if he had not, telling him we will give the Dauphin Asti; we have his promise not to be
attacked by him, and he is bound to help us with a good number of horse and foot (how many and for how long we do not
yet know). We approve your sending to the Cardinal Camerlengo and to the man from Capua; tell us what you want done at
the court of Rome and we will do it as for ourselves. On offering Padua or Verona to the Duke of Ferrara: as written
before, that is your business; offer what you think fit and we will confirm it; Pietro da Pusterla has gone to Ferrara
with that commission and to press the business of the lord of Faenza [later gloss]. On the Malatesta lords: we have
offered Malatesta his present condotta and Sigismondo 1,600 horse, and can do no more except through the Church. We
keep our agreement secret and will not fail over the money. We thank you for Ser Angelo's warning of the Count of
Urbino's planned raid towards Milan; as for watchfulness in Milan and the other cities, we do what we can, but some
here are unhappy with our agreement and we can hardly guard against friends and enemies: advise us what to do, for we
will follow your counsel. Milan, 11 March [1447].
