# Key additions D (worker D: BnF ital. 1583, Nov-Dec 1446, DECODE R7902-R7915)

Independent decipherments: no clear text of any of these slips is available (the translation f. 131 of f. 130 is
not on DECODE). Values are fixed by sense only.

## f. 136 (R7907, Milan 29 Nov 1446): key R5323

- Key: **R5323**. L1 "A di quindece del pasato nui recevesemo" reads with it at once; the date ("Data Mediolani die
  vigesimo nono Novembris") and the full intitulatio come out in it.
- **x (a plain x shape, no loop) = o**: "uno" (m x), "Novembris" (υ x ƀ ...), "larghissimo" (... ∂+ x), "meglio"
  (ɔ b+ ħ̄ x), "seguente ... mandasemo" (b° x). The copy's o = x-loop (ꭓ); on this slip the loop is often dropped.
- **m (no tail) = un** ("uno", "unite", "alcuni"), the copy's un sign; **ɱ (with tail) = s** ("seguente", "sono",
  "pensare", "simile"). Keep them apart.
- **f (long s) = s** ("subito", "siando", "siano", "considerare"); **S = f** ("fino", "facti", "fare").
- **ħ (h-shaped barred 5)** stands for i, ar, as, l and c by the word: i ("seguente" el d-ħ, "piu" q ħ 8), as
  ("pasato", "mandasemo"), ar ("pensare", "considerare", "pare", "fare"), l ("larghissimo", "col", "le"), c ("campo",
  "vincere"). ħ̄ (with a clear top bar) is mostly ar/l. At 1694 px the bar and the hook cannot be told apart reliably.
- **ħ- (h with a tail) = at** ("pasato", "andate", "vogliate", "Data"): the b-tail sign.
- **g÷ = che** ("che erano", "che siando", "che siano"), a g-form of q÷.
- **ɣh (the "zh"/ɣ+h sign) = ur** in "pur" (L3), as B found (Urbino, perturba). ꝗ (q with a top bar) = p before it.
- **ɣ- (ɣ with a bar) = am** ("recordiamo") and **ad** ("ad es[..]"), cf. B ("ɣ with a bar = am").
- **ꝗ (q with a stroke through the descender) = z** ("posanza"), as B f. 64.
- **b- = ef** across a word boundary: "d(e f)are" = ꝯ b- ħ̄ ·‖· (L2), "l'effecte" = ħ b- ▪ b3 gtt ⊢ (L3: blot = f).
- **p̄ (barred p) = od** ("poderesemo"); **ƀ̿ = es** there and in "stesi".
- **ꝯ̄ (looped d with a bar) = ic** ("vindicte", "inimici"); plain ∂ also = ic in "inimici" (L4).
- **∂÷ = ig** in "vigesimo" (L9) and il in "Filipus" (L10): the copy's ∂-hook and ∂e; the hook reads as ÷ here.
- **ẋ (a 9/g with a cross above) = perché** (L6), the copy's form.
- **ϕ = ux** in "Dux" (L10): the Duke's title is spelled out in letters here (+o ϕ c bo ħ p+ 3 ⊢), not written
  with the name sign looped δ∞ as on the 1447 slips.
- Name signs: none used except ÷9 = gente d'arme (L3 "tute le [÷9] vostre"). Zohane del Maino, Cremona, Cremonese
  spelled out.
- Nulls: π, oto, ı, ffp, 3°, +, ‖ and q in the null run after the date (as ff. 30/31), and probably ♀ (L2, L5).
- Padding after "Dominus": q- o+ ħ S ffp 8 ƀ ÷ -o ⊢ ħ b ♀ ꝯ (14 signs).
- Open values on f. 136: gꝓ (g + looped p; come/quando?) 4 times; the noun after "uno vostro" (L ƀ3 ·‖·); the stretch
  ꝗ ħ̄ ⊢ υ ƀ3 before "stesi" (L2); "ħ ħ̄ pr ·‖·" (twice, "-one"); ɣ per ♀ 8 ⊢ (L8).

## ff. 130, 110, 154, 158 (key identification)

- **All five ital. 1583 cipher slips (ff. 110, 130, 136, 154, 158) are in key R5323**, the key of the 1447 slips.
  None uses R5324. Checked on the date and intitulatio (ff. 130, 136, 154, 158) and on the Latin salutation
  "Illustris gener et fili noster carissime" enciphered at the head of ff. 110 and 158.
- **The looped d (the copy's ꝯ, drawn as a big "<" loop with a small circle below) = d**; it opens "Data" (ff. 130,
  154, 158) and "Dux" (ꝯ ϕ, f. 158). It is easy to take for one of the looped δ name signs: those carry a suffix
  (δ∞, δ÷, δo, δ7, δtt). On f. 136 L6 the sign read "ꝺ° = t[?]" should probably be d ("de le stanze" ->
  "[..]d ...") - to recheck.
- **The year is enciphered in words** on f. 130 L18: "mile quatrocento quarantasei" (c ∂e ÷ = m-il-e; 9 = t,
  ꝑ̄ = oc, bı = en in quatrocento). The 1447 slips give only day and month.
- **gto = qui** in "quinto" (f. 130), "quindece" (f. 136), "qui" (f. 136 L1, L3): the copy's qui sign.
- **ꝛo = is** in "Illustris", "Novembris" [?] (the copy has ir; is is otherwise 2p).
- **∂÷ = n** as well as in: "nostre" (f. 130 L3), cf. the copy's δ = n.
- **Salutation in cipher**: ff. 110 and 158 begin with "Illustris gener et fili noster carissime"; ff. 130 and 136
  begin directly with the text. The 1447 slips examined so far have no salutation.
- f. 130 uses Á = Cosimo de' Medici twice (L5, L10), with dots either side.
