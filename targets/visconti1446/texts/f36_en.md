# BnF italien 1584 f. 36 -- Filippo Maria Visconti to Francesco Sforza, Milan 12 Feb 1447 (cipher slip): English

The Duke acknowledges two briefs of 2 Feb. On Asti: he agrees that refusing it to the Dauphin would anger him, so he
has decided to hand it over, but will follow Sforza's advice. He will try to secure Rolando Pallavicino. He explains
that neither Gasparo degli Ubaldini nor Zohane del Maino nor the Count of Urbino (Federico) ever asked, on Sforza's
behalf, for Angelo da Urbino or Cristoforo da la Massa to be sent. The idea came up in his own talk with Gasparo and
Zohane, when he said he wanted one of Sforza's men in Milan to press the money business. Angelo or Cristoforo seemed
suitable because one of Sforza's own men would raise more suspicion, while Federico's men could pretend to be there
on Ottaviano degli Ubaldini's affairs. It is true that it was suggested he write to the Duke of Ferrara to get Cosimo
de' Medici to urge Sforza to come to terms with him. Finally, the rumour in Milan that Sforza has renewed his condotta
with the Venetians badly hampers raising the money, but no time is being lost.
