## English summary (gist)

The Duke acknowledges four [?] briefs of Sforza's, one dated the 18th and the others the 21st of last month, and what
Marcolino Barbavara has written. On the points Sforza asks to be answered: as to his binding himself to the Pope (i.e.
the Church) and to the King of Aragon [?], Sforza should do so in whatever form seems best to him, at his own expense,
or only as far as he is obliged; for his part, provided he does not harm them, Sforza should at once tell Marcolino
which course he takes, so that Marcolino may follow it, and report on the affairs of [Ferrara?] and of another
(unlabelled name sign). The Duke has sent Pietro da Pusterla to Ferrara with other letters and has written to Rome and
to the King [?]. He has heard from Sforza's secretary Angelo Simonetta what Sforza writes and will try to satisfy his
request concerning one Iacomino; he is glad to do so. Milan, 7 March [1447].

Context: Pope Eugene IV had died on 23 Feb 1447 and Nicholas V was elected on 6 Mar; Barbavara was the Duke's envoy and
Pusterla was writing to the Duke from Ferrara on the same day (f. 71). The reading of the δ÷ name sign as Re de Ragona
(as on f. 102) rather than Duca di Ferrara (the copy gives both labels to near-identical shapes) is not settled; in L9
the sign is drawn ꝺ+÷, beside "a Ferrara", and is read Duca di Ferrara [?].
