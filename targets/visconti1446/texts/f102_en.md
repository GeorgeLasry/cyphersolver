# BnF italien 1584 f. 102: cipher slip, Filippo Maria Visconti to Francesco Sforza, Milan 23 Mar 1447: English

Most dear son: since we understand that the new Pope is not inclined to do what he should, we advise and urge you to
come to an understanding with the King of Aragon, to whom we have written the same; and when you see that the Pope will
not do what he should and what the King and you desire, you and His Majesty together should make sure of his affairs
and act effectively before the Pope has set his feet more firmly, so that even if he wished to do harm he could not.
Milan, 23 March [1447].
