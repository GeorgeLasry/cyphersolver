# BnF italien 1584 f. 64 -- Filippo Maria Visconti to Francesco Sforza, Milan 6 Mar 1447 (cipher slip): English

The Duke has been told by Fosceo and Contuzo, who ask for his help, that the enemy's troops (the Venetians') have
gathered and crossed the Oglio into the Cremonese and mean to march on Cremona to take the city; many other places
report the same. He has at once sent Messer Carlo (Gonzaga), Messer Alberto Pio and Count Carlo with their men, and
Messer Biagio d'Assereto with the fleet by water, and has ordered Pietro Visconti at Pizzighettone and Giorgio
d'Annone, who holds the troops in his name, to go to the aid of Sforza's people at their first request and do all
they would do for the Duke himself to save Cremona; he will spare no effort to help. Had he been warned in time, he
would have arranged things so that the enemy would not even have thought of it.
