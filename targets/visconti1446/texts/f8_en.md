# BnF italien 1584 f. 8: cipher slip, Filippo Maria Visconti to Francesco Sforza, Abbiategrasso 11 Jan 1447: English

As we have advised before, we think it right, and urge and ask you, to arrange to send us one of your fully trusted
men, ostensibly as if he had left you (on his own account): we will take him on, and he is to make it appear he has no
business with you. Through him you will learn everything done here, since it is our intention that nothing be done
without your consent. Do not hesitate over whom to send, friend or enemy of ours, even our greatest enemy, provided
he is fully trusted by you; whoever he is, we will treat him so that within a month he will be as warm a servant and
friend as we have. Abbiategrasso, 11 January 1447. Signed in cipher with the full title: Filippo Maria Anglus, Duke of
Milan etc., Count of Pavia and Angera and Lord of Genoa.
