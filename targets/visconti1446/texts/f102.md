## Comparison with f. 103 (`f102_clear.md`)

The cipher and f. 103 carry the same message clause by clause, in the same order, but f. 103 is **not** a
decipherment of f. 102 word for word: it is the formal clear letter of the same day (Latin address, chancery
abbreviations, "Datum die xxiij Martii 1447"), and the cipher is a shorter Lombard version:

| clause | f. 102 (cipher) | f. 103 (clear) |
|---|---|---|
| address | Illustris[?] fili noster carissime | Illustris fili noster carissime |
| the pope | pur **el novo Papa** (name sign ꝺo) non inclinarse ad volere fare que[l] debe | pur **la S.tà de N. S. el papa** non inclinarse ad volere fare quello se debe |
| advice | dicemo che **a nui pare** et cosi ve confortiamo et pregamo ve voglate intendere **[con] Re de Ragona** (name sign ꝺ÷) | dicemo che **ad noy par** et cossi ve confortiamo et pregamo ve vogliate intendere **cum la M.tà del Re de Ragona** |
| | al[qua]le havemo scripto el simile, e quando **vedereti** esso papa non volere fare quel debe, [che] desidera esso Re et vui | alquale havemo scripto el simile, et quando **vedati** esso papa non volere fare quello chel debe, et che desidera esso Re et vuy |
| | intendandove la Maiest[?]ate soa et vui insieme ve as[..] talmente de li facti soi et provediati e faciati con effecto | intendendove la M.tà sua et voy insieme ve assecurarete talmente dele facti suoy, et provediati et fazati con effecto |
| | **inanci** che esso papa habia piu **firmato** li pedi, per modo et per manera che se ben [esso papa volesse sinis]trare non posa | che **anzi** che esso papa habia piu **firmati** li pedi, per modo et per manera che se ben esso papa volesse sinistrar non possa |
| date | Data Mediolani vigesimo tertio martii, then "et duplicata [..] vigesimo [..]" (duplicate note, worker E) | Datum die xxiij Martij 1447 |

Token match: of the 279 read cipher tokens, about 262 have their counterpart in f. 103 at the same place; the
differences are wording, not reading errors: "el novo" (5 tokens, against "la S.tà de N. S."), name signs for "la M.tà
del Re", "vedereti/vedati", "inanci/anzi", "firmato/firmati", "Mediolani" (6 tokens) and the second date (not in
f. 103). **Match about 94% (262/279)**, against a clear text that is a parallel letter, not a copy. The struck "pe-"
before "pedi" in f. 103 and the "13" for xxiii in the archivist's heading are slips of the clear letter.

**Conclusion**: f. 103 is a contemporary (1447) clear letter of the same day and content, probably the chancery's fair
version or a covering letter, written by a secretary from the same minute as the cipher; it is the clear text of
f. 102 in substance but not a decipherment. The cipher's "el novo Papa" dates the letter after the election of
Nicholas V (6 Mar 1447); the clear version names him with his title.

## English summary

Most dear son: since we understand that the new Pope is not inclined to do what he should, we advise and urge you to
come to an understanding with the King of Aragon, to whom we have written the same; and when you see that the Pope will
not do what he should and what the King and you desire, you and His Majesty together should make sure of his affairs
and act effectively before the Pope has set his feet more firmly, so that even if he wished to do harm he could not.
Milan, 23 March [1447].

## Worker E pass (29 Oct 2026): gaps re-examined, f. 103 used as a guide to sense only

Each gap re-read at the hl zooms; a value was accepted only when the slip's signs give it with the key or with values
established on other slips, f. 103 serving to say which word to look for. Changes (old -> new):

1. L2 start "[..] debe" -> "[e][?] debe": the edge sign is ·‖· (cut); "que e debe" / "que debe" (f. 103 "quello se debe").
2. L2 "ve [..]l[i]at[i] intend-" -> "ve voglate intend-": `ƀ̄ p- ħ ħ- ⊢` = v-og-l-at-e (p- = og, the key's og; the old
   list had ħ ħ ⊢, the second is ħ- = at). Lombard "voglate" (f. 103 "vogliate"; f. 31 has "voglati").
3. L3 "al[..]le" -> "al[qu]a[?]le": `8̶ ♀ ꝑ ħ ÷`; ♀ = qu and ꝑ = a only from f. 103 "alquale" [?].
4. **L4 the 15-sign gap** -> "la Maiest[?]ate soa et vui": `5̄ q- σ- b ƀ̄ y b- ⊢ g ꭓ σ- ħ ƀ 8 ʒ` = l-am-a i-es-[t]-at-e
   s-o-a et v-u-i; q- = am (barred q), g = s (long s), ħ = et, ʒ = i (the key's i = 3). y for t is the one misfit [?].
   Same words as f. 103 "la M.tà sua et voy", in Lombard "soa", "vui".
5. L5 "soi[..] provediati" -> "soi et provediati" (ħ = et, the unbarred-5 et); "de li [f]acti": the sign is ∂- = if
   ("l-if-ac-t-i", as B's "li facti" on f. 64); "faciati [con] effecto" -> "con effecto": a faint small 6 = con.
6. L6 "-a [..] piu" -> "-abia piu": `co b ♀` = ab-i-a; with L5's `∴ ft` the run is p-ap-ab-i-a, "papa (h)abia" with
   the a shared across the word boundary (cf. B, "syllable signs run across word boundaries").
7. L6 the old list had the barred 5 twice at the junction of zooms b and c (tile overlap): one token removed (331 -> 330).
8. L7 "[..7..] vigesimo [..]" -> "et[?] duplicata [..] vigesimo [..]": `ħ̄ ꝯo ẋ ŧ ∂ ħ- ꝏ` = [et] d-up-l-ic-at-a, the
   duplicate note as on f. 23 (f. 23 "duplicata die vigesimo quarto eiusdem"); the day after "vigesimo" (`2p` | L8 `bo`)
   is unread. So f. 102 is a duplicate, re-sent on the 2x of March, with f. 103 the clear letter of the original day.

**Where the cipher's wording differs from f. 103** (beyond the table above): "ve voglate intendere Re de Ragona" has no
"cum la M.tà del" (name sign only); "ve as[ħ̄] talmente" has one sign where f. 103 has "assecurarete" (the cipher seems
to abbreviate or drop the verb: unread, not forced); the end of L6 has 16 signs (`·‖· ħ ħ̄ ʒ̶ o## g‖ fa ♀ f 8 -o b p+ ƀ
÷ ɱ`, then L7 "trare") where f. 103 has "esso papa volesse sinistrar": no papa name sign and no spelled "papa" (∴ ft σ-)
in it, so the cipher's clause is worded differently (it ends -s-trare; "volese" may sit in `8 -o b p+ ƀ ÷`) and is left
unread; f. 103 has no duplicate note.
