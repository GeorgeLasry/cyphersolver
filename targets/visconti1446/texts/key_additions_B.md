# Key additions B (agent B: ff. 30/31, 36/37, 64/65)

## 0. Decipherments exist for all three pairs (important)

The leaves before the slips carry later clear texts in one 18th-c. hand: f. 29 (for 30/31), f. 35r-v (for 36/37),
f. 63 (for 64/65). Transcribed in `texts/clear_ff29_35_63.md`. Each notes "Altra simile" with a "duplicata" date, so
f. 31 (24 Feb), f. 37 (25 Feb) and f. 65 (6 Mar) are duplicates of ff. 30, 36, 64. The other slips of ital. 1584
should be checked the same way: look at the leaf before each slip (and its verso) for such a clear text.

## 1. Name signs

- **Á (A with accent/stroke) = Cosimo de' Medici** (blank label in the key copy): the f. 35v margin puts "A" against
  "Cosimo de Medici".
- f. 35v margin "nello duplicata Messere Federico" against "Conte d'Urbino": f. 37 spells out (or uses another sign
  for) Federico where f. 36 has the Conte d'Urbino sign Ɛ. To verify on the slips.

## 2. From f. 30 (read in full against f. 29)

Slip shapes and values confirmed (copy's sign -> how it looks on the slip):
- c = ħ: on the slip an h with a hook at the foot, usually no bar. l = barred 5 with flourish: an h-like sign with a
  bar at the top. **i = plain barred 5** (piacere, liati, Signori): keep it apart from l by the flourish.
- el = b with a short bar at the top-left (cancelero, del, Castelano, Angelo, "de la" written d-el-a).
  er = b with a bar over the bowl (b̄). v/u also = b with a double bar (ƀ̿) besides 8 (volerse, volerve, ve).
  at = b with a tail (liati, grati, fidati, Data). eb = b̄ with flourish (Februarii).
- et = a 5/ʒ-like sign (word "et": "gi et cetera", "et contentamento", "et fidati"); also barred 3 for "et" (L6).
  In "cetera" the -et- is b with bar [?].
- ut = a ÷-like sign with a cross (±), copy "n with cross above" (venuto, havuto, aiutare, tuto).
- us = ɱ with a cross above (Filipus, Anglus, Dominus).
- g = f: the copy's second f homophone "g" is used for f (fidati, Februarii); g also = t. Context decides.
- per (word) = q-barred + o; "per" (syllable sign) = or (Monsignore, confortamo, Signori, recordandove) -- the trap
  in key.md is real.
- q÷ is "che" and also written where f. 29 has "chi" (twice).
- quanto = q with dots (÷-like dots).
- "o with tail" = n (Signori).
- Name signs: **looped δo = Papa** (confirmed: "con el Papa"); **looped δ∞ = Dux Mediolani** in the intitulatio.
- Nulls used: + (often), oto, ϕ, π, ffp, 3°, ♀ (in the null run after the date). Note ♀ is both a null and a/b.
- The slip carries the date ("Data Mediolani die nono Februarii") and the full intitulatio ("Filipus Maria Anglus Dux
  Mediolani et cetera Papie Anglerieque Comes ac Ianue Dominus") in cipher, followed by a line of filler signs that
  give no sense (18 signs on L11).
- f. 30 has "el Camerlengo" where f. 29 has "lo Cardinale Camerlengo": the clear text expands.

## From f. 36 (read in full against f. 35; `texts/f36.md`)

- **Name signs confirmed**: Ɛ = Conte d'Urbino (4 times: L9 twice, L16, L18; at L16 f. 35 has "Duca d'Urbino");
  looped δ÷ = Duca de Ferrara (L20); **Á = Cosimo de' Medici** (L20, written ·Á· with a dot either side); looped δ7
  (with a dot) = Veneziani (L22, "con [Veneziani]", no "li"); looped δ∞ = Dux Mediolani in the intitulatio (L26).
- **Spelled out, no name signs**: Ast, Dalfino, Rolando Palavicino, Gasparo de li Ubaldini, Zohane/Zohanne del Maino,
  Angelo da Urbino (Meser/Mesere Angelo), Christoforo da la Masa, Otaviano de li Ubaldini.
- **g## = af** confirmed by sense twice ("a fare", "da fare", L24), besides g## = t; the key trap is real.
- **ɣh (y with h joined) = ur**: Urbino (L8), perturba (L22). Not in the key copy (its ur is barred ɱ, also used:
  assecurarsene L5).
- x-loop with a stroke: ot in poteremo/poteriano/Otaviano (L6, L17, L18), ub in Ubaldini/publica (L7, L18, L21).
  The key has ot = + and ub = crossed xo; on the slip the two shapes look much alike: read by context.
- b with a short bar at the top-left is used for **et** (haveti, "et Zohanne", cetera, retrare, "et tanto") and for
  **er** (poteremo, poteriano, esere, esergli, perderà, Anglerie, cetera); ƀ for el. The b-with-bar family is the main
  source of [?] on f. 36; the value is chosen by the word.
- p-crossed (ꝑ-like) = od (duodecimo L25; key od = barred p). p3 = oc (voce; "dicto cosa" = dict-oc-os-a). x- = uc
  (inducese). b- = ef (refirmato). ∂e = il (darglilo, Filipus). ∂+ = im. ∂-hook = ig (miga). ∂ = r and ic.
  ·z = ip (scripto, Filipus). b3 = ec / ev / ib / ic (recevesemo, brevi, deliberamo, ricordi). 2p = is (Cristoforo,
  avisamo, impedise, also in "Misere" for Mesere).
- g-barred (q/g with a bar) = ad ("ne ad alcun", "cadesemo"); key ad = blot-g.
- ɱ with a cross (ɱ+) = us: fusse, suspecto, Filipus, Anglus, Dominus (as on f. 30).
- "o with tail" = n in né (L6, L7, L9, L18, L24); 4 with a bar = z (Zohane) and also opens the null run on L26.
- ÷ without its dot (two bars) = e (voce, impedise, "quelo è").
- go = quando (L2) and go = come (L5, L11, L12, L19): both key forms look alike on the slip.
- q-dots = quanto (L21). "perché" is written per + che (ꝗo q÷), not with the perché sign.
- par is written once where per (= or) is needed (L13 "loro"), probably a slip error.
- Nulls used: + (often), oto, ɯ (after "Ast"), π, ffp, 3°, ϕ, 4-barred and ♀-barred (start of L26). The letter is
  followed by "Data Mediolani die duodecimo Februarii", a null run, the intitulatio, and 16 padding signs after
  "Dominus" (as on f. 30).

## From f. 37 (duplicate of f. 36, read in full against f. 35)

- **Messere Federico spelled out** (L15: c E-barred-b b̄ ·‖· | g bo b̄ ∂ y = Mesere Federico) at the one place f. 35v
  marks; no separate sign for Federico. Ɛ = Conte d'Urbino is used three times on the slip (L8 x2, L14), including where
  f. 35 writes "Duca d'Urbino". Á = Cosimo de' Medici (with dots either side), looped δ÷ = Duca di Ferrara, looped δ7 =
  Veneziani, looped δ∞ = Dux Mediolani confirmed. Asti, Delfino, Rolando Palavicino, Gasparo, Zohane del Maino, Angelo da
  Urbino, Christoforo da Masa, Otaviano de li Ubaldini all spelled out.
- es = b with an E-like double/triple top bar (presente, dasesemo, Mesere, esere, eso), distinct from el (single short
  top bar) and er (bar over the bowl or from the top-left).
- ꝑ (p with a stroke through the descender) = ot (poteremo, poteriano, Otaviano): the key's ot homophone.
- ·z on the slip = L with dots above = ip: scripto, scripsemo, Filipus.
- barred p = od (duodecimo); p3 = oc (voce); b- = ef (refirmato); crossed xo = ub (Ubaldini x2, publica); x- = uc
  (inducesse); ∂e = il (darglilo, Filipus); ∂-hook = ig (miga, vigesimo); po = of (Christoforo); all as the key.
- ɱ with ♀ above = up (duplicata); ɱ / "uto" with a cross above = us (suspecto, eiusdem, Dominus, Anglus).
- blot-g (g with bar) = ad (ad alcun, cadesemo); tt (double-barred) = g as a separate sign (esergli, Anglerieque).
- o-tail serves both n (ne, nui, non) and a (miga, saria, cosa): context decides.
- barred 3 (ʒ̶) = an throughout (Rolando, Zohane, mandare, Angelo, tanto): the copy's "barred z (≠)".
- q with a stroke through the descender = z (Zohane x3); q-dots = quanto again.
- "perché" written as a q/9 with a long curled tail (L12), not the copy's q-with-cross [?].
- Nulls: π (often), oto, ɯ, 1, ffp, 3°, +, and in the null run after "Februarii" also q, ‖, ⊢, 7.
- The duplicate date is enciphered after the date: "duplica[ta] die vigesimo quinto eiusdem".
- The filler after "Dominus" is an alphabet run in key order (o-tail o+ ħ +o ÷ g ‖ ‡ b ħ ħ ɔ o-tail = a b c d e f g h
  i l l m n). The fillers on f. 30 L11 and f. 36 may follow the same pattern: worth checking.

## From f. 64 (read against f. 63; `texts/f64.md`)

- **No name signs for persons or places**: Fosceo (the slip's spelling of f. 63's "Fesino/Tescino": S par o## ÷ y),
  Contuzo (6 g ϕo ꭓ), Oglio, Cremonese, Cremona, Mesere Carlo, Mesere Alberto di Pii, Conte Carlo, Mesere Biaxo de
  Azareto, Petro Vesconte, Piziguitone, Zorzo de Anone are all spelled out. **÷9 = gente d'arme** confirmed (L9, "le
  dicte [gente d'arme]"); plain "le gente" is spelled (L2, L6). looped δ∞ = Dux Mediolani in the short intitulatio.
- Rare syllable signs confirmed from the key copy: **ϕo = uz** (Contuzo), **barred 3 = ax** (Biaxo), **5° = az**
  (Azareto, fazano), **ʃ with hook = iz** (Pizighitone), **gf = ex** (sexto), **∂- = if** (li facti), **barred p
  (ꝑ-like) = od** (modo), p-hook = op (propria) and on, p3 = oc (lochi, socorso), crossed xo = ub (dubio, subito,
  subsidio), ∂+ = im (inimici).
- z has three slip forms: 4 (za, Zorzo), barred ff (Zorzo, s'inzegnaremo, perforzaremo), q with a stroke (posanza).
- **perché = q/9 with a cross above** (the key's form), twice (L12, L13); f. 36/37 wrote per + che instead.
- The ± sign serves both **ut** (aiuto, tuto, voluto, metuti, proveduto) and **us** (fusemo, L14).
- l is often written as an h with a hooked foot and no top bar (quella, quelle, Carlo, loro, queli, quelo, la,
  pensarlo), i.e. the shape the f. 30 notes give for c; c in the same hand is the same h-hook (Cremonese, Conte, così).
  On f. 64 the two are separated only by the word; the barred-flourish l (ŧ) is also used.
- Syllable signs run across word boundaries: ft = ap in "nostr(a p)osanza", "l(a p)osano"; b-tail = at in
  "quell(a t)erra", "quel(a t)erra"; b- = ef in "n(e f)usemo".
- g with two bars = ad (L13 "ad che"); y = o is distinct from the closed 9 = t (L3 has y where t is needed: a slip).
- Nulls: π (after "et cetera"), a slashed oo (L5), + (L9), and short slanting strokes "/" between words (L7, L8, L12
  twice), read as separators/nulls.
- Signs not in the key, read by context [?]: a dotted ı = r (rechesti), a 2/z-like sign = ic (inimici), a cross with
  side dashes = ur (pur), "-·-" = a worn ⁘ = p (Pii), a dot with a wavy dash = ut (tuto, L13).
- Scribal corrections: "et" and v written above the line with a caret (L10).
- The slip ends "Data Mediolani die sexto Marcii [Dux Mediolani] et cetera", π, and 22 padding signs that run through
  the alphabet in key order (♀ o+ o## ꝯ ·‖· g S ‖ # b ŧ ... ⁘ 4 ∂ 3 ⸸ ffo = a b c d e f f g h i l ... p ? r ... z),
  the same device as on f. 37.

## From f. 65 (duplicate of f. 64, 6 Mar 1447, read against f. 63; `texts/f65.md`)

- **The duplicate date is the f. 63 note**: f. 65 ends "Mediolani sexto Marcii, et duplicata die eodem. Dux Mediolani et
  cet[er]a" (no "Data", no "die" before sexto), the words of f. 63's "Altra simile" line. f. 63's first date is f. 64's.
- First informant spelled **S par o## ∂÷ y = Foscino** (f. 64: S par o## ÷ y = Fosceo; f. 63 "Fesino"). S = f is written
  lying, like a flat 8. Second: 6 g ϕo ꭓ = Contuzo (ϕo = uz: a circle with a stroke and dots).
- Names spelled out, no name signs except ÷9 = Gente d'arme ("le dicte [÷9]", L10) and looped δ∞ = Dux Mediolani (date).
  Biaxo: o+ b barred-3 y, i.e. **x written with the ax syllable (barred 3)**; Azareto: 5° = az. Piziguitone: q ʃ-hook b
  tt 8 fa p-hook ·‖· (8 = u). Zorzo: barred-ff per 4 β. Anon[o]: barred-3 p-hook y.
- **small + = i** (diversi, Pii, ogni, facti, Marcii), besides + as a null: read by context.
- **∂ with a bar or cross = ic** (inimici, dicte, and "richesta" where f. 63 has rechesta); plain ∂ = r.
- **ɱ (s) on this slip is a y/ɣ-like sign with a tail**, often preceded by a short stroke (Siamo stati, sono, questo).
- **ɣ (q with a curled tail) = n** (nome, ogni, nostra); ɣ with a bar = am (Siamo, voglamo).
- ∂- = if ("li facti" = l if ac t i); p-hook = op (propria); ft = ap (posanza, la posano, sapiati); gf = ex (sexto);
  ÷ with a cross and o = us (fusemo); barred p = od (modo, eodem); ɱ with ♀ above = up (duplicata).
- **perché = q with a cross above**, twice (L15, L16; in L16 the cross is the foot of the L15 descender).
- x with a stroke and a dot = null/separator before "Mesere Carlo" (f. 64 has a null at the same place).
- ƀ (b with a top-left bar) = el (de le, e'l), er (Mesere, termini, inzegneremo) and et (Azareto, metuti, cetera).
- Padding after "et cetera": 8 signs (♀-cross E-barred-b ŧ S 2p b-tail b ɣ) and π; not visibly an alphabet run.

## From f. 31 (agent B)

- f. 31 (duplicate of 24 Feb) is the slip the f. 29 clear text was made from: it alone has "lo Cardinale", "chi non
  sono", "chi può", "cum el Papa", "voglati" and the enciphered duplicate date "duplicata die vigesimo quarto eiusdem".
- perché = q with a cross above (one sign) on f. 31, where f. 30 writes per + che.
- ɱ with ♀ above = up (duplicata). b with 1 = ig [?] (vigesimo; copy has ∂-hook = ig).
- Null run after "Februarii", identical in content on ff. 30 and 31: π oto 1 ffp ϕ + 3° ♀ oto π ‖ 7 -- so 1, ‖ and 7
  are nulls here (1 is otherwise i, ‖ otherwise g).
- Padding after "Dominus" runs in alphabet order on f. 31 (a b c e d g f g h i m i m n a) as on ff. 37 and 64; the f. 30
  filler is looser (b c c d a a e i e i e i o o u o v ab) but of the same kind.
- "+" before "Dominus"/at line start stands for e (⊢) in "Ianue", as well as being a null elsewhere.
