## Summary

Duplicate, sent 25 Feb 1447, of the Duke's letter of 12 Feb. He has received two briefs of Sforza of 2 Feb. On Asti:
Sforza is right that refusing it to the Dauphin would offend him, so the Duke has decided to give it, but will follow
Sforza's advice. On Rolando Pallavicino he will try to provide and secure himself. Sforza says he never charged Gasparo
degli Ubaldini or Zohane del Maino to send Messer Angelo da Urbino or Cristoforo da la Massa to Milan: the Duke answers
that neither they nor the Conte d'Urbino said so; the idea came up in conversation, when the Duke said he would like
one of Sforza's men in Milan to press the money matter, and Angelo or Cristoforo seemed suitable because one of
Sforza's own men would arouse more suspicion than men of the Conte d'Urbino, who could appear to be there on Ottaviano
degli Ubaldini's business. Messer Federico (in the original: the Conte d'Urbino) said nothing of it either; what was
said was that the Duke should write to the Duke of Ferrara to have Cosimo de' Medici urge Sforza to come to terms with
him. There is a public rumour in Milan that Sforza has renewed his contract with the Venetians, which badly hinders the
raising of money, though the work goes on.
