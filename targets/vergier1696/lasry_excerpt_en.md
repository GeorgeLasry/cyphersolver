# Jacques Vergier to Monseigneur, March-April 1696 (BnF Clairambault 1108): Lasry's excerpt, English translation

Translation of `lasry_excerpt_reading.md` (Claude Opus 5.5, 23 Oct 2026). An excerpt: George Lasry's reading of f249v, as quoted in his BnF cipher research report; his full decryption is not in the repository, and his own English in lasry_entry.md is not reused. Same holes (? and [?] unread).

## Translation

… said only that he believed — and I find it is with good enough reason — that the affair is at present almost impracticable on the coast of Kent, because of the forces that the Prince of Orange has placed on that side; and that it would now be necessary to attempt the landing in the province of York, where almost all the studs of England are, and where consequently the horses needed for the army would be found, or else in one of the provinces of the West.
