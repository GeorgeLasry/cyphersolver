# Clairambault 1108: translation of the excerpt

George Lasry, report PDF `26_clairambault-1108.pdf`, "Excerpt and Translation".

## Translation 1 (f249v)

…said only that he thought—and with good reason, I believe—that the enterprise is now almost impracticable on the coast of Kent because of the forces the Prince of Orange has stationed there. It would now be necessary to attempt the landing in Yorkshire, where almost all England’s stud farms are situated and where the horses needed for the army could consequently be found, or in one of the western provinces.
