# Key of Spinelly's cipher passage, R8416 (copied verbatim from NOTES.md, "The cipher", 22 Oct 2026)

signs. Word division is kept.

| e | o | n | b | | |
| h | ρ | o | 7 | p | q |

Word signs: ϑ = of; ꝁ (k with stroke) = this; w̄ = that; ✱ (star with superscript) = the Emperor; ʒ at the end of line
2 after "the" = probably the King (Charles); δ̄ = ll (shall).
