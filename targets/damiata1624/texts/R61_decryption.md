# R61, Bernardino Naro, Paris, last day of February 1625: the contemporary decipherment (copied from NOTES.md)

Copied verbatim from NOTES.md (R61 paragraph): the decipherment written above the two lines of figures at the foot
of f. 186r. The one line of letter-sign cipher (marked +) on the same page is not transcribed and not read.

*A cenar l'occasione di rispettar i Francesi sospettosissimi e gelosi di loro natura.*

Check with the Biermann-Bosbach key: the end of the figures decodes as *… di loro natura*.
