# R59, Bernardino Naro, Paris 14 April 1625: the contemporary decipherment (copied from NOTES.md)

Copied verbatim from NOTES.md (R59 paragraph): the decipherment written above each of the three cipher passages
on the leaf, with the project's check of the figures against the Biermann-Bosbach key.

- f. 126r: *il Generale delle galere* (figures `14 18 17 08 41 40 33 18 [2] 89 [25] 33 18 [55] 14 40 33 18 41 08` = *generale de le galere*)
- f. 127r: *di procurare l'Ambasceria ordinaria di Roma* (figures begin `33 [5] 40 34 47 40 37 48 49 40 41 …` = *l'ambasciar…*)
- f. 127v: *Il card. di Lione lo contrarià* (figures end `… 74 93 41 40 41 49 40 [25]` = *contraria*)
