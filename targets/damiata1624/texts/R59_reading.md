# R59, Bernardino Naro, Paris 14 April 1625: the three cipher passages, reading

Rests on: the contemporary decipherment written above each of the three cipher passages on ff. 126r-127v (ASV Segr.
Stato Francia 64), as transcribed in `R59_decryption.md` / NOTES.md, with the figures checked against the
Biermann-Bosbach key (generale de le galere; l'ambasciar...; contraria). The passages are short runs inside a clear
letter; the clear text around them is not transcribed. Italian (the entry was tagged Latin). Written for the v3
verification, 23 Oct 2026.

## Reading

f. 126r: il Generale delle galere.
