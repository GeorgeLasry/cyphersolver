# R2159, Fra Giovanni di Lucca, Brody, 30 May 1644, f. 18r cipher paragraph: English translation

## Translation

As to the Prince of Transylvania, I shall prevent by every means that the Turk gives him help, and I shall turn the Prince of Moldavia against him, if need be, and give him two thousand Cossacks; and I shall send to you, with a letter of his to the Emperor, [asking] that he make no peace until he has humbled or defeated him.
