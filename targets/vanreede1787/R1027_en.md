# R1027 — Van Reede to Van de Spiegel, Berlin, 4 March 1788: English

Translation of `R1027_reading.md` (same holes).

## Translation

Berlin, 4 March 1788. Sir,

The news from Russia that came in this morning says that Messieurs de Ségur and Cobenzl are quite dumbfounded that the
Empress is supposed to have [?decidedly] refused the triple alliance. Monsieur de Keller, however, has still had no
answer, and does not expect to receive it until the Empress has the one she is awaiting from Vienna to the request she
made to the Emperor, whether he was resolved or not on a vigorous and sustained attack against the Turks; a request
which, it is believed, decided the Emperor to make the declaration to Turkey that is now known. Monsieur de Keller
further believes that the Empress will want to await the return of the first courier sent to London, which was
followed by two others, before declaring herself on the King's proposals.

There being nothing more, Sir, that could deserve your attention, I have the honour to present my respects to you and
to be, Sir, your very humble and very obedient servant, A. W. van Reede.
