James C. Mountflorence to James Madison, Paris, 18 March 1801
Source: Founders Online full transcription, https://founders.archives.gov/documents/Madison/02-91-02-0009
(abstract: https://founders.archives.gov/documents/Madison/02-01-02-0037 ; RC DNA: RG 59, CD, Paris, vol. 1; "Partly in code; key not found")
Transcribed as printed (code groups as the editors give them; not checked against the manuscript).

I have it from very good Authority, 357. 723. 640. 544. 529. 684, that 679 has ceded 719, 434, not 432, 249, 688, 521, with all its Dependencies. This was done by some of the 666 Articles 228, 475. 723. 529. 377. 723. 460. 475. 475. 377., as Compensation for 684. 723. 664. 250. 207. 529. 756, given 250, 434, 719, 640. 207. 660. 500. 207.

[clear text follows: no later accounts from the US than 29 January; Council of Prizes; six vessels in quarantine at Malaga]
