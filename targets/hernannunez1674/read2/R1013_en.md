# R1013, Hernán Núñez to Fuenmayor, Stockholm, 15 Dec 1674

## Translation

With great promises of the protection of Sweden, which has cost them good presents; and when the envoy of
Brandenburg wished to send a gentleman of his in their ship, these deputies answered him that they did not dare, so
as not to give umbrage to Sweden. This, together with my knowing the great reliance they place here on friendship
with Hamburg and the advantages they have drawn from it at other times (for, as Your Lordship knows, they owed to it
the putting of their army in a state to invade Denmark in the last war this kingdom had with her), has produced the
good effect of greatly satisfying this envoy of the Elector of Brandenburg; and I hope it will produce that of the
Hamburgers, being intimidated, taking away from the Swedes a great part of the confidence they place in them, which
will certainly hold them back much and will equally encourage the Danes.
