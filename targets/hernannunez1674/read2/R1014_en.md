# R1014, Hernán Núñez to Fuenmayor, Stockholm, 19 Dec 1674

## Translation

I have already told Your Lordship in my previous letters that the principal aim this kingdom has in having sent its
ambassador to that [court], as I understand, is to gain time by this to satisfy the instances this ambassador of France
makes, and that of not assisting him for now with the succour he claims by virtue of their last treaty; and I have not
learned that the said ambassador carries orders to conclude the marriage of this king with the sister of that one,
but I understand that he will speak of it. As to the treaty between this kingdom and England of which Your Lordship
speaks to me, they assure me that it is only a renewal of the last one which Monsieur de Coventry, ambassador of that
king, concluded at this court, of which I sent a copy to Your Lordship on 24 October; but in the state in which I find
myself, almost deprived of dealings with people, I may be deceived in everything even more than hitherto. And as to
keeping that crown in our union, my opinion has been, is and always will be that there is no other means of holding
Sweden back, and that if that crown came to terms on neutrality, this one would lack the only pretext with which it
excuses itself to France, and it seems impossible that it should [?fail] then to succour her.
