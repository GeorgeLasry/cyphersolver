## 2. Read on the image

Not redone. The third pass measured the ceiling set by the hand at the 450 dpi of the only images: the oracle bound of
the present transcription is 71 % of letters on known-plaintext blocks, two independent careful readings agree on 69 %
of glyphs, and the three glyph families g/looped g, m/hooked m, r/2-shaped r differ by a hook. A fourth reading at the
keyboard would not raise the decode above fragments; the edition would read both letters outright.
