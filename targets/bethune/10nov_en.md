# Henri IV to Béthune, Paris, 10 November 1601 (BnF fr. 3484 f. 34): English translation

Monsieur de Béthune, after the taking of … [the Este–Aldobrandini debt and the Parlement's judgment] … given after
hearing both parties by the Parlement of this city, *but I was very glad to have this pretext to gratify the said
Cardinal and show him my goodwill, and that he too should have it for receiving my gratification, so [that] I had ten
thousand écus paid to him at once through the agency of the Sieur de Sillery.* As the Cardinal d'Ossat, who knew of
it, will tell you. … Wherefore I have *decided to gratify the said Cardinal, to let him know in the prosperity* in
which I now find myself by the grace of God *that I mean* also *to acknowledge the kindnesses he has done me just as I
sought his friendship in my necessities. Therefore I have ordered [Sillery's brother] to have the sum of ten thousand
écus paid to the said Cardinal within the first four months of next year, by the same hands through which he received
the first,* without regard *to the decree obtained by the said Duchess of Nemours, nor to the want of money in which I
find myself. For I have affection for the said Cardinal, and wish to discharge the promise I made him, and on every
occasion to show him my goodwill.* By means of which, after conferring about it with the said Cardinal d'Ossat, you
will let the said Cardinal Aldobrandini know the above, *in such a way that he receives and values this favour as it
deserves, [for otherwise] it would be necessary [ … ]* As the said Aldobrandini wishes, for the reasons you will learn
from the said Cardinal d'Ossat. *So make the most of this goodwill of mine by your good conduct, and [pretend]* that you
have not received the present letter, so that you may be *the first to inform the said Cardinal Aldobrandini of it.* I
pray God, Monsieur de Béthune, to keep you in His holy care. From Paris, this 10th day of November 1601. Henry.
