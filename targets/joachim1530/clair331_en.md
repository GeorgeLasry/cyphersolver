# BnF Clairambault 331 f. 157r-v, Giovan Gioacchino da Passano (attrib.) to Francis I, copy dated 15 March 1530: English translation

Translation (Claude Opus 5.5, 23 Oct 2026) of `clair331_reading.md`, the reading of the cipher (key recovered from the
ciphertext). Same marks: `[D]`, `[Q]`, `[S2]` are code signs not established in the key (by context [D] = the Cardinal,
Wolsey; [Q] = the King, Henry VIII; [S2] open); `[?dese]` a word whose sense here is unsettled (perhaps "ten"); the
amounts are clear numerals with a raised m read as thousands. A further page and three lines of cipher were omitted by
the copyist and are not in the copy.

## Translation

Your Majesty will learn that this [S2], of the goods of the [D], so it is said, has received about 800,000 scudi, in
the items written below, namely: in ready money, 150,000 scudi; in credits of his [D], frozen by [Q] and then released,
50,000 scudi; for the fruits of one year of the revenues of his [D], 60,000 scudi; in jewels, about 20,000 scudi; in
vessels of gold and silver, 250,000 scudi; in household furnishings, at the least, 200,000 scudi.

For the revenues of the bishopric of Winchester and the abbey of St Albans, deducted [m/m notation] scudi [?dese]
income to the said [D], together with the archbishopric of York, assigned and ordered, [Q] will have yearly about
20,000 scudi.

Sum: 750,000 scudi.
