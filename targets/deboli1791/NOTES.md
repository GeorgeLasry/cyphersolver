# Deboli → Stanisław August, cipher despatches from St Petersburg, 1791 (AGAD Zbiór Popielów 5; catalogue 420)

Status: in progress

The 105 cipher despatches of 1791 were read at the time. The decipherments are the ones the AGAD inventory lists
as Deboli's "clear Polish despatches" of the same dates, a separate unit, **Zbiór Popielów 421** ("Korespondencja króla
z Debolim 1791"). They are the cabinet's fair copies, docketed "reçu le …". Matched on despatch 002 (7 Jan 1791) group
by group at the opening and at two repeated names. The work left is transcription of the cipher and checking it
against these copies. So far 3 of 105 despatches are transcribed (about 3,100 groups) and one is aligned.

## The system

A large Polish syllabic code (nomenclator), about 2,200 groups. The clear frame is in French: "Sire!" at the head and
"Je suis avec le plus profond Respect … Deboli" with place and date at the foot. The coded body is Polish. Whole
words for common words and titles (ani, od, WKrMci); names spelled in syllables (Stahremberg = 686 1405 1255 207, with
1307 = Gra- in both Graff and Graffowi); about 2.3 groups per Polish word. One-digit groups (8, 5, 3, 1, 9 …) are
frequent. Small letters a/b/c (rarely e, z, o, oo) are written under many groups. They probably mark homophones or
variant values of the same number. 1786 is the most frequent group (111 in 3,123, 3.6%). Catchwords repeat the first
group of the next page.

This is not the code of Deboli's 1792 Danzig letters (ZbPop 415 items 084, 086, transcribed with their clear copies in
`transcription/zbpop415_1792_mixed.txt`, 794 groups). The most frequent Danzig groups (1053, 1151, 281, 114, 335,
1172) do not occur in 1791, and 1791's one-digit groups do not occur at Danzig.

## Scribe and digits

The looped θ = 8; an open o = 0; b-shape = 6; s-shape = 5; q-shape = 9. From despatch 002 on the 4 is often closed
like a q, so 1490/1990, 1498/1998 and 1405/1905 need the clear copy to decide. The full doubtful list is in the
`{notes}` lines of the transcription.

## Escalation

- 0a siblings: done. The 1792 Danzig letters (ZbPop 415) have clear copies but use a different code.
- Clear pages: done. This is how the decipherments were found (ZbPop 421).
- Known keys: ZbPop 444/451/453/454 not online. Not needed now that the decipherments are in hand.
- Print: Zielińska & Danilczyk edit only 1780–1782; nothing for 1791 found in print (Szczygielski 2014 cites only
  the King's clear letters, ZbPop 420).
- Next: transcribe cipher and decipherment despatch by despatch, align, and build the code table, grading values C
  (from the contemporary decipherment).
