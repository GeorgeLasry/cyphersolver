# R1069, d'Affry, The Hague, 1757: English translation of the contemporary decipherment

I have the honour to send you the copy of a letter that Mr de la Salle wrote to me from Hamburg, which came with a
letter from Mr Saltykov, of which I also enclose the copy. Mr Saltykov wrote to Mr Golovkin, who sent me this letter
this morning. You will see, Sir, that Mr de la Salle believes his justification complete, and I wish it were; but
Mr Golovkin the son told me that he had spoken of Mr de la Salle to Mr Laurenzi, who told him that he knew well that a
project had been proposed to Mr Yorke, but a chimerical one, to which that minister had paid no attention. However that
may be, I think there will be no harm in speaking of Mr de la Salle to Mr de St Sauveur when he passes through here, so
that on arriving at Petersburg he can have his conduct watched. A Dutch ship called the Five Brothers, captain
Jarighates, has been taken and brought into Dunkirk. The owners of this ship and those with a stake in it are
considerable people. One of the latter in particular is a man who has very great influence in Friesland, and who makes
the best use of it. Allow me to ask you to speak of it to Mr de Moras, and to tell him that if, as the owners assert,
this prize is not validated, it would be desirable for the Prize Council to settle the matter promptly, because in that
case the detention of the ship and its crew would offend those concerned in it and would please our adversaries.
