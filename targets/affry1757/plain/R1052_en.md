# R1052, d'Affry, The Hague, 1757: English translation of the contemporary decipherment

I cannot refrain from informing you of a muffled rumour that has spread here, of a supposed prophecy made by a man
living in Amsterdam, who, while telling Mr Thomas Hop that the ship he was worried about had not been taken by the
English but had perished at such-and-such a latitude, added that he also knew that an attempt was to be made on His
Majesty's life on the 15th of this month. I shall know by tomorrow whether these words were really spoken, and in that
case I shall not lose an instant in asking that this madman be shut up in the madhouse.
