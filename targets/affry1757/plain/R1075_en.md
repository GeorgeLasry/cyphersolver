# R1075, d'Affry, The Hague, January 1757: English translation of the contemporary decipherment

This morning I saw a Republican with whom I had a long discussion on the augmentation. After I had shown him its
uselessness and even its danger as regards foreign affairs, he admitted to me that if it were made, it was only with
regard to what was to be feared from the (+) few will be necessary.

(You will see in the placard for the fast day a rather ridiculous expression, "that it may please Heaven to make the
ambitious sword return to its sheath".

I enclose the copy of the letter that Madame Tronchin wrote. Her gazette was apparently already printed when she
received my letter; the supplement was not. The more our enemies try to find mystery in the horrible deed of the fifth
of this month, the more I think I should have it published that it appears this execrable assassin had no accomplices.

[clear in the original: I have the honour to send you ... ordinary cipher.]

I would be very sorry that the courier I sent you had been useless, if I had not also had to inform you of the request
that is to be made in London.
