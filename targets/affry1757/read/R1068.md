## Summary

An Austrian courier passed through (The Hague) the evening before, on his way from Vienna to London. D'Affry says the
minister will know better than he does what the courier carries. Colloredo, the imperial minister in London, is to send
the answer he gets there to Starhemberg, the imperial ambassador at Versailles. The note is a quick
intelligence report sent beside the long N.171.

## Fagel register (added 21 Oct 2026)

The clerk's clear copy in NA 1.10.29 inv. 5186 scan 181L (found by WAINUT, PR #65; `../wainut/register_clear.txt`)
reads: "Il a passé Vendredi au Soir un Courrier de Vienne à Londres. Vous savez mieux que moi ce dont il est chargé.
Mr. de Colloredo doit faire passer à Mr. Starrenberg ce qu'on lui aura repondu." So 664 is *Vendredi* (not *hier*),
806 is *mieux* as proposed, and the name read above as A-re-m-be-r-g is Starrenberg (Starhemberg, the imperial
ambassador in France), not d'Arenberg: the register implies 982 = st (key d) and 245 = n (key m), each from this one
occurrence. The reading above is left as the key gives it.
