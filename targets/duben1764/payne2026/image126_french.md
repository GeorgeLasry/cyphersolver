## English translation of the visible French

[Continuing from the previous page:] In offering this advice I have no other aim than to remove anything that might cause the slightest coolness between two courts that have so much interest in remaining united. I know your zeal in serving our dear master, and therefore trust that you will make common cause with me in the matter at hand.

The crisis in which we find ourselves is terrible, and I assure you on my honour that it is more important than ever to handle the Empress carefully and secure her friendship by strengthening a system aimed solely at preserving peace in the North. If, on the contrary, people continue to obstruct this princess’s aims, I cannot answer for the disastrous consequences that will follow.

I write this in the strictest confidence, hoping, my dear count, that you will use it only as your friendship for me and your usual discretion dictate. Allow me at the same time to ask for a brief reply, which can only give great pleasure to someone as devoted to you as I am. [Unresolved signature.] A million compliments to Madame [unresolved name].
