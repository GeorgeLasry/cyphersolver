# Copy-book of letters to Francisco de Melo (marquês de Sande), 1660-61 (Unsolved Catalogue no. 389)

Status: no write-up. Blocked on access: neither the copy-book nor the volume with its keys is digitised (ANTT, Condes da Ponte mç. 19 n.º 1).

Blocked on images: neither the copy-book nor the volume holding its keys is digitised. Both have to be ordered
from the Torre do Tombo. Nothing below rests on a sight of either volume.

## The document

- ANTT, Arquivo dos Condes da Ponte, mç. 19, n.º 1 (old shelfmark Códice 12); reference code
  PT/TT/CPN/0019/00001 (old id ANTT9375760). DigitArq:
  https://digitarq.arquivos.pt/documentDetails/91d7ad9ba8bb41078124b2fca31110fe
- Title on the volume: "Livro de cartas que escreveram ao Exmo Sr. Marquês de Sande, sendo Embaixador
  extraordinário em Inglaterra, o Conde de Miranda, o Cardeal Orsino e Gaspar Dias de Mesquita, nos anos de
  1660 e 1661". Description in full: "Copiador de cartas numeradas do n.º 1 a 113. Contém documentos cifrados."
  Languages: Portuguese, Spanish, Italian, French, Latin. It is the only child of mç. 19.
- "Marquês de Sande" is the later title. In 1660-61 Francisco de Melo e Torres was ambassador extraordinary in
  London (the Catherine of Braganza marriage treaty, 1661).
- Writers: D. Henrique de Sousa Tavares, 3rd conde de Miranda, ambassador extraordinary at The Hague (Dutch
  peace of 6 August 1661); Cardinal Virginio Orsini, protector of Portugal in Rome; Gaspar Dias de Mesquita,
  the Portuguese agent in Rome.
- What is in cipher, how much, and in which system is not stated. A copy-book may carry the cipher as
  received, or the copyist's clear text, or both.

## The keys: MMCG tomo 3L

The catalogue's lead is the Miscelâneas Manuscritas do Convento da Graça, tomo 3L (PT/TT/MMCG/3L, old id
ANTT4639944, DigitArq id 5cf265562d124ceb903b0aedae232369): "Cartas do conde de Miranda, que escreveu sendo
embaixador extraordinário em Holanda a Francisco de Melo sendo embaixador extraordinário em Inglaterra", 84
items, 1658-60. Its note: "A folhas 3 deste está a cifra para se decifrarem as cartas até folhas 30, onde está
outra para se decifrarem as que se seguem."

- 3L/000003 (f. 3): Cifra "para se decifrarem as cartas primeiras deste livro" (first key, letters to f. 30).
- 3L/000020 (f. 30): Cifra "para se decifrarem as mais cartas que estão neste livro até a última" (second key).
- The Miranda letters in 3L run from 14 Nov 1659 (f. 1) to 22 Oct 1660 (f. 132). DigitArq marks 14 items as
  partly in cipher and one as wholly in cipher:
  f. 9 (26 Dec 1659), f. 12 (undated, writer unknown), f. 52 (26 Mar 1660), f. 65 (9 Apr), f. 81 (27 May),
  f. 92 (25 Jun), f. 93 (28 Jun), f. 99 (23 Jul), f. 101 (30 Jul), f. 113 (17 Sep), f. 123 (24 Sep),
  f. 125 (5 Oct, **entirely in cipher**), f. 128 (8 Oct), f. 130 (15 Oct).
- The two volumes overlap in 1660. 3L holds the originals as Melo received them. The CPN copy-book holds
  copies of letters from 1660-61, so its Miranda items for Jan-Oct 1660 are probably copies of letters in 3L,
  and its 1660-61 cipher should be the second 3L key unless the cipher was changed. The Orsini and Mesquita
  items need different keys: neither is in 3L. Unchecked until imaged.

## Related material (none digitised)

- PT/TT/CPN/0017/00001 (mç. 17 n.º 1, Liv. 549), 1657-60: letters from the Duke of Courland, Mazarin,
  Cardinal Orsini, Francisco de Sousa Coutinho (Rome) and secretary of state Pedro Vieira da Silva to
  Francisco de Melo; "contém documentos cifrados do cardeal Orsini e do secretário de Estado Vieira da Silva".
  It is the earlier volume of the Orsini series, and the place to look for an Orsini key or a clear copy.
- PT/TT/CPN/000210 (catalogue 386, `targets/rome1658/`): a 1658 letter to the ambassador in Rome, partly in
  cipher.
- PT/TT/CPN/002285: Sande papers including "Cifra para Manuel Barreto" (1662).
- BL Add MS 38038: despatches from Afonso VI, Castelo Melhor and Sousa de Macedo to Sande, 1662-66, "many in
  cipher, some deciphered in the margin by a contemporary hand, others with modern decipherments not always
  to be trusted" (BL catalogue). Different writers and later, but Lisbon's cipher to Sande, and partly
  deciphered. Not digitised.

  false`, zero scan files. Of the 2,713 Condes da Ponte records in the crawl, 68 are imaged, and none of

## Prior art

None found (26 Oct 2026). `research/archive_sweep_2026-10/known.py` on "Miranda 1660", "Orsini 1660" and
"Add MS 38038" gave no hits (DECODE, Cryptoole, repo). "Sande" matched only "Aleksander" strings. Web searches on the
correspondents, the Hague embassy, the shelfmark and Cryptiana found no edition, calendar or decipherment.
E. Prestage, *As relações diplomáticas de Portugal com a França, Inglaterra e Holanda de 1640 a 1668*
(Coimbra 1928), is a study that may quote Miranda's letters. It has not been checked for deciphered passages.
STCN 142818666 is Miranda's printed memorial of 2 Jan 1660 to the States General (clear text, a possible crib
for the Dutch business, which also appears in clear at 3L f. 20).

## Escalation

- Siblings: MMCG 3L (originals of the same Miranda letters for 1660, plus the two keys) is the first thing
  to order. CPN mç. 17 n.º 1 for the Orsini cipher. None is digitised.
- Clear pages: the copy-book may give the cipher passages in clear, or interlined. Check that first once it
  is imaged. If not, the 3L originals of the same letters are the pair to compare.
- Known keys: the two 3L key leaves. Nothing on DECODE or Cryptoole for Portuguese diplomatic ciphers of
  1659-61. The 1649 Portuguese ciphertexts in HistoCrypt 2022 ("Prey to a Privateer") and the 1646 Brazil spy
  paper show the period's Portuguese systems (nomenclators with numeric codes), not this key.
- Print: Prestage 1928; Miranda's printed memorials (STCN) as cribs for The Hague letters.
