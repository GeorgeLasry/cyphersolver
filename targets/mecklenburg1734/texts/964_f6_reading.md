# Hann. 9g Nr. 964 f. 6: George II to the Privy Council in Hanover, St James 6/17 December 1734

Reading = clear text + the contemporary interlinear decipherment of the four cipher lines (marked ⟨ ⟩).
Transcription and line division in `964_f6.txt`.

> Euch Veste und liebe Getreue. Ist dasjenige Unserer Willens Meinung völlig conform, ⟨was ihr wegen des
> Mecklenburgischen Reichs Contingents und dass dessen Stellung von des Königs von Preussen Maj. nicht übernommen
> werden möge⟩, Unserem Abgesandten von Erffa zu Wien und dem Geheimen Cammer Raht von Haus rescribiret zu haben,
> vermittelst eures allerunterthänigsten Postscripti vom 7. huius berichtet. Ut in Rescripto. St James den 6/17 Dec.
> 1734. George R. — Hattorf.

**English.** Trusty and well-beloved: what you report, in your most humble postscript of the 7th of this month,
that you have written to Our envoy von Erffa in Vienna and to Privy Chamber Councillor von Haus — ⟨about the
Mecklenburg Reich contingent, and that its raising should not be taken over by His Majesty the King of Prussia⟩ —
is entirely in conformity with Our will.

The ciphered clause is the one sensitive point: Hanover did not want Prussia to take over the raising of the
Mecklenburg contingent to the Reich army (the 1734 war of the Polish Succession).

Check against the cipher (26 Oct 2026): four cipher lines, each with its decipherment line above it; the decipherment
reads as one clause that closes the sentence opened in clear ("Ist dasjenige ... conform, [was ...] ... zu haben").
No group-by-group check: the figures are undivided and no key is in the repository.
