## Legend
{G}  capital-G-like sign with a hooked/looped top (e.g. "{G}38mp7s", "{G}7d7s", "s4l37 {G}7"); occurs inside cipher where a consonant is expected; may be a scribal variant of t (cf. "{G}38mp7s") or a separate sign -- left unresolved. Plain-letter "G" in "G7nc4l8z" (p01) and "Garay"/"Gomara" is written as an ordinary capital G.
6    in "m23628n4s", "628n4": a 6-shaped form in a consonant slot (likely the scribe's b), transcribed as 6.
d    the round-backed "∂" form, transcribed d throughout.
^x   superscript; ^(...) an interlinear insertion; " marks are the scribe's insertion marks.
Notes: p01 l.9/p02R "4 2s^a" etc.: "^a" is a superscript a over the s. p04L l.4 "d8m7n37": first letter blotted. p02R l.20 "^4r{G}7" is written above "l4m8nt4".
