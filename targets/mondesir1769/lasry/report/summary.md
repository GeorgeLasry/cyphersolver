# Français 5581 — Letter from Mondesir

Source: George Lasry, report PDF `12_francais-5581-letter-from-mondesir.pdf` (received 2026-10-24), page 1.

**Contents:** A private letter from Isle de France criticizes the governor’s conduct, weak coastal defence and food shortages caused by price controls. The writer also discusses an artillery appointment, the forge enterprise, debts, and selling his share to provide for his wife.
