# Français 5581 — Letter from Mondesir: translation of the excerpt

George Lasry, report PDF `12_francais-5581-letter-from-mondesir.pdf`, "Excerpt and Translation".

## Translation 1 (f50v)

Even in his own profession, he is, one may say, ignorant, presumptuous, and stubborn as a Breton. [?] lets himself be strongly prejudiced by the first person who comes along, clings to ideas hastily seized upon, and puts them into action without reflection or consulting anyone. This produces many foolish decisions and orders that have to be reversed or left without effect. [?] is impetuous, to the point of anger and excess.

## Translation 2 (f51r)

The ban on speculating in the king’s bills against piastres, on which they have been obliged to turn a blind eye, as with fixing the prices of provisions of every kind.

## Translation 3 (f56r, continuous paragraph, lines3–6 — 56r)

And if they then wish to give me men and money, and I have two years of peace to prepare for war, people will see the difference between [one?] man and another.
