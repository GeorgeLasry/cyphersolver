Sir, I dowt not but that yow know what [?Pyne] hath to delyver from me to hys master, for he promised me to doo the same
to yow. Ther ys not ony nation (and yf ther be mony) that [?sayeth] so of the D[uke]; but severally, and [?together],
spoke that ther [?inbasied] unto Englande, saying that they wylle ether doo yt at the [?barkeday] or at the [?forder].
Therfor my poor opinion ys to strengthen our havens at home, boothe by lande and see, and to drawe our forces from

[…] the haven is […]*; and moreover the wynde hath blown here these three wekes out [of the] north east, and ther hath
not come owt of Holland and Zelande above three boottes, and yf the wynde [had been] good to doo hether. By that yow may
see what help ther ys nor hope to be had from those parties.

Touching *hostages*, yt ys honorable for *[7] to […] their surety to these termes*, as usuall yn all *treaties* in
[?numbers], that at the *treaty of [?Breda]*, which was the 20, and the *subiects* ther were delivered Julio Romeo,
Cristophero Mondragon, Mor Daussy, as they have bene done by a [?sovereign] prince to hys subiect: much rather to his
[?royall] for the surety of her subiect and saufgarde.

Mr Controller was sycke [?yesterday], and hys owne [?] for me; he informes us [?tacitely] that he hath informed ther
Mr of hys doynge, and satisfyed hym yn these [?] proceedinges: such [?touching] the [?] of armes, and the [?] for the
better confirmation [?] according to her Mty's direction. Doctor Dayll yowr [?] [?moved] to [?] […].

I am informed that ther preparation doth dayly encrease; they chose the place of rendevous ys at Dunkergh. *[22]* is so
*forwarde* yn the *action* that he promised Q[ueen] to *send to […] [27]* to begyn the *openning of the […]*, which
myght bee to the *[?towne] of […]*, that a *[?brother]* about *[15]*, who promised the mayor *[…]* a *haven* to open.

*[22] hath a desire to retourne* from theym, yf he be not more *[?restrained]*. Ther ys great want of us to cumfort our
[…] and kepe them [?here], hoping that by yowr good meanes that we shall [be] [?relived] here. I commyt yow to the

Yowr very loving frend, W. Cobham
