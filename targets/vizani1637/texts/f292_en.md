# Vizani to the Comte de Césy, Prinkipo, 8 October 1637 (BnF fr. 16158 f. 292): English translation

"Signor [231] has sent to ask me whether the four thousand scudi [?] to [31] of Monsignor Beria, by Your Excellency's
order, were promised to the said Signor, who … is close to obtaining the patriarchate for himself. … [64?] Monsignor
Beria hopes that within two months he will be changed; the [36] has been given(?) to a partisan of his, and for his
[198] to obtain his aim at [227] is most uncertain." The clear passages say that the orders changed on 30 May and that
nothing can be said by letter while the plague lasts.
