# m0054: English translation

Thanks for your telegram. Here everything and everyone very well. You may give [it], but only after having arranged it
with Rosa and after she has spoken with the French Government. The little ones kiss your hand. I embrace you. Carlos.

Notes. The telegram is in French with one coded sentence in Portuguese. The coded sentence is taken from the decode
of the time on the card m0060; the French words are in clear on the telegram. "Podes dar" has no object in the
decode; what is to be given is not said. "ella" (she) has no clear antecedent in the telegram. "Rosa" is probably
Tomás de Sousa Rosa, then Portuguese minister in Paris [our identification, not checked].
