# m0058: English translation

Here everyone and everything well. [I] have written to you at Malta. [I] embrace you, and the little ones greet
everyone. Carlos.

Note. "salue" is singular as written. The line can also be read "[I] embrace you and the little ones; greet everyone
[from me]". The mention of Malta fits the Queen's Mediterranean cruise of spring 1903 (m0056 reaches her on
board the yacht Amelie at Oran on 5 March 1903).
