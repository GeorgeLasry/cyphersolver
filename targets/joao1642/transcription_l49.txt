LETTER: João IV to the marquês de Nisa, Évora, 9 October 1643
SCANS: PT-TT-MSLIV-1017_m0068 (folio 64; whole page), enclosure "Carta de ElRei Inclusa"
       to Luís Pereira de Castro begins m0068 line 25 and continues on m0069.
IDENTIFICATION: this is the 9 Oct 1643 letter whose enclosed "Carta de ElRei inclusa"
is addressed to "Luys P.ra de Castro" and refers to "a instrucção q levastes" — i.e. the
letter sending/covering the instructions for Luís Pereira de Castro's special mission in
Paris (DigitArq 00049). The MS letter-number written beside the heading is a two-digit
figure beginning with 4; at the resolution of these scans it reads 44 / 47 / 49 and I
cannot settle it (see NOTE ON NUMBERING at the end). The instruction paragraphs themselves
(numbered 1.º-8.º, "Plenipotenciarios de ElRei de Cast.a", Junta, treaties, truce) are on
m0069-m0073, followed by "22 Advertencias de Conveniencia" (m0074-75) and a "Relação"
(m0076-77).
NO CLEAR COPY of the ciphered passages exists on these pages; the key below is the draft
key (key.py), which works unchanged on this 1643 letter.

Convention: "CIPHER:" = as written (one line per manuscript line); ? = doubtful letter;
[?] = word not read with confidence. "PLAIN:" = decipherment; clear text in ( ).

ENCLOSURE (m0068 l.25 ff., "Carta de ElRei Inclusa", to Luís Pereira de Castro)
Clear text, no cipher on the part transcribed:
"Luys P.ra de Castro Am.o. Com as novas do falecim.to de ElRei Chr.mo resolvi m.dar
representar o sentim.to q ella me causou a ElRei seu f.o e a R.a Regente sua M.ce pello
Marq.s de Cascaes. E porq, fiado na justificação da m.a causa e das rezoens q vão
apontadas na instrucção q levastes, e sobre tudo em vos sendo os q passais à Junta da
Pas q[uando] tenho por certo se resolva nella q se deve admitir Plenipot.ro em meu
nome ..." (continues m0069).

NOTE ON NUMBERING: folio numbers run scan - 4 (m0065 = f. 61, m0085 = f. 81). Each
letter carries a letter-number in ink to the right of the word "Carta"; m0085 (20 Nov
1643) carries 56, which matches DigitArq 00056, so these ink numbers are the DigitArq
document numbers. The figure on m0068 is damaged/faint; identification above rests on
content, not on the number.
