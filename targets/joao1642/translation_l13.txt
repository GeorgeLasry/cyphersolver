# Letter 13, 31 Dec 1642: English translation of reading_l13.txt, same holes.
A. (... and of the licence) that the Most Christian King was granting to his vassal Rigão and others to send their ships and men to the island of São Lourenço [Madagascar], open trade and build forts.
B. (... although the news is not certain) you [..] most fomented the wars and discords with Castile, and that on his account the offers made of a settlement and cessation of arms were often not admitted; and that if the Cardinal's death should prove true, it is to be feared
C. that Castile may pursue the same approaches and have better success; (wherefore I charge and command you) above all the other matters in your charge to try, with the caution and secrecy that are needed, to penetrate the secrets of Castile and the mind of the Most Christian King and his ministers, so as to warn me of it.
D. [..] (legible only in part; the clear copy gives the text: to report by every ship, and that São Lourenço belongs to this crown and no foreigner may trade or build forts there without the king's licence)
