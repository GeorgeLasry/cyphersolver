# Letter 9, 28 Dec 1642: English translation of reading_l09.txt, same holes.
1. (... with which you were handling and seeking) the release of the Infante Dom Duarte, my most beloved and esteemed brother, (and how you had sought) letters from the Most Christian King by which Prince Thomas and the Duke of Longueville were to intercept the troops escorting the Infante to Milan, (and how you also sought to be given) some Spanish prisoners of the Most Christian King, persons of rank, so as to obtain the Infante's freedom in exchange for them.
2. ... such as those of exchange or escape, rather than[?] those of force, like the ones you intended to attempt with the Most Christian King's letters, would be very dangerous for the Infante's life, since it is reported that those who moved him from Germany to Milan carried orders to kill him should anyone try to take him on the road; [and with the] letters the secret could not be kept, which is the most essential point of this business, and in Castile they would set greater [guards].
3. ... and to try to advance it in favour of the Infante's freedom by means of the exchange you had attempted with the son of the Duke of O[..]a and other prisoners of rank.
