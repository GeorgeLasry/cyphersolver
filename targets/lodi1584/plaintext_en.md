# R119, Englefield to the Cardinal of Como, Madrid, 15 Nov 1584: English translation of the deciphered runs

Translation (Claude Opus 5.5, 23 Oct 2026) of `plaintext.txt`, the contemporary interlinear decipherment of each cipher
run, run by run in the letter's order. **The runs only**: the clear text between them is not transcribed in the
repository, so each run is translated as the phrase it is. `u[...]` is a cut-off word. Spanish.

## f. 210r

- the cipher sent
- u[...]
- of receiving some help from these parts to bring back wretched England to the bosom of the holy Catholic Church
- to delay the business
- at the end of twenty years
- every intention of attempting something with forces from these parts
- is now cooled
- until that of Flanders is finished, the Queen of England dead, or some other change
- in that kingdom
- more despatch
- to open the purse wide for the contribution to the expenses
- should be despatched promptly at the time set
- His Majesty the Pope [sic]
- let them take up this action, and more matter from here

- will greatly increase the difficulties and expenses of the reformation of those kingdoms, by the multitude of the
