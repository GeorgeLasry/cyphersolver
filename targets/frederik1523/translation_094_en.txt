# Duke Frederik to the council of Lübeck, Gottorp, 15 February 1523 (Hanserecesse III/8 n. 292). Coded names in CAPITALS.

Our gracious greeting first. Honourable, wise, dear particular friends.
Following the agreement last made with you, we have negotiated further with JUTLAND, which [JUTLAND] had set a time
for the meeting with DUKE FREDERIK on St Matthias' day (24 February) at HADERSLEV; DUKE FREDERIK has had it put off
until Reminiscere (1 March), because DUKE FREDERIK's TROOPS are not yet assembled. Since the KING is so strong in
HORSEMEN and FOOT SOLDIERS that DUKE FREDERIK cannot yet go into JUTLAND, DUKE FREDERIK asks most kindly that
LÜBECK will certainly have sent the agreed number of HORSEMEN and FOOT SOLDIERS by Sunday Invocavit (22 February),
to LÜBECK or into the LAND OF HOLSTEIN, without any neglect or delay, so that they can come to DUKE FREDERIK when
DUKE FREDERIK calls for them, to carry out the agreed undertaking.
Enclosed new tidings have been sent to DUKE FREDERIK, from which it can be seen that LÜBECK's HORSEMEN and FOOT
SOLDIERS, together with SWEDEN, have not yet done the KING much damage. DUKE FREDERIK therefore asks that LÜBECK
urge its HORSEMEN and FOOT SOLDIERS and SWEDEN as quickly as possible to set about it in earnest, and, where needed
and convenient, to use them against ZEALAND, FUNEN, COPENHAGEN, SCANIA and elsewhere, to take from the KING.
DUKE FREDERIK has also received true tidings from the BISHOP OF BREMEN, whose embassy is now with DUKE FREDERIK,
and tidings from HAMBURG, that in the COUNTY OF OLDENBURG up to 4000 FOOT SOLDIERS are actually at hand, meaning
to be of help to the KING and to do LÜBECK and DUKE FREDERIK harm, by which DUKE FREDERIK would be much hindered in
his enterprise. DUKE FREDERIK has therefore told the embassy to ask the BISHOP OF BREMEN to prevent FOOT SOLDIERS or
HORSEMEN from passing through his territories, and DUKE FREDERIK has assured the BISHOP OF BREMEN on that ...
[continues on image 095: that Lübeck would raise his yearly pension]
