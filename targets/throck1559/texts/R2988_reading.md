# R2988: Nicholas Throckmorton to Elizabeth I, Paris, 10 July 1559 (BL Add MS 4136 f. 32), partial reading

Re-read 23 Oct 2026 (Claude Opus 5.5, REREAD_BRIEF batch b36); log `R2988_reread_log.md`. **A partial reading, not a
reading**: the sentences build, but 30 of the 496 letters of lines 5-15 (6%) rest on the other member of a look-alike
pair (ρ/℘ t/d, 7/¬ r/g, μ/M h/b, x/X i/y, ⊥/┘ o/a ...), above the brief's 3%, and there is no contemporary or printed
text to confirm them. Lines 1-4 follow `../main_decrypt.txt` pass 2; lines 5-15 rest on the new sign transcription
`R2988_signs.tsv`, decoded by `research/reading_audit/b36_throck.py` with Tomokiyo's Cipher 1 key (DECODE R2988 key
image) and its code list, which the September passes had not used (*have*, *had*, *as*, *so*, *whom*, *upon*, *at*,
*all*, *this*, *in*, *be*, *your Majesty*).

Conventions: plain = carried by the figures and the key; `[?word]` = a code read from sense only, or a word with a
sign not matched; `[word]` supplied; `[…]` unread; `[k̲]` = an unidentified code sign. Spelling as the figures give it.

## Reading

It may please your Majesty to be advertised that, whereas I sent [?letters] to the same by maister Gray of the eight of
this moneth at five of the clock in the afternoone, and […] Carew [?letters] of the same date written at midnight
touching the French King's state, […] I iudge your Majesty will be desirous to be further enformed thereof. […] then
[…] it […] conuenient to put in to writing the [?hoole] [?discours] of [1̲] [k̲], as I wish your Majesty to have
knowledge of, considering the present [?time], have I iudged it best to dispatche this gentelman, to [?whom] [?I]
enforme the same of the state of all [r̲] here at [?good] lenght, to whom it may [?please] your Majesty to giue credit.
He [?hath] since [?his] coming on [?this] side done your Majesty diuerse and sundry wais so [?good] and [?painfull]
seruice, as he [?hath] deserud to be well considered and rewarded, beseching your Majesty to be [?so] much [?his]
[?good] and gracious lady, as he may perceiue [?his] seruice [?hath] bin acceptable, wherof I have had [?good]
experience, as I shal be able to declare more plainli and particularly to your Majesty when it shall please the same to
command me to attend upon your Majesty. And [v̲] I beseche [?God] to preserue your Majesty in health, welth and all
felicite. […] Paris the tenth of [?Julii].

## Notes

- Code values new in b36, from sense: q̄ *hath* (three contexts: "he hath since", "as he hath deserued", "hath bin
  acceptable"; Tomokiyo's table has ꝗ̄ *both*, which fits none of them); ō *good* (four: "at good lenght", "so good and
  painfull", "much his good and gracious", "I have had good experience"); ġ *letters* (two, lines 1 and 3; Tomokiyo's
  table has *long* / *hundred*); π̄ *God* (one: "I beseche God to preserue"); ῡ *his* (one); б̄ read *his* twice in l. 11
  where the key's б̄ is *have* (look-alike with the key's v̄ *his*); ϱ̲ *time* (the key's e̲); V *I*.
- Open codes: 1̲ k̲ (l. 6, the subject of the discourse), ť (l. 5), r̲ (l. 8; the key's *then* does not fit "of all
  then here"), v̲ (l. 14), ℓ̄ (l. 15, before Paris), the end of l. 4 (`i б υ`) and the sign after *and* in l. 2 (`L T 8`).
- "to [?whom]" (l. 7): the figures give *who*, with a 4 corrected to 3 before it.
- The starred insertion after *plainli* (l. 13) keys to the margin note in the separate word code (throck1559margin).
