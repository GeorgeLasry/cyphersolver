# Spagna 423/5 (DECODE R182), Rome, 22 September 1736: Secretariat of State to Abbé Guiccioli, Madrid. Reading

Rests on: (1) the contemporary clear copy of this letter, headed "Copia. Roma 22. Settembre 1736", bound as f. 375r
and attached to DECODE R182 as the "cleartext" png document DOC_R182_D1442 (transcribed in texts/423-5_copy.txt,
23 Oct 2026); (2) the DECODE transcription (decode/keep/DOC_R182_D1620_1620.txt) re-segmented and decoded with
Lasry's key as corrected and the nomenclator of key_extra.txt and texts/b04_key_additions.txt, where the copy fixes
1236 = quando and 1201 = ancora (decode: texts/423-5_decode_b04.txt), checked on the DECODE image IMG_R182_I1171_P1.
Copy and decode aligned token by token (research/reading_audit/r2d_align.py; texts/423-5_r2d_align.tsv): 177 non-null
tokens, 174 agree. The date, missing from the cipher page, is the copy's. Word division, u/v, accents and apostrophes
normalised. Marks: [x] = word supplied, carried by no figure and not in the copy.

## Reading

Non si dovrebbe mai temere che, dopo tanti attentati, giungesse codesta Corte ad un nuovo atto di ostilità con
l'espulsione di V.S.

Ma per pensare ai casi ancora più strani, quando mai ciò seguisse, dovrà ella chieder tempo per darne avviso a N.S., e
quando non le venisse accordato dopo una replicata istanza, le sarà duopo di cedere.

## Notes on the figures

- "giungesse": the page has 33 (h) for g, a dot under it on the image; "duopo": 68 (r) for o. Both slips of the
  cipherer; the copy has "giungesse" and "duopo".
- "a N.S.": the copy writes "a Sua Santità".
- A lone figure 2 after "V.S." and a closing "?" in the transcription carry nothing.
