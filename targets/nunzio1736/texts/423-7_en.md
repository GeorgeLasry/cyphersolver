… the continual injuries that that junta on the patronage inflicts on the rights of the Holy See and on ecclesiastical
liberty, which the same has [?always] had from time immemorial of conferring the [1449] [?benefices], and the bishops
[?also] in their respective [1942]s, and the many other novelties being planned against the Dataria and the faculties of
that Nunciature in the other junta newly set up, have obliged my solicitude [of] His Holiness to think seriously of
building a dam against a torrent that threatens ruin to the authority of this Holy See and to the [1449] power in those
kingdoms.
Before taking even the most rigorous remedies, he has chosen to use his fatherly gentleness, praying and exhorting that
King by a Brief, of which a copy is sent to Your Lordship, to have the course of the said undertakings suspended; and at
the same time he has written another to all the bishops of those kingdoms, in the terms of the enclosed copy.
The news is given to Your Lordship so that you may be informed of the steps taken here and, hearing them spoken of, may
make it known that His Holiness is not [1698] to dispense with giving them without grave injury to his conscience.
To the [?end] [1232] the Brief should reach [?His Majesty]'s hands safely, it has been sent to the father [?confessor] of
the same, charging him to report its content to him; and two other Briefs have been sent to the same religious, one for
the [?Queen] and the other for Sig. [..] in [1469], praying them to support with their [?good offices] the just requests
of His Holiness.
