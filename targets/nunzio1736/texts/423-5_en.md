# Spagna 423/5 (DECODE R182), Rome, 22 September 1736. English translation of 423-5_reading.md

One should never have to fear that, after so many outrages, that Court would go so far as a new act of hostility by
expelling Your Lordship.
But to provide for even the strangest cases: should this ever happen, you are to ask for time to inform His Holiness;
and should it not be granted to you after a repeated request, you will have to give way.
