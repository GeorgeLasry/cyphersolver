# Spagna 423/5 (DECODE R182), Rome, September 1736. English translation of 423-5_reading.md

One should never have had to fear that, after so many outrages, that Court would [?come] to a new act of hostility by
expelling Your Lordship.
But to provide for [?even] the strangest cases: [?if] this ever happened, you [?will] ask for time to give notice of it
to His Holiness; and [?if] it were not granted to you after a repeated request, you will have to give way.
