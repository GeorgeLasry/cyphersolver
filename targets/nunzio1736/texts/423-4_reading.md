## Notes on the figures

- "Mons. [1513]a[?Consiglio]": the same group string (1596 1513 a 1382) recurs in 423/1, 423/9 and 423/11 for the
  prelate who is Molina (Gaspar de Molina, Bishop of Málaga, Governor of the Council of Castile); 1382 alone is
  "consigli" in "più sani [?consigli]i". 1513 is not valued.
- "In tale stato [..]": two figures unread in the transcription (<??>).
- "a lo scorso": the figures give a lo, not dello.
- "[?occasioni]": 1368 followed by e; the value fits both its contexts but is not fixed.
