# Spagna 423/6 (DECODE R183), Rome, 29 September 1736. English translation of 423-6_reading.md

Let Your Lordship take care to find out, discreetly and with full certainty, whether that Court is disposed to send an
ambassador of its own to Portugal, and in that case with what instructions he will be charged concerning the visit to that
Secretary of State for [?foreign] affairs, above all under the new regulation established there of having divided
into three Secretaries the charges formerly united in Don Diego de Mendoza alone, who, as you know, was visited
beforehand by the Marquis de los Balbases: an example that that Court has used on other occasions to insist that the
Nuncio too should visit the said Don Diego beforehand.
Such news may give much light to my decisions, when, should the case arise, this claim were put forward, although it
would seem now much stranger after such a new regulation.
