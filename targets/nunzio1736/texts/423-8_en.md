# Spagna 423/8 (DECODE R185), Rome, autumn 1736. English translation of 423-8_reading.md

… to the printed copies, straight by the post.
Of all these things Your Lordship is not to let it be seen that you know, except in [1636] of [?answering]; it will however be
needful that you inform Mons. the Bishop of Ávila of everything and pass him the copies of the said Briefs; and that you take care,
also discreetly, to find out whether the Briefs sent to the Bishops have reached them, and what effects they have
produced in the minds of the same, of that Court and of the people, informing me in detail of all that concerns this
most important [?business].
