# Spagna 423/4 (DECODE R181), Rome, 4 August 1736. English translation of 423-4_reading.md

Same marks as the reading: [x] supplied, [?x] partly carried, [1668] unvalued group, [..] unread.

The situation of our dealings with the Courts of Spain and of Naples cannot remain more doubtful and grievous, and there
seems to be no room to hope for any good so long as ministers of the character of that Mons. the Governor of the
Council have influence in affairs, and so long as our own ministers, who could set things in their true light, stay
away.
In such a state [..] the only remedy can and must be hoped from God, who will enlighten His Holiness in taking the most
fitting decisions and will move the hearts of the princes to lend their ear to sounder counsels.
His Holiness has heard, with an increase of the greatest bitterness, all that is contained in your letter of the 7th
of last month, which arrived only yesterday.
Continue you too to employ all your possible diligence in these most thorny junctures.
