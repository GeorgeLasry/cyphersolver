# Smallwood to Washington, James Mechlans Tavern [Chester County, Pa.], 19 Sept 1777
# Text as printed in Papers of George Washington, Rev. War Ser. 11:272 (Founders Online 03-11-02-0272),
# from the ALS (owned 1994 by Joseph Rubinfine) and the Sprague transcript (DLC:GW). Not seen in manuscript.
# The five marks [*] [§] [§§] [†] [§] stand where the ALS has erased words. Line breaks are the edition's sentences.

James Mechlans Tavern [Pa.] September 19th 1777
Sir,
Last Night Colonel Gist joined me he has [*] Troops—our united Force amounts to [§].
I am now on my march and rest assured every Effort shall be exerted to [§§]—as expeditiously as possible which I hope to effect Tomorrow—
the Shameful neglect in the Commissarys Department throws many Obstacles in my way and retards my March—I am reduced to the Necessity of sending Officers forward to provide for the Troops which has been the case ever since I set out notwithstanding I have acquainted Mr Buchannan with this Defect I cannot obtain any Remedy—
once more allow me to assure you that my utmost endeavours shall be exerted to comply with your Requisitions of yesterday—
I have—[†] this County Lieut. and others of Integrity with me but cannot ascertain my Route so precisely as to point it out.
my Baggage I shall send by [§]—but believe I shall fall more down to the Right with the troops.
in much haste I have the honor to be yr Excellency's most Obedt Sert
W. Smallwood

# Mark inventory (5 tokens, 4 distinct marks)
# pos 1  [*]   "he has ___ Troops"                 a number (Gist's strength)
# pos 2  [§]   "our united Force amounts to ___"   a number (combined strength)
# pos 3  [§§]  "exerted to ___ as expeditiously"   a verb phrase: the junction with the main army
# pos 4  [†]   "I have—___ this County Lieut."     a verb or participle (engaged? got?)
# pos 5  [§]   "my Baggage I shall send by ___"    a route, place or person
