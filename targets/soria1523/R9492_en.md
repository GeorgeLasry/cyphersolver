# Alonso Sánchez [de Soria] to Charles V, Genoa, July 1523 (R9492-R9493; key A body, key B closing): English translation

From Lyon they write on the twenty-third of last month that they expected that the King of France would come here very soon to cross into Italy; but so far men of war were not being gathered so that they could cross so soon with an army; it might be that they thought of crossing with the men-at-arms alone, in the hope that the [qed] would come down, and in the hope of the Venetians, and having thought to make use of the five thousand infantry he has in Provence to put in the fleet at sea. And I have word from Brescia that from here [an] [fec] [?that] [?the] [rip] are carrying two hundred bales of corselets [Qo] [Locia], the land of the Grisons, and I think they are to arm the [qed]; and I believe that the said [rip] will use with [pur] the dealings that Ottaviano Campofregoso used when the general league was made and this King of France was crossing into Italy, who one time told us he wanted to enter the league, and at other times gave words to delay until the said King had crossed, and then declared himself our enemy. And so I think the [rip] will do: they will delay the agreement with [pur] until they see what the King of France will do; and if they see that he has the means to come or to send an army into Italy, they will be his friends and will make no agreement with [pur]. And since it is to be this way, it would be good to begin the war on them at once, because two good effects would follow from it: the one, that the State of Milan would be relieved of quartering the army, and it would be better lodged in the Bergamasco and Bresciano; and the other, that Bergamo would at once be taken, from where they could draw money for some pay for the infantry; and it would be no miracle if Brescia or Verona were soon taken, and so much more money had for the army's expenses. [Key B:] Monsieur de Beaurain has arrived here, and since Your Majesty has been informed by him of everything else that happens, as the said Beaurain wished to embark on this brigantine, I advised him not to go [pu2] except in a good carrack, because, the galleys of France and other brigantines being along this coast [E], they could easily come upon him and take him, which they will not do with the carrack; and for this we have thought that this brigantine should leave with Lorenzo Mormino, and he will leave here in three days with a carrack.
