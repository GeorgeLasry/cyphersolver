# Alonso Sánchez [de Soria] to Charles V, Genoa, June 1523 (R9488-R9490, key A): English translation

[p] Likewise they write from Milan that they had news that a secretary of the Duke of Milan had arrived in Venice, who had brought the Infante's mandate to conclude the league with the Venetians, and that for this the Duke of Milan had promised the said Infante eighteen thousand ducats, and that with this they held it certain that the said league would be concluded; and for this the protonotary Caracciolo was leaving with diligence to go to Venice. And likewise they write from Milan that, the infantry having begun to mutiny, from [dus] they had been sent a pay, and that with this they thought to quiet them for now; and to finish paying them what is owed them, the Duke of Milan and the lord Prospero have written to the Duke of [cop] asking him to make an effort to help with some sum of money; and he answered that he cannot give money, but that he will take five hundred infantry of those of the army to be here, and will pay them for a month; and it seems to them that with this he relieves the army of expense, and guards this city better, and takes away the habit, from those who have charge on behalf of [dus], of asking him for money, and from him of giving it; so that no one attends to anything but his own interest …
