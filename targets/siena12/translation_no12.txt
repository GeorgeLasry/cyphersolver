# No. 12 (R4790): George Lasry's translation, verbatim from his report of 17 Oct 2026 (p. 12)

Letter
Magnificent captain, know(?) that Carlo di Ludovico da Cesena has come to Naples, and he has told me that the
lord King is content to make a marriage alliance with you. But of the two girls(?) of [?] [?] [?] nothing(?) seems
possible, because the one who was promised ? to the brother of messer Ludovico da Fermo cannot be undone;
as for the marriage, her sister ? is perhaps ten years old, so she would not be suitable(?).
Now it is new(?), but the lord King says that he has [?] two beautiful girls of his house: one is the daughter of
Ludovico di messer Gianne de' Malatesti; she is very beautiful and legitimate, eighteen years old. The other is a
niece of the lord King on the side of [?]; she is the daughter of Niccolò Filippo(?) di Brancaleoni of Castel
Durante: [she is] very beautiful and legitimate. The lord King wants to give you whichever of the two you like,
provided that Your Lordship(?) has her asked for.
Now that is all. This I have said to Your Lordship(?). Now it rests with you: if you write to me, I shall do what you
tell me. Now it rests with you. I for my part advise and beg you to it. I have already written it to you three
times. Given at Naples on the tenth day of March, sixth indiction. Vescovi(?) de Fermo, wholly yours in soul and
in body.
Address
To the [stre]nuous man Sforza, [magn]ificent captain, lord ... let it be delivered : [?] lord [?].
