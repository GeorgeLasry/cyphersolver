# R4157, Catherine de Médicis to Villeroy, Chenonceau, 7 February 1581: the cipher block, English translation

[… it being said over here that he] is to go to Flanders to receive the oath of fidelity of those of the Low
Countries for my son, and that he is to stay there; and that the prince de Condé is to lead the forces that are
assembling and do all they can for the service of my said son, who also says that he is to go there at the time you
wrote to us and not before. But we shall not fail to name him, with the said sieur de Montpensier, in the said power
of the legation to [?England].
