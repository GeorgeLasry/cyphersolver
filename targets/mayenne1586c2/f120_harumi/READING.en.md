# Reading (cipher of BnF fr. 15573 f. 120r–v)

[日本語](READING.md)

- The spelling is kept close to the 16th-century spelling as decoded (u and v, i and j are not distinguished). Punctuation and capitals are added for readability.
- [ ] marks added letters or words. [?] is a sign with no value. [?word] is a reading guessed from form or context and not checked. [escus] is the three-loop sign (ooo) read as escus by inference (it fits all three places after mil). [∞] is the two-loop sign (oo), with no value.
- The line-by-line mechanical decoding is in `data/decoded_*.txt`, the sign-by-sign transcription in `data/transcription_*.txt`. Line numbers (c01 etc.) are those of those files.
- Based on the first transcription (`data/`, corrected in seven places after looking at differences with the transcriptions used for checking on the image). No person has checked the transcription. Details may be wrong ([METHOD.en.md](METHOD.en.md)). Word divisions were added afterwards to the decoded letters.
