## Notes

- `V.` stands alone for *Vostra Signoria* (lines 1, 6, 7: *adviso V.*, *che V. per altra via*, *ne adviso V. a la quale*).
- Sense hypotheses, **not adopted** (each sign class occurs once or twice in f. 70 and nowhere in f. 68 with a
  context that tests it; flagged for review): (25) = *Duca* (*il Duca se move ad fare contra Cremona*; Filippo Maria
  Visconti moved against Sforza's Cremona in 1446); (19) = *ordine* (*da ordine ad tute le sue*); (26) = *gente*
  (*le sue gente*); *se mono* = *se move*.
- `#25`, `(4)`, `(6)` stand in clusters at openings and closings in both letters (f. 70 *me raconmando #25 (6) (4) data*;
  f. 68 *#35 (6) (4) ... ad la parte*), consistent with nulls or a closing formula; not adopted (f. 68 l. 10 *n #25
  intita* is undecided).
- The signature: no assignment of the open classes makes *Vincentius de Amidanis* or any other name of the
  sender; Mazzatinti's attribution is not confirmed by the cipher.
