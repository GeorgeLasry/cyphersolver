# Paolo Rosson's reading (contributed)

Copied 20 Oct 2026 from https://github.com/pangoleen/desportes-1593 (commit 33c854a, 6 Oct 2026; the reading is
v0.2 of 1 Oct 2026). Text licensed CC BY 4.0. Credit line requested by the author:

> Paolo Rosson with Claude (Anthropic), "Two cipher letters of July 1593, read for the first time", 2026,
> https://github.com/pangoleen/desportes-1593

- `f189_reading.txt`: BnF fr. 3984 f. 189, Desportes to Girolamo Frachetta, full reading and translation.
- `f186_reading.txt`: BnF fr. 3984 f. 186r-v, Desportes to Pietro Aldobrandini, cipher passages, reading and translation.
- `fr4715_f61_reading.txt`: BnF fr. 4715 f. 61r, a slip in a variant of the same cipher (not checked here).
- `comparison.md`: what the two letters add to the two that the royal office deciphered in 1593.
- `accuracy.md`: his held-out and blind-test figures.
- `raw/dump_*.txt`: the machine output, each word with its sign-class string (A = a/n, B = b/o ... M = m/z;
  Q que, K qui, P pour, R par, & et, # other sign; parentheses = second choice).

The images and code of his repository are not copied. Our checks are in `../NOTES.md`.
