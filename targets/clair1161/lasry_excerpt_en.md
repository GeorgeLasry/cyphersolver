# BnF Clairambault 1161, unidentified sender and recipient (1554/1555 a hypothesis): English translation of Lasry's excerpt

Translation (Claude Opus 5.5, 23 Oct 2026) of `lasry_excerpt_reading.md`, **an excerpt only** of the letter, the passages George Lasry printed in his BnF cipher research report (f. 163r, a complete eight-line cipher paragraph); the rest of the letter is not in the repository. Same holes: [?] unread, [x] a word he supplied. His own translation is in `lasry_entry.md`.

## Translation

This lord has twice written and sent word to this queen that she should let many things in the matter of religion in this kingdom pass in silence and dissimulation, so as to win back her nobility and people, and so that her son and she may reign and command there in greater quiet.
