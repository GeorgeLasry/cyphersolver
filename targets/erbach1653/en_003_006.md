# Letters No. 1 and No. 2 (1666): English translation

## No. 1, Fürstenau, 22 March 1666

"... so without an order or occasion given I may not venture on it in future, which Your Highness will graciously
forgive me. That otherwise Your Highness [...] has been pleased to remember our plan and certain changes, of which
nothing has been reported, gives me occasion to [tell] you of the changes that have happened here in the meantime,
and what I ..."

## No. 2, 27 April [1666]

"... [I have] reported by a letter, in the hope that Your Highness will think my reasons weighty enough and not
accuse me of [fickle]ness and inconstancy, since in May or June, God willing, our division of property is to go
ahead ..." (the division of point 7 of the eight-reason letter, which this "letter" with "my reasons" very likely is).
