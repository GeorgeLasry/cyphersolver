# R9417, Hieronymus Łaski to the Bavarian secretary Waisenfelder, Kraków, 16 June [1530] (English)

## Translation

Dearest Herr Waisenfelder, I have your letter dated the twelfth of this month, in which (he) wished to [?excuse] his most illustrious princes and himself for neglect in the matter of the army that besieged [?King] [King John] at Buda; in which he also urges that concord cannot be brought about more conveniently than through certain princes of the Empire, to whom your lords too should be joined. On this matter I have already written to [King John]; but because Lord Nicolaus Min[?k]uics is now being sent to the King of France and to others, it was necessary to send the bearer of the present letter, [?a Jew], to [?you].

First, [King John] wishes [...] the princes to be content to stay at peace when the Duke [?of Württemberg] [?strives] to recover his dominion, and he asks this earnestly.

The Emperor of the Turks has been persuaded, by my great labours, to a truce of a year; he is ready [sic: "paratur"] to take into his friendship and alliance all whom [King John] names and commends to him; and unless there is concord this year, he will come out next summer with unspeakable power by land and sea, which [...] I will write at another time. Yet your princes need not fear, for we will take care in time.

It would be most necessary that you be appointed to the fourth day of July at Coburg in Saxony, for Min[?k]uics will certainly be there. That he could not meet you earlier as he had written was not his fault: the roads were so beset that he could in no way get through. But it is necessary that he go.

About the King of Poland's daughter, I wonder that nothing is being done, since the King of Poland had already declared he would give her, and I suspect some ill will, because this business begins so coldly. So [...] it must be: let something be done quickly, for [owl] too is now soliciting, through Sigismund Herberstein, who is here, that she be given to Frederick the Count Palatine. So quick action is needed, lest another snatch away the best and richest prize, etc.

Your princes, together with others, ought to send to Charles [?the Emperor] so that he holds back his brother from attacking [King John], because the King of Poland, the King of France and the other Christian princes will send. Finally, I wish to be most warmly commended to your princes, to whom I offer myself and all I have, etc. Kraków, the sixteenth day of June. Łask[i]. Written in his own hand.
