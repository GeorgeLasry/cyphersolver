## Translation

You will see, Monseigneur, from the news that Monsieur de Launay sends you, the state of the King of Navarre's army. The legates will come too late. I shall be here, from where I shall not stir without your advice and command. As soon as we have more news we shall not fail to send it to you; and with that I pray God, Monseigneur, that He give you a happy and long life in health. This 5th of November 1589. Your very humble and devoted servant, Dampierre.
