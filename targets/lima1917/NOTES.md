# German Minister at Lima (Perl) cipher telegrams, May 1917 - Jan 1918 (Unsolved Catalogue no. 658)

Status: in progress

Blocked on images: no ciphertext is available. Nothing below rests on a sight of a telegram.

## The document

- NARA RG 59, Central Decimal File 1910-29, 862.20223 (German military activities in Peru), sub-nos. 4-99.
- Known only from the purport list (M973 roll 207 frames 1008-1014, naId 87662081): about 70 cables from
  Callao-Lima, each carrying 1-7 "telegrams in German cipher" (June 1917 - Jan 1918); Lima despatch 342 of 6 June 1917
  (copies of cipher code messages exchanged between German diplomatic officers); relays "for Bell from Harrison" to London.
- London (Room 40 via the US Embassy) cabled back the substance of most batches; Lima 85 (9 Oct 1917) says some
  could not be deciphered.
