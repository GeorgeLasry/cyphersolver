## P1 (clear close of the letter of 1 February 1620; outline only)

The Tatars/Turks and a tribute or war with Poland ("to the payment of a yearly tribute, or to fall into open war with
this mighty monarchy"); talks with the Emperor's ambassador over important fortresses in Hungary. The Turks promise
help to Bethlen, Prince of Transylvania, and to Hungary. The ambassador of the Hungarian Estates had no audience with
His Imperial Majesty's [the Sultan's] representatives, "mainly because no one has any advantage from the Turks".
Closing formula, then signature.
