## 0413 | 17 July 1916 | Tokyo → Shanghai (中華新報) → Yunnan | JACAR B03050090000 | sent 4.30 pm

In order to create an aviation corps, buy four military aircraft of the latest type and engage the aviator Suzuki Shigeru as instructor; funds to be raised in Tokyo. Do you approve? Reply quickly. — Shen. The 17th.

## 0416a | 19 July 1916 | Tokyo → Shanghai (中華新報, 轉文羣轉滇督) | JACAR B03050090000 | sent 5.50 pm

The ten thousand rifles requested by Yunnan can be obtained; but as the newspapers report the abolition of the Military Affairs Council, [Japanese] policy has suddenly changed. Is it true? Tell me quickly, so that I know whether to act or stop. — Shen. The 19th.

## 0449 | 8 September 1916 | Tokyo → Kunming (唐都督) | JACAR B03050090100 | sent 7 pm

Shen is ordering for Yunnan six military aircraft of the latest type and eight wireless sets. Please telegraph Cen to forward to the General Staff, to avoid any obstacle; the weapons matter is under negotiation. I have also been informed. The 8th.

## 0455a | 13 September 1916 | Tokyo → Kunming (唐都督) | JACAR B03050090100 | sent 7.30 pm

Telegram of the 11th received. The matter of the weapons and aircraft has not yet been concluded; it is difficult for me to return right away. As for the assembly, have the vice-president stand in according to the rules; do not go. — Shen. The 13th.

## 0467 | 11 October 1916 | Tokyo → Kunming (唐都督) | JACAR B03050090100 | sent 5.46 pm

The commemorative medals, each with its leather (?) case, cost 9,500 yuan as agreed; please remit the funds. Details will follow by letter through Mr Jia [?]. The Terauchi cabinet has just been formed; the weapons matter has a chance and will be settled shortly; I will return at once. — Shen. The 10th.

## 0473 | 20 October 1916 | Tokyo → Kunming (唐督軍) | JACAR B03050090100 | sent 7.30 pm

Loan: on the basis of bonds […], amount one million, interest 8 per cent, repayment in one year (?), without guarantee, issued at 94. If the loan goes ahead, instruct Shen to sign the contract and hand it over; reply quickly. The weapons require the approval of the central government; only then will the authorities agree to deliver them. For information. Reply by telegram; please deliver directly (?) to Shen (伸), Aoyama Minami-chō, Akasaka. […]

## 0489a | 5 November 1916 | Tokyo → Kunming (唐督軍) | JACAR B03050090100 | sent 11.55 am

I plan to return south on the 10th. Respectfully. — Shen. The 5th.

## 0496a | 9 November 1916 | Tokyo → Kunming (唐督軍) | JACAR B03050090100 | sent 6.25 pm

Forward to the provincial assembly and the newspaper offices (報館 ?): Lord Cai [Cai E] died of illness at Fukuoka on the 8th, at two in the morning; his merits are as high as a mountain. It must be announced throughout Yunnan, with sincere mourning, and the arrangements and commemoration carefully prepared. — Shen. [The 9th?]

## 0498 | 9 November 1916 | Kunming → Tokyo (趙伸 c/o 関正雄) | JACAR B03050090100 | sent 2.35 pm

(From Yunnan to Zhao Shen.) Your telegrams of the 30th and the 1st received. Thank you for negotiating the loan with care […]. A few points remain […]: 1. amount: two to three million [yuan]; 2. annual interest: 6 to 7 per cent; 3. repayment: over at least three years; 4. discount: at most to 98. On these four points, please negotiate further. Yunnan urgently needs money and is very keen for the matter to succeed. If the amount, repayment and discount can be accepted, the annual interest can be eased a little further. What is the outcome? Reply.

## 0494a | 11 November 1916 | Tokyo → Kunming (財政廳) | JACAR B03050090100 | sent 8.35 pm

The war in Europe goes on: even [Japanese] domestic loans are not placed at 95. If it is so even for Russia, all the more for Yunnan, without collateral: it should be somewhat more expensive. The rest can be negotiated, but this point is difficult; I plan to return to discuss it. Reply at once. — Shen. The 11th.
