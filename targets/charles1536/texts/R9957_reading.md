# R9957 — Charles V to the Empress Isabella, Borgo San Donnino, 17 May 1536 (AGS Estado leg. 496, ff. 270-274): the cipher passages

Rests on: (1) for the cipher of ff. 270r-273v (archive stamps 0001-0014), the figures, read run by run on the DECODE
images at 1.6-2.4x native resolution under the key AGS Estado leg. 1.1.1 doc. 169 as reproduced by Luo (2021, pp.
603-606; summarised in `R9957_key169.md`); the sign transcriptions, decodes and per-run notes are in
`R9957_runs_A.md` (stamps 0001-0006), `R9957_runs_B.md` (0007-0011) and `R9957_runs_C.md` (0012-0014). (2) For the
cipher of ff. 273v-274r (stamps 0016-0017), the contemporary decipherment written below the date and signature on
stamp 0017 and at the head of 0018, transcribed here (`R9957_decifrado_f274.txt`). Wanruo Luo's edition (thesis,
Universitat de València 2021, pp. 287-290) was used only as a check; where the figures and her text differ the figures
are followed (each place is in the run files). Her edition is in copyright and is cited, not copied.
Each paragraph is one cipher run. The clear words around it, transcribed from the same image, are in round brackets
so that the sentence can be followed; the full clear text of the letter is not part of this reading. Spelling is the
figures' (rreynos, rresolutos, deura, aquela, seyscientos, mill); doubled-letter and code signs are written out.
Marks: [?word] partly carried, [word] supplied, […] unread, [24] a figure with no value in the sentence.

### ff. 273v-274r: the last cipher passage, as deciphered on the leaf below the date and signature (stamps 0017-0018)

Teniendo mas de los alemanes que ya estan en mio canpo con Antonio y de los otros que avemos proveydo que abaxen a
cunplimiento de xxv o xxx U honbres, como en la [?otra] que esta dicho, para hazer por desta parte de Ytalia con estos
y con los españoles y con la otra gente y provisiones que se juntan todo lo que se pudiere contra los enemigos,
apercebido en Alemaña mayor numero de gente, y siendo neçessario entretenerla y servirnos della para no dexarles
ocasion que, estando como estan apercebidos, no nos sirviendo dellos vayan a servir al Rey de Françia; y que hazer
baxar mayor numero de xxx U alemanes en Ytalia seria confusion, y que ninguna cosa puede ser mas provechosa que
divertir las fuerças del enemigo y darle trabajo en muchas partes; y teniendo para este [?efecto] […] en Alemaña hasta
cien mill ducados y otros tantos en Flandes, de los quales para lo de aca no nos podriamos bien servir, avemos acordado
que se hagan y junten los dichos alemanes que, demas de los que an de baxar a Ytalia, tenemos como dicho es
apercebidos, y que con estos y con lo que para este efecto avemos proveydo que se aderesce en Flandes, para lo qual,
siendo venido de alla nuestro mayordomo mayor, lo avemos tornado a enbiar a solicitar y poner en orden lo que sera
menester, y tanbien para que se procure de aver de Alemaña alguna ayuda de gente como otras vezes se nos ha
offresçido; y pudiendose hazer a tiempo que ayude a esto, se junte con lo demas, y si no, con los dichos alemanes [y]
lo demas de Flandes el conde de Nasa[?u] y el dicho nuestro mayordomo entren en Françia por la parte de Lucenburg
hazia Mason y haga el mayor daño que pudiere; que esto en tiempo, juntamente con lo [que] por esta parte de Ytalia y
por la mar se hara, no sera de poco provecho para apretar y danificar al enemigo y ponerle en mayor necesidad, etc.

## Notes

- The letter answers the Empress's letters of February to April 1536 on the Algiers fleet, munitions, the frontier
  garrisons (Perpignan, Salses, Navarre, Pamplona), Andrea Doria's galleys, and above all money for the war with
  France: 400,000 ducats to Barcelona, the Peruvian gold to be coined in Seville, loans from the Fuggers, Welsers and
  Ansaldo de Grimaldo, sales of juros and vassals. The last passage sets out the plan of the 1536 campaign: 25-30,000
  Germans to Italy, more held ready in Germany, and the Count of Nassau with "nuestro mayordomo mayor" to enter France
  from the Luxembourg side towards Mouzon ("Mason").
- Runs with holes: run 1 "se [?gra…] e" (82 r ꝝ, no word) and a tear after "bien de"; B16 (stamp 0009, about 14 signs
  that give no sentence; Luo prints no cipher there); B25 and B28 (stamp 0011, two dense blocks partly carried); C2
  (stamp 0013, faded fold, two figures [43], [6] with no value, a gap where Luo also has one). "di[?ne]ro": the figure
  is 52 (na) on stamps 0005-0006 and 53 (ne) on 0007-0014; not adopted as a key change.
- Figures that differ from Luo's text (followed here): "En lo de los cinco mil" (her "En los"), "apercebimientos",
  "que le scrivimos", "aquella frontera", "deura", "pongo", "aquela", "toda las", "asientos de trezientos", "asienten",
  "seyscientos", "haver haver havido", "me embien", "pl[ata]"; and "Gonçalo de Ayora" (stamp 0005) is cipher that she
  prints as clear.
- The f. 274r decipherment: "con Antonio" (the figures have the code 97, Antonio de Leyva); "[?otra]": a short word
  after "en la"; "[?efecto] […]": the end of the line after "este" is cramped; "[y]" after "alemanes" is a stroke;
  "Nasa[?u]": the decipherer wrote "nasaot" or "nasau"; "[que]" after "con lo" is under a blot. The cipher on stamps
  0016-0017 was not re-read sign by sign; the code 97 (Antonio de Leyva) stands where the decipherment has "Antonio".
