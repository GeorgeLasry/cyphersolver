# Key of R9957: AGS Estado leg. 1.1.1 doc. 169 (Isabella of Portugal - Charles V, 1535-1538)

Source: Wanruo Luo, *El lenguaje cifrado de Isabel de Portugal (1530-1539)*, thesis, Universitat de València 2021,
pp. 603-606 (PDF pp. 611-614), where the key is reproduced as images of the original table. Summarised here for use on
the figures of R9957 (b18, 22-23 Oct 2026); sign names are ours. Read from her images at 230-260 dpi; where her table
is blurred the value is marked (?). The key itself is a 1530s document; this is a description of it, not a copy of
Luo's edition.

## Nulls

The key's note: every sign with a dot over it, and every number from 212 up, is a null.

## Calibration on R9957 f. 1r (image P1), last cipher line

`63 82 L+(?) 7+(?) a a+ ŧ̲ 53 9̲ 58 55 63 U# a 1 a̲` = se gra ge(?) [?] e de ma ne ra que no se pi e r da: "se grage e
de manera que no se pierda" (Luo p. 288 reads "se grage e de manera"). A plain `a` is the letter e; `a̲` (underlined)
is da: the underline must be looked for on every a.
