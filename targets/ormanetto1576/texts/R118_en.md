# Spain nunciature, 15 October [1576 or 1577], ASV Spagna 10 f. 365 (DECODE R118): English translation

Translation (Claude Opus 5.5, 23 Oct 2026) of the Italian reading in `texts/R118_decryption.md` (code groups resolved;
six null signs left out). No holes.

## Translation

Written on the 15th of October. The Nuncio has communicated to Secretary Pérez everything that Your Most Illustrious
Lordship writes in the cipher of the 19th of September; the said Secretary has promised to give an account of it to the
King and to procure an answer, of which Your Most Illustrious Lordship will then be advised at once.
