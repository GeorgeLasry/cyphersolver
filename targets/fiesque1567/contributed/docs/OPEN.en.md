For the certainty of each line of the first five letters see the marks in [READING.en.md](READING.en.md).

## 2. Nulls
- Groups of plain letters (fo, Ea, of, fv, Il, par, moy, bro, au, ri, ol, ti, fi, Dav, Day, La) are treated as nulls and left out. Tomokiyo's nulls column has only fo, Ea, of, fv, Il, par and "…"; the others were inferred from these letters.
- Plain words inside lines ("receu", "Jay", …) were left out as plain text. Whether any belong to the cipher was not checked.

## 3. Transcription
- No human has checked the transcription. It was made by Claude from the images and compared with an independent second transcription by Codex (second/run0/, second/ind/). Agreement between AIs is no substitute for human checking.
- The four cross shapes (⊢ = q, t-shape = c, bold + = o, ⊣ = r) were sometimes decided on slight differences after enlargement. Three places that could not be decided between ⊣ and t-shape (f. 39r c27 #25, f. 39v c19 #16, f. 100 c24 #22) and one between + and 4 (f. 100 c04 #8) keep the first reading (t-shape).
- In f. 94 c08–c15, signs at the join of the left and right halves of the line images were duplicated; the duplicates were removed by eye.
- f. 100 c01 #4, c21 #1 and c25 #23: the crops missed the sign, so the shape was not checked.

## 4. Reading
- Word division, supplied spelling, and the s/p choice for the y-shape are all conjectural. "dir." is given only to lines where the key output reads directly as words.
- Not checked against historical sources. The identifications (battle of Moncontour, arrest and death of Don Carlos, Archduke Charles's journey to Spain, Duke Johann Wilhelm of Saxony) are conjectural.
- No calibration: no contemporary decipherment (interlinear or separate) was found in the volume (all 183 views seen at reduced size), and La Ferrière's edition prints only Catherine's replies, not the plain text of Fiesque's ciphered passages.

## 5. Scope
- (First version) About 40 pages with cipher were found in the volume and only the five letters were read. This version transcribes and reads all 44 pages found ([PAGES.en.md](PAGES.en.md)).

## 6. Disagreements with the independent second transcription
- For ff. 39r, 39v and 79r the value agreement with the independent second transcription is below 85% (76.1, 82.4, 81.5%). The main reasons are that Codex left out the faint "//" as separators, called C a small c, and called the y-shape C; in the places checked, the first reading was right (second/ind/RESULT.md). The remaining disagreements were not checked one by one.

## 7. Found and left open in this version (whole volume)
- ρ (rho): taken as e from view 35, "l(ρ)empire", "l(ρ) royaulme", and given that value (the "no value" in the table of §1 refers to the first version).
- ꝑꝑ (pp): taken as ff from the context of view 125 right ("profite") and view 149 right ("affec…"), and given that value (perhaps a variant of Tomokiyo's ꝥ = ff).
- ŧŧ (H#): read as o by shape, but in the later hand reading ss gives words in some places (view 125 right "commi(ss)aires", views 155, 159 and 161 right). Replacing it by ss on all pages raises the French-likeness slightly on most pages, but in the earlier hand o fits some places (courrier); undecided.
- In the later hand the triangle ▲ (Δ = i) is distinguished from the round a (e), and the ℌ-shape (n) from the ʒ-shape (y), by shape. On views 150, 155, 159, 161 and 168 right the large ʒ-shape is split by context into y (3y) and p (3p).
- Calibration: the volume does contain a contemporary decipherment (view 23 right, headed "deschiffré de la precedente", a plain-text copy of the cipher letter on view 22 right). The first version's statement that none was found is wrong (it was missed when the views were looked through at reduced size). Read by AI (Sonnet, Codex), this cursive plain text agrees only 51–55% with our decipherment, and reading errors cannot be told from decipherment errors, so it is not used for calibration. A human reader, or a reliable reader of the cursive, is needed. The margins of views 103 right and 125 right also have what look like contemporary decipherments (not read).
- Scope: pages were found by looking through all 183 views at reduced size; some may have been missed.
- Second transcriptions and image checks: the runs used for the status and the number of corrections are in [PAGES.en.md](PAGES.en.md) and second/check/decisions*.tsv. Places where the readers did not agree ("split") were not corrected; which reading is right remains open.
