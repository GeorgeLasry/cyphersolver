# 1 Oct 1569 (date conjectural), Scipion de Fiesque to the Queen Mother, Vienna

[v155r] You will have been able to see [?more] fully explained, in the letter I sent you by my man whom I dispatched on
the thirteenth of last month, what you write to me in yours of the twenty-eighth of the month of August last; of which,
being unable to write anything else at present, except that I know for certain that Messieurs de Chantonnay [and] […]
have pressed hard [?to] advance the marriages, who however have not yet been able to settle anything, except that the
courier will be awaited whom, as I wrote to you, [the Emperor] lately sent to Spain, who cannot be back before next
Martinmas. I told [the Emperor] what you wrote to me of the state of your affairs, at which he was very glad, especially
that your forces are together; he told me that he has had news that your enemies have raised the siege before Poitiers,
and wishes to learn what road they have taken; also that he has no report of any levy of men being made in Germany;
nevertheless he is informed that there are several intrigues, of which nothing has so far been discovered. He shows a
great wish to see [the King] out of these troubles.
