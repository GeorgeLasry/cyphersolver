# 2 Mar 1569, Scipion de Fiesque to the Queen Mother, Vienna

[v146r] [The Emperor] told me that he has had other news from Germany, by which [he] is informed that Monsieur d'Aumale
[has] burnt and sacked everything near Saverne, which [?] seems very strange. I told him that I believe none of it, and
that this is news invented by […] who are afraid, judging by their own consciences from the offences they have done the
King; and that I am sure that the King will not allow any harm to be done to the lands of the Empire, [?as] Monsieur de
la Forest was charged to give him to understand. He told me that he has had all the reports he has received copied, to
have them given [?to you], but I believe [?we] shall not be able to have them before tomorrow; and as the ordinary
courier is leaving, I did not want to fail to inform [?you] of this. For the rest, nothing new is heard, except that the
