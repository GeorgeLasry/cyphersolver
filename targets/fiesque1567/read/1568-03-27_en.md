# Fiesque to Catherine de' Medici, Vienna, 27 March 1568 (cipher passage)

[v049r] [...] on the subject of the marriage, he told me that [...] that he had written to you about it, it was not [his intention] to keep the matter in any delay, but that he had to wait for letters from Spain, [...] had not yet [...] in what state the affairs of the Prince of Spain stood; and that afterwards you [?would see] that it would not be for want of him that the marriage was not soon made. I told him that the said Sieur de Grignan was leaving well content, seeing that he could assure the King and you of the good will he bears you [and] the King, and that I hoped in God that the marriage could soon be accomplished, since the beginning was good and both parties were proceeding with good affection. And thereupon the Emperor [...] come to sup with him, which [?was] the reason I could not hold longer discourse with him; and I did not want to fail to write you this, so that you know that he has no less desire [than] you that the marriage be made soon.
