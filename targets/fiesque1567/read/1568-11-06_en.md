# Fiesque to Catherine de' Medici, Vienna, 6 November 1568 (cipher passage)

[v125r] The rumour runs that Duke Wolfgang of Zweibrücken [?has gathered] some six thousand reiters and three regiments
of foot to send to the help of the Prince of Condé; and that the King of Denmark is marrying the sister of the Prince of
Orange, and that he is making a league with all the maritime towns to send help to the said Prince of Orange. I have
heard from a good source that Germany has not for a long time been as united as it is now to defend the quarrel of the
said Prince of Orange. The commissioners have continued their journey towards the Duke of Alba and the Prince of Orange;
but most people believe they will achieve nothing.
