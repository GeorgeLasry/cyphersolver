# Fiesque to Catherine de' Medici, Vienna, 4 June 1568 (cipher passage)

[v065r] No occasion has ever [?arisen] to raise anything with the Emperor about the marriage, and he has said nothing to
me about it either; and as far as I can tell, he has had no resolution from Spain, without which I believe he will
hardly bring himself to conclude the marriage with the King, for the effecting of which it is said at this court that
you are sending him an ambassador.
