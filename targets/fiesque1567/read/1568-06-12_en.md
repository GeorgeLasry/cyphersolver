# Fiesque to Catherine de' Medici, Vienna, 12 June 1568 (cipher passage)

[v070r] [Dz] [...] had told me that the Sieur [?Dietrich]stein had written to him by letters of the twelfth of last month that the King of Spain was about to send him a courier, by whom he would inform him both of the cause of the detention of the prince his son and of the decision on the rest, which concerns the marriage of his daughters. As soon as I hear that the said courier has come, I will look for an occasion to learn [...] he has decided to settle. And as far as I can understand, [he has] a great wish that his eldest daughter be married soon, who on the first of November will complete nineteen years, and the second this month [?will have] completed fourteen.

I have heard on good authority, from one who told me, that he has been informed that you, with the whole of the King's council, have decided that [ϖ] shall marry, and that you were about to send him Monsieur the Duke of Montpensier or some other great personage of this kingdom. And when I asked that man what [ϖ] said about it, he told me that he said nothing else about it except that you have decided to learn the decision on it this time. Still, holding to my [v071l] opinion, I believe that he will hardly decide anything until he has had an answer from Spain, which I hope will not be long in arriving.
