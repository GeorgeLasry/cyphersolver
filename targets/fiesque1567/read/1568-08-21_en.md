# Fiesque to Catherine de' Medici, Vienna, 21 August 1568 (cipher passage)

[v103r] […] that he well knew you would offer the Emperor's brother, Archduke Charles, the country and passage when he
returned from Spain; and also that if by chance on his way there he touched at Marseille or some other place in
Provence, [you would] order the Count of Tende to give him the welcome and attentions that belong to such a prince. For
the Emperor loves this brother dearly, since he has always been obedient to him, and even in this last mission, in which
he showed himself very ready to do his will, […] that all the honours and pleasures done to the Archduke he will count
as done to himself. Although he has told me nothing about his brother's passage through France, I have gathered by
conjecture that if you offer it to him, he will not fail to return by that road, for the Emperor much wishes it. And
[?it is held for certain] that he will leave on the fourth of September.
