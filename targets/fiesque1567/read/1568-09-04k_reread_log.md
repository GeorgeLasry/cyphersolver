- Built line by line from the current decodes (about 4,000 signs). ff. 100v-101v read much better than f. 100r: most

- **Code signs standing alone** (not in Tomokiyo's table as codes): ϖ (key m) where a person is meant, 8 times in this
  letter: "le ϖ fut bien guery" (the Emperor, ill with fever), "ϖ porte le grand deuil avec toute sa chambre" (the
  Emperor), "l'edict que ϖ leur a faict", "ϖ les traicte doulcement", "secours contre ϖ" (the King of France). It reads
  as a title code (Sa Majesté?) used for both monarchs; also ϖ before "les persuader" (f100v c27) where "pour" would fit,
  so not settled. pp (ꝑꝑ, key ff inferred by the contributor) stands alone twice in f. 101v: "que pp y voulust
  condescendre", "que pp asseureroict de leurs vies et biens" (the King of Spain by context). ss stands alone once
  (f. 100 c21 "que ss en fut le mediateur"). All left as codes in the reading.
- The text of f. 100r begins "Madame" (READING.en.md) and f. 100r c12 has "il vous trouverroit tousjours preste"
  (feminine), so the cipher is addressed to the Queen Mother although the docket says "au Roy".
