# 4 Nov 1569, Scipion de Fiesque to the Queen Mother, Vienna

[v168r] I have kept back the courier in order to send you by him the answer that [ϖ] (the Emperor) will give me on what I
asked him concerning what you write to me about the ceremonies he intends to be performed at the marriage, so that you
[?may] decide to send such a person as is fitting. He told me that he will soon give me an answer and decision, and
having it I shall not fail to send it to you at once by the said courier.
