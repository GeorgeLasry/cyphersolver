# 15 Oct 1569 (date conjectural), Scipion de Fiesque to the Queen Mother, Vienna

[v161r] ⟨…⟩ except that I asked [the Emperor] to tell me whether he had learnt that anything is being negotiated [at]
the assembly being held at Erfurt that might turn to the prejudice of [ϖ] (the King). He told me that he has heard nothing more
than what he had lately told me, except that the Queen of England's ambassador is persuading the Protestant princes as
hard as he can not to abandon your enemies, and to help them, promising that she will do the same on her side; but that
he has no report that any stirring of arms is yet being made in Germany, where he has given good orders to be informed of
whatever is stirred there.
