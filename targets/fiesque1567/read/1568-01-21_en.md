# Fiesque to Catherine de' Medici, Vienna, 21 January 1568 (cipher passage)

[v034r] [...] learn other things, except that [?he] has decided that his children shall return to Germany as soon as possible. He has asked me several times whether there was news of the arrival of Monsieur de Lignerolles, to which I could say nothing else except that I have had no letters since the fifth of November. And as far as I can tell, I believe he wants some answer to those letters, to know how satisfied you have been with him. It seems to me that he has a real wish to keep up good friendship, and to keep him in this good opinion I [v035l] [...] to maintain this friendship, and that he will continue the ancient confederations that have been between the Empire and the kingdom of France.
