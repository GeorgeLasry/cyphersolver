# 20 Oct 1569, Scipion de Fiesque to the King, Vienna

[v159r] ⟨… to this war.⟩ He says that he has since heard that the Protestant [?princes] and several others of their
religion are holding an assembly [?at] Erfurt to negotiate a defensive league, fearing to be attacked by […] Count […]
more than by any of the others. He [says] that the Queen of England has her ambassador there, […] after he hears what is
decided there, of which he has promised to inform me. He also told me that, although someone had informed him that a levy
of men was being made to go to France, there is no foundation for it; and that he has given such good orders that if
anything happens he will be informed of it.
