# 20 Oct 1569, Scipion de Fiesque to the King, Vienna

[v159r] ⟨… to this war.⟩ And since he has heard that the Protestants and several others of their
religion are holding an assembly [?at] Erfurt to negotiate a defensive league, fearing to be attacked by […] Count […]
more than by any of the others. He [says] that the Queen of England has her ambassador there, who is about to learn what is decided there, of which he has promised to inform me. He also told me that, although someone had informed him that a levy
of men was being made to go to France, there is no foundation for it; and that he has given such good orders that if there is anything he will be informed of it.
