# Français 3198: translation of the excerpt

George Lasry, report PDF `02_francais-3198.pdf`, "Excerpt and Translation".

## Translation 1 (f63r, passages A21–A23 — 63r)

The Poles remain divided as before; although they show Batory a friendly face, in their hearts they feel the opposite, as I hear not only from the said Warsawicio but also from several others.

## Translation 2 (f75r, five complete body lines)

The said Weber has gone to Augsburg to see a lordship he recently bought in those parts, called Rombals, and will return to Vienna when His Majesty arrives there. He will continue following the court for two years to become versed in its affairs, but many would not weep if he never returned there.
