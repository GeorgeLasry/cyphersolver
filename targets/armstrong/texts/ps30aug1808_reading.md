# Armstrong to Madison, coded postscript of 30 Aug 1808

## Reading

P.S. Russel ought to be the consul: he is an American by birth, and is much better qualifi[ed] than any other
candidate. In a word he is above men in general. Next to him in fitness is O'Mealy, but he is like Warden an
Irish[?man].
