# Dinteville, 1 July 1592 (BnF fr. 3621 f. 128, no. 114): the cipher passage, English translation

⟨…[a gentleman of Monsieur de Gondi coming from Florence]⟩ told me that he saw two million in Spanish gold unloaded at
Genoa. He left at Besançon forty-five loaded mules, which are to leave in three days and take the road to Vesoul, having
only a hundred horse as escort [blot]
