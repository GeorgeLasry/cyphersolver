**Code groups** are written as lower-case letter groups (`sud`, `zre`, `gso` …), copied as they appear; `gso` is
*g* + long *s* + raised *o*. Their values are in `keys/groups.json`. On the image, `sin` is written with two minims
(*ſin*) and `sim` with three (*ſim*, p. 3, ll. 8–10); the transcription has 151 `sin` and 4 `sim`, and the split of
the `sin` tokens is by context (`work/splitsin.tsv`). `X` probably covers more than one sign (the header notes a form
"often with a hooked lower-left tail"); its split into *r*, *s*, *z* is by context (`work/splitx.tsv`).
