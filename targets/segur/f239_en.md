# 500 Colbert 401 f. 239, Henry of Navarre to Ségur, Montauban, [January/February] 1586: English translation

… Only I will tell you **{900} to spare no means {gne} {gna} to succour with all diligence, and to set every piece in
motion** for so necessary an effect; **commit everything to it**. And for the rest, make very certain account of me … From
Montauban … 1586. Your most affectionate master and perfect friend, Henry.

Postscript in another hand, signed D.: **since {e} we have had no {na} from you.** Sir, I am perpetually your servant. We
hope that God will bless your designs. Drake has made a very great prize. He is the first man of the sea, and among the
most valiant, in the world …
