# 500 Colbert 401 f. 233, Henry of Navarre to Ségur, [Sainte-Foy?], October 1585: English translation

Monsieur de Ségur. I am troubled at having had no news of you since your departure from England; I have written to you
four times … Only I will ask you, if you have done so from over there, **{X} to treat and conclude promptly with those
you shall judge fit,** if it is possible **with Duke Casimir or by his consent, and to make the largest levy and have it
march** as soon as may be. For since some **prompt succour** **is necessary**, I am writing to Monsieur de Clervant to make
**the levy of two thousand reiters** which he is **bound {gla} the contract[?] with the Churches[?] … and[?]** in the year
**1579[?]**, at least some part if he cannot [raise] the whole; and if he has not the means to do it, **let him use the ten
thousand écus of {180}**, as the said Sieur de Clervant will tell you, to which I pray you lend a hand, and that I may soon
be informed of everything … of **the road he will take in that case**, avoiding the danger of the[?] … **the Vivarais or
Dauphiné roads** might be suitable, giving us early warning. I pray you send my news to my brother Monsieur the Prince
wherever he may be. Our enemies have wanted to triumph over the separation … [news of the war in Guyenne and Languedoc] …
Your affectionate master and perfect friend, Henry.
