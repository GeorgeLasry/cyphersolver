## What the gist suggests (not part of the reading)

The despatch is a military report from Guyenne to the King in the autumn of 1586. The evidence weighs toward that reading, but it is unconfirmed:
- places on the Garonne, the Isle and the Dordogne;
- Castillon, which Mayenne besieged in August–September 1586;
- the Mas (de Verdun);
- the Huguenots' revenue from trade on the rivers;
- the army's movements, artillery and regiments, the King's service.

This is the same theatre as the sister letter fr. 15572 ff. 277–280 (Forget gone to Mayenne at Bordeaux; Caumont; the Mas de Verdun; the Dordogne and Garonne). It fits Tomokiyo's attribution to Matignon's cipher. The sender's name does not appear in the readable stretches.

  - a 9 in a circle = *par*;
  - a figure 6 = s;
  - the barred double o = the code group *le roy de Navarre* (one aligned occurrence, doubtful);
