# Matignon's Cipher-3, BnF fr. 15571 f. 179 (1586): English translation

Translation (Claude Opus 5.5, 23 Oct 2026) of `f179/f179_reading.md`, the plaintext of the whole cipher leaf
(Tomokiyo's interlinear decipherment, checked with his key). Same holes: `[…]` unread, `[?]` doubtful, `[x]` supplied,
`[code sign]` an unread code sign (probably a person). Names as in the original.

## Translation

Frontin will come to terms with the children of La Rousière, as the matter has been sent to him. Since then he is said
to have resolved to go into [Q]uercy[?], but the rumour that ran that it was to go and see la Croix turned him from
it. He is now resolved [to] attack Pons; it can be taken in a month. I much fear […] that the sickness among our foot
[s]oldiers may increase, and that after the siege a […] long rest will be needed before putting the army back in a
state to serve, while [code sign] gains a great foothold in Gascony. The answer that will be made to us o[…] will make
everyone wise. Iehan Durant will be written to, to employ Lansac in […] the sea army; he is not fit for it. The
commandeur de la D[…] could serve[?] there.
