# Matignon's Cipher-3, BnF fr. 15572 f. 276 (1586): English translation

Translation (Claude Opus 5.5, 23 Oct 2026) of `f276/f276_reading.md`, the plaintext of the whole cipher leaf
(Tomokiyo's key). Same holes: `[…]` unread, `[?]` and `[?word]` doubtful, `[x]` supplied. Names as in the original.

## Translation

La Guiolle is in doubt of Le Puy because of the friends of La Rousière, [who] are in great number with him, of whom
there are some who wish Polidor ill. La Saussaie takes care of him […] has helped your friends of la Madeleyne with
what he could. [They] are spreading the rumour that la Chambre and the brother of la Mare are on as good terms as they
ever were, and are soon to assemble to the prejudice of Front[?enay]. The procureur and his son have promised Aubois
to put him back in possession of the priory, which La Saussaie cannot believe, although they write to each other,
being assured that the sieurs de la Fontaine, la Chaie and Mirebeau, and his procureur, in whom he trusts, would not
allow, even in time of […], that he should be dispossessed of the priory; and above all he begs that le Bois and he
should n[…] but mingle together […]. Le Pui is weary of the war, and nevertheless fears the […]; whatever he does, it
is necessary for the good of maistre Iehan Durant, be it peace or war, that la Mar[?e] be kept away, for that is the
lodging which the friends of la Mare and of la Rousière desire […].
