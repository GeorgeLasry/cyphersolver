# Brussels, 9 October 1721 (ASV Arch. Nunz. Colonia 5, f. 26r; DECODE R24): English translation

Heading in clear: Brussels, 9 October 1721.

About the curate [45] [9441] [43]te divinis, [?grateful] to know something of [9336] [9485] of Tournai. About the
affair of Cologne; but he, from what I see, is informed of it. If you think it fitting that I [76] write in [9356] to a
correspondent of yours, I shall do it gladly, but I await your orders on this.
