# V1921: Caprile to Alfonso I d'Este, Esztergom, 25 February 1521 (ASMo Ungheria b. 4): English translation of the passage with the cipher

… everything for the information and discharge of whoever is to blame for it. **And he [?told] me there is a man here**
who, besides committing a thousand follies and bringing little credit to the master's affairs, **tells everything and
[?reveals] it**, and has said he wrote to his [messenger] that **it was the custode who persuaded [..]** to say that
**[.] Eger had been given away before his arrival, and that he should take him into office**, and has much disturbed his
affairs, and has put him in great disgrace with **his lord**. If that is so, as someone who has seen the letters assures
me, it will be little trouble for Your Excellency to begin reclaiming what is his **from his goods, even though they are
in the king[dom]**. And Your Excellency in this way …
