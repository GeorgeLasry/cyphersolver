## Notes on the figures

- "[?Asi] transferse a Buda il custode": 8 T n+ B | h x q DIV m+ f y AST n+ y ss | T o o LAM PSI q | RC ff | PHI o n+ f oSLo r y
  — 8 is a null or o; "transferse" has one e too many (y ss); o o = b.
- "in oficio questo episcopato, et a epso custode": B p | QB EL B TH RC CE | z+ LAM y m+ f oSLo | y AMP RC n+ PHI CE EL UT h
  oSLo | K f | q y EL m+ QB | TH LAM n+ h CE r (QB = o, EL = f then p; K f = et).
- "Lactantio, ho inteso, qual": n q TH h T DIV f RC oSLo | o c | B DIV h y m+ c | xp LAM T ff.
- "a in gran presia": T RC DIV w AST UT p AMP x y o+ L q; "[…]amato" o o σ T HH q h oSLo (o o σ give no c-h-i).
- "a Buda, et dicono eser conclusi": UT D o PSI T | K | PSI Z TH CE DIV QB | y o+ ss x | PHI oSLo DIV TH n LAM n+ B (D = b,
  o = u, K = et).
- "congregati … Bachiensis, che ogni episcopa-": PHI oSLo p w x ss U q f B | … | D T PHI o RC y DIV m+ L n+ | o TH y | CE w DIV B
  | y EL B n+ TH CE AMP q.
- "cosi [17] ne tenir": PHI oSLo o+ L | 1 7 | p ss | f y DIV L x — 1 7 also in R1139 L02, no value.
- "et l'altro in titulo, [?ma] questo modo excluderà": K | ff T n f x oSLo | RC DIV | h B f o ff oSLo | K q xp o o y n+ h QB | z
  CE PSI QB | y D TH ff o EL ss AST q (K read m here, t or et elsewhere; EL = d in "excluderà").
- "li daran poco … vo[…]stra Excelentia, quel si": L CE ff B PSI T x q DIV | AMP oSLo TH QB | … | LAM QB f n+ h x q | y D TH y A y
  DIV h L T | z+ LAM y ff | m+ B (f in "vo-f-stra" has no place).
- "potrà far al custode par questa via de Ungaria sopra il su[…]": AMP QB f AST T | f UT x | T ff | PHI a n+ f oSLo QB ss | EL UT

- "secondo patrone": m+ y PHI QB DIV EL CE | AMP q h x QB p ss; "in questo loco niente": B DIV | xp a ss m+ f oSLo | n CE TH QB |
