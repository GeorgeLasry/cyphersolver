# Caprile to Alfonso I d'Este, 16 February 1521: the ciphered postscript (R1138 / Vestigia 1919), English translation

If Your Excellency means to reclaim from the kingdom his forty thousand, and the eight thousand from the King, and the
other credits for which there are writings, a codicil must be made the same day as the testament, with the said
[testament], in which it is stated that the lord [the late bishop] leaves to the King, out of observance towards him
and by the custom of the kingdom, so that his testament may be valid, one or two thousand; because these people say
that, since he did not leave him a legacy, everything has devolved to the King. But when I told him what I write to
Your Excellency, I gave him a slap in the face [I silenced him]. The writings I shall bring or send by a safe way. By
the other mounted courier I sent this cipher.
