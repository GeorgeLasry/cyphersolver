## Translation

Postscript. The custode, when [he was] at Buda, as I wrote, has also come back as governor of [4817]. […] tithes, and
he does everything for […] I [17] […]; [he] has thus held Eger in office. [?And] I believe he gives every [?act] and
something into the hands […] custode; as yet he has not entered [?there] into the castle. And so his coming to Italy,
[?of] which I wrote to you, which he held as firm, has [?indeed] gone [off]. Seeing this, I gently raised with him the
money of his debt, as messer [?a] Alfonso told me. He answered me, saying he wants to go to Italy when at the giving
[…]. […] Milan […] you go. We shall talk at length about it, and if they need money on [?loan], I will give you some for
the journey. I shall see what he gives me, [?and] I shall report the whole to Your Excellency.
