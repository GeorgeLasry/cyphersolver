# A white-on-black photostat of burnt and torn fragments pasted on a sheet; clear words mixed with sign groups;

# Signs as shape labels (the labels of ../greenhow1861/transcription_side1.txt; values in ../greenhow1861/key_1864.md):
#   hk + with small x (A)   b7 7 (B)   c3 3 (C)   cx + with small x lower right (D)   yf Y (E)   tr triangle (F)
#   e8 8 (G)   cm comb (H)   f4 A with a star stroke (I)   o0 O (J)   ls long s (L)   dd diamond with dot (M)
#   qm ?-hook (N)   f5 5 (O)   cc circle with cross (P)   ph psi / looped 4 (R)   bx box with X (S)
#   dt D with dot (T)   e8s struck 8 / $ (U)   fl flag triangle (W)   ys Z over a stem (Y)
# "clear:" = words written in clear. | = gap between groups. ? after a label = uncertain sign. [..] = torn or faint.
