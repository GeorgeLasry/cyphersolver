# Key: Philip II to Don Juan of Austria, 28 Aug 1574 (KHA G016-A0204)

Rebuilt here on 24 Oct 2026 by aligning the cipher on scan 1 with the clear version on the same item
(`clear_version.md`). Letter cipher on one- and two-digit numbers, close to alphabetical order with j and k
counted (d 4, e 5, j 10, l 12, m 13, n 14, r 19, s 20, t 21, v 22), plus vowel marks written on the preceding
consonant and a handful of code groups. Word division is kept. Numbers are written in clear with an overline.

Code groups (one context each): pe = *como*; co = *año*; Lo = *carta*; 161 = *turco*(?); g 90 = *Porque*(?).
The vowel marks are the crux: a cross after a sign adds e, a dot adds i, a stroke above adds u. Read without
them, the groups look like a two-digit code (as they did on the first look).

Not yet seen: k, ñ (written n: *compania* 0 6 13 17 ρ 14 1 2), and the full homophone sets. The numbers for f
(7), g (8), h (9), p (17), q (18), r (19), s (20), t (21) run one above their alphabet place, those for d, e, j,
l, m, n at it: the table was laid out by hand, not by a rule.
