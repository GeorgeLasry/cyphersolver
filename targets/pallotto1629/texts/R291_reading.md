# R291 — Pallotto to Barberini, Vienna, 11 August 1629, despatch "B." (BAV, Barb.lat. 6960, register pp. 38-39; DECODE R291)

Reading, 22 Oct 2026 (reread batch b01, Claude Opus 5.5). What it rests on:

* **Kiewning**, *Nuntiaturberichte* IV/2 (1897), **Nr. 157** (Wien 1629 August 11, dechiffr. 6. September; Arch. Vat.,
  Nunz. di Germania 119 fol. 37-40), prints this despatch from the Roman decipherment, but almost entirely as a German
  regest. Only two passages are printed in Italian and are taken here from the print (OCR corrected): the Duke's question
  "È possibile che S. S.tà si compiaccia ... casa mia?" and "così per la spesa accennata ... come si usa in Germania".
* **Everything else** is read from the figures: the decode of the sheet with the 6956 key and the batch additions
  (`reread_b01/R291_dec.txt`), checked group by group (`reread_b01_show.py`) and on the DECODE scans (IMG_R291_I2505-2508)
  where it broke; DECODE digit corrections in `R291_corrections.tsv`. The regest is used as a check on the sense only.
* The clear words on the sheet: the date "Vienna 11 di Agosto 1629", the address "All'Ill.mo Sig. Card. Barberini", the
  register letter "B." and the page numbers 38/39; the docket line "Con occasione di visitare il Sig. Duca di Neoburgh"
  (underlined, above the cipher, which repeats it); and on p. 2 the clear "non potendosi perciò [?indovinar] se".
