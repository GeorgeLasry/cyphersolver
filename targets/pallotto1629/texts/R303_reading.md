# R303 — Pallotto to Barberini, Vienna, [28 August 1629], end of despatch "A 79" (BAV, Barb.lat. 6960, register pp. 83-84; DECODE R303)

Reading, 22 Oct 2026 (reread batch b01, Claude Opus 5.5). What it rests on:

* **Which despatch**: the sheet has no heading (register number 83 only) and begins with the end of a sentence. Its
  content is the last part of Kiewning, *Nuntiaturberichte* IV/2 (1897), Nr. 167 (Wien 1629 August 28, dechiffr.
  13. September; Nunz. di Germania 119 fol. 61-66; edition lines 24736-24763): the haste to send Wallenstein to Italy
  heard from the chief ministers, Eggenberg and the Emperor himself; the indult for Archduke Leopold Wilhelm; the
  imperial letter to Barberini; a closed letter for the Emperor received by the last ordinary; the visitor or vicar
  apostolic for Aquileia; Savelli's moves about the promotion. So R303 continues R302 (register "A 79", pp. 79-80),
  whose last sentence is about Pallotto's letter to Torquato Conti; the first words here finish that matter.
  Register pp. 81-82 lie between the two sheets and are not among the DECODE images (IMG 2536 is followed by 2537).
  Kiewning's regest has, between the Monterey letter and the Conti paragraph, news (Mazzarini from Milan, Toiras and
  Grana, the Swedish victory over the Poles, Liège, the Tuscan envoy) that is in neither sheet: probably the clear part
  of the despatch, which this cipher register does not hold; R302 already runs on from Monterey to the Conti matter.
* Kiewning gives only a German regest. The reading is from the figures (decode with the 6956 key,
  `reread_b01/R303_dec.txt`, checked with its codes) and the clear words, re-read on the scans IMG_R303_I2537, I2538
  (DECODE's clear-text transcription is mostly garbled, `R303_corrections.tsv`). The right edge of both scans is cut.
