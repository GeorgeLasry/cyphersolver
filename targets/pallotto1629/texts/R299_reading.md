# R299 — Pallotto to Barberini, Vienna, [25 August 1629], last leaf of despatch "A 67" (BAV, Barb.lat. 6960, register p. 71; DECODE R299)

Reading, 22 Oct 2026 (reread batch b01, Claude Opus 5.5). What it rests on:

* **Which despatch**: the leaf has no heading (only the register number 71) and begins in mid-word. Its content is the
  end of Kiewning, *Nuntiaturberichte* IV/2 (1897), Nr. 164 (Wien 1629 August 25, dechiffr. 13. September; Arch. Vat.,
  Nunz. di Germania 119 fol. 53-57), i.e. the continuation of DECODE R297 (register "A 67", pp. 67-68), which breaks off
  at "non restassi pregiudica-". Kiewning's regest runs on without a break ("... obwol er celebrierte und einen andern
  Platz hatte. Wenige Tage vorher bei der Grundsteinlegung der Camaldulenserkirche ..."), so at most a few words are
  missing between the two records.
* **The middle** ("ho stimato conveniente ... con l'imperatore ancora") is printed by Kiewning in Italian; the decode
  aligns with it at 0.33-0.55 cost per letter (`reread_b01/R299_align.txt`), and that stretch follows the print (OCR
  corrected). Where the figures differ from the print it is said in the log.
* **The opening and the close** are read from the figures (the decode with the 6956 key, checked on the DECODE scan,
  `R299_corrections.tsv`); Kiewning's regest confirms the opening, the close is not in Kiewning.
