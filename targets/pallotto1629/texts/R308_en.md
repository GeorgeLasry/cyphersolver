# R308 — Pallotto to Barberini, Vienna, 15 September 1629, despatch "B": English translation

Translation of `R308_reading.md` (22 Oct 2026, Claude Opus 5.5), with the same holes. Read from the figures and the clear
words on the sheet; Kiewning (1897) Nr. 177 has only a German summary of this despatch.

---

They have not been satisfied here with the decision about the matter of the visitor in the diocese of [?Aquileia]; they
have, however, reserved giving me an answer until it has been discussed in council. It has been said in recent days that
the [?Patriarch] had died; it is true that he has been gravely ill, as Your Most Illustrious Lordship will no doubt have
heard. I do not know whether they will want to wait to hear what becomes of him before giving an answer.

I have heard that on the occasion of the said [?illness] they have [?talked] of handing the free election over to the
[?chapter], as the other churches that are principalities of the Empire have it. I do not doubt that His Holiness's
supreme prudence will not be taken unawares. The man they had appointed to come and negotiate this business there has
died at Trieste, his home town, while, having been dispatched from here, he had gone there to prepare for his departure
for that court.

On the point of the visitor I shall not fail to tell Your Most Illustrious Lordship that they show much feeling here that
a foreigner is to be deputed, who, not having the language, will not understand nor be understood; and it will be a
burden on the people because of the expense, which would be avoided if someone otherwise [?suitable] in the country were
deputed; who, however, […] does not claim that it is at his nomination, nor that the discretion and will of Our Lord be
restricted; to the [?alienation] of which it seems they rather attribute this, what they call the usual difficulties and
[?hardness] when His Majesty's pleasure is in question. Your Most Illustrious Lordship with His Holiness will take the
decision you judge most opportune with your most singular prudence.
