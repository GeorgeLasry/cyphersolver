# R304 — Pallotto to Barberini, Vienna, [28] August 1629, despatch "B" (BAV, Barb.lat. 6960, register pp. 86-88; DECODE R304)

Reading, 22 Oct 2026 (reread batch b01, Claude Opus 5.5). What it rests on:

* **Which despatch**: the sheet is headed "Vienna 25 di Agosto 1629 / All'Ill.mo Sig. Cardinal Padrone / B / 86", with
  "Non ostante l'appuntamento preso col P. Magno" in clear over the first line. The day on the sheet reads 25, but the
  text speaks of "la lettera di sabato" (25 August 1629 was a Saturday; that letter is R301, "C 76") and of what
  Valeriano did "il giorno seguente" (Sunday 26). It is Kiewning, *Nuntiaturberichte* IV/2 (1897), Nr. 168 (Wien 1629
  August 28, dechiffr. 13. September; Arch. Vat., Nunz. di Germania 119 fol. 66-68; edition lines 24763-24768), the
  second despatch of 28 August (register letter B). Kiewning gives a three-line German regest only ("Trotz ihrer
  Verabredungen hat P. Valeriano am 26. August dem Kaiser ein Schreiben und die Schrift Slavata, einem der
  Deputierten, übergeben und an Barberini Copie gesandt").
* The reading is from the figures (decode with the 6956 key, `reread_b01/R304_dec.txt`, checked with its codes); the
  regest confirms the first paragraph; the rest (Pallotto's handling of Valeriano, the Father's exemptions and
  patents, the dignity of the nunciature) is not in Kiewning.
* The cipher ends on p. 3 after two lines with "… appoggiata qu-" and a line-filling stroke; the left edge of p. 3 is
  cut in the scan.
