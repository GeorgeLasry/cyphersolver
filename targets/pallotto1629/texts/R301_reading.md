# R301 — Pallotto to Barberini, Vienna, 25 August 1629, despatch "C" (BAV, Barb.lat. 6960, register pp. 76-77; DECODE R301)

Reading, 22 Oct 2026 (reread batch b01, Claude Opus 5.5). What it rests on:

* **Which despatch**: headed "Vienna 25 Agosto 1629 / All'Ill.mo Sig. Card. Padrone / C 76". Kiewning,
  *Nuntiaturberichte* IV/2 (1897), Nr. 166 (printed "100."; Wien 1629 August 25, dechiffr. 13. September; Arch. Vat.,
  Nunz. di Germania 119 fol. 59-61), edition lines 24580-24601: P. Valeriano Magni's second writing on the Carolina.
  Kiewning gives a German regest with two Italian passages.
* **The Italian passage** "quando l'efficacia ... e della sede apostolica" is Kiewning's print (OCR corrected); the
  figures and the clear words on the sheet agree with it (log).
* **The rest**: the sheet mixes cipher with words in clear. The cipher is read from the figures (the decode with the
  6956 key, `reread_b01/R301_dec.txt`, checked with its codes), the clear words from the DECODE scans (IMG_R301_I2533,
  I2534; DECODE's clear-text transcription is often wrong, `R301_corrections.tsv`). The right edge of p. 2 is cut off
  in the scan, so the last word or two of some lines there are missing. Kiewning's regest confirms each step (the
  writing shown to no one, more against the Jesuits than the first; Klesl and Pallotto with the Father; to the
  Emperor tomorrow and then to Nikolsburg; Bohemia; never to see the Emperor again; persuaded to show it to Pallotto
  tomorrow; the Jesuits "pupilla de gli occhi dell'imperatore").
* The sheet ends in the middle of a sentence ("la qu-"); the continuation (register p. 78) is not among the DECODE images: IMG 2534 is followed directly by 2535 (R302, "A 79", 28 August). R303, the unheaded sheet, is the end of R302, not of this despatch.
