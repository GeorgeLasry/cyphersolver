# R295 — Pallotto to Barberini, Vienna, 18 August 1629, "B 55": English translation

Translation of `R295_reading.md` (22 Oct 2026, Claude Opus 5.5), with the same holes. The middle follows the Roman
decipherment printed by Kiewning (1897), Nr. 161; the opening and the close are read from the figures and the clear
words on the sheet.

---

When [His Holiness] is inclined, and things are in a state where His Majesty and Signor von Stralendorf can be gratified
with the provostship of Halberstadt, then, so that the favour may be truly recognised as coming from the goodwill of Our
Lord and from the intercession of Your Most Illustrious Lordship, it seems that it would matter a great deal if, when the
Lord Ambassador speaks of it, some difficulty were raised with him: for instance, that the provostship has been asked
for by others and that some expectation of it has been given, as I do not doubt can be said truthfully; and that before
the ambassador can send any other notice, His Majesty and Signor von Stralendorf be informed here that Your Most
Illustrious Lordship has obtained the favour from His Beatitude, having taken it upon yourself with the person to whom
an expectation had been given to provide for him at the first opportunity.

This, however, I leave to the supreme prudence of Your Most Illustrious Lordship, who I do not doubt will be persuaded
that the motive and the boldness of hinting at this is only the effect of the desire I have that everyone should love
and remain obliged to Your Most Illustrious Lordship. […]
