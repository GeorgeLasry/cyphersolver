# R299 — Pallotto to Barberini, Vienna, [25 August 1629], last leaf of despatch "A 67": English translation

Translation of `R299_reading.md` (22 Oct 2026, Claude Opus 5.5), with the same holes. The middle follows the Roman
decipherment printed by Kiewning (1897), Nr. 164; the opening and the close are read from the figures. The leaf
continues R297.

---

[…In]deed, a few days earlier, walking in front of the Emperor in sight of the whole court and of [?many] people gathered
for the ceremony of my laying the first stone of the church of the Camaldolese, I went all the time on the right hand of
the said Duke [of Neuburg]; and although I believe the Duke himself would make no difficulty about giving me precedence,
nevertheless, having seen what happened afterwards in the person of the [?Spanish ambassador] on the day of Our Lady, I
have judged it proper not to put myself in danger of calling into doubt the last state of things, which is in my favour,
and of having to come to some point of ill satisfaction, if a more worthy place were prepared for him, not in a separate
and secret spot but in the same church and chapel; in which case I should have to take it up not with the Duke alone but
with the Emperor too. I am persuaded that Your Most Illustrious Lordship will approve my proceeding with this caution, so
