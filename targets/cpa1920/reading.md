# Coded words inside clear sentences: A0493, A0527, A0939 and B0494

The letters are typed in clear English. Only the words in capitals were in the book code; they are decoded with the
grid in `key_new.tsv` (see `decryption.txt`). None of these runs has a Bureau decode, except A0939, where the
Bureau wrote "I W" and "SOVRYS". The plaintext is English, so no translation is needed.

## A0493, March 1921

As to the application by this district for MINOR, I can state that I have not the slightest knowledge.

The word reads MINOR. Who or what is meant is not known. Robert Minor is only a guess.

## A0527, 27 April 1921

We hereby inform you that you must have your delegates to the unity convention elected by MAY 12. This means that
you are supposed to have all your delegates ready to begin travel upon this date.

## A0939, report of D.O. 9, undated

Being watched very closely now, since distribution of leaflets. Members of I. W. doing distributing for us.

Have you any objection to working up a route for SOV. RUS. Magazine in order to make up deficit?

I. W. most likely stands for the I.W.W., which the same letter names in clear. SOV. RUS. is the magazine Soviet
Russia. The Bureau decoded it as SOVRYS.

## B0494, Frederick Mayer, D.O. 13, typed date January 24, 1920

I shall see to day Comrade S. RUBIN (this is his right name). He can speak English, Russian, Jewish and German, and
he can understand Polish.

The typed year may be a slip for 1921: the same letter says the man left the Socialist Party "after January raids".
