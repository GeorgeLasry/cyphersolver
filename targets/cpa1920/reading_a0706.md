# A0706: report of 23 March 1921, signed Grey, D.O. 12

The report is typed in clear English. Only the words in capitals were in the book code; they are decoded with the
grid in `key_new.tsv` (see `decryption.txt`). The Bureau left no decode. A hole `[?]` is a cell with no value.
The plaintext is English, so no translation is needed.

AT[?]OOD, Delegate of P A C, arrived here Monday. He convened a joint meeting of representatives of the [?]P and our
Dist. Comm. to discuss the situation and advise the Council as to the best methods to be adopted to carry out their
plans.

The committees to be composed of two members of U[?]P and three members of [?]P. Grey and Conner were elected
from our side, and Brown, Mitchell and Jones from the [?]P side.

The [?]P has a C E C member here masquerading as an organizer, and is apparently trying to organize to smother us if
possible.

## Notes

- AT[?]OOD is very likely ATWOOD. W is not attested at 5/29.
- The two-token group (5/12 6/13) occurs four times, once typed 5/13 6/13, and twice with a clear "and" between the
  tokens, most likely a slip; the reading leaves that "and" out. Typed "decuss" and "us apparently" are read
  "discuss" and "is apparently".
- The three-token group is 11/15 5/12 6/13. If 5/12 is C, the groups read CP and UCP, the Communist Party and the
  United Communist Party. C at 5/12 is not attested.
- The sentences make the three-token group the writer's own side ("two members of U[?]P ... Grey and Conner from our
  side") and the two-token group the other side ("three members ... Brown, Mitchell and Jones from the [?]P side").
- What "P A C" is, and where the report was written, are not established. The report mentions "the S.P. of the C."
  and "Finns" and a convention "within two months". These are leads only.
