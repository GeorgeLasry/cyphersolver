# R72, ASV Segr. Stato Francia 283C f. 52, [the Secretariat of State to the nuncio Dandini], 16 May 1580: English translation

The rumour and opinion of the Most Christian King's affair with the nun has become so public that it is now talked of
everywhere and held to be true by everyone: so that, even if the fact were not true, as Your Lordship is told by someone
who presumes to know the whole of it, the scandal that arises from the idea of His Majesty in that place is still so
great that, to rid himself of the infamy that comes to him from it, he ought to abstain altogether from going to that
monastery any more; and Your Lordship must bring this point up and urge it on him strongly, since they have tried to
persuade you that the fact is not true. As to the scheme uncovered concerning the credit and the forcing of money for
the expense, Our Lord persists in his decision, persuaded to it by many reasons that need not be written.
