# C. Balfour to Lt Col. Alexander Stewart, Charleston, 12 August 1781 (pp. 733-736), cipher runs

The letter is in clear with runs of cipher; only the cipher runs are given here, in order. They join the
clear text recorded on the C lines of `transcription/p733.txt`-`p736.txt`.

... the escort will not be at Martin's till Tuesday[?] ... day notice is sent ... detachment to wait [?] ...
for the militia will not keep Orangeburgh ... possible at present to supply a post there; our present
communication assisted by water carriage ... keep it ... two hundred guineas are rent[?] ... spare, or is
safe to send at once ... not one dragoon is here; employed to the southward getting provisions, rice ...
your mounted convalescents belonging to your army ... by then [the next?] escort ... officers when
recovered ... as to Thomson, I am clear he will be better here; very dangerous; be assured [it] is greatly to
be guarded against, as we are vulnerable in so many posts. The depot near Monk's Corner put in a state of
defence ... to get possession of the boats upon the Santee by means of the negroes ... the garrison [?] ...
Caldwell near you, but is not to act with the cavalry, by Lord Rawdon's directions ... from your intelligence
that the enemy are in force on the other side low down, cross either at Manigault's or Murray's ferries,
intercept the escort; a small movement of a corps towards Nelson's would effectually cover them, if
convenient.
