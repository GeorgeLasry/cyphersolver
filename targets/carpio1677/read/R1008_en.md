# R1008, Carpio to Fuenmayor, Rome, 16 Oct 1677

⟨In the pending difficulties⟩ of this court there is nothing new: the government stands firm in its view, and I have
resolved not to move until His Majesty's orders reach me, so as to carry out whatever he is pleased to decide.
