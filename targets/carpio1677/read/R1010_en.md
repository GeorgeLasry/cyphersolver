# R1010, Carpio to Fuenmayor, Rome, 4 Sept 1677

The French fleet has left Messina. It was rumoured to be heading to attack Brindisi in the Kingdom of Naples, but I
have now come to understand that the design is to surprise Catania; and given their great good fortune we must fear
the success of whatever operation they undertake.

I am left so confused and downcast by learning, from letters I have had from Brussels this week, that the Prince of
Orange has raised the siege of Charleroi out of season, that I confess to Your Lordship I lack the heart to speak of
so irregular an event and one of such pernicious consequences; nor do I wish to discuss it with Your Lordship, so as
not to add to the grief it will justly cause you.
