# R1006, Carpio to Fuenmayor, Rome, 2 Oct 1677

⟨I have informed Your Lordship of⟩ the offences our nation has received at this court. To that I can now add only
that, as the Pope and his ministers persist in giving no satisfaction, I am awaiting the advice I have asked on this
matter of the governor of Milan, so as to take, with his advice and that of these cardinals of our party, the final
resolution that best serves His Majesty. The French fleet and galleys, after keeping station for some time off
Catania and its coast, are said to have turned back towards [?Toulon] and [?Hyères], and the galleys very badly
knocked about.
