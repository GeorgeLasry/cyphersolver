# R1002, Carpio to Fuenmayor, Rome, 4 Dec 1677

On the pending affairs of this court, the King our lord, whom God preserve, is pleased [?to order me to] ask His
Holiness for an audience and tell him what his despatch contains about the satisfaction His Majesty is to expect for
what happened here. Having asked for the audience on Tuesday the 30th, His Beatitude was pleased to answer that he
could not receive me. Going back the next day, the 1st of this month, to beg His Holiness, through my maestro di
camera, to grant me the audience, since His Majesty orders me most strictly to [?speak] to His Beatitude in person on
the business he entrusted to me (for without so pressing an order I would not trouble His Holiness), His Beatitude
answered the same as the day before, without giving to understand the reason for the refusal, and added that if I
had business of the King's to treat, I should communicate it through some Cardinal. I again set out His Majesty's
orders, and that they left [me] no discretion to accept such a proposal, but nothing has availed to obtain the
audience. So I am not minded to ask for it again, nor to treat the principal business until this point is settled;
nor can I [yet] tell Your Lordship what resolution I shall find myself obliged to take, for I fear His Holiness will
persist in his intention, this being a point as unbecoming to His Majesty's service as the first.
