# R1007, Carpio to Fuenmayor, Rome, 9 Oct 1677

⟨In my previous letters I informed Your Lordship of⟩ the offences our nation has received at this court. I will now
add only that this government still holds out without giving the satisfaction so justly owed to us, although for my
part I have pressed with the most urgent representations that the justice on my side calls for. Not having gone so
far as to take a resolution, I am waiting for the answer of the viceroy of Naples, to whom I have written on this;
meanwhile, on the advice of these cardinals of the party, I keep away from His Holiness's audiences.
