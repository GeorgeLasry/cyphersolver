# R1009, Carpio to Fuenmayor, Rome, 25 Sept 1677

⟨I go on to tell Your Lordship that last week I reported⟩ what was happening at this court over the false rumours
raised against the Spaniards and fomented by our enemies. I can now only add that, although their groundlessness
has been proved, and although I have kept up with His Holiness the humble and urgent representations for a remedy to
such disorders, not only has no [remedy] been applied and no satisfaction given me, but I have not even been able to
get a single step taken towards finding out the truth, which only gives the populace more boldness against the
King's subjects. So, after several discussions and conferences with these cardinals of the party, it finally seemed
best to report, as I have done, to the viceroy of Naples and the governor of Milan on the state of the matter, and I
am awaiting their answers for my guidance.
