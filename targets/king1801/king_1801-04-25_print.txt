Source scan: https://archive.org/details/lifecorresponden03king (leaf n471 = p. 438)
Only the final paragraph (p. 438) contains code. The edition prints the coded words in italics,
footnote on p. 438: "* Italics in cipher." Below, _underscored_ = italic in the print = in code on the
original; plain = clear words left in the despatch. Transcribed from the page image, 26 Oct 2026.

p. 438
_The King's recovery has not been_* complete, _the same remedies
have not had the same_ effect _as on a_ former occasion, _the fever and
the delirium having subsided_ it was believed _that he would_ soon
recover, _but by too early_ attention to business, _or the news_ respecting
_Hanover, or perhaps the_ nature _of his disease, he has_ suffered _a
relapse, and tho' not_ quite _as ill as he has been, he is so much so as to
inspire great, and I believe well_ founded, _anxiety lest he should never
completely recover: his present state is that of extreme debility. Cir-
cumstanced as the Ministers are their_ difficulties, as well as those of
the country, are much encreased _by this uncertain state of the King's
malady._ With perfect Respect & Esteem &c. &c.
                                        Rufus King.

* Italics in cipher.
