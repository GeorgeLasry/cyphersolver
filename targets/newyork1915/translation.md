## 0211b | February 1915 | New York → “Sunwen”, Tokyo | JACAR B03050087600, PDF page 49

If the moment is ripe, the risk must of course be taken; but we fear that Japan … afterwards: do not stake everything on one throw. Come quickly to America …; funds can also be raised here. … Will you come? Reply.

## 0198 | 18 February 1915 | New York → “Sunwen”, Tokyo | JACAR B03050087600, PDF page 36

The Japanese pirates press …; the situation is perilous: what attitude should our party take? Awaiting your reply. — Ma Su, …, Lin Sen, [Huang] Boyao (?), …, [Deng] Jiayan (?)

## 0201 | 20 February 1915 | New York → “Sunwen”, Tokyo | JACAR B03050087600, PDF page 39

Japan's twenty-one demands are published in full in the American press; we are treated just like Korea; our ruin is imminent. You may have been deceived, Sir … Reply in detail. — Lin [Sen] and others
