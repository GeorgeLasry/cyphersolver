# R4939, 1648: English (23 Oct 2026, r2h)

Translation of `R4939_reading.md`, same holes. "His Highness" = György I Rákóczi, the writers' father; "my younger
brother" = Zsigmond Rákóczi.

We would have wished to be able to write happier news from God to Your Lordships: on our gracious lord our father [?his
health ...] God's great visitation is upon us; [?it has grown very much worse], so that by the doctor's judgement there
is no [?further] [hope] for his life ... taking it to mind, His Highness ordered that Your Lordships should conduct
yourselves ... knowing ... His Highness did not want the election for himself but meant it for us ... As for our own
state, we certainly do not want it, for we do not want to throw this poor country ... into a more dangerous condition;
Your Lordships know how things stand with the Porte. For good security ... may God preserve him ... after His Highness's
death we must think, and not leave this country without a head [?in danger]. If we wanted to win the principality for my
younger brother it would take a long time, and meanwhile ... the state would be in great danger, and [...] an uncertain
matter. Therefore we bind Your Lordships, by the duty you owe to our gracious lord and father and after His Highness to
us, to employ our grace with every means where it is needed in my younger brother's promotion; Your Lordships, being
there, are to work on everyone, since His Lordship's fortune ... assuring those whom it concerns, and it must be known
that as his father, who was ready to lay down his life for him, so we will strive to further him with all our means. May
God preserve him; we will inform Your Lordships of His Highness's death too, if God has so ordained, which we do not wish
to live to see. We will use our money so that by spending it ... it may be turned to his promotion. May God preserve him;
if His Highness should happen to die, we will send letters, if needed, so that Your Lordships can deliver them to those
whom it concerns, and further the business of my younger brother in the best way.
