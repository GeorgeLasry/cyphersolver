# R4940, Zsigmond Rákóczi to Ferenc Bethlen, Munkács, 2 Aug 1648: English translation

Translated from `R4940_reading.md` (Claude Opus 5.5, 23 Oct 2026): the clear letter, transcribed from the DECODE
image, with its two cipher spans (in *italics*) deciphered with the family alphabet. Holes as in the reading:
`[?word]` doubtful, `[…]` not read. "Your Grace" renders *kegyelmed*; "His Highness" *ö Naga* (ő Nagysága; Hungarian does not mark
gender: the Prince, György I Rákóczi, Zsigmond's father, is the likeliest).

## Translation

I offer my service to Your Grace, and wish you every good thing from God.

Mr Földvári [?Mihály] [?this] […] [?having arrived], not wishing to delay the answer, although even without that His
Highness did not wish [it] for Your Grace, we [?indeed] myself […] have sent it by the servant, which I know Your
Grace will not delay. Thanks be to God, we are now all in good health. If *their votes* in the matter were as Your
Grace writes, *it would be a won matter*. Your Grace, now send by my servant the […]. May God let us hear every
joyful news […] of Your Grace. At Munkács, 2 Aug. 1648.

Serving Your Grace with affection, Zsigmond Rákóczi.
