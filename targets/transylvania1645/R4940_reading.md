# R4940, Zsigmond Rákóczi to Ferenc Bethlen, Munkács, 2 Aug 1648: reading of the whole letter

Written 23 Oct 2026 (Claude Opus 5.5, translation wave v9). Until now the repository had only the two cipher spans
(`R4940-reading.md`); the clear letter around them was not transcribed. It is transcribed here from the DECODE image
`IMG_R4940_I28653_P1.jpg` (main checkout, `transylvania1645/`, git-ignored), read line by line on 1.5x crops. Clear
text in plain type; the two cipher spans in *italics*, deciphered with the family alphabet (`R4940-reading.md`:
adqgzndl = *votumiok*, inyhqodkduadkiv = *niert dolog volna*). The hand writes e as a raised dot and abbreviates
*kegyelmednek* / *kegyelmed* (k.gyld.k, k.gyld); these are written out. Spelling otherwise as on the page, without
added accents; punctuation as the page's commas and stops. A word struck through before the first cipher span is a
deletion and is left out. Holes: `[?word]` a doubtful reading of the hand, `[…]` a word not read.

## Reading

Szolgalok kegyelmednek, es Istentul kivanok minden jokat.

Földvari [?Mihaly] [?ez] […] [?erkezven], az valaszt nem akarvan kesleltetni, noha annelkul is nem akart ö Naga
kegyelmednek, mi [?ugyan] magam […] szolgajatul küldöttem meg, kit tudom nem fog kesleltetni kegyelmed. Istennek
hala most mindnyajan egessegben vagyunk. Ha a dolognak *votumiok* ugy volna, az mint irja kegyelmed, *niert dolog
volna*. Kegyelmed az szolgamtul most küld[?jen] az […]. Engedgyen Isten minden örvendetes hireket hallani […]
kegyelmed felöl. Munkacson, 2. Aug. 1648.

Kegyelmednek szeretettel szolgal Rakoczy Sigmond.
