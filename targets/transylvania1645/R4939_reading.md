# R4939 (Keresdi_Bethlen_15-03), 1648: reading (23 Oct 2026, r2h)

Rests on: `R4939-transcription.txt` (the cipher of images P1-P2 transcribed letter by letter, 23 Oct 2026, at 2-3x on
the DECODE scans of 1,704 px; labels corrected on the crops, `R4939_corrections.tsv`) decoded with a key recovered
ciphertext-only by annealing (`R4939_key.md`; `research/reading_audit/r2h_r4939_anneal.py`, hu-modern 5-gram with word
divisions). The key is **not** the family key of R4936/R4937/R4940/R4942: a different simple substitution, in a
different hand. The clear parts (date, signature) and the address on image P3 give the setting: the Rákóczi brothers
(signed "Geor. Rakoczy" and a second hand) to Ferenc Bethlen and András Klobusiczky, Transylvanian envoys to the Polish
election diet, from Gyulafehérvár (Alba) [?9 Oct] 1648, while their father György I Rákóczi lay dying (d. 11 Oct 1648);
the cipher asks the envoys to go on promoting their younger brother Zsigmond for the Polish crown.

`[?word]` = partly carried by the figures, `[…]` = unread, `[word]` = supplied. Spelling as the cipher gives it (oe = ö,
cz, -unc/-nac for -unk/-nak, kegylme- abbreviations kept); line numbers are the cipher lines.
