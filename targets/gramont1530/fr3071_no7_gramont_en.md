## Translation

Monseigneur, I wrote to you the other day how it went with the Pope and with all others over here regarding the
good news you have let them know, which is such, and of so great effect, that everyone takes good hope from it that
things will be restored to their entirety; and little praise is given you for the perfection of the work. As I hope
to let you know more fully within three or four days by an express messenger, who will carry the full resolution
from here as to the matter of the coming of the sa[id] Francisque ...

⟨... will is always good, and continues more than ever [...]cution. And because the[...] [...] that the Imperialists
might conceive greater suspicion if I went there, because [...] beginning [the Pope] has found it good that the said
Francisque should go there [...] that there is nothing but good in it; inasmuch [...] being here I shall be able to
forestall all that might be put to him to turn him aside. To which I can assure you that all possible diligence is
being used. Monsieur the bishop of Faenza, nephew of the Count of Carpi, will leave within three or four days to [?go]
on behalf of the [Pope] to congratulate the [King] on the coming of the Queen and of my lords [his sons].⟩

I do not wish to forget [?to tell you] ... of good sums of promise ... and good ... that fourteen months ago ...
