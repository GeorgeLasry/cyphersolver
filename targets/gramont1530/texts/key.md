# Gramont's cipher (1530), key as used for fr. 3071 no. 7

Key: "Gramont's cipher (1530)" (Lasry 2023, cryptiana GL.htm `GL/BnF_fr3071_f17.png`; Tomokiyo's table from
fr. 3019 f. 20, `img/francisGramont.png`). Substitution with 2-7 homophones per letter, LL/RR/SS signs, and
COM/CON signs. Lasry's table as transcribed:

    A λ x          B g            C 5            D ɛ f          E ơ ę ʃ
    F ρ            G Ϛ(2-loop) ɱ  I ʒ(loop) ɑ H ñ ü xr   L ʌ c   M ₳ p
    N ∂ ı          O ʀ b t ß      P Δ Ϙ Ŧ        Q D N-hook     R ₿ E ʃʃ ₿B ʒ(4-z)
    S o 2 4r       T m ɣ          V d ɑ g h ɾ    X ▣ ɑy
    LL ⱡⱡ  RR ℐ  SS T-bar  COM ᵬ  CON ₠    unknown: crown, ꝛ-bar, 8Δ, ꟿ, key-ring

Readings on the leaf settle three of Lasry's five unknown signs from context: **8Δ = PAPE**
("de la part du 8Δ", "8Δ trouvé bon"), **key-ring sign = ROY** ("congratuler avec le ⚷ de la venue de la
royne"), and **ʃʃ-on-a-stem is a null in word-initial position** (line 1). g serves for B, V and E: context decides.
