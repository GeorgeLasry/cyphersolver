# Ernst von Steinberg (London) to J. G. von Ilten, 29 Mar / 9 Apr 1743: English translation

## Translation

⟨By His Royal Majesty's most gracious command I am not only to enclose herewith a copy of the Royal rescript going
today to General du Pontpietin, but also to add, for information, an⟩ extract of the instruction which has just been
sent to My Lord Stair, ⟨and to this end, that Your Excellency⟩, though with the utmost care for secrecy, ⟨may make use
of it with His Royal Majesty's⟩ German generals.
