# Unsigned letter from London to J. G. von Ilten, 15/26 Feb 1743: the cipher, English translation

## Translation

As regards the instructions which Monsieur the General Du Pontpietin has given to Monsieur the Lieutenant-General
von Sommerfeldt with regard to forage, they too are approved, as I have orders to state, just as there was no doubt of
the care that would be taken to continue always to keep good and exact discipline in that country. Nevertheless it is
wished that the troops consume, with r[?] the same good discipline, what forage there is in those quarters, so that
nothing of it remains if the French should take a fancy to take up quarters there after you in that country, so that
they would find there neither forage nor anything of what would be necessary for the subsistence of the troops. You
will make use of this last point according to your usual prudence.
