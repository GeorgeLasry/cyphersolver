# Sample transcription, AGAD Zbiór Popielów 4 (Deboli cipher despatches, 1790)

Only the opening lines of two despatches were transcribed, to test whether the same-date Polish texts in ZbPop 417
and ZbPop 420 are their decipherments. One token per code group, as written. Suffixes written under a group (a, b, c)
are given as `1049a`. The writer's 0 and 8, and his 6 and b, look alike; doubtful readings are marked `?`.
The upper half of each first page is blank except for "Sire!" (show-through from the verso is visible there).

ZbPop 417 k. 353 (image 1_12_0_0_417_0405), 2 Jan 1790, same heading and receipt date:

> W oświadczoney nawet y tą razą opinij, na którą w samey rzeczy zarobiłem sobie, co nie do życzenia woyny
> pomiędzy Polską y Rosyą, y do uniknienia co tylko tę Potencyą sprawiedliwie przeciwko nam iątrzyć może;
