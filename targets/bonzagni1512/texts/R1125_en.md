# R1125, Bonzagni to Ippolito I d'Este, Eger, 28 Dec 1514: the cipher block of p. 3, in English

By Ladislao, the bearer, I am sending at once six horses, whose coat and value are given in the enclosed list; then I
am sending you a basin of nine marks and six ounces, three pesetti, and a bronzina of three marks, six and a half ounces.

Money I cannot send ("I am in need"), because I have not yet had a single florin, and I have to send [...] to the
camp, as Your Lordship will see by the enclosed copy.

I pray God that one day He may free us from so many burdens; still, as soon as I have [31], I will do the impossible
to help Your Lordship with what is yours, for by now it would be time.
