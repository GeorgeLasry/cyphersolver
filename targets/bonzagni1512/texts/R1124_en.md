# R1124, Bonzagni to Ippolito I d'Este, Eger, 4 August 1512, in English

Most Illustrious and Most Reverend Lord, my most honoured Lord.

‹Madonna Beatrice coming, [and] Andreas being the bearer of this letter, I am sending the four cups and twelve plates
I wrote to Your [Lordship] about; and I am sending you a small gilt cup, [which] the Lord Custodian left you; then
[...] two small gilt cups with a foot, and two small ones gilt on top with a foot, two large ones, also with a foot,
and two small cups made with reliefs, then six rings›; all of which things he was leaving in Italy, except the two
large ones, as the will that Vosinta brought you shows; and I am sending everything to Your Most Illustrious Lordship
now, so that you may give me your opinion.

I have [...] and kept here the two ‹large cups›, and the parish priest told me that it was his intention that, if he
[...] to reach Hungary after his death, he wanted [...] everything to be his. The rest of the goods found [...] his
death, as Your Lordship can see from the enclosed inventory, [writ]ten in the presence of his nephew named Sigismondo
de M[...] and of some others of the household, has been kept here, awaiting [...] from Your Lordship what you want
done with it; for if Your Lordship wishes his will to be fulfilled, I believe it will not be enough, and what [...]
debt and the legacies he made, and the expense incurred for the burial.

I am sending a carriage with six of the finest horses [...] been in the household, and another dapple-grey horse which
I believe will please Your Most Illustrious Lordship; it is [?second] of seven years, to my poor judgment very fine;
it was given to me, and I make a present of it to Your Lordship, to whom I shall not cease to send [...].

And I beg you to send back Vosinta and the carter named Martino soon. I kiss your hand and humbly commend myself to
you. Given at Eger, 4 August 1512.

I am also sending three [?arquebuses]: one has a leather cover, and Messer Alberto bought it for [...], and two bought
by Messer Goro for four florins.
