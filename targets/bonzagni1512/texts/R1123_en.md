# R1123, Bonzagni to Ippolito I d'Este, Eger, 2 August 1512: p. 1 (partial), in English

Most Illustrious and Most Reverend Lord, my most honoured Lord.

On the 20th of last month, by [?Vosinta], the bearer of this, I received one from Your Most Illustrious Lordship, from
which I understood the need in which you found yourself and how urgently you asked me for money. But Your Lordship
should know that I think of nothing else, and little by little I had already gathered up to a thousand gold ducats at
small interest, and had decided to send them; but the King of Hungary's order that has since arrived, that the troops
must ride out at all events this [?August] feast of Our Lady, as you will see by the other copy as well, has made me
[?hesitate].

And so that you should not wonder at my long delay in answering, being urged as I know you are, I have wished to
[?dispatch] this [?Vosinta]; and so that he should not come entirely empty-handed, Messer Alberto Pratonerio has
written the enclosed to his father, that he should pay Your Most Reverend Lordship three hundred gold florins in all,
which I shall then send by [...].

I hear that Madonna Beatrice, sister of the late Messer [?Isadro], [?silver] that up to the sum of two hundred [?seven]
florins will be worth [...] I have [...] from one who was [...] of a hundred and [?twenty-five] florins [...]
