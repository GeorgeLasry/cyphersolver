# R1125, Bonzagni to Ippolito I d'Este, Eger, 28 Dec 1514: the cipher block of p. 3

Header: the letter is in clear on pp. 1-4 except this block (p. 3, 12 lines); the clear p. 4 goes on with the same
business. Not transcribed here: the clear pages.

## Reading

Mando per Ladislao lator presto sei cavalli del pelo et valore si contiene in la inclusa lista; poi te mando uno
bacino de marche nove et oncie sei, pesetti tre, et una bronzina de marche tre, oncie sei et mezo.

De denaro non posso mandare (indigeo), perchè non ò ancora posso uno fiorino, et mi bisogna mandare in [...] in
campo, como per la inclusa copia vederà Vostra Signoria.

Io prego Idio che una volta ne liberi da tante oppressione; pur como habia [31], farò lo impossibile per subvenire
Vostra Signoria del suo, perchè hormai saria tempo.

## Notes

- "denaro": the cipher has the clear-looking word "valent" (in the margin, keyed into l. 5), which the slip writes and
  glosses "denaro"; "valent" is also in R1123 (glossed "argenti"). Such Latin-looking words ("potest", "valent",
  "indigeo", "parte") stand in the cipher among the invented signs; "indigeo" is copied by the slip in brackets.
- "in [...] in campo": the cipher has the clear-looking "parte" (its end struck through) after "in"; the slip leaves
  a blank and adds "in campo" above the line. Read as a code word not resolved.
- ".31.": a number between dots after "habia" (l. 10); the slip omits it.
- "poi te mando": the figures give m + t ("le"?); the slip's "te" kept.
