# m0003: English translation

Today in the House of Peers Arroio, at odds with everyone, interpellated the Foreign Minister about Your Majesty's
journey. He pointed to the absence of the authorities at Livorno [?] and to the President of the French Republic not
having paid a visit. The Foreign Minister answered that it was a false rumour, setting out the facts in suitable terms.
Hintze Ribeiro.

Notes. From the decode of the time under the strips and on the back; in the reading [ ] marks our addition and [?] a doubtful word. Arroio is presumably João Arroio (Arroyo), a peer who had broken with Hintze's Regenerador party, which fits
"divorciado de todos" [our identification, not checked]. "Livorno" is a hard reading of a smudged word (L + "-vorno");
it fits the Italian leg of the Queen's cruise. The decoder's last word on the face is "contestar" or "contestou". The
Diário da Câmara dos Pares of about 11 May 1903 would confirm the day and these words; it was not consulted.
