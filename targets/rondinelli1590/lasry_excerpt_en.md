Translation of `lasry_excerpt_reading.md` (Claude Opus 5.5, 23 Oct 2026). ## Translation

… from necessity, I cannot believe that the lord Duke of Mayenne is sufficient to defend it, since it is seen that he takes counsel and governs himself by the advice of those who desire his ruin.
