# R4238, Mâcon to Montmorency, Rome, [xiii?] September 1536, BnF fr. 3053 ff. 32v-33r: English translation

**Passage 1 (f. 32v L01-L11).** This bearer is sent by the exiles of Siena to the King, to persuade him to be willing to
make an enterprise against the said Siena. *[clear: And although I know for certain that there is no purpose or
likelihood]* in the said enterprise, I did not wish to dissuade him from his said journey, having been informed that
Duke Alessandro was busy winning them over so as to make himself master of the state of the said Siena, fearing that,
seeing themselves abandoned, they might throw themselves into the said Duke's arms.

**Passage 2 (f. 32v L12-L24, f. 33r R01-R03).** *[clear: Monseigneur, I send you a letter]* which a German gentleman
named Christofle de Seris[n]ech(?) *[clear: who is here has given me to get to]* a captain of lansquenets who is with
Count Wilhelm von Fürstenberg; *[clear: which it seems to me it would be good that you have]* translated in Paris
before handing them over. *[clear: And he has given me to understand that he wishes to withdraw into the King's
service, saying that he is]* of King Ferdinand's chamber, and wants to leave him because of a quarrel he had with one
of the greatest of his court, whom he killed(?); and he says he knows many of the said Ferdinand's secrets, of which he
will inform(?) the King, and can serve him greatly; and he says he is known to two captains who are with the said
Count Wilhelm, named [Nolgang(?) de O[t]inden(?)] and Jehan Lois de Nendeburg(?), to whom the said letters are
addressed.

**Passage 3 (R04).** *[clear: I beg you to send me word what I am to do]* with the said German.
