# R4234, Mâcon to Montmorency, Rome, February 1536 o.s. (= 1537), BnF fr. 3053 ff. 16r-18v: English translation

**f. 16r.** [...] to find a means of coming to an agreement by a[r]dent(?) with the Emperor and the King, if it were
possible for him; and seeing the said lord Renc[e] [Renzo da Ceri] would [?] declare himself his enemy. And that he
had understood that the said Pope wanted to wrong me in the said affair through an intermediary, and deftly, to induce
the King to consent to the said de Post(?); which Cardinal Cesarin[i] has since done in the same terms as he had done
to Monsieur se[... ], a thing which made me give some credit to the [?] of the said Bishop of Lodi; besides which he
is not of the opinion that the King ought ever to consent to the said de Post, and he told me further that it seems to
him that the said Pope wants to send him there to speak rather covertly, yet that there would be means of doing this
and of de[x]tir(?) the Venetians in the said affair, saying that it seems to him that he wants to employ him there
with the private persons of those Venetians, of which he has promised to inform me. If so, before he goes back to the
said Venice; and on this subject, Monseigneur, I inform you that Cardinal Pisan[i] told me that, when the old
ambassador of the said lords took leave of the said Pope and sov[ereign?], His Holiness begged him to be pleased to do
