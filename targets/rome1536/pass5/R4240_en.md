# R4240, Mâcon to Montmorency, Orvieto, 2 September 1536, BnF fr. 3053 f. 40r-v: English translation

For I have learned from a good source that Vergerio, who was nuncio of the late Pope Clement and since then appointed
by the present Pope, brought them word, before his departure from [ . ] Ferdinand, and they, on the said Pope's behalf,
as it had been written to him by His Holiness, that she was very content — as the college of the lord Cardinals is
also said to be — that, attending the said council, they might be free to say and do there as they should be inspired
by the Holy Spirit. Whereupon they had resolved to be present there, hoping to have a decisive peace there. But the
said Pope, having considered that this would be the total ruin of the Church and the Holy Apostolic See, has, moved by
counsel, dispatched an auditor of the Rota named Vuors[t] [...] to go and summon them to the said council, sending them
express word that it will be proceeded with according to the disposition of the law and the custom observed of old.
To which, I have learned from a good source, the said Germans will never consent, for they say there is need to reform
the morals of the said Pope, cardinals and bishops, which they could not do without them alone th[...]. I leave you to
think from this, Monseigneur, what will come of the assembly of the said council.
