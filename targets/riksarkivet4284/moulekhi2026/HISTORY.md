# Search history and corrections

This is a retrospective account assembled on 2026-10-24 from the project's
dated notes and test descriptions. It is not a preregistered experiment.
It documents selection, rather than proving the result is free of cherry-picking.
No original search runs are rerun by this compact package.

## Earlier work and candidate keys

- Inherited source notes report earlier Latin homophonic searches and trials
  of R4310 and R4296 without a reading. Those are historical notes, not fresh
  trials certified here.
- R4327: an earlier local trial used a conservatively transcribed numeric
  alphabet and produced no supported reading. The later public-key survey
  skipped this record; that does not mean the earlier test never happened.
- R4126: tested literally on the original and reviewed transcriptions without
  support for an unmodified match. Later homophone-partition screening does
  not rule out every possible variant.
- Public-key survey, 2026-10-19: R4300, R4324, R4326, two R4328 variants,
  and a single R4329 row were tried as literal mappings.
  Reported alphabet assignments in the original transcription:
  R4300 455; R4324 415; R4326 484; R4328 (1625) 595;
  R4328 (1639–1645) 341; R4329 row 44 220.
  Each trial used 754 numeric occurrences, including 22 uncertain ones.
  These are lookup counts, not correct-decoding counts.
- R4328's later reconstruction is fragmentary; it was not conclusively rejected.
- R4329 has multiple rows and a crossed-out Camerarius attribution. Only the
  tested fixed-row model failed to yield a reading; the full switching-row
  mechanism was not established or rejected.
- Follow-up, 2026-10-20: R4321 and R4325's first grid failed literal trials.
  Relabelling-invariant partition and layout screens were also examined.
  R4326's apparent partition signal did not survive shift controls.
- R4319 was inspected and associated in project notes with Jacob du Moulin;
  do not describe this as an exhaustive cryptanalytic rejection.
- R4325's separate horizontal strip on I25940 was then applied literally.
  Its fixed alphabet produced the current Swedish/Latin candidate reading.
  This match was selected after examining other keys and decoded passages.
  The continuation was also inspected before the present validation protocol.

The source notes contain older paragraphs that precede later updates. This
account distinguishes the tested model from rejection of an entire record.
The full scripts and historical logs remain in the private project; they are
not all reproduced in this minimal, anonymized package.

## Withdrawn claims and readings

- The earlier claim “733/754 verified, about 97% complete” was withdrawn.
  729/754 is the reviewed transcription's alphabet coverage, not accuracy.
- Calling every large group a nomenclator code was premature.
- Dictionary lookups such as 244 = Infanteria and 336 = Pulver were not
  established readings of R4284; exact digit splits offer alternative readings.
- 692 = Anschlag and 1302 = Concussio from a separate long list were not
  accepted in the letter.
- A proposed change from 26 to 20 on line 24 was reverted after image review.
- Lines 17 and 49 have apparent letter anomalies, not independently certified
  encipherer errors. They remain uncorrected in literal output.
- The prior heading “independent passages” meant different locations in the
  letter, not statistical independence or a prospective held-out experiment.

## Limits

No independent transcription, confirmed full translation, novelty search,
or scholarly peer review is represented as completed here.
AI assisted analysis and drafting; computational tests do not certify handwriting.
The working identification with 1628/Camerarius and the key compilation's
later catalogue date require archival review.
