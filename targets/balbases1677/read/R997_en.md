# R997, Balbases to Fuenmayor, Nijmegen, 8 Mar 1678

I see how in the business of the Brunswicks' claim with the Danes about the title of ambassadors for their ministers,
nothing has been [?granted], nor at the first step the other day was [...] a formal act in writing. [...] I also take
note of the other [?designs] that [?are being sketched] on this point by the Danes and [...]bourg, and of the reasoning
V.S. makes about the ends this may come to, and the aim of the Duke of [?Hanover] in drawing his brothers into
[?neutrality]. It all seems to me very well weighed by V.S.'s great prudence; but I believe that none of these
[?intrigues] [?will come] to an end [...] decision until we see the one that [His] [...] has just [17] [...] how much
the rest will depend on [...], [...] since the Brunswicks [...] give at the embassy here [...] for the answers they
ask. I do believe that from here to the outcome [...] things clearer, and they will remain [...] as to what it is best
to do, since now we are left [...] in conjectures, without freeing ourselves from the grief in which meanwhile the
ceaseless dangers of Flanders put us, on which I forbear to dwell so as not to increase my melancholy.
