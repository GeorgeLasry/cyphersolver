# R991, Balbases to Fuenmayor, 5 Oct(?) 1677 (cipher): English translation

Your Lordship reasons about the matters of the Swedes' losses, and the growth of those of Denmark and Brandenburg, as
[?shrewdly] as your great understanding of German affairs guarantees; and I am of the same opinion, that as much harm
is to be feared from the collapse of the first as from the increase of the second, especially since the Elector of
Brandenburg can cause so much jealousy towards the Emperor's dominions. But matters of war are so delicate, [?] of these
reflections, that we must wish for their success in the present juncture rather than act on them; and so we must leave
it to the progress of the general peace to open to us the door to the settlement in which it would be just to place
the powers, for at present it is dangerous to show on our part that very intention to which our own interest obliges
us.
