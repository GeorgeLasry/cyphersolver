# R989, Balbases to Fuenmayor, 21 Dec 1677 (cipher): English translation

[…] the [?] Arquinto, about the Dukes of Brunswick's minister at that court having asked for his masters the investiture
of the Duchy of Bremen, which is what the one who resides there denies; but since the last letters I have from Your
Lordship are of 9 and 16 November, whose receipt I acknowledged by the last post, and in them Your Lordship does not say a
word to me on those particulars, nor have I received any other since, I thought I should bring it to your notice, so
that you may know that I am without the letter you suppose, and that Your Lordship may see whether it is a matter
worth repeating for what may arise here; for I believe the letter in which Your Lordship reported it must have gone
astray. I have no remarkable news to add to what I told Your Lordship the previous week, except that the loss of
Saint-Ghislain in Flanders takes our distress a step further and increases the need to restore ourselves to whatever
peace can be had, at which we are working on our side as much as possible; although until the Prince of Orange finishes
declaring what he brings from London, where lively solicitations are also continuing, we cannot form a sure judgement
of where all our efforts will end, however much we consider ourselves bound by necessity and mortified by the ill
fortune we suffer.
