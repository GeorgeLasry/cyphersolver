# R985, Balbases to Fuenmayor, 3 Aug 1677 (cipher): English translation

I am glad of the good hope that Monsieur d'Amerongen had given about his masters' disposition towards obtaining the
guarantee sought for the town of Bremen; for unless there is some hidden reason that I cannot see, I always consider
the settlement of that assembly advisable, in the form that Your Lordship sent me as a proposal.
