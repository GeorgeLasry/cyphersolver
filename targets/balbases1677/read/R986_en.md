# R986, Balbases to Fuenmayor, 28 Aug 1677 (cipher): English translation

Your Lordship will have learned the [?] of those of Flanders, and the inexpressible infamy with which it [fortune] has
treated us, for no other reason than our being unfortunate in everything. I have no words with which to weigh my
[?bewilderment], and so I express it better by keeping silent; and since there is no point in [?] [?], it must serve
us to do it with [?]. What Your Lordship may think is that here we are already beginning to feel the effects of the
event, because after the French had [160]ed, as I have informed [?Your Lordship], that they would satisfy the proposals
of Lorraine when these were presented to them, and after this step had been taken very punctually, they now come out
with the half-sword that Your Lordship will see in the enclosed — astonishment rather than an answer — which they gave
yesterday; from which one may argue the soft intention they have of bringing themselves to a more honourable peace than
the one their desires may dictate to them.
