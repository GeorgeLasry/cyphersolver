# R987, Balbases to Fuenmayor, 18 (or 28) June 1677 (cipher): English translation

I have stressed to Your Lordship how right and advisable [?then] the dissolution of the Bremen conference has been,
without any [?] particular having been done that would dispel our hopes for the general treaties in this congress, or
give occasion for disunion or distrust among those princes that would undo the little fruit we draw from them today; so
I have nothing to add but to praise highly Your Lordship's prudence in dealing with Count [?Windischgrätz] while finding
his conduct strange. I am glad to know that the Dukes of Brunswick have sent their approval, and that with the defeat
the Swedes have suffered at sea the distress into which the setback at Malmö had put that King has ceased. In this
congress the only news is that Ambassador Temple left here last week, recalled by his King, without its being known for
what; but it is thought that it may be about some peace project that has been advanced in London, and that in that
office nothing favourable to our interests can be forged.
