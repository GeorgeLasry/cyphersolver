# R988, Balbases to Fuenmayor, 29 June 1677 (cipher): English translation

Through the Lord Duke of Villahermosa I already had advance notice of what Your Lordship now informs me about the state
of affairs there; and indeed, His [C.] having asked my opinion on signing on our side the project of dissolving that
assembly into a suspension, I answered him that I not only found it without drawback, but considered it most fitting
for the principal aim that has always been held, of assigning those negotiations to this congress, without any prior
commitment to a positive objection. So I neither know for what reason His [?] has not agreed that Your Lordship should
sign the recess, nor understand why the ministers should want to adopt the maxim of instilling in the interested
princes greater hopes of [?] to [?] settled matter; for I see that the orders of the Imperial ambassadors residing here
are in this particular very much in line with the same view that I wrote to Your Lordship from Vienna that court
holds. And so all that remains to me is the impatience of awaiting the final resolution that may have been taken,
wishing above all that the troops of those princes, which after so much solicitation they have granted for Flanders,
may be of some benefit there for the improvement of our affairs and the encouragement of the negotiations of this
congress, in which up to now we have nothing remarkable [?] worth reporting.
