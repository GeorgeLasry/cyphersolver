# R990, Balbases to Fuenmayor, 19 Oct 1677 (cipher): English translation

Here we are without any news that might change the face of affairs, and I believe that, given the reluctance of the
French, we shall be able to make little progress until the Prince of Orange's journey to England brings out some more
positive clarity; for these ambassadors have just answered, on the point of the Bishop of Strasbourg's minister, what
Your Lordship will see in the enclosed copy, which almost closes the door to the principal business of Lorraine as long
as the other is not settled — since it has the lack of substance that can be understood; so that everything comes down
to the outcome of Orange's mission with [168] Britain.
