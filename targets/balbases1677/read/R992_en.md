# R992, Balbases to Fuenmayor, Nijmegen, 19 Sept 1677

I see the protest that the Imperial ministers published [fol] the [?dissolution] of the [?assembly] of Bremen, and
[?all] the rest that V.S. tells me [187] the conference of Altona. [?So], what [...] nothing occurs to me to
[?discuss], since on our side there is nothing [85] for us to do but [...] by our misfortune [...] to fear [182] [150]
violent resolution of those confederates, chiefly if, after the Charleroi business, they insist in the assembly that
the ministers of Celle [...] must be done [...] the [30] of that table, in which without [85] thinking [...] a
definite division of their conquests, which is the worst that [...] us.

Here it has been heard that the [?journey] of the Chancellor of Denmark to Altona [...] aimed at negotiating a
[?marriage] of the brother [...] with [...] of the Duke of Celle; but since V.S. has not found it out nor told me of it,
[...] I have given it credit, although they base it on the supposition that the Duke's [...] would bring as dowry the
share of her father's conquests [...] in the two [duchies] of Bremen and Verden.
