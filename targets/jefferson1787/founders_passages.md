## A. Barclay to the Commissioners, Madrid 10 Apr 1786 (Jefferson/01-09-02-0338; LoC mtjbib001863)
Coded (decoded interlineally by TJ, "two minor errors"), all except "Mr. Randal says":
no peace can be made until Congress grant a larger sum to pay for it, and that he will return to Spain to wait for
orders. [Mr. Randal says] Dey of Algiers has refused to treat with Mr. Lamb without assigning any reason for it. It does
not appear by either letter that Mr. Lamb has had any audience of the Dey of Algiers but I shall think it very strange
if he returns to Europe without knowing on what terms a treaty may be made and I find that to be the case. Unless I
have your direction to the contrary I shall hold myself justifiable at the expence of some time and money to attain
this knolege.
