# Benedict XIII to Francesc Climent, Avignon, 22 June 1400: the cipher runs on the p. 475 plate (excerpt), English translation

R1. ... [of the King of Aragon] with the Duke of Burgundy.

R2. And therefore the business is to be held thus in suspense, [?neither] [?approving] [?nor] [?disapproving]. [?Of]
[?the] [?Cardinal] [?of] [?Catania],

R3. that he is going back to Sicily; but others assert that [the King of Aragon], having been made chancellor, is to
remain in Catalonia.

R4. And above all write how he behaves in our affairs. On the matter of the goods of Pere de Bosc: we therefore wrote to
[the King of Aragon] that you would tell him our mind, so that no one else should claim this for himself, and that [the
King of Aragon] should rather thank him than us. So you will tell him that it pleases us and we are content that he
receive [them] at his will; but that, considering our tribulation, our state and our expenses, he should be willing to
have some regard in bearing the burdens, such as embassies and the like; because, as you know our need, unless some other
regard is had in future, we fear we shall fail altogether, the provisions made elsewhere failing, as you can imagine; nor
shall we be able in future to suffice for necessities.

R5. ... as if terrified by the ways of these adversaries of ours, who keep us here without leave.
