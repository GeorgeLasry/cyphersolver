# Gramont's cipher (1529): additions to Lasry's table from the reading of fr. 3091 no. 23

* `#` is **A as often as M**: the loose ǂǂ (A) and the compact # (M) are not distinct in this hand. Context decides.
* The ♀-like "triangle or ring on a stem over a foot bar" is a **null** (Lasry's "4 with a flag"), in about 20
  places. It is distinct from the true ♀ (O).
* The dotted ring ◉ is the V "eye". CON also serves for COM (comme, combien, complexion). ρ-o-ρ = FF.
* R (E) and K (V) are easily confused. The 4 of I is sometimes a plain cross, like the + of D.
* Code signs not in Lasry's table: the heart (person sign) is **the King** every time ("escript au ♡ et à
  vous", "le ♡ me commanda"). A cross with a spiral or loop head (Lasry's second "unknown") and a large barred
  ƒ-like sign stand where **the Emperor** is expected ("du costé dudict —", "fondement avec ledict —"). f. 47r
  has three more: ⊞, probably the Florentines; ▯ (Lasry's "pencil"), the Pope or the Medici; and a large looped
  ♀, the Emperor. These identifications come from context and are not readings.
