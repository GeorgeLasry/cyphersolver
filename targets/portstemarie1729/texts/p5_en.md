# Port Sainte-Marie report, c. July 1729: English

The obligation I owe you, my Lord, is very great for your letter of the [?]. I answered it this sixth of March at the first address. When you let me know, as I say, it concerns [what follows] the departure of [?]. For Mr Keene had [told] me nothing, and there has been no order for me for weeks [?] in the past affair. I got him to approve [?] this, without his ever telling me what I could do for the best towards the accommodation. [?] I do not know your intentions, and I shall be useless in ignorance. Send me then some order for my conduct ...
