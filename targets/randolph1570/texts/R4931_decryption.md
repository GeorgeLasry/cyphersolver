# R4931, Randolph to Sussex, Edinburgh, 5 July 1570: the letter's cipher runs as deciphered on f. 278 (DECODE R4932), checked against the f. 277 cipher

Copied verbatim from NOTES.md, 22 Oct 2026.

## Reading (clear roman, deciphered *italics*, from f. 278 checked against the f. 277 cipher)

f.277r: "I have receaved your L. lre of the seconde of this present, and what maye answer some parte thereof your L.
hathe I trust seen by myne wrytten from hens the same daye, wch in effecte as this. *The S. Q. partie in Scotland
wyl reke ther owne [?] the S. queene handes [?] fortefy, have litle confidens [in] the Q. of England, that so ofte
changeth her course; and now of late of all, whereas they were determined to have made a regent, dyd staye it*
full sore agaynst their willes, *upon hope of better comfort, and shall be forced to yeld [to] ennemies, and
[be] conforted [with] murderers and traytors, bothe of kyng and regent, and to obbey her whome nether by lawe,*
duetie or conscien they thinke them selves bounde unto. This your L. shall reyde not spoken of my selfe, but this
is a great parte of their greif..." [the words in brackets and "reke", "handes", "fortefy" are the clerk's glosses as
written; the sense of the opening phrase is uncertain in the original decipherment too]

f.277r l.16-18: "...to be sorte wth your L. *it thinke* utterlye *undon*, what so ever *be come of us*. I have
*spoken with therle of Morton*, who is of that mynde that before I have wrytten..."

f.277r l.27: "...*the mony*, whiche I knowe will marveylouslye discomforte him..."

f.277r l.29: "...that the *soldiars may be continewed in pay* untill the Convention be ended..."

f.277v l.14: "I knowe he loketh not *[for] the S. Q. home cumming*."
