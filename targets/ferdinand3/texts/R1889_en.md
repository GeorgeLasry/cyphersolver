# R1889: the Cardinal-Infante to Ferdinand, King of Hungary, Brussels, 16 November 1635: the enciphered passages, English translation

As to the winter quarters, [which Your Royal Dignity and Dilection] desires to be made for the said imperial army in the
archbishopric and city of Trier ... But since the city of Trier has for two hundred years stood under the protection [of
His Royal Catholic Majesty], and [on that account] has been furnished with a garrison by his soldiers [whenever necessity
required it], [as it also was when] the Frenchman, overcoming the royal banners, occupied it, [I think it fitting that]
this royal garrison [be kept] in the said city and the surrounding places, [from which] it will draw the necessary
sustenance, [both for] itself, as is fitting, and for the upkeep of the Prince Elector (who until now has been provided
for from the treasury [of His Royal Catholic Majesty]). [...] [that] winter quarters can hardly be granted to the
imperial soldiers in the said places, [will take it in equally good part].
