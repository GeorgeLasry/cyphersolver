# R1890: Ferdinand III to the Cardinal-Infante, Vienna, 22 February 1640: English translation

The ambassador of the Most Serene Catholic King, the Marquis of Castañeda, will communicate to Your Dilection what we
have discussed with him at greater length concerning the new levy of soldiers begun in the Westphalian Circle by the
Elector of Cologne and the estates of that Circle, and the disadvantages we fear from it to the prejudice of the public
good and also of our House. Since that business therefore needs a swift remedy, and no other means presents itself than
that we too should hastily set up in those parts a levy of soldiers that would extend to a larger number than the said
levy, if we wish otherwise to keep free disposal there and to take care that that Circle is not subjected to another's
will, we have therefore ordered that Count Hatzfeldt should transfer himself at once to Cologne and set his hand to this
work with all the speed he can. But as we lack the necessary funds for it, we again most urgently ask Your Dilection to
give full credence to what the said ambassador will set out to you more distinctly in this matter, and, considering the
gravity of the matter, to be pleased to have the hundred thousand florins requested so far consigned at Cologne to the
keeping of our commissaries there (whom we expressly enjoin to spend nothing of them except by our express order or by
Count Hatzfeldt's assignment).
