# Huang Xing's reply, 25 May 1916: English

Translation of `reply_reading.md` (23 Oct 2026).

## Translation

To brothers Yin[qing] (Lin Hu), Yin[quan] (Li Genyuan), Song[sheng] (Cheng Zikai), Zhao[yun] (Wen Qun), Chang[qing]
(Yang Yongtai) and Xing[yan] (Zhang Shizhao): just as I was sending a letter, your telegram of the 20th arrived, and I
have respectfully noted everything. That the National Protection Army can enter Hunan and Jiangxi quickly is very good.
On what day does Xingyan cross east? I hope he sets out soon; wire me first. Xing. The 25th.
