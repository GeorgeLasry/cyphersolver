# BnF Espagnol 318 f. 122r–v (no. 95, 8 Jan 1497): George Lasry's decipherment of ll. 1–13 and Michael Brackx's reading of the rest

State 21 Oct 2026. Two sources, kept apart:

- **ll. 1–13: George Lasry's decipherment** (May 2022), published by Tomokiyo on cryptiana's GL page as an image,
  "provided by George Lasry" and marked approximate (`targets/esp318/lit/crypt/GL/BnF_es318_f122_decipher.png`). It
  is the reference reading of these lines. Transcribed here from his red overlay; word division and `[ ]` added here.
  Where his overlay leaves a blank over a code group written in clear letters on the leaf, the group is named in
  brackets.
- **ll. 14–33 and the verso: Michael Brackx's reading** (GitHub issue #63, 21 Oct 2026), with Lasry's alphabet and
  sign values he proposes for Lasry's "Unknown" signs. First reading of these lines. Spot-checked here on the scans
  (see NOTES). Line numbers are his 33 line bands of the recto.

Brackx's proposed corrections to Lasry's ll. 1–13 are listed after the text. They are proposals pending George
Lasry's review and do not replace his reading.

Notation: `[bac]` code group, not read; `[burn]` burn hole; `…` unread; `[?]` doubtful. u/v and i/y as deciphered.

## Proposed corrections to Lasry's ll. 1–13 (Brackx 2026, pending Lasry)

Compared here against Lasry's image itself (some of the issue's quotations of the image are loose: his overlay
leaves blanks over the code groups `bon` and `bnh` rather than misreading them, and has *provee como suele mui
tarde*). Only differences of letters are listed; word division and the naming of code groups are not corrections.

| l. | Lasry 2022 | Brackx proposes | sign at issue | checked here on the scan |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | *i* (first sign) | null | the large ꝺ-shaped sign, Unknown in Lasry's table | no |
| 1 | *no a lo ve debir* | *no ay que dezir* | y, q, and the small solid circle = z | no |
| 1 | *[r]emitiendo e* | *remitiendome* | | no |
| 5 | *haben* | *hazen* (*los que hazen la seda*) | small solid circle = z | yes |
| 8 | *dib* | *diz* | small solid circle = z | yes (as *diz que vienen con la mas [bon] que pueden*) |
| 10 | *con la ladras* | *con palabras* | Lasry's P sign, then his second B (⊢) | yes |
| 12 | *hama* | *haga* | ⊥ without the o is Lasry's G, not M | yes |
| 13 | *en no ven estado* | *en no buen estado* | | no |
| 13 | *i a ices* (end) | *y* + null + one unread word | | no |

Signs Brackx proposes for Lasry's "Unknown" row (from two or more words each): the ∝-like alpha with a tail = *l*
(*por alla*, *logaria*); the b-bowl with two crossbars = *z* (*hiziese* ×2); the 8-shape = *a* (*nadie*); the hooked
lightning variant = *d* (*de aqui*); `9.` = *con* or null (l. 13); ꝑ = *h* in *haura* (l. 22). Not in Lasry's table:
the small solid circle = *z* (*hazen*, *dezir*, *diz*); a stroke with two crossbars and no bowl doubles the preceding
consonant (*llegan*, *perdiesse*, *viniesse*); the π/o sign with two crossbars = *o* (*estoruo*). He separates three
double-crossed shapes that read inconsistently as one sign.

Code groups on the leaf: `bac bon bnh def del dae bed dah dal boo`. `dah` (l. 23) and `dal` (ll. 29, 31) are
different groups; `oht`, in Lasry's list, was not found on the leaf. Brackx's contextual suggestions, not readings:
`def` the King of the Romans, Maximilian I (gone to Germany, "nobody knew of his going"), `bed` cardinal (Carvajal,
cardinal of Santa Croce), `dae` the Pope?, `bac` the city the letter concerns, `bon` gente?, `bnh` an Italian power
or prince, `dal` the recipient?, `boo` a commander or fleet.
