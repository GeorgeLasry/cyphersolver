# no. 95: the key

**George Lasry's alphabet (May 2022)** is an image, published by Tomokiyo on cryptiana's GL page: https://cryptiana.web.fc2.com/code/GL/BnF_es318_f122.png (local copy `targets/esp318/lit/crypt/GL/BnF_es318_f122.png`). Homophonic, one to three signs per letter, plus unassigned ("Unknown") signs and the code groups `bac bnh bon dah daf dae oht bed dal boo del`, listed without values. It is not re-typed here.

**Corrections made in this repository (Sept 2026)**, from NOTES.md: the n-with-cross-tail is M, not Y; Lasry's two 7-forms (plain 7 = D, 7 with a small o = S) confirmed.

**Michael Brackx's proposed values (21 Oct 2026, issue #63), pending Lasry's review**, copied verbatim from NOTES.md:

**Sign values proposed for Lasry's "Unknown" signs** (Brackx; for Lasry's review): ∝-like alpha = l; b-bowl with two
crossbars = z; 8-shape = a; hooked lightning variant = d; `9.` = *con* or null; ꝑ = h in *haura*; and, not in
Lasry's table, the small solid circle = z, a two-crossbar stroke that doubles the preceding consonant, and a π/o sign
with two crossbars = o. `dah` (l. 23) and `dal` (ll. 29, 31) are different groups; `oht` was not found.
