# no. 95, f. 122r ll. 1–13: Michael Brackx's proposed corrections to George Lasry's decipherment (21 Oct 2026, pending Lasry)

Copied verbatim from `f122_reading.md` (section "Proposed corrections to Lasry's ll. 1–13 (Brackx 2026, pending Lasry)"). These are proposals from GitHub issue #63; they do not replace Lasry's reading (`no95_decryption_lasry_ll1-13.md`), which stays the reference.

Compared here against Lasry's image itself (some of the issue's quotations of the image are loose: his overlay
leaves blanks over the code groups `bon` and `bnh` rather than misreading them, and has *provee como suele mui
tarde*). Only differences of letters are listed; word division and the naming of code groups are not corrections.

Signs Brackx proposes for Lasry's "Unknown" row (from two or more words each): the ∝-like alpha with a tail = *l*
(*por alla*, *logaria*); the b-bowl with two crossbars = *z* (*hiziese* ×2); the 8-shape = *a* (*nadie*); the hooked
lightning variant = *d* (*de aqui*); `9.` = *con* or null (l. 13); ꝑ = *h* in *haura* (l. 22). Not in Lasry's table:
the small solid circle = *z* (*hazen*, *dezir*, *diz*); a stroke with two crossbars and no bowl doubles the preceding
consonant (*llegan*, *perdiesse*, *viniesse*); the π/o sign with two crossbars = *o* (*estoruo*). He separates three
double-crossed shapes that read inconsistently as one sign.

Code groups on the leaf: `bac bon bnh def del dae bed dah dal boo`. `dah` (l. 23) and `dal` (ll. 29, 31) are
different groups; `oht`, in Lasry's list, was not found on the leaf. Brackx's contextual suggestions, not readings:
`def` the King of the Romans, Maximilian I (gone to Germany, "nobody knew of his going"), `bed` cardinal (Carvajal,
cardinal of Santa Croce), `dae` the Pope?, `bac` the city the letter concerns, `bon` gente?, `bnh` an Italian power
or prince, `dal` the recipient?, `boo` a commander or fleet.
