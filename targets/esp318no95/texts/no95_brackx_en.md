# BnF Espagnol 318 no. 95, f. 122r ll. 14-33 and f. 122v (8 January 1497): English translation of Michael Brackx's reading

[Messer] Juan Luys we have brought here with great trouble, and he shows that he is an enemy [of] France; and the [def]
deals every day with those who govern here and promises them very great things; but I do not believe they will come to
terms, since they tell me everything, although they hide from the [bnh]; and it seems they wish to govern themselves as
[del] may please; and [burn] great evil thoughts against the [bnh]. I do not know [burn] they will put anything into
effect; and I believe Claver will have sensed something, and will write to [dah]; and I hinder the harm of the [bnh] as
much as I can, but I cannot speak of it except with great difficulty. And the [dae] says he wants the [bed] of Santa Cruz
to go to France to procure peace; and certainly, to achieve it as it should be, the best would be for the League [burn] to
do what it ought; it seems that [dal] over there should do what he can, and so the [def] would be begging those who come
to him; otherwise I think this will do more harm than good. [dal] ought to order [boo] that, if necessary, he should come
to defend this, for if it were lost, which God forbid, there would be nothing safe in Italy; and of the [def] I do not
know what follows, except that he is in Germany; and nobody knew of his going, nor is there hope of his coming.
