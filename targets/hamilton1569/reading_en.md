# Mary Queen of Scots to John Hamilton, Archbishop of St Andrews, Bolton, 18 January 1569: modern English translation

Translation (Claude Opus 5.5, 23 Oct 2026) into modern English of `reading.md`, the transcription of the letter's clear
opening and close (Mary's hand) and of the contemporary decipherment of its cipher body (ff. 74r-74v, in Scots, a
secretary hand). The Scots is unpunctuated; sentence breaks here are the translator's. Same marks: `⟨sign⟩` a code sign
the decipherer copied through without resolving (kept as it stands), `[?]` a doubtful word, `[…]` illegible; words the
decipherer struck out are left out, his interlineations are taken in.

## f. 73v, clear close

As concerning the commission you desire us to send to the earls of Argyll, Eglinton, Cassillis and Boyd, we do not
know in what manner you desire the same, for we understand that there is already a general Lieutenant. Always, what
commission you think shall be necessary, advise upon it with the council, have it made in such form as may be
profitable, and send us the same, which we shall sign and put any day [as] the date [cipher, glossed "as shall be
necessary"]. And if it be needful to make such a commission to a Lieutenant, so that he may appoint any of the said
lords his deputies in his absence, send us the same in form and we shall sign it. So we commit you to the protection
of God Almighty. From Bolton, the [18th] of January 1568 [old style, = 1569].

Your good cousin and assured friend, Marie R.
