# Knorre and Schenk von Tautenburg → Duke Wilhelm III of Saxony, 1462-63 (Unsolved Catalogue no. 520)

Status: no write-up. Blocked on access: the volume is lost (SächsHStA Dresden, 10005 Wittenberger Archiv, Loc. 4356/01, "Fehlt: Kriegsfolge"; lost as a result of the war, 1945).

Nothing below rests on a sight of the letters. No images exist online and the originals are recorded as lost.

## The documents

Saxon embassy reports on the Luxembourg succession (Peter Knorre, provost of Wetzlar, and Rudolf Schenk von
Tautenburg, at the Burgundian court in Brussels and Bruges), filed in SächsHStA Dresden, 10005 Hof- und
Zentralverwaltung (Wittenberger Archiv), section 061 Luxemburg, Loc. 4356/01 "Luxemburgische Sachen, I. Einzelne
Schriften", 1400/1485. Item records on Archivportal-D, with the full titles on archiv.sachsen.de:

- Bl. 540, [1462]: "Bericht D. Knorres an Herzog Wilhelm, vollständig chiffriert. Ein Teil ist dechiffriert,
  worin die Hoffnung ausgesprochen wird, dass von Burgund ebensoviel werde erlangt werden als von Frankreich
  versprochen worden sei" (fully in cipher; part of it is deciphered: they hope to get as much from Burgundy as
  France had promised). https://www.archivportal-d.de/item/U3X2S4D5M5BBDK2KYT2TTKJZBSKWLUBD.
  Bemerkung: "Fehlt: Kriegsfolge."
- Bl. 538, [1462]: Knorre to Wilhelm, "vollständig chiffriert", Fehlt: Kriegsfolge.
  https://www.archivportal-d.de/item/F76RU5Q6UFIPD4U2XIFKBIMOCGPD4LTK
- Bl. 575, [1463]: "Bericht (D. P. Knorres an Herzog Wilhelm?), größtenteils chiffriert. Sine dato (lag bei den
  Schriften von März 1463)", Fehlt: Kriegsfolge. https://www.archivportal-d.de/item/JW2SA4UGVDTWKIVVED4LXBMUZ5YTW5RX
- Another "Bericht D. P. Knorres an Herzog Wilhelm, größtenteils chiffriert":
  https://www.archivportal-d.de/item/YS3YTSOL2CRM7BSQ4FFBG6CLST2SGG54
- Bl. 530, [1462]: "Zettel mit Nachrichten, zum Teil chiffriert, deshalb meist unverständlich", Fehlt: Kriegsfolge.
- Bl. 531, [1462]: the embassy at Brussels to Wilhelm, "teils chiffriert".
- "Vollständig chiffrierter Befehl Herzog Wilhelms. 62", Fehlt: Kriegsfolge (Wilhelm's ciphered reply):
  https://www.archivportal-d.de/item/TKO62VCJFQ75SVSZTYSELKNHNFAKMROG
- "Aufsatz über gesandtschaftliche Verhandlungen, unverständlich, da das Wesentliche in Chiffren ...":
  https://www.archivportal-d.de/item/3D2JICIGHGPKYXJBQAPDA27IU5YR3DWN
- Catalogue no. 520 also lists Bl. 380, 490, 494, 564, 565, 572 and 574.

The volume record itself, "Luxemburgische Sachen, I. Einzelne Schriften (Fehlt: Kriegsfolge.)",
https://www.archivportal-d.de/item/L3WJHZEDY6EIIZ5T5WHDHWLNCZ2FSTWZ, carries the loss note. The item-level
records were made before 1945 and copied into the online finding aid. So every sheet of the volume is gone,
including the Bl. 540 crib and the sibling Bl. 252 that `research/archivportal_d/README.md` names. Brice Dudout
reported the loss notes by email on 27 Oct 2026. This session checked them on archiv.sachsen.de on 28 Oct 2026.

## Prior art

None found. Web searches on 28 Oct 2026 covered Knorre + Wilhelm III + Luxemburg 1462 + Burgund + chiffriert,
Knorre as provost of Wetzlar + 1463 + Philip the Good, and the Luxembourg regest series (Würth-Paquet). They found
no edition of the reports and no decipherment. An Archivportal-D search for "Knorre" (607 hits) turned up no
second copy outside Dresden. One related Dresden item is a clear-text "Befehl" from Wilhelm to Knorre and
Tautenburg (Weimar, Sunday before Vincula Petri 62): https://www.archivportal-d.de/item/OSY3PORJN7DZPEWKSFZO2DZCKCX2GWPY

## Remaining gaps

- All the ciphered sheets: needs-physical-access. In fact they are lost: the originals were destroyed or went
  missing in 1945. No image, transcription or edition is known. Nothing can be transcribed or measured.

## Escalation

- Siblings: every sibling in Loc. 4356/01 is lost with the volume. The ciphered reply ("Befehl", 1462) is lost too.
- Clear pages / crib: the deciphered part of Bl. 540 is known only from the archivist's one-line summary. That is
  not enough for a crib.
- Known keys: no Saxon key of the 1460s is on DECODE or in the repo.
- Print and copies still unchecked (outside leads, not workable here):
  1. Ask SächsHStA whether a pre-war film or photograph of Loc. 4356/01 survives (Ersatzverfilmung), or whether
     the volume came back after 1945 (some "Kriegsverlust" files returned from the USSR).
  2. 19th-century studies of Wilhelm III's Luxembourg claim that quote the Dresden reports.
  3. The Burgundian side: AGR Brussels and ADN Lille, series B, for copies or intercepts.
  4. ThHStA Weimar, Ernestinisches Gesamtarchiv, for duplicates of Wilhelm's Thuringian chancery.
