# Sessa to Charles V, Rome, 18 April 1524 (RAH Salazar, f. 128, R9877): English translation

Having written and closed the packet of the duplicate of the 14th of this month, which will come with this, I saw a
letter of the Bishop Verulano, who is at Constance, of which I send a copy herewith. I then sent to His Holiness to beg
him to have me told of any particular, so as to give notice of it where fitting. He sent me Felice, his secretary, who
has told me that the letters he has are from the said Verulano, of the 6th and 7th of this month, where he says that
the diet was over, and that Lucerne did not conclude to give help to the French; that the cantons of Bern, Fribourg and
Solothurn — and to make it easier and win over the minds of the people they publish that the Swiss in Lombardy were
surrounded and in great danger — with which he was gathering up to the number of eight thousand, who gave out that
they would leave very soon, but that he could not know when, being at Constance, where some of his friends from
Switzerland wrote to him to exhort His Holiness to seek some good form of peace. This is what he has reported to me.

The said [?vo]s they did not show him, for they make him suspect that [he] wishes to have something to report of the
above […] […] not. It seems to me that it agrees closely with the first and last things that the said bishop says to
the master of the posts. I spoke at once to His Holiness to ask him that there be provision [?for] the affairs of the
Swiss, persuading him los m-re que r-ido los que no m-r-e-van, since he has a pretext for it, having begun the talk of
peace and truce r-[…]. I shall learn what more I can. I am writing to the Viceroy on the same point.
