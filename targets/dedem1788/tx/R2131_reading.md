# R2131, Van Dedem to Van de Spiegel, Pera 9 Feb 1793: the cipher paragraph (p. 4)

Rests on: the cipher as re-read on DECODE IMG_R2131_I15124_P4 (`R2131_cipher.txt`, corrections in
`R2131_corrections.tsv`), decoded group by group with the codebook "Kroll. Un Chiffre", Nationaal Archief 1.02.20
(legatie Turkije) inv. 785, scans 0002-0005 (values in `../key/kroll785_values.tsv`, decode in `R2131_decode.txt`).
The rest of the letter (pp. 1-4) is in clear Dutch and is not transcribed here.

Conventions: words are carried by their groups; `[?word]` partly carried; `[word]` supplied (nothing carries it);
spelling as the codebook gives it. The opening groups 701 1996 2504 3445 and the closing 3739 2364 901 301 are the
code's "begin des briefs" / "einde des briefs" / "fin de la lettre" markers (1996 = "eerd", a null before the last
begin marker, as the 1775 Berigt in inv. 686 prescribes) and are not read.

## Reading

Ik heb de eer UwHoEdGestr: te informeeren [?als dat] de Ottomanische Porta sig op eergister volkomen aan de
Envoyé van Pruissen [?verbonden] heeft, om gedurende deze oorlog met de Franschen sig in [?alle] [?deelen] neutraal te
houden, zonder eenige alliantie meer [met] meergemelde Natie aan te gaande.

## Notes

- 1245 = "ik heb de eer"; 2576 = "UwHo:Ed:Gestr:"; 633 1108 = eer + gister ("eergister", the day before yesterday).
- 317? "als dat": the last digit is doubtful (311 = afzonder gives no sense).
- 2535 591 = "verbond" + "g": the codebook has no "verbonden"; the "g" (591, foot table of column 501) is unexplained,
  so "verbonden" is only partly carried.
- 770 541 = fransch + en = "Franschen"; 1510 985 696 = meer + gemeld + e = "meergemelde".
- 319 = "Son Altesse" in the codebook; the sense needs "alle", which is 314 in the same column (a slip of five rows is
  likely but not proven), so "[?alle]"; 502 = "deel" without the ending "en".
- "met" before "meergemelde Natie" is not in the groups.
- 973 = "gaande" (the codebook has no "gaan" in that slot); kept as written.
