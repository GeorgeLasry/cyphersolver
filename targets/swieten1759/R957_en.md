# R957: van Swieten to Cobenzl, Bonn, 14 January 1759, cipher passage, English

Translation of `R957_reading.md` (23 Oct 2026, Claude Opus 5.5, batch b36).

## Reading

… of the administration; the new convention leaves the revenues to France while reserving the sovereignty to the
Empress. The French go on acting on that assumption, although we have no order from the court on the matter; this
causes a great deal of ill feeling. On France's behalf the Prince de Croÿ, who has claims to it, has just been put in
possession of the principality of Moers, without so much as informing the Empress, who is its sovereign.
