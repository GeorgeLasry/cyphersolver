## Translation

If he is there? I shall see Monsieur d'Entragues, whom I [shall] make speak to me frankly, and he will tell me the state of the Duke of Lennox's affairs. On that I shall be able to build something good. You know how easy it is to raise a revolt in England, how many malcontents there are, as many on account of religion as other malcontents living in the country. Scotland will favour an enterprise. I see it as easy because of the short distance England is from you. [?] you will tell him that it will be easy for you to have the King of Spain put his forces and means into the hands of some French prince; or else, if he will not do so, nothing will have to be done against him, if it must be so. And I assure you that he does not mean to deceive you; a French prince will make the landing if need be, [?] you will be able to make it; he will be beaten? at his own cost. And if God allows this conquest to be made, the said King of Spain will wish it to be for you, and because you will be invested with it you would have nothing to fear; and then the marriage of his daughter with you will take place on your return from the country, when the places you will have put in deposit are handed back. That is what Monsieur d'Entragues thinks you might say; but do not reveal yourself further, for I should fear that Monsieur himself might pretend to listen to it in order to ruin those you know of, who I fear will not be willing to trust because of that wretch Morres?. As soon as Monsieur d'Entragues is at court, I shall work at building something good while waiting for your news; and if you were sent to come over here, let me know of it in good time.
