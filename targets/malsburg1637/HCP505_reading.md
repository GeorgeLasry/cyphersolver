# HCPortal 505 (HStAM 4 h Nr. 1411, ff. 23-24), Malsburg to Landgrave Wilhelm V, 7/17 Feb 1637 — reading of the cipher

Rests on: `f023.txt` and `f024.txt` (Bourdeau's transcription of scans 0023 and 0024, one cipher line per scan line)
decoded with `key.txt` unchanged (`research/reading_audit/b08_dec.py`), the breaks checked on the HCPortal original
scans hstam_4_h_1411_0023 and 0024 (fetched 23 Oct 2026 into the session scratchpad; batch r2b, log
`HCP497-506_reread_log.md`). No contemporary decipherment, clear copy or edition (NOTES, Escalation). Code values:
`{Cöln}` = 128, `{die Staaten}` = 188 (grade I, `codes.txt`).

Conventions: `{...}` a code with a context value; `[120]` an unvalued code; `<f>` an unvalued sign; `[?word]` partly
carried; `[x]` a letter or word no figure carries; `[…]` unread. Spelling as the cipher gives it (u/v, i/j merged by the
key); nouns capitalised and punctuation added. ⟨...⟩ = clear words. The crossed signs: Z# = st (Estat, Stillstant), as
the plain Z; 7 = st (gestalt), as in f. 28. "1 t. Reichst." = 1 thousand Reichsthaler (t = tausend, as written).

## Notes

- f. 23 block 1 follows "… durch beygelegte verpflichtung heraus gegeben,"; the cipher runs on to the top of f. 24
  ("…burg wegen der [140] …"); f. 24 block 2 follows "… ist er zwar nicht abgeneigt, allein".
- "[?gestehen]": `13 79 48 O 61 21 49 63` = (nicht)s e e st e h e n: an e-group where g is needed.
- "<f> Gelidder": the underlined f-sign, no value (also before "Verehrungen").
- "dan[?n] verpflichtet": `55 31 P 71 48 30 12 23` = a n ss u e r p f: the ss-sign after "dan" gives no word.
- "[?alle] Gefangene": `O? 62 H 79` = st a ll e (the st-sign doubtful in the transcription): "stalle" gives no word.
- "uns an. [?…] beit sich": `88 44 74 39 83 77 Y 42 49 30 | 76 Y 60 74 66 N` = u n s a n s ei m e r | b ei t s i ch,
  checked on the scan: "seimer" gives no word ("er beut sich" is the sense; the letters do not give it). "1 t. Reichst."
  = `1 60 25 Y D 74 26` on the scan.
- "[?doch]": the line-final `3` (on the scan a 3 with a blot) then `35 N` = o ch: a 3 where a d-group is needed.
- "[?selbige]": `37 35 99 26 49 79 84 15 10 11 54 16` = w o l t e e e l b i g e, checked on the scan: an e-group
  where s is needed.
- "Wir [?…] weise": `18 66 97 10 10 16 25 24 23 40 11 16 | 37 Y 13 84` = w i r b b e r u f z i e | w ei s e,
  checked on the scan; "aber auf die Weise" would need a, a and d where the figures give b, (none) and z (the writer
  puts a z-group for d also in "zer" and "gehanzelt" in this letter). Left open.
- "[?vermoge] Verschreibung": `58 61 75 {struck 42? 73} 29 48` = u e o (struck m o) g e: the writer struck two groups;
  "vermoge" needs an r.
- "[?in der] Bruhe": `11 49 32 61 25` = i e d e r: no n-group.
- "in [d]er That", "gehan[d]elt": `11 31 52 16 25`, `83 40 61 15 94`: a z-group (52, 40) stands where d is needed.
- "fellig gewesene <H#> t. Reichst.": `… 61 H#? 60 25 Y D 13 60`: the crossed H before "t Reichst" is a figure sign, no value read.
- "[?angenomene] [?newe] Werbungen": `70 63 43 16 22 84 31 16 18 49 98 48 30 76 …` = a n g e n e n e w e w e r b …:
  the letters give "angenene wewerbungen".
- "angewend[e]t [w]erden": `61 22 32 27 26 19 61 25 27 79 83` = e n d d t c e r d e n.
- "Estat" = `79 Z# 70 60`; "Stillstant" = `Z# 66 H Z 70 63 26` (crossed Z = st as the plain Z).
- "als [?…] einen": `39 15 13 37 70 84 G G Y 22 48 83` = a l s w a e ei ei ei n e n: no word before "einen".
- "[?Artret]": `39 30 26 25 79 94` = a r t r e t, perhaps a place in Picardy; not identified.
- "tr[a]ctiren": `94 97 89 80 96?` = t r o c t: an o-group where a is needed.
- "Schuldern, sorge al[?s]o": `X 71 15 27 48 30 44 77 35 97 29 49 39 15 19? 73`; the c-group 19 is doubtful.
- "<S> t. Reichst.", "das <F> <F>": the au-signs S and F stand alone where no "au" fits.
- "Soldaten": the crossed 39 (a) between d and t.
- "weil es mit de[m] Reiderlant": `98 Y [blot] 15 16 13 56 11 60 32 61 {struck 57} 25 G 46 84 97 99 70 22 41` = w ei
  [blot] l e s m i t d e (struck m) r ei d e r l a n t: the writer struck the m of "dem"; the Reiderland (East
  Frisia) is named again on f. 28.
- "[M]eckelburg": the writer struck a 57 (m) and wrote "eckelburg"; the m is supplied.
- "einzuschi<8>en": a large 8 where ff is needed (sense "einzuschiffen"), left as the sign.
- "la<V>en": the triangle sign stands where ss is needed.
- "bringen [?…]": the cipher ends with an sch-sign before the clear text.
