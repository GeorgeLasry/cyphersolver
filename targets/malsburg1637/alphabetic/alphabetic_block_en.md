# Malsburg, Wesel, 7/17 January 1637 (HCPortal 497, image 0012): the alphabetic block, English translation

Translation (Claude Opus 5.5, 23 Oct 2026) of `alphabetic/alphabetic_block_reading.md`, the contributors' editorial
German of the ten-line block. Same marks: `[43]`, `[45]`, `[LL]` unidentified code labels; `[?word]` an uncertain
sign or interpretation; `†word†` an unresolved literal form (translated as far as it carries, kept between daggers);
`[BLOT]` a blot. The contributors' own cautious English, with their alternatives, is in `alphabetic_reading.md`.

## Translation

The [43] is now on the point of leaving. If [45] wish to be rid of him and can do without him, it is now time for
[LL] to write: since they see that he †would† not come to agreement with the Swedish generals over the command, but
the state of [45] required that they join their †troops† [?troops] to the Swedes and the Swedes in turn to them, and
also [LL]'s actions no longer pleased him, and moreover they could not give him such absolute authority as he
demanded, they left it to what [?pleased] him to do; nor would they keep him back against his convenience, but would
have him satisfied according to [?their] †present† [?present] state and ability.
