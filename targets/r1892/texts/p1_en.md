# R1892 page 1: English

Translation of `p1_reading.md` (23 Oct 2026), same holes.

## Translation

Written in cipher in the letter of […] dated […] December.

My father is charging Nagel to go again to the ministers to insist on some answer to the various representations
made to them concerning the matter of the rassemblement; I venture to flatter myself that at last, [if it] is ended,
one will come. But as it might happen in the meantime that General Dundas declares to you […] that he will stop the
pay, or else that the troops must let themselves be embarked, you would have to subscribe to it and limit yourself
solely to trying to obtain leave to give a gratification to those who would not submit, in case you were […] obliged
to this. I ask you, however, to make known to the troops on what footing they will enter the service of His Britannic
Majesty, and for that purpose you may give them a paper roughly like the one that was meant to be given to them at
the first [moment] when there was question of their embarkation. You can find this paper among [my] papers, and you
can make in it the changes you judge fitting in present circumstances. Be so good as not to put on the account for
[…] the expense of my lodging, [not wishing] […] to be under the least obligation, but to pay it from the money of
mine that you hold.
