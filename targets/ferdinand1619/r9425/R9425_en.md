# R9425: Archduke Albert's resolution for Licentiate Morreus, [Brussels], 15 December 1619: English translation

**f. 278r.** Albert, by the grace of God [Archduke …]. We have praised the letter … and what … title: ⟨45⟩ and ⟨149⟩ of
the said lord ⟨45⟩ L. ⟨218⟩ and agent Licentiate Morreus [… and other] highly important and pressing matters [… notwith-
standing Your Dilection's letter, both orally and in writing most humbly presented and devoutly, heard and understood at
greater length, and have it shown to him for our good declaration … as follows …].

We would also gladly have understood from the request and from Morreus's representation that Their Dilections have
commended to themselves the preservation and maintenance ⟨160⟩ of our most gracious, friendly, beloved lord and ⟨31⟩
Imperial Highness, and of our most praiseworthy ⟨154⟩ interest, as faithfully. And we must therefore thank Their
Dilections most cordially for having it communicated to us so trustingly. And we beg Their Dilections that they will
continue so with us henceforth without hesitation, and be assured that we will meet Their Dilection with the same
correspondence in return, and will recognise in every possible way and gratefully requite the faithful, cousinly
devotion they show to us and to our most praiseworthy ⟨154⟩.

entirely agreed with Their Dilection that on the side of our ⟨154⟩ every utmost power should rightly be applied to that
end; and also that others, Their Imperial ⟨47⟩ and Royal Majesty and Dilection, to ⟨225⟩ and. We [are] entirely agreed
that ⟨41⟩ princes and estates, who are to give us some ⟨157⟩ and assistance to that end. We have therefore also, with
the said most exalted [Majesty] and Dilection, [in] good time … rendered all possible faithful good offices, and in part
already {Majesty} and Dilection ⟨215⟩, but are still awaiting further answer. Meanwhile, nevertheless, [we have] so far
resolved that, in any unexpected case, since ai[…] ⟨155⟩ and some troops ire[…] now on foot,

**f. 279r.** to turn the army thereto [and] send up the Catholic ⟨41⟩ princes and estates and ⟨221⟩ districts; on the
other hand, would also at once order up as many of the Hungarians for the rescue and defence of the oppressed Catholic
estates, with the express command to join the Catholic troops and to submit to and obey whoever shall command them and be
set over them. As regards a muster place, and also a regiment of foot and five hundred ⟨216⟩, we would indeed, in view
of the highest need and general danger, be inclined to oblige Their Dilections in a friendly, cousinly way, as gladly as
we would wish; but we cannot gratify Their Dilections in this at present, however gladly we would, considering our own
daily levies and also … the reflections concerned, and they are destined for our ⟨136⟩ Luxembourg, in particular because
of its situation.

**f. 279v.** Since ⟨149⟩ L. hesitates to have the fortress of Breisach garrisoned with the lord ⟨41⟩'s troops, we are
