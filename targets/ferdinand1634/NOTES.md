# Ferdinand (King of Hungary and Bohemia) to Maximilian von Trauttmansdorff, 13 Nov 1634 — catalogue 359

Status: closed unread 26 Oct 2026 (not solved: ciphertext not obtainable online); written up as docs/ferdinand1634.html.

SOA Plzeň, pracoviště Klášter u Nepomuka, Rodinný archiv Trauttmansdorffů, karton 6, inv. č. 68.
Catalogue 359 (class B), from Cryptoole HUXXXX-1 / Cryptiana's unsolved list (Tomokiyo), source Mírka 2012.

## What is known (Mírka 2012)

Jakub Mírka, "Raně novověká šifrovaná korespondence ve fondech šlechtických rodinných archivů Státního oblastního
archivu v Plzni", Západočeské archivy 2012, pp. 44-73. Offprint supplied by Daniel on 26 Oct 2026; read for
notes 37-39 and 135 and the passages they belong to:
- The item is a contemporary copy (opis) of Ferdinand's letter of 13 Nov 1634, the only enciphered one among the
  copies of Ferdinand's letters to Trauttmansdorff in the family archive.
- A clear passage at the end says Ferdinand enciphers the letter and sends it by his own messenger.
- "The cipher alphabet is formed exclusively of two- and three-digit numbers" (n. 39). From the number and form of
  the signs Mírka takes it for a nomenclator with homophones, bigrams and probably codes.
- Not deciphered; Mírka could not break the key (n. 38). He suggests the matching key may be in Vienna, in FA
  Trauttmansdorff or the Haus-, Hof- und Staatsarchiv.
- The article reproduces 17 figures; none is this letter.

  The archive library's Portaro catalogue (knihovna.soaplzen.cz, directory 260104) holds only the cover and table of
  contents. No sign that RA Trauttmansdorffů is digitised.
- ResearchGate publication 313678538: behind a security check (CAPTCHA), not passed.
- Šimsová, "Šifrovaná korespondence Matyáše Gallase s císařem Ferdinandem III." (Charles University MA thesis 2024,
  hdl 20.500.11956/191516): edits Ferdinand's 1635-38 cipher letters to Gallas, deciphered at the time; their key
  does not survive (p. 108). Does not mention this letter.

## Sibling and key check

- Ferdinand's own cipher of 28 Oct 1634 (DECODE R1887, targets/ferdinand3) was the planned first test. It is
  graphic signs plus three-letter codes; this letter is all numbers, so it is a different system. Not applicable.
- Ernst's "Zifra Piccolominea" (Ferdinand III to Leopold Wilhelm, 1640, number pairs 00-44): a later two-digit
  system of a different office; not testable without the ciphertext.
