# George Lasry, "The cipher documents of Concistoro 2308, fasc. 2" (17 Oct 2026)

Consolidated report sent by email to Daniel Bourdeau, 17 October 2026. The report gives this piece no section of
its own and no appendix part; it lists it on its contents page only. Quoted verbatim:

Contents page (p. 3), heading **H. Pieces with an interlinear or separate plaintext**:

> • No. 7 = R4796 — c. 330 symbols, a few words with interlinear plaintext — unsolved as of 17 October 2026

Heading H groups nos. 9, 13/16, 10, 7 and 8 by the kind of plaintext they carry. The contents page also says "Pieces under one heading share a cipher key"; for nos. 7 and 8 that cannot hold: the gloss of no. 7 gives 7 = l, + = o, 2 = e, the decipherment of no. 8 gives 7 = d, + = c, 2 = a (this project, 18 Oct 2026).
