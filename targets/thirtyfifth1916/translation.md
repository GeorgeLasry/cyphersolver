# “Thirty fifth” table (Hu Hanmin): English translation

Working translations from the Wenmi site; […] = passage not translated for want of a sure reading.

## 0627 | 22 April 1916 | Tokyo → Shanghai (Junzaburo) | JACAR B03050088600, PDF page 23 | sent 10.15 am

To be forwarded to Hu Hanmin. The Japanese government has decided to support Cen [Chunxuan]; its intentions are unfathomable: the situation must be retrieved urgently. I ask you, brother, as if on your own initiative, to consult in secret with Tang Shaochuan (Tang Shaoyi) and to persuade Yang Shande to declare independence; to take Shanghai as the provisional seat and organise a government there: Tang prime minister, […] for the Army, Wu [Tingfang] for Foreign Affairs, Chen Yingshi (Chen Qimei) commander-in-chief, Feng Guozhang chief of staff, Duan Qirui custodian (留守) of Peking; […] Yuan: the conditions of preferential treatment will be decided by us. In this way internal troubles and foreign affairs could be settled at one stroke. I hope you will work hard for it; reply quickly. — Wen (Sun Yat-sen).

## 0642b | 24 April 1916 | Shanghai → Tokyo (Rosamonde) | JACAR B03050088600, PDF page 38 | sent 6.26 pm

Tang [Shaoyi] represents Yunnan (滇) and favours electing Li [Yuanhong]; moreover, there is no way to move Lu [Yongxiang] or Yang [Shande]. — Zhan (展, Hu Hanmin).
