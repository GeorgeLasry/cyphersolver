# De Burgo cipher: sign-by-sign alignment with the contemporary glosses (27 Oct 2026)

Pages: 25-2-27 image 04 (de Burgo, 12 Aug 1511, cipher block of 15 lines), b22-3-18 image 08 (fol. 32v, 21 cipher
lines), g25-2-25 image 02 (fol. 40v, top lines). Crops: `img/small/bt_27_<line>_<third>.png` (2x),
`bt_18_<line>_<third>.png` (2.4x), zooms `bt_z*.png` (2.4-3x). Line numbers here count cipher lines only
(25-2-27 l. 1 = native y 1492; b22-3-18 l. 1 = native y 851).

Sign names follow `key_delaude.md`. New names used here (shape):

| name | shape |
|---|---|
| `Δ` | open triangle / capital delta, sometimes with a short tail |
| `dd` | o with a straight upright stem on the right (plain "d" shape); the key's `ꝺ` (looped round d) is treated as the same sign unless noted |
| `4` | figure 4 (= the key's `U`) |
| `2‡` | figure 2 whose foot is crossed by a vertical stroke (= the key's `2A`) |
| `ttt` | three or four minims under one bar (= key `TTT`); `ttte` when followed by the round e |
| `e` | round c-like e (the key's `e`/`E` round form, not `ꝑ`) |
| `Γ` | gamma / r-like sign with a tall descending stem |
| `Γc` | Γ joined to a c (= the key's `G`, "ʃe", long s with looped e) |
| `H` | capital H |
| `x` | plain x (key `X`) |
| `+o` | small cross joined to o (= key `D+`) |
| `ʃ̡` | long s with a hook at the top ("ſ" turned) |
| `⊓` | square box open at the bottom, with a bar (`ʃ̄` in the first notes) |
| `ꝑo` | p with crossed descender, followed by `otto` / `otto` code groups: see the word signs |

Alignment notation: `sign=letter`; `sign=(x)` = sign at the place of letter x but doubtful; `-` = plain letter
not written (dropped); `?` = sign not aligned. Confidence per word: **A** = every sign accounted for and the
gloss word is certain; **B** = gloss word certain, one or two signs doubtful; **C** = alignment uncertain (not
used for the tally unless stated).

## b22-3-18 image 08 (de Burgo and the envoys, Blois, July 1510; fol. 32v)

The glossator writes a continuous sentence over each cipher line and separates gloss words with short vertical
strokes; gloss words are not always above their cipher words (they drift left or right by one group). Not every
cipher group is glossed in a way I can read. Only words whose alignment holds letter by letter are graded A/B.

Sign names added for this hand (shape):
`ȡ` = o with a straight upright stem rising on the right (the key's `ꝺ`/`dd`); `ɑ` = round open a/o with a short tail
(key `O`); `+o` = small cross joined to an o (key `D+`); `‡` = t with two crossbars (key `A+`); `ƀ` = b with one or two
crossbars (key `I`); `đ` = d with a crossed ascender (key `T`); `ff` = crossed ff / A-like double stroke (key `FF`);
`ttt` = minims under one bar (key `TTT`); `ſe` = long s with looped e (key `G`); `s` = small plain s / 5-like (key `5`);
`r` = small r / v-like hook (key `v`); `q` = 9-like with crossed descender (key `q`); `gt` = g joined to t (key `Q`);
`4` = figure 4 (key `U`); `e` = round c-like e; `H` = capital H; `Δ` = open triangle; `Φ` = circle with a vertical stroke;
`⊞` = boxed cross; `⊓` = box open below; `ʃ` = long s without loop; `ω` = w-like double loop; `ħ` = h with a crossbar;
`7` = Tironian et (⁊); `20` = the `ro`/`2o` et sign; `93` = the M.V./Cesar sign; `m` = plain three-minim m; `L` = L with
a long foot; `δ` = 8-like s with a tail (key `S`).
