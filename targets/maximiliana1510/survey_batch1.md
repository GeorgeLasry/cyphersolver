## Per-item summaries

**22-2-51 (Veit von Fürst, Rome, 25 June 1510).** Code-group cipher (letter-like and digit groups embedded in
Latin clear text) on images 01-07, about 89 lines carrying cipher in all (01: ~6; 02: ~20; 03: ~22; 04: ~16; 05: ~15;
06: ~9; 07: 1). Every passage has interlinear contemporary glosses; coverage is full or near full. The only groups seen
without their own gloss are on image 02: line 18 a short group after `8` (gloss `&` covers only the `8`) and the last
line, where only part of the group string carries glosses (`ope`, `&`). Image 08 is the address leaf.

**22-2-55 (Andrea de Burgo, Blois, 28/29 June 1510).** 16 images, one cipher passage only: image 08 (fol. 88v), about
21 lines of letter-sign cipher filling the top two-thirds of the page, fully glossed interlinearly word by word. The
rest is clear Latin (letter, memorial and dated notes of 28 June), blank versos, the address leaf (10) and a clear
pasted slip (08/09). Every cipher passage is deciphered.

**22-2-56 (Veit von Fürst, Rome, 30 June 1510).** Code groups on all eight images, about 88 lines carrying cipher
(01: ~8; 02: ~12; 03: ~18; 04: ~3; 05: ~8; 06: ~17; 07: ~21; 08: 1 plus two sign-names). Interlinear glosses are
everywhere, but they get thinner on the densest pages: images 03, 06 and especially 07 leave many short groups without a
gloss of their own. A gloss word can stand for two or three groups, so some of these are covered (multi-group words).
Others are probably unglossed, notably at line ends.

**22-3-5 (Andrea de Burgo, Blois, 2 July 1510).** One passage: image 04 (fol. 8v), from the end of line 7 through
line 15, about 8 lines of letter signs, fully glossed interlinearly. Images 01-03 are clear text. Fully deciphered.

**22-3-8 (Veit von Fürst, Rome, 4 July 1510).** Code groups on all four images, about 69 lines carrying cipher (01:
~15; 02: ~22; 03: ~19; 04: ~13). Glossed interlinearly throughout, but only partly on 02 and 03 (about 70-75% of
groups carry a gloss), and on 01 at about 80-85%. Many line-initial and line-final groups have no gloss.

**22-3-18 (de Burgo and the imperial envoys, Blois, 10-12 July 1510; files b22-3-18_*).** 18 images. Two cipher
passages, both letter signs in de Burgo's own letter: image 08 (fol. 32v, ~21 lines, ll. 4-24) and image 15
(fol. 36, ~21 lines, ll. 2-21). Both are fully glossed interlinearly. Everything else is clear (the joint envoys'
letter of 10 July with its postscripts, de Burgo's letter and postscript of 11 July), blank, or address leaves
(12, 14, 18).

**25-2-25 (de Burgo, Valencia, 10 August 1511; files g25-2-25_*).** Two passages of letter signs: image 02 (fol. 40v,
5 lines at the top) and image 08 (fol. 43v, a 6-line cipher postscript written small at the lower left, after the
date "xj"). Both are fully glossed interlinearly, as the archive's description ("Chiffren sind interlinear aufgelöst")
says. Fully deciphered.

**25-2-27 (de Burgo, Valencia, 12 August 1511).** One passage: image 04 (fol. 46v), about 17 lines of letter signs
(ll. 8-24), fully glossed interlinearly. Images 01-03 are clear. Fully deciphered.

**25-1-36 (de Burgo, Grenoble, 15 June 1511).** One passage: image 02 (fol. 55v), about 3.5 lines of letter signs at
mid-page, fully glossed interlinearly. The rest is clear text and the address leaf. Fully deciphered. Image 03 refers to
a separate cipher letter ("in zefra") sent to the Bishop of Gurk, which is not part of this item.

No separate decipherment slip or leaf was found in any of these items. All decipherments are interlinear.
