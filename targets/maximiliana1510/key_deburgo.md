# Andrea de Burgo's cipher (1510-1512): sign table from the glossed lines (27 Oct 2026)

Built from the letter-by-letter alignments in `bt_alignment.md`: b22-3-18 image 08 (fol. 32v, Blois July 1510,
lines 1-18), 25-2-27 image 04 (fol. 46v, Valencia 12 Aug 1511, lines 1-2; lines 3-4 held out as the control),
g25-2-25 image 02 (fol. 40v, Valencia 10 Aug 1511, first full cipher line). Only words graded A or B in the
alignment file are counted. "Count" = number of glossed letter positions where the sign was aligned with that
value. Sign names follow `key_delaude.md` where the shape is the same; the de Burgo hand is larger and clearer than
de Laude's, so several shapes are described again.

System (confirmed): one sign per letter, word gaps kept; vowels dropped freely inside words (`debent` = d b n t,
`sapientem` = s p n m, `faceret` = f a r t), written vowels have their own signs; whole-word signs for *et*,
*pro* and the addressee; a few name groups.

## Summary of conflicts with `key_delaude.md`

1. `e` (round c-like e) = **u** here (3 words); key: c (one word). The key's reading of *commendat* (`e Gm E FF c T`)
   should be re-checked.
2. `Δ` = **b** here (3 words, A-graded); key: p?/c? unsettled.
3. `ʒ`/`3` = **b** here (1 A-graded word + 2 C); key: r (de Laude, 2 words). Either the two writers differ for this
   sign, or the yogh with a tail (de Laude) and the plain 3 (de Burgo) are two signs.
4. `ʃ`/`ſ` = s / p / l here; key: c (one word). The long-s family is not settled.
5. `Φ` = s once (sapientem) beside b (debitis; key: b).
6. `đ` = d once (agendo) beside t (5 times; key t).
7. `4` = i in *illum* (twice, same word).
8. `⊙` = p (plus; ipsi): the key listed `⊙` as never glossed.
9. `~` = n (3x *non*), settling the key's "n? / r?".
