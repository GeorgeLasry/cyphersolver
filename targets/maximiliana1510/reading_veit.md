# Veit von Fürst: the unglossed groups, read one by one

Key: `key_veit.md` (sign names as there). Every group was cropped in context from the full-resolution image
(`img/small/vf_*.png`). Line numbers follow the surveys (`survey_batch1.md`, `survey_batch2.md`): written lines of the
main text from the top of the page. "Covered" = the group is part of a word whose gloss stands over another piece of
it (a word split over a line end, or a sign-name abbreviation), so the decipherer did read it.

A layout rule found on 22-3-8 f. 11 (img 01): when a cipher word does not fit at a line end, Fürst writes its head at
the right end of the line and its tail at the left start of the SAME line (fenced off by a vertical stroke `|` on
img 01 ll. 20-21). The glossator writes the whole word over whichever piece he reached first. This explains most
"line-initial" and "line-end" groups the surveys listed as unglossed.

## Count

Every group named in the two surveys for 22-2-51, 22-2-56, 22-3-8, 22-3-42, 23-3-6 and the two 23-2-62 margin
groups, plus a few the surveys flagged with "e.g."/"include" that were found next to them: **59 rows**.

- **Read firm: 40.** Of these, 18 turn out to be *covered*: the tail or head of a word split over a line end (Fürst
  wraps cipher words to the start of the same or the next line) whose gloss stands over the other piece; 17 have a
  gloss of their own that the survey missed or placed on a neighbour (e.g. `3 9 8/ ϯ d δ d V` = confidit, `G V` = et);
  5 are plain unglossed words read with the key (`G S B` eum, `8/ G` + `ɏ G` ne se, `G V` et, `B 2 8/ S` manu,
  22-2-51 *auxilio* by comparison with its glossed twin).
- **Read in part / uncertain: 13.** Letters read but the word or one sign uncertain: 22-3-8 02 l.14 `n- G 2` (rea),
  03 l.8 first sign of `? G h/ d`, 03 l.15 `n- G` (word with the clear "op-"), 04 l.3 final `8` of *summam*;
  22-3-42 02 l.14 `2 M`, 03 l.18 the large `B` sign; 22-2-56 01 l.18 `?er`, 05 l.13 tail after *esse*,
  06 l.6 tail of *sufficien-*, 06 l.13 `3 9 B d ɏ` (comis), 07 l.16 `G G G` (filler?), 07 l.17 `-issem`,
  07 l.20 `d S 8/` (iun-).
- **Open: 6.** 22-3-8 02 l.18 `h/ d n ɏ` (a sign not in the key); 22-3-42 01 l.20 tail after the fence (cursive, not
  the glyph alphabet); 22-2-51 02 l.18 `ɱ V` (a different alphabet, 25 June, the pair not covered); 23-2-62 10 margin
  `θ - ꞇ 8` (a changed alphabet, Dec. 1510, too short to fix); 23-3-6 09 l.21 `ꟿtra` (probably a clear abbreviation);
  23-3-6 02 `ʒ23 6` (probably not cipher: a number or later mark).

Why they stay open: each is one to four signs, outside the 22-3-8 alphabet (22-2-51 and 23-2-62 use different
letter values) or written in cursive rather than glyphs; none has a sibling occurrence that would fix the value.
No passage of sense is lost: every open group sits next to a glossed clause.
