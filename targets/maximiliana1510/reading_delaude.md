## Target 2: 26-4-10 image 07, line 29 (the cipher line above "De Arnulo filij Regis Francie")

**Result: not an undeciphered line. Its decipherment is the end of the gloss written over line 28.**

Line 28's gloss reads: *respondi ut melius scivi que non scribo quia sunt conformia suprascriptis.*
Line 28's cipher ends with `Q U I A+` = **quia**, letter for letter (4-10/07 crop `dl_1007_U4`), and there is
nothing after it on the line. The three words after *quia* in the gloss (*sunt conformia suprascriptis*) must
therefore belong to the next cipher line, l. 29, which is short (3 cipher words) and has no gloss of its own.
The glossator, having room on the gloss line above l. 28, finished the sentence there.

Transcription (crops `dl_1007_t1`-`t3`, native x 280-1550, y 2360-2480), 16 tokens in 3 words:

    SO FF T | TTT L O: R~ { A+ | SO DELTA S C DOT-O E S

| cipher word | tokens | values from the key | gloss word |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `SO FF T` | s n t (all three firm) | sunt (skeleton s-n-t, u dropped) |
| 2 | `TTT L O: R~ { A+` | n/con, n?, (o:=t?), r, ?, a | conformia (con . . r . a: con, r, a fit; `L`, `O:`, `{` not independently known) |
| 3 | `SO DELTA S C DOT-O E S` | s, ?, s, c?, ?, e/i, s | suprascriptis (s . s c . i s: initial s, the second s, c, i, final s fit) |

Latin: **sunt conformia suprascriptis** — "[because] they are the same as what is written above".
Full sentence of ll. 28-29: *respondi ut melius scivi; que non scribo quia sunt conformia suprascriptis*
("I answered as best I knew; I do not write it out because it is the same as what is written above").

Count: 16 tokens; 10 carry values established elsewhere that agree with the gloss (SO x2, FF, T, TTT, R~, A+,
S x2, E); 6 (`L`, `O:`, `{`, `DELTA`, `C`, `DOT-O`) are read here only by alignment with the gloss words.
All 16 belong to words that read as sense. The reading rests on the gloss line, not on the key alone.

   Cipher: `TTT ʃ W U O B | q 4R S R' I? FF V? q T | O B ʃ PHI S | TTT ↄ | A+ W`.

Burgo* (1) = **24 tokens of about 196, 12%**. With the uncertain *quod* (4), *orator* (6) and *eandem* (3):

mandatum Andreas de Burgo …"**. No sentence can be built from this, and I have not tried to.

### What stays open, and why

1. **Transcription.** The paragraph sits in the water stain. At 2.5x the crossed forms (`‡`, `T`, `đ̃`, `9‡`,
   `ꝺ‡`, `B‡`) and the small hooks (`v`, `~`, `ψ`) cannot be told apart reliably, and word gaps are unclear
   in D0 and D5. A large part of the misses may be misreadings, not unknown key values.
2. **Unknown signs.** `ꝸ` (3x in D4-D5), `ꝶ̊ f` (3x), `-u-` (2x), `ʒ` in D4-D5 (r by the key, but it gives no
   words there), `ㅁ`, `⊙`, `ω`, `9‡`, `l`: none occurs in a glossed word that I found. D4 and D5 are made up
   mostly of these, which suggests a second layer of code words (names or titles) that is not in the glossed
   text of 4-9/01 or 4-9/04.
3. **Skeleton spelling.** Even where every sign has a value, the vowel-free skeletons (`t o d o d`,
   `n o t o m`, `s t a d b o`, `o t d r`) match no Latin word. Either a sign value is wrong in this context
   (several signs have more than one value: `O`/`O:`, `E` e/i, `~` r/n) or the transcription is wrong.
4. **Not done.** Image 02 of 26-4-9 (same letter, its postscript) and 26-4-10 / 26-4-15 were not searched for
   the unknown signs; the next step is to find `ꝸ`, `ꝶ̊ f`, `-u-` and `ㅁ` under a gloss there. A higher-quality
   scan or a raking-light photo of fol. 14 would help more than further key work.
