# Veit von Fürst's cipher (Rome, June-July 1510): working key

Rebuilt from the contemporary interlinear glosses in 22-3-8 (fol. 11-12), extended 27 Oct 2026 from 22-3-8, 22-2-56,
22-3-42 and 23-3-6 (crops `img/small/vf_*.png`). A letter-for-letter substitution with glyphs, written as separate
"words" inside the Latin clear text; some letters have two or three signs. Sign names are ASCII stand-ins.
Readings of the unglossed groups: `reading_veit.md`.

Homophones: u/v has three signs (8, δ, S); s has two (h/, ɏ); q has two (4, X), and X also serves for x, so X is
ambiguous between q and x (quia, quicunque in 22-3-42; ex, auxilio in 22-3-8). Letters with no example (h, k, y, z)
are rare in the Latin vocabulary these letters encipher; no sign in the unglossed groups needs them.

Separators and nulls: `·` and `:` between groups are separators; `=` and a long horizontal dash close a line or mark a
word continued elsewhere; a vertical stroke `|` fences the tail of a word written at the start of the same line
(22-3-8 f. 11 ll. 20-21; 22-3-42 f. 71 l. 20). `G G G` at the end of 22-2-56 img 07 l. 16 looks like a line filler
(read as nulls, uncertain). No other null was found.

Abbreviations inside cipher: `B 2` `d ɏ` `S` = Ma(iestat)is V(estre) (22-3-8 f. 12 l. 13), as "Ma V" in clear;
`8/ n- 2` = n(ost)ra (23-3-6 f. 15v l. 1).

Layout: a cipher word that does not fit at the line end is split, its head at the right end and its tail at the left
start of the same line (fenced) or of the next line; the glossator writes the whole word over one piece only. Most
"unglossed" line-start and line-end groups are these pieces (see `reading_veit.md`).

## Sign-names (whole words)

hooked A = Papa; AV = Rex Francie (also `AV ‖ T p ‖` = Regem Francie, 22-2-56 img 03 l. 13); ⊞ = Card. S. Crucis;
a barred-A sign = Rex Aragonum (22-3-8 f. 11 l. 13); `1 ʘ` = Navarra (22-3-8 f. 11v l. 15); M with flourish
(`M V` in clear = Maiestas Vestra). A large double-stroked B at 22-3-42 f. 73 l. 18 is a sign-name not glossed
anywhere seen.

Short unglossed groups read with this table: `n- G` = re; `G V` = et; `3 9 8/` = con; `G S B` = eum; `8/ G` = ne;
`ɏ G` = se.

## Other alphabets of the same writer (not interchangeable)

- 22-2-51 (Rome, 25 June 1510) uses different values: o/0 = a?, 9 = o, b = p, ʃ = e, 6 = d (`9 b ʃ` = ope,
  `o n ʃ 8 l 7 ʃ ...` = auxilij / auxilio). Only partly rebuilt.
- 23-2-62 (Bologna, Dec. 1510) changes again: `n- 9 8 2 B` = Romam and `3 9 n- 9 8 2` = Corona put 8 for m and for n
  (crossed/plain not distinguishable at this size); `d ʃ t G n- d` = imperi-. Not rebuilt here.

## Control

Four glossed groups not used to build the key decipher letter for letter to their gloss: armata, presens, Nam,
vendicare (`reading_veit.md`, Control).
