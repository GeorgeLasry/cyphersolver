## Per-item summaries

**22-3-42** (Veit von Fürst, Rome 20.07.1510). About 42 cipher lines on images 01-04, Fürst code groups. Every cipher passage has its contemporary interlinear decipherment. Only odd groups have no gloss of their own, mostly at line ends where the neighbouring gloss probably covers them: 01 l. 20 tail `/7-Nr`; 02 l. 4 `7dB`, l. 10 end `vd`, l. 14 end `ʒtn-d`; 03 l. 18 two large `B` signs before `Bʒʒʃ` ("manu"), and a few short line-end groups. No undeciphered passage.

**23-2-62** (Veit von Fürst, Bologna 12.12.1510). About 114 cipher lines on images 06-15, code groups. Every passage in the text is glossed. Two marginal insertions have no gloss: **image 10, left margin opposite l. 20, "Ad solum `ʃ-ʃʃ` preparare fecit"** (one group), and on **image 12 the small group `27d` under the glossed insertion "Corona Imperiali"** (it may be the tail of that insertion).

**23-3-6** (Veit von Fürst, [Rome] June 1510). About 135 cipher lines on images 01-09, code groups and name signs. Every passage is glossed, at near-full density. Isolated groups with no visible gloss: 01 last line `dʃ·trn-r` (only "in" above it); 02 `ʒ23 6` after l. 6 (probably a number); 06 l. 9 end `ʒʒ83` and the last-line `dʃvn-dʃ`; 08 l. 9 long group `trrnʒʃʒʃy`, partly glossed; 09 l. 19 `Ʒ` + `2ʃʃdn-r` and l. 21 `Mtra`. None is a whole undeciphered passage.

**23-5-4** (Mercurino Gattinara, Seville 03.02.1511). About 30 cipher lines on images 04, 07, 10, 11 and 12. This is Gattinara's own letter cipher (digits and symbols, one sign per letter, dot separators), a third system distinct from both Fürst's and de Burgo's. Every passage is glossed word by word, except **image 07, end of l. 17: "sit matrimonium `S°ʃ° ꝗ ꝶcto`"** (3 sign groups, no gloss above; `S°ʃ°` and `ꝶcto` are name signs glossed elsewhere).

**23-5-17** (Andrea de Burgo, Milan 07.02.1511). 3 cipher lines on image 04, ll. 14-16, in de Burgo letter signs, fully glossed. The other pages are clear text, blank pages or the address leaf.

**23-5-25** (Veit von Fürst, Bologna 11.02.1511). About 69 cipher lines on images 01-06, code groups. Fully glossed throughout. No undeciphered passage.

**23-5-47** (Gattinara, Seville 20.02.1511). About 21 cipher lines, all on image 04, in Gattinara's letter cipher. The main text (about 16 lines) is fully glossed. **The 5-line postscript at bottom left beside the signature is only partly deciphered:** ll. 1-3 are glossed; l. 4 has glosses only at its two ends; **l. 5 (the bottom line, from `ʒ7apʃ.1.÷` to `HT6.1.6`) has no gloss at all.**

**26-3-50** (Paulus de Laude to de Burgo, Blois 31.01.1512). 2 short cipher lines on image 03, ll. 12-13, letter signs, fully glossed.

**26-4-6** (Paulus de Laude, Blois 03.02.1512). About 145 cipher lines on images 01-08, de Burgo/de Laude letter signs. Every line is glossed. No undeciphered passage; on 04 and 06 the glosses on the lowest lines are faint in the stain but present.

**26-4-24** ("Chiffriert", Paulus de Laude to de Burgo, Blois 17.02.1512). About 124 cipher lines on images 01-04: the whole letter except the salutation, in letter signs. **26-4-25** (Paulus de Laude to de Burgo, Blois 17.02.1512). About 38 cipher lines on images 02, 04, 05 and 06, letter signs. Everything that survives is glossed. On 04 (top right of ll. 3-5) and 06 (right half of the top block), holes and tears have taken cipher and gloss together.

**26-5-7** (N.N., Modena 08.03.1512). About 14 cipher lines on image 01. The system differs from both Fürst's and de Burgo's: rounded signs (8, 9, ʒ, Θ ...) plus numeric codes (`370`, `398`). Every cipher word carries a contemporary gloss, and a later hand has added a few pencil glosses. No undeciphered passage.

**26-5-10** ("Chiffriert", Giovanni Colla, Rome 09.03.1512). About 66 cipher lines on images 01-03. The whole letter is in a letter-sign cipher, one sign per letter (ff, ʒ, 5, #, ϕ, ½, ≡, ⊡ ...), different from de Burgo's. **The whole letter is deciphered.** A contemporary decipherment in light ink runs as continuous Latin above every cipher line. It starts in the left margin and covers about the first half of the cipher line, because the plaintext is more compact than the spaced-out signs. In addition, a later pencil letter-by-letter transcription appears on some lines. No line is without decipherment.

**26-5-31** (Andrea de Burgo, Blois 27.03.1512). About 155 cipher lines on images 01, 02, 04, 05 and 07-10, de Burgo letter signs. The running decipherment in dark ink sits over every line. Fully glossed.

**27-1-5** (Andrea de Burgo, Blois 02.04.1512). About 110 cipher lines on images 02-07, de Burgo letter signs. The running decipherment over every line starts in the left margin. Fully glossed.
