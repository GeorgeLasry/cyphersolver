# Malicorne: label → sign shape

Every label in the round-2 transcriptions, with its count, its value(s) in the round-2 key (`null` = no letter; `—` = no value in the key), and the shape it stands for.

**Basis of each shape:**
- *Tomokiyo table*: the label is the sign drawn in S. Tomokiyo's Malycorne table (cryptiana, henryiii page), identified through the map from the first-round labels to these.
- *contact sheet, f. 172*: seen on tiles cut from the full-size images of canvas 172 at the recorded sign positions. The positions are approximate, so a tile can show a neighbour; a shape is recorded only where several tiles agree.
- *notes / label name*: described in the round notes or by the label's name, not checked on the image here.
- *not confirmed*: no description exists. These are mostly one-off signs (`U…`, `u…`, `?…`) on ff. 162–171, where no usable positions exist (f. 163 and canvas 171 have none; the f. 162 positions are misaligned).

**Coverage:** 195 labels, 4432 tokens. Tomokiyo table: 43 labels (3091 tokens); contact sheet, f. 172: 11 labels (760 tokens); notes / label name: 46 labels (301 tokens); not confirmed: 95 labels (280 tokens)
