# Telegram No. 205 (groups 0-146) as sentences, in German

Bernstorff to the Auswärtiges Amt, Washington, 10 Jan 1917. Word division, punctuation and spelling follow the text
Bernstorff printed in 1920 (Deutschland und Amerika, pp. 325-326), against which the groups were aligned.
Marks: `[958]` = a group without a value; `[?words]` = words of the print that sit on a run of groups whose split is
not fixed, or on a group whose figures are doubtful, so they are not read from the groups; `[der]` = a word of the
print with no group of its own. The line-by-line decryption is in `decryption_tel205.txt`.
