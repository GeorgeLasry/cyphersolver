Memorandum handed to Lansing. In my opinion, action along the lines of this memorandum will [?wreck] Wilson's peace
mediation [?and instead] bring about a break with the United States, [?unless, at least for as long as it takes,]
action is held back until agreement with the government here has been reached.

It may perhaps be possible to have Americans warned against taking service on ships [?that are armed for purposes
of attack]. For this, however, [the] government here must in any case be given time.

Since everything [7918] is decided by Wilson, a discussion with Lansing is a mere formality. He never answers until
he has instructions from Wilson. In the present case the latter must first read the memorandum.

How much weight Your Excellency places on Wilson's peace mediation I [?cannot judge] from here. Apart from that,
[?duty compels me to state positively that I] consider a break with the United States [?unavoidable] if action is
taken along the lines of the memorandum without further ado.
