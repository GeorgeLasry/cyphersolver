continouarò", "obedire", "auuenimento sucesole così intieram.". The dotted 129 after "sig." was genuine (decoder had
dropped it).

Image checks (crops): 313r l. 3 (as transcribed, unread), 313v ll. 9–10 (the "21" correction above "mandato"), 314r
ll. 8–10 (3 7, not 3 3, in "conseguito"; the close as transcribed).

## 3. Structure

315r begins inside a sentence and is not the continuation of 314r (which ends with a flourish): a separate cipher leaf,
probably a postscript; its first words are lost with the preceding sheet.
