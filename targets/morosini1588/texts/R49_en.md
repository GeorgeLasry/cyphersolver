# R49 — English (Morosini to Montalto, [Blois, early 1589]: unpaid troops; the Archbishop of Lyon and the King's commissioners)

Translation of `R49_reading.md`; holes as there.

## Translation

[f. 404v] […] if they are not paid they will try to seize some city or territory of His Majesty; which he has thought
to remedy with the assignments already written of. All these disturbances serve the greatness of the former King of
Navarre and of the heretics, and much more so if some mishap should befall the King's person.

His Majesty had ordered that, since the Archbishop of Lyon refused to answer his commissioners because they were not
churchmen, […] [should be] questioned by the Bishop of Beauvais with the assistance of the said commissioners, letting
him know also that what he wished to learn from him would not be to his harm, since he had already assured him of his
life, but simply by way of testimony against others. Even so the said Archbishop has also refused to answer this Bishop,
saying that he was Primate of France and could not be examined by one who has no jurisdiction over him. The King being
much angered at this, the Archbishop's relatives have come to me, begging me to work with His Majesty that he not
prejudice the dignity of the Church, and to beg Our Lord, if His Majesty should ask him to commit his case to someone,
not to commit it to any bishop of France, since he trusts none of them. I desire […]
