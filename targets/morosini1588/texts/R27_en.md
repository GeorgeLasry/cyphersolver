# R27 — English (Morosini to Montalto, n.d. [Blois, October 1588], fragment: Épernon's governments and Guise)

Translation of `R27_reading.md`; holes as there.

## Translation

[f. 137r] [the m]arquisate of Saluzzo, and that of Angoulême, of Saintonge and of Cognac, with an office of the crown
for each, with which they can [live] in great dignity, there being no prince in France who has so many governments as
they; and to restore Metz and Boulogne to His Majesty, because in concluding the peace with these lords of Guise he
had thus promised them to do. And nevertheless they show themselves most resolved not to restore anything; on the
contrary, from what His Majesty has told me, they are in very [close] dealings with the Queen of England, with the
former King of Navarre, with Casimir and with Montmorency, of which the said Majesty assures me he has most certain
proofs, so that […] without great suspicion of them; besides which, if he were content to leave them everything, he
would put himself on bad terms with these others. I certainly do not fail, nor shall I fail as far as lies in me, to
promote every good, but I have little hope of being able to make peace between the Duke of Guise and these others,
because it does not suit Guise to have their friendship, since being their enemy has been the chief cause of winning
him the affection and the following of the people; just as, on the contrary, the King's having [f. 137v] chosen to
support and favour [them] has been the cause of his ruin and of all the evils that have followed […].
