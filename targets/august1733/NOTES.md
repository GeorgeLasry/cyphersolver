# August III to Johann Adolf II of Saxe-Weissenfels, siege of Danzig, 1733-34 (Unsolved Catalogue no. 592)

Status: in progress

Blocked on images: the file is not digitised and has to be ordered from the Hauptstaatsarchiv Dresden. Nothing
below rests on a sight of the documents.

## The document

- Sächsisches Staatsarchiv, HStA Dresden, 11237 Geheimes Kriegsratskollegium, Nr. Loc. 10932/09 (provenance
  Geheime Kriegskanzlei). Archivportal-D: https://www.archivportal-d.de/item/QQFMEDQXJRIUQN3AOSG47OXWHNYVN7U2
- Full title: "Befehle des Königs August III. von Polen an den Herzog Johann Adolf II. von Sachsen-Weißenfels
  wegen der Belagerung Danzigs [Polnischer Erbfolgekrieg]".
- Enthältvermerk, in full: "Enthält auch: Verschlüsselte Berichte.- Truppenverlegungen innerhalb Polens.-
  Besetzung von Offiziersstellen."
- Laufzeit 1733-1734 (the catalogue entry says 1732-1734; the finding aid says 1733-1734). Classification
  01.3.6 Befehle. Type Akten.
- Who wrote the "verschlüsselte Berichte" is not stated. They are reports, so they are probably from officers
  or agents to the Duke or to the king, not the king's orders themselves. Language unknown (German expected).

## Key material

- DECODE holds about 95 Saxon key records from HStA Dresden 10026 Geheimes Kabinett, Loc. 03233/02: the keys of
  Jakob Heinrich von Flemming, dated 1697-1728 (Flemming died 1728). None is dated 1731-1736 and none names
  Weissenfels, Danzig or the 1733-34 campaign. They show what Saxon cabinet ciphers of the previous reign looked
  like (number codes and nomenclators), which is a starting point once the reports are imaged.
- DECODE 2331 (10024 Loc. 8236/11, comments on a cipher with Lautensack, 1723-1751) is the only Saxon record whose
  dates span 1733; it is a cipher-office note, not a key for this file.
- Loc. 03233/01-07 and 03234/01-07 (the Saxon key volumes named in the catalogue) beyond what DECODE shows from
  03233/02 have not been checked; they are not digitised on Archivportal-D either.
