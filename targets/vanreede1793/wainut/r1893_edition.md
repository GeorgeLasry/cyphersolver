## Gist (English, hedged)

The letter reports on Polish politics around a Diet (*landdag*) that is to be held. It mentions a deputation and
foreign affairs, the expedition and money, a proposal of the King to be laid before the Diet, difficulties and
prejudices to be overcome, the Russian party, and a party "of which the marshal Potocki ..." that the writer seems to
judge the strongest but ineffective (this join is over tentative pieces). It also mentions working through the
(Prussian) headquarters, advantages to be proposed there, a cession (*afstand*) to be deliberated and guaranteed,
Danzig, the minister of Austria and a correspondence, and the writer's hope of winning someone over. A continuous
translation is not possible at this coverage.

**Which Potocki is open.** In Van Reede's own letter of 16 June 1792 (`runs.json`, 1792-06-16PS) the same group
sequence names a Maarschalk Potocki who came to the Prussian King and went home without result, which fits Ignacy
Potocki's Berlin mission. Nothing in the decoded text decides between Ignacy and Szczęsny Potocki for March 1793.
