## Translation

The wise judge otherwise; I shall defer to their opinions. To have the right to complain of the Duke of Mantua for the favours he bestows on the Spaniards, one would have to ask him for similar or equivalent ones; and if he made difficulties, one could justly reproach him with his conduct. But to impute to him…
