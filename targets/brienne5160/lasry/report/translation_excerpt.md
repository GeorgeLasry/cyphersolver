# Français 5160: translation of the excerpt

George Lasry, report PDF `38_francais-5160.pdf`, "Excerpt and Translation".

## Translation 1 (f18r)

The wise judge otherwise; I will defer to their judgment. To complain about the Duke of Mantua’s favors to the Spaniards, one would have to ask him for similar or equivalent favors; if he proved reluctant, one could then justly reproach his conduct. But to impute to him…
