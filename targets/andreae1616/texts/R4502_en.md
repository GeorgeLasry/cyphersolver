# Andreae, Chymische Hochzeit (1616): the two letter inscriptions, English translation

p. 95, the copper door to Venus's vault: Here lies buried Venus, the fair woman who has robbed so many a high man of
fortune, honour, blessing and welfare.

p. 97, the tablet behind Venus's bed: When the fruit of my tree has wholly melted away, I shall awake and be the mother
of a king.

Note: "umb ... gebracht" is the Early New High German idiom "bring someone out of (deprive him of)" his fortune, honour,
blessing and welfare.
