# R164, the Secretariat of State to the nuncio in Madrid, Rome, 19 April 1718 (Spagna 364D/11, f. 235r): English translation

## Translation

The news brought me by Your Most Illustrious Lordship's cipher dated the 14th of last month, touching the present
dispositions of that Court, and principally those concerning the armament, are most important; and Our Lord desires
that you go on sending them to me as far in advance as possible, since you can well understand how much it matters to
Our Lord's good service to be informed of everything promptly. To which end let Your Most Illustrious Lordship use all
diligence and attention to find out with certainty both the designs held there and the measures it is intended to
take.
