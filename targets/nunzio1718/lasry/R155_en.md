# R155, the Secretariat of State to the nuncio in Madrid, Rome, 18 January 1718 (Spagna 364D/2, f. 24r-v): English translation

## Translation

I have several times made known to Your Most Illustrious Lordship Our Lord's regret at learning that, notwithstanding
the first chapter of the well-known Concordat, the authority and the immunity of the Church continued to be oppressed
and violated there as before; and I have also expressed to you Our Lord's astonishment at receiving from you no
account of the notorious particular outrages which the Royal Ministers were heaping up every day in this regard. But
it has never happened that I had expressly to complain of your conduct, as I am forced to do now that you are silent
to me about one of the greatest excesses that the lay power can commit against the ecclesiastical, and of which we
have, alas, authentic and irrefutable documents in hand. This is the expulsion of the Bishop of Vich, and the order
given by the Royal Ministers, which means in substance by whoever is at present the arbiter of that government, to
that Chapter to proceed to the election of a Capitular Vicar, as if the see were vacant, when it has in fact been
filled for about six years now. The whole took place as long ago as the 29th of October last, that is to say a month
and a half before the date of your last letters, a time quite long enough for you to be informed of so strange an
event and to give us a hint of it. What Our Lord's resolutions are to be, I do not know; I know well that he will take
such as to make the world know that he is not capable of suffering such proceedings without resenting them and
exacting the due reparation.
