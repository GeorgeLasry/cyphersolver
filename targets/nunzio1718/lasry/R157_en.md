# R157, the Secretariat of State to the nuncio in Madrid, 1718 (Spagna 364D/4, f. 53v; the end of a despatch): English translation

## Translation

... a lawful and canonical faculty. Let Your Most Illustrious Lordship therefore inform me of the truth of what has
happened in this matter, so that Our Lord may make the appropriate provisions; and meanwhile do you fulfil there those
parts which the precise obligation of the Ministry you hold will suggest to you.
