# R172, the Secretariat of State to the nuncio in Madrid, Rome, 12 July 1718 (Spagna 364D/19, ff. 340r-341r): English translation

## Translation

From the short despatch dated the 27th of last month, which Your Most Illustrious Lordship handed to the courier who
returned here from Lisbon, we have heard of the full execution you gave to the orders prescribed to you under the 9th of
the said month. Our Lord received the news with much satisfaction, and gave due praise to the punctuality with which
you carried them out, waiting impatiently to learn all the circumstances of it with the return of our courier. His
Holiness knows the indignation Spain will conceive at it, and all that may follow from such indignation; but, to tell
you, it matters much more to the Pope not to fall into the hands of the living God by not fulfilling his obligation in
the circumstances in which Christendom finds itself, from which men cannot free him, than to expose himself to falling
into those of men, from which the mighty hand of God can easily save him; nay, he certainly hopes for it, because His
Holiness has had no other aim in the resolution taken than to fulfil the parts of the Ministry committed to him by God
and to promote the advantages of His cause. And be assured that no regard for the Court of Vienna has had the least part
in it, from which everyone sees the treatment we receive; unless it be that of seeming now to be in a position to do our
duty with it too, as we are now that it has been done with Spain, which was the first to fail, and gave occasion, or to
put it better, pretext, to the offences done to us by the Emperor.
