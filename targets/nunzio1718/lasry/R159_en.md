# R159, the Secretariat of State to the nuncio in Madrid, Rome, 22 March 1718 (Spagna 364D/6, f. 133r): English translation

## Translation

Your Most Illustrious Lordship's last despatch, dated the 2nd of this month, which reached me with an express sent to
Cardinal Acquaviva, would require from me a very long reply; but since a new, unexpected and most grave development,
which has surprised not only Our Lord but this whole Court, obliges me to make an express despatch to you promptly, I
refer to what I shall convey to you by it by Our Lord's order.
