# R160, the Secretariat of State to the nuncio in Madrid, Rome, 29 March 1718 (Spagna 364D/7, f. 139r): English translation

## Translation

The express which, as I indicated to Your Most Illustrious Lordship with the last despatch, was to be sent by us to
that Court has not yet left. It will leave shortly, however, and with it you will receive the answers to your
despatches of the 14th and 21st of February and of the 2nd of March.
