# R165, the Secretariat of State to the nuncio in Madrid, Rome, 17 May 1718 (Spagna 364D/12, f. 266r-v): English translation

## Translation

It would be no great thing if, peace being concluded between the Emperor and Spain, the Catholic King took occasion to
answer, as every duty and propriety would require, the letter written to him in Our Lord's own hand, by declaring to
His Holiness that his fatherly remonstrances too had induced him to lend a hand to the conclusion of the Treaty, so
as not to hinder the progress of the Christian arms against the Turk. This is very little; and yet, besides being very
much in keeping with that distinguished piety of which the King makes [?profession], to his great praise, and not at
all unbecoming to his royal dignity, it would be taken by Our Lord as some satisfaction for the most grave failure
done to him last year, which nonetheless remains, and will always remain, deeply impressed on His Holiness's mind.
Let Your Most Illustrious Lordship therefore see to obtaining at least this little, with the assurance of bringing by
it no small consolation to Our Lord's bitterness.
