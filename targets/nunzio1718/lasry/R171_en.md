# R171, the Secretariat of State to the nuncio in Madrid, Rome, 21 June 1718 (Spagna 364D/18, ff. 332r-333r): English translation

## Translation

The news which Your Most Illustrious Lordship gives touching the present public emergencies has been very welcome to Our
Lord, to whom it matters very much to be faithfully and entirely informed of everything. Do you continue, then, with
your usual attention, and as long as you are permitted, your reports, with the assurance of His Holiness's full
appreciation. For the rest, no less the resolution, which you report has been taken and carried out there, of laying
by royal order a sequestration on the revenues of the Churches of Seville and Málaga, than the threats made of attacking
the Ecclesiastical State for no other reason than that Our Lord has deferred Cardinal Alberoni's translation from one of
the said Churches to the other, not only fully justify the delay of such a translation, but are going, to my great
regret, to put His Holiness under a precise and necessary obligation to refuse it absolutely and perpetually; since it
matters too much that, at whatever cost, an example of violence so pernicious to the Apostolic authority and to the most
sacred laws of the Church should not be admitted. I say again that I am extremely sorry that things are reduced to this
state. But Cardinal Alberoni ought to have considered that the conquest of the Church of Seville is very different from
the conquest of Sardinia, and that those means which were suitable for the second are execrable for the first. From
these expressions, which are Our Lord's own, gather, Your Most Illustrious Lordship, what His Holiness's sentiments are.
And since Sardinia has been named, be content that I tell you that Our Lord has not forgotten, nor will ever forget, the
terrible trick that was played on him last year at this time by that Court, with so much damage to his honour and with
so many prejudices to the Holy See and the Catholic Religion following from it; and [?therefore] do not wonder that I add
finally that His Holiness is waiting impatiently for the news that the extraordinary courier lately sent to you has
arrived there, and that Your Most Illustrious Lordship has carried out without the least delay those commissions which
were given you by means of that despatch.
