# R168, the Secretariat of State to the nuncio in Madrid, Rome, 31 May 1718, duplicate (Spagna 364D/15, f. 314r-v): English translation

## Translation

The present despatch is sent with the express which Cardinal Acquaviva is sending there; but let Your Most Illustrious
Lordship expect very soon another, with which you will receive a Brief suspending all the Indults which that Crown
enjoys by Apostolic authority, to be executed whenever His Majesty does not refrain from diverting the Emperor from
continuing the war against the Turk. I do not repeat the reasons Our Lord has for proceeding to this step, because you
will be able to read them in my despatch of the second of April and in that of today. The ministry the Pope holds, and
the engagement he contracted with the Emperor to urge him to war against the Turk, make this indispensable for His
Holiness, who is ready to suffer everything, but never the reproach of not having tried every road to prevent an
irreparable harm to Christendom, and of not having fulfilled the engagement he took with the Emperor for so just and
holy a cause.
