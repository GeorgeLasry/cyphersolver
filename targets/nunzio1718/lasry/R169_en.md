# R169, the Secretariat of State to the nuncio in Madrid, Rome, 9 June 1718 (Spagna 364D/16, f. 320r-v): English translation

## Translation

Cardinal Acquaviva, immediately after the despatch of the courier sent there on the 31st of last month, on the morning
of the first of this month had all the Spaniards called to him, notifying them by royal order that they must promptly
withdraw from Rome and refrain from making any application at the Dataria or at any other tribunal of Rome, under pain
of the royal indignation, and of resentment being taken also against the relatives of transgressors. So strange an
order, and so injurious to the Pope's majesty, carried out for the simple deferment of a mere grace, in circumstances in
which the contradiction between His Eminence's information and that given us by Your Most Illustrious Lordship and by
Father Daubenton about the requested satisfaction for the affairs of Vich and Tarragona had left no room to discuss and
agree together on the matter, and already before, not only the Consistory had been held, which took place only
yesterday morning, but before it was even known that a Consistory was to be held, give us good reason to foresee that
the outrages committed there against the Apostolic authority will have matched in their gravity and atrocity those
already committed in Rome. You see how we are treated; so you will not wonder, on this head too, at the orders given you
with this despatch.
