## Translation

The King.

Don Bernardino de Mendoza, of my council and my ambassador: your letters of 27 July and of 2 and 8 August have been
received these days, but not that of 30 July which you mention in that of the second; and in a time of such confusion
as there must be over there, it has been well to send duplicates by many ways.

The death of the King my brother has been a thing of great pity, and so it has caused me [grief] for all the
[?considerations] that may be weighed. God forgive him, and set His hands to the affairs of that kingdom, giving them
the remedy most fitting for His service and the good of Christendom.

All things over there being in the confusion in which they are, I believe that with [?your] good zeal you will have
suspended using the [?licence] you have to leave that kingdom before you are informed from here; because, [?your]
[gu] being so important in the [bu] for the good settlement of affairs, I cannot fail to charge you in no way to
attempt to absent yourself from there without a new order from me, but to attend to what is necessary with your
accustomed care, and to go on steering what is most fitting for the public good, which is the only end I have, and to
give me notice of everything, so that you may be advised of what you are to do.

There is a great want here of news from [?England] concerning their [?designs] and what they are plotting and
scheming; for the route by which their [?armada] returned, and such things, are well known from the prisoners some
ships have taken that were sent to this [Jam], and from various particulars. Do you take care to keep the [lo]s, and
often, of all that passes over there, and send it in the form the business requires; and it is to be believed that
between them and the man of Béarn evil things must be being woven in [bem] of the [par]. It all matters to understand,
and in particular their armaments. From San Lorenzo, 7 September 1589.

I the King. Don Martín de Idiáquez. Duplicate. Address: From the King. To Don Bernardino de Mendoza, of his council and
his ambassador in France.
