# fr. 3984 no. 47, f. 108: the Duque de Sessa to Philip II, Rome, 30 June 1593 (copy): English translation

here in particular on the affair of France, of which [I have] sent duplicate couriers by sea and land; and the despatch
has gone on being put off from day to day, another courier to Your Majesty, waiting to see whether some certain news
would come from Paris, from where the [∂ir] to Your Majesty a fresh letter with which I find myself of the [hir] of Your

that […] […] [xir] that he has had no letters from the legate for many days, and I believe without doubt that the
