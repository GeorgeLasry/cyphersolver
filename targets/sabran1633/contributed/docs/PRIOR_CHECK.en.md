## Conclusion
- Category: **not found.** In the places below (20 October 2026) no decipherment of the cipher passages of fr. 4134 (contemporary decipherment, edition, other project's reading) was found.
- We nevertheless do not claim a first reading, for two reasons:
  1. Tomokiyo writes "George Lasry confirmed this cipher is also used for many letters of Sabran in BnF fr.4134 and fr.4135" (GL.htm). Lasry may have read them at least far enough to confirm the key; any such reading is unpublished.
  2. fr. 4134 is Sabran's own copy book (BnF catalogue: "Recueil de copies … Troisième livre des negotiations du Sr de Sabran à Gennes … Année 1633"). The originals received by Bouthillier, possibly with a contemporary decipherment, are probably in the French diplomatic archives (Correspondance politique, Gênes). A web search ("Archives diplomatiques Correspondance politique Gênes 1633 volume numérisé Sabran") found no digitised volume; the archives' catalogue was not consulted directly.
