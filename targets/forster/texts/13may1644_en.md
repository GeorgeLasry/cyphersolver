# Forster papers, 13 May 1644: English translation

There is no ground for scruple about failing God; ⟨I answer to you for it, and even within⟩ the rules of perfection: only
take the ways of prudence to preserve your life, so as to make of it a greater sacrifice to God by multiplying your
services for the salvation of your brethren; ⟨and to measure by this whether it is better to act or to sigh. 13 May
1644.⟩
