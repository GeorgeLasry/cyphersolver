## Translation

Mine was that in most of the world's affairs the sovereign counsel is to gain time, and that it would be soon enough to answer the ministers of Monsieur de Mantua when they renewed their requests; and perhaps, having at present various things to pursue, they may forget that one, and so the title to the government of Nivernais will be found strengthened by a possession maintained since his absence from the kingdom.
