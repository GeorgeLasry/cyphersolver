# Clairambault 1067: translation of the excerpt

George Lasry, report PDF `40_clairambault-1067.pdf`, "Excerpt and Translation".

## Translation 1 (f226v)

My view was that in most affairs of the world the wisest course is to gain time, and that it would be soon enough to answer Monsieur de Mantua’s ministers when they renewed their requests. Perhaps, having various matters to pursue at present, they will forget this one; thus the title to the government of Nivernois will be strengthened by possession maintained since his departure from the kingdom.
