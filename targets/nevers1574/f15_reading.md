# Henriette de Clèves to the duke of Nevers, Lyon, 16 September [1574]

## f. 15r

Monseigneur, je ne veulx faillir a vous advertir que ce jourd'huy a esté rendu a monsieur de Savoye Pignerol et Savillan, au grant contentement du Roy et de la Royne mere, qui ont faict ce beau chef d'euvre. respondu que c'estoit chose que vous n'a[…] rien jamais que les estatz de ceulx qui sont encores vivans, que l'un vous estoit trop proche et que l'autre avoit trop moien pour faire son appoinctement. ## f. 16r (clear, in the duchess's hand)

Je vous supplie m'excuser si je ne vous escris davantage, car il est minuit et les yeulx me font si grand mal de pleurer que je ne vous [?…] ansi ; que je n'ay aultre chose a vous dire pour ceste heure, sinon que, voyant de la fasson que l'on nous [?mene], je vouldrois estre au paradis. Je supplie Dieu, mon bon frere, qu'il vous donne patience. De Lion, ce 16 septembre.

Address (outer leaf): A Monsieur, Monsieur le duc de Nevers, pair de France.

## Notes

* Context. Henri III, back from Poland, met Emmanuel-Philibert of Savoy at Turin in August-September 1574 and
  agreed to return Pignerol and Savigliano; the duke of Nevers was governor of the French places in Piedmont, so
  the cession left him "plus de gouvernement". The letter reports the news, the duchess's audience with Catherine
  de Médicis (la Royne mere / la Royne), a rumour at court that Nevers had written to the King about a "ligue" of
  four or five, and his friends' advice about the remaining governorship. The two governorships "qui ne pouvoient
  faillir" are Picardie and Languedoc. Birago's letter of 28 Sept (f. 21) speaks of the same "governo".
* 15r l.3: the group after "du" is not spelled out; it is glossed "Roy" (same sense as `SS` elsewhere).
* 15r l.12: "et recevoir une conte…": signs `F4 TFCF687T 62F C824F..`; the end of the line is crossed by the gloss.
* 15r l.17: "tiens": the sign group is partly struck; the gloss is "t[?]".
* 15r l.21: "veid": signs `6Fb74`; the gloss reads "veit/veid".
* 15v ll.1-5 (after "25 ans ceste couronne si fidellement"): the cipher is overwritten letter by letter by the
  decipherer and the gloss above is faded; only "ayant tant", "aviez", "la moitié", "elle a", "recompense" and
  "France" are carried. This is the main hole of the letter (about 70 words).
* 15v l.7: `T1b879` after "chose que" does not resolve ("racontoit"?); `XXm` is glossed "24".
* 15v l.9: "qu'il c'estoit faict" as written (`CF94874`), i.e. s'estoit.
* 15v l.10: the group `XX` before "une ligue" is not spelled; left unread.
* 16r l.1: `21 CF(99F` after "vous n'a…" does not resolve.
* 16r l.14: `C824F2bTF` (glossed illegibly) left unread.
* The f. 16r clear ending was re-read on the native image: "de Lion ce 16 [one word struck] septembre". The word
  after "je ne vous" (underlined with a flourish) is not read.
* Ernest Lavisse, *Histoire de France* VI.1, prints an emotional appeal of Henri III to the duchess written before
  his departure for Poland. It is not this letter and was not used as a crib.
