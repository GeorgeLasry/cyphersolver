The lords of Pompadour and of Clermont-Vertillac, holding for the other party, have written to the consuls and
inhabitants of this town offering them every assistance provided they throw off the just obedience they owe Your
Majesty. The Duke of Mayenne has likewise written to them, together with the leading men of the companies and to
private inhabitants, to dispose them to receive his orders; all his letters, twelve in number, are on the same subject
as the copy of one of them that I send Your Majesty. Although the wills of several are shaken, I believe that with God's
help all these rebel tricks will in no way shake the loyalty of this town or of the neighbourhood. And yet, to keep
everyone to his duty, I think it would be useful if Your Majesty were pleased to write several letters following the
memorandum I send, from which I hope some fruit will come.
