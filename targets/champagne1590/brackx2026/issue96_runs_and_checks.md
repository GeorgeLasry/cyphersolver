### Supporting checks on nos. 24 and 60

In no. 24's first difficult run, the manuscript supports **56**, previously transcribed as 30:

```text
P:99 P:38 P:39 P:45 P:56 P:93 P:38 P:67 P:92 P:52 P:37
  ∅    d    ∅    e    l    u    d    o    u    i    c
```

This gives **de Ludouic**, supported independently by no. 60's contemporary Ludovic gloss:

```text
P:19 P:56 P:93 P:38 P:67 P:93 P:53 P:37
  ∅    l    u    d    o    u    i    c
```

No. 24's other passage remains provisional. A closer crop suggests a single **barred 9 = Roy** where the
flattened transcription had 95, giving mechanical le Roy luauoit with the following figures. The join needs
checking; it is not silently restored to lui avoit. The earlier le nauarois crib is not adopted.
Barred 22, 44 and 51 in no. 24 correspond to the selected name entries above; the separate item previously
read 84 is unresolved. No. 60's second name is mechanical de launan and is not certified as a proper name.
Neither letter has a complete new reading/translation in this contribution.
