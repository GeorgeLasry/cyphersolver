# Jo. Antonio Bilia to Francesco II Sforza, Burgos, 22 November 1527 (BnF fr. 3013 no. 43, f. 102r): English translation

Today I found myself at dinner with Signor [B.58] [?pu] [?le] much; he complained to me, having had letters from his
son-in-law, that Your Excellency [?is having] Count [?Theophimo], his [?son] [?tavolo], troubled on account of the
prohibitions made to him at other times. I have already striven with all skill to draw him out of this impression and
to make him understand that Your Excellency would not have sent such a thing except for the [?service] of His Lordship,
whom you have always loved as a [?father]; so he was calmed. It would seem to me [?good], were it not that Your

I say this so as not to drive him to despair in this [?old age] of his, although the [?times] do not seem to me to allow
it; nevertheless I do [?] according to your virtue and prudence; and perhaps it would not be bad to do …
