## Summary (partly inferred)
Bilia dined with a person (name code B.58) who complained to him: letters from his son-in-law said that the Duke was troubling "conte Theophimo(?)" on account of earlier prohibitions. Bilia calmed him by explaining that the Duke had acted for his sake and had always loved him as a father. Bilia advises the Duke to write a letter showing in deed the good will promised in words — a guarantee that, as master of the state, the Duke would not only not take back what he once gave, but would make him lord of something greater — so as not to drive the old man to despair, even if the times seem not to allow it.
