## Key
- Archivio di Stato di Milano, Carteggio Sforzesco cart. 1591 no. 24, DECODE R5258 (https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/5258), docketed "Cum equite Bilie / extracta ex cifris".
- Transcription of the key sheet: Cyphersolver ledger, targets/gilino1527 (key_R5258.md, key_r8572.tsv), https://github.com/dbourdeau/cyphersolver (origin/main 0258b8ab, 2026-10-18).
- Independent recovery of the same key: George Lasry (targets/gilino1527/lasry/ in the same ledger, version of 2026-10-07).
- Another key for the same man (does not fit this letter): DECODE R5237 (cart. 1591 no. 3, "Cum Egr[egi]o Bilia", a numerical alphabet).
