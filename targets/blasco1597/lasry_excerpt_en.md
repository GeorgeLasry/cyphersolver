# BnF Français 23054, Juan Blasco de Aragón, 2 May 1597: English translation of Lasry's excerpt

Translation (Claude Opus 5.5, 23 Oct 2026) of `lasry_excerpt_reading.md`, **an excerpt only** of the letter, the passages George Lasry printed in his BnF cipher research report (f. 72r; f. 72v); the rest of the letter is not in the repository. Same holes: [?] unread, [x] a word he supplied. His own translation is in `lasry_entry.md`.

## Translation

f. 72r: by force [?], with the opinion of all the friends. There is no doubt that the matter of coming with the princess, as Your Excellency says, if it were on the conditions Your Excellency indicates, would be the best; but so far there is nothing in it, at least we do not know of any.

f. 72v: We are discussing with Don Baltasar making some present to Don Cristóbal, who is the one who comman[ds] everything, and no occasion that arises to do Your Excellency service will be lost without its being done.
