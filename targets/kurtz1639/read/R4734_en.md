# R4734: Kurz von Senftenau to Count Schwarzenberg, copy, 8 April 1639: English translation of the cipher runs in their clauses

Translation (Claude Opus 5.5, 23 Oct 2026) of `read/R4734_reading.md`, **an excerpt**: the deciphered runs (in ⟨ ⟩)
with the clear clauses round them; "…" marks clear German not transcribed in the repository. Same holes: `[?]` a run

## Translation

*Image P2.* … has [told] me … at … treaty … nothing … and will nothing … and therefore desired to confer with them what
⟨way to take, the interposition having expired⟩, towards dear peace.

*Image P4, left.* … could let nothing be heard about it, ⟨until every form of conjunction is assured. Let this be what⟩
may … and only in their means. Thus, however, one will not declare oneself … the same matter, and so causes that …
itself: ⟨the King remains the more [?unintrigued] in the matter⟩, and ⟨we now have a free hand to embrace as often as
[?]⟩, and one … therefore with complaint ⟨that one has been passed over [?] [?]⟩. I am sure that … nothing else shall
be ascribed by Your Excellency than … the 8th of April 1639.

*Image P5.* … in which I [have] nothing at all with Your Excellency …, that ⟨the interposition must [?] reach⟩; secondly,
what was reported … concerning the mediation: ⟨the interposition has already expired; the King says he has his hands
tied and can do nothing more in the matter; by force he will do nothing⟩. So this impediment holds, so that … and
their … are by treaties. ⟨I believe that [?] [would rather have the] land in others' hands than in his own⟩. The
effect of neutrality … should write itself ad casum feudi [for the case of the fief], … as 3 smokes [households], …
with … the term of redemption … would; ⟨the Lower Saxon Circle are [to] have peace, so they will contribute either to
the war or [?] to compensation⟩. I do not fear that either … Instead of it, ⟨[if] Stralsund, perhaps, [or] Hessian or
other lands of those who still persist with the French,⟩ [were] ⟨granted⟩ [to] His Electoral Highness, ⟨I hold it also
for fair⟩.
