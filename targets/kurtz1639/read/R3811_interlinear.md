# R3811: the contemporary interlinear decipherment (transcribed 22 Oct 2026, b06)

Small brown-ink letters written above the figures by a contemporary decipherer, syllable by syllable, on DECODE image
I23099 (p. 1, all cipher lines) and on I23100 left (the first eleven lines). No glosses on I23101 or I23104. Read on
crops of the DECODE images (2x zoom); the glosses are tiny and faded, so single letters marked ? are uncertain. The
clerk wrote the Kurrent e, which looks like n/r/v; it is given here as e where the figure is an e-sign. Gloss
per line, in the order of the figures (figure groups in parentheses where the gloss settles a label); | separates
words as the plain text runs.

## Where gloss and figures disagree, and what the gloss settles

- 4 2 (I23099 ll. 2 and 9) is one group 42 = ri (glossed r / ri): "richten", "virium".
- The hooked p-like sign after #l-loop (I23099 l. 8) is glossed vi; the key gives #hook = a.
- I23099 l. 12: the first 9 is glossed w (#sect), the 0-like sign z (φ): "Weser zu".
- I23099 l. 12, last figure 28 (r) is glossed t: "ist" (a slip of the cipher clerk).
- I23099 l. 14: sign 1 is glossed su ("subsidia"); the key has 1 = i.
- I23099 l. 23: 9 is glossed w (#sect): "wir".
- #s-long is glossed ti (quartir, recrutiren, competiren); the key has t.
- I23100 l. 2: "9 2" is 72 = hi ("Hinterpommeren"); l. 6: "9" is 91 = bo ("Böhmen").
