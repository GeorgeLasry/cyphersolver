# C03020059400, frames 0118-0119: Division commander Inoue to the War Minister, January 1904: reading

Rests on: the telegram form (frame 0119, received 18 Jan [1904], from Kokura; JACAR PDF of the item, fetched 23 Oct 2026
through the viewer's `raw/` path) and the **contemporary translation** filed with it (frame 0118, 訳文, stamped 満密受第
八二號, "大臣宛 井上師団長"). The enciphered run is the twelve digit pairs at the head of the message (`../ct_C03020059400.txt`,
re-read on frame 0119: columns 1-3 "一七一〇三六一九〇?" "二八八八二九一七三一" "一九七六", then kana in clear); the rest of the
telegram is clear kana, carried here by the translation. Cipher run under the 1902 table: シ ン テ イ ハ ク ゼ ウ シ レ イ ブ,
the translation's 辛碇泊場司令部 at 12 of 12 pairs: pair 5 is 04 (ハ), as Tomokiyo reads the glyph after 〇 (24 Oct 2026; read
九, ア, here before), and pair 7, written 八八 (ゼ), gives ゼウ, a kana spelling of 場 (Harumi-Matsuba, issue #97). `[…]` =
illegible in the translation.

## Reading

辛碇泊場司令部ニ要スル下士以下及器具材料ハ門司補給廠支廠長ニ交付済。[…]ノ結了ハ支廠長ヨリ直接報告ノ筈。

## Notes

- The cipher covers only the unit name 辛碇泊場司令部 ("Anchorage Command Shin", the eighth of the anchorage commands
  designated by the ten stems); everything after it is clear in the telegram.
- Pair 5: the glyph after 〇 at the foot of column 1 is unlike the 九 two rows above it; Tomokiyo reads it 四 (04 = ハ),
  which the translation's 泊 needs. Pair 7: both digits are 八 (ゼ); ゼウ is taken as a kana spelling of 場 rather than a
  slip for ジ (68).
- `[…]`: three cramped characters after the ○ (前 and two more: 隊/陽, 半/準?); "前項" or "前隊" is possible, not
  settled.
- Read from the translation, not from the kana of the form: the clear kana are cursive and were not transcribed here.
