By the United States in Congress assembled, August 10, 1781.

The report of the committee on a letter of the 9th from the Superintendant of Finance was taken into consideration; and, on the question to agree to the following proposition:

"That the said minister be and he is hereby authorised and empowered to make such FURTHER CESSIONS OF THE RIGHT of these United States to the NAVIGATION OF THE RIVER MISSISSIPI as he may think proper, and on such terms and conditions as he may think most for the honor and interest of these United States."

The yeas and nays being required by Mr. Sherman, every member present voted no, and every state voted no.

So it passed in the negative.

Extract from the minutes. James Lovell.

Endorsed on the verso: "Aug. 10. Negative. J. Jay."
