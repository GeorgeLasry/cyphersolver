By the United States in Congress assembled, August 10, 1781.

The report of the committee on a letter of the 9th from the Superintendant of Finance was taken into consideration; and, on the question to agree to the following proposition:

"That the said minister be and he is hereby authorised and empowered to make such FURTHER CESSIONS OF THE RIGHT of these United States to the NAVIGATION OF THE RIVER MISSISSIPI as he may think proper, and on such terms and conditions as he may think most for the honor and interest of these United States."

The yeas and nays being required by Mr. Sherman:

New Hampshire: Mr. Sullivan no, Mr. Livermore no; the state no.

Massachusetts: Mr. Lovell no, Mr. Partridge no, Mr. Osgood no; the state no.

Rhode Island: Mr. Mowry no, Mr. Varnum no; the state no.

Connecticut: Mr. Ellsworth no, Mr. Sherman no; the state no.

New York: Mr. Duane no, Mr. L'Hommedieu no; the state no.

New Jersey: Mr. Boudinot no, Mr. Elmer no; the state no.

Pennsylvania: Mr. Montgomery no, Mr. Clymer no, Mr. T. Smith no; the state no.

Delaware: Mr. McKean no, Mr. Vandyke no; the state no.

Maryland: Mr. Jenifer no, Mr. Carrol no; the state no.

Virginia: Mr. Jones no, Mr. Maddison no, Mr. Bland no, Mr. Randolph no; the state no.

North Carolina: Mr. Sharpe no; the state is marked "x" (one member only, so the state vote was not counted).

South Carolina: Mr. Bee no, Mr. Eveleigh no; the state no.

Georgia: Mr. Walton no, Mr. Howley no; the state no.

So it passed in the negative.

Extract from the minutes. James Lovell.

Endorsed on the verso: "Aug. 10. Negative. J. Jay."
