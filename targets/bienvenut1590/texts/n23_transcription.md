# Bienvenut, Paris, 28 February 1590: diplomatic transcription (BnF fr. 3623 ff. 35r-v)

35r.06 pour rien du ~~une~~ monde elle ne veult plus laisser retarder son ~~voyage~~

35r.29 la France & pour oster l'opinion que l'on a conceu que 1. veuille empieter [?]
35r.30 17. soubz pretexte ou de protection ou de la Religion, qu'il sera mieux q[ue] dores
35r.31 en la q[ue] la pluspart du secours que 1. fera a ceste cause se donnera par voye
35r.32 du 10. tant d'hommes que d'argent & que soubz main 1. l'en remboursera avec
35r.33 les interestz a taux de banque, & par ce moyen la guerre se fera au nom

## Doubtful readings (second pass, 27 Oct 2026, native-resolution crops of every doubtful word)

Settled at native resolution:
- 35r.04 "émotions": é-m-o-t-i-o-n-s, the final s long; certain.
- 35r.22 "Roccas", "Vier": every letter certain; the place and the captor are not identified (see NOTES).
- 35r.24 "aff[air]es": "affes" with a superscript abbreviation sign, after the struck "françoys": "parmy les affaires de
  ce Royaume". Corrects the first pass's "affect[ionné]s".
- 35r.26 "de bon~~ne part~~ lieu": the stroke starts inside "bonne" and runs through "part", so the copyist turned
  "de bonne part" into "de bon lieu". Corrects the first pass's unread word.
- 35r.31 "q[ue]": a q with a long tail, the period's repeated "que" after "d'ores en là".
- 35v.05 "a" (qu'il a / d'accabler): a plain a, as written.
- 35v.22-23 "stylé", "cogneu", "occurran[ce]s": all letters clear; the last has a superscript ending.

Still doubtful:
- 35r.11 "fer[r]oit": "fer" then an ending written over another (r + oit?); the conditional fits the sentence.
- 35r.29 "empieter": the letter after p is r-shaped ("empretes"); "empieter 17" (encroach on Picardy) is the sense.
- 35v.05 "baste l'ame": b or h, and "ame" has no visible r or s; "haste l'arme" and "baste l'asne" were tried and do
  not fit the strokes better.
- 35v.12 "po[ur] me": "p" with superscript, then m or n with a hat and e.
- 35v.20 the heading: "Ce" + q with a long stroke + "suytte"/"suyt e[st]" + "mys en chiffre".
