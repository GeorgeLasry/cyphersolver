# Aerssen to Oldenbarnevelt, Paris, 25 May 1601 (excerpt): English translation

… the English agent assured me that the peace negotiation is being resumed, and that I must not ⟦doubt the effort of
{47'} to dispose the Queen of England to it and to obtain it by all means⟧. Without wishing to dissect this for me, ⟦he
is nevertheless implicated in the party of the late {45'}⟧, and by the denunciation of the Ambassador Neville, who has
since been put in the Tower.
