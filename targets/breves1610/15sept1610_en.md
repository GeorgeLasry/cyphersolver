# Marie de Médicis to Brèves, 15 September 1610, f. 17r (R2075): English translation

If on top of that they still raise difficulties about it, it will be showing too plainly their malice and ambition,
which I judge, should it come to action, must be accompanied by imprudence and [?by] weakness, given the present state
of their affairs.
