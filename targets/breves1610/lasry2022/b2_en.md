# Additional document in the Henri IV-Brèves key, image b2 (George Lasry's CTTS decryption, edited): English translation

Adding that la Halle and le Cormier still press keenly for themselves for the said Cheville, and that their children
are of an age more suitable and fitting for this [doing?] than the younger sister of Saluste; that to manage the peace
of the state it is expedient, indeed necessary, to manage likewise the friendship of the neighbours; that if France
has ever needed to practise this precept, it is today that it is necessary, amid the multitude of factious and
trouble-making spirits of both religions who are found in it, against whose designs nothing more powerful can be set
than the calm of the kingdom; for, the trouble [?being at once] fomented by the envy of the neighbours of [74̄],
religion and the state would suffer together not a little, to the great displeasure of His Holiness. [Continues beyond
the image.]
