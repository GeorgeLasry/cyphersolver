# Marie de Médicis to Brèves, 10 Nov 1610, image 3: George Lasry's CTTS reading

Rests on: George Lasry's CTTS transcription and decryption (2022; `lasry2022/ctts/decryption/3.txt`) and his edited
text (`lasry2022/ctts/edited/3_edits.txt`), joined here into running text (the line break in "recognoi-ssant" closed).
His "…" at both ends marks that the excerpt starts and ends inside the letter; they are not gaps in the cipher and are
left out. Cipher part: "sa saintete recognoissant leur arogance et sentant sa timidite"; the rest is the clear text
around it as he gives it. Written for the v3 verification, 23 Oct 2026.

## Reading

Aussy vois je bien que Sa Sainteté, recognoissant leur arogance et sentant sa timidité, ne leur pressera jusques a
l'extremité du desarmement.
