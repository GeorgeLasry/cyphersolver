# Marie de Médicis to Brèves, 10 November 1610, f. 19r (R2076): English translation

So I see clearly that the Pope, [?recognising] their arrogance and feeling his own timidity, [would] not be pressed to
the extremity of disarmament only to receive in the end the shame of an absolute refusal.
