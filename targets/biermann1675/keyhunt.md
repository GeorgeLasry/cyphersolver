## Families: result so far

No key in the volume was found that reads any of the seven open families (L, M, R, N, K, C, Y).
Tested: 114 against Lilienkron and Meyercron 2 Jan (no match; it is dated after 1676 by "Gross-Cantzler
Graf von Ahlefeldt"), 265 against Meyercron 2 Jan (no match), 075 against Lincker 10 Apr (no match),
054 against the Lilienkron small numbers (no match).

Contact sheets of 053-309 were viewed at low resolution; images with a number-and-name layout were
cropped (listed above). Not cropped at reading size: 083-113, 117-123, 125-138, 140-152, 169-216,
224-231, 233-260, 264, 266-309 (mostly later keys, loose letter tables and the 153-168 register by
their contact-sheet appearance). These remain to be checked closely for a 1670s key.

### 1670s keys seen and still unmatched
- 265: Poland key with Griffenfeld as count (1673-76), names 28-95, syllables 96-325, letters with two sign rows and 1-25.
- 114-116: Danish key naming Ahlefeldt as Gross-Cantzler (so 1676 or later), names 45-300, word list 340 to past 600; close in layout to 055 (two sign alphabets + numbers). Not the key for L or Y.
