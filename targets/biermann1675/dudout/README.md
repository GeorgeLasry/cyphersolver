# Brice Dudout's readings in the Biermann copybook (catalogue 465)

Contributed 28 Oct 2026. Write-ups: https://bdudout.github.io/europe-chiffree/en/documents.html#hoeg-1675 and
#meyercrone-1675 ; history article: https://bdudout.github.io/europe-chiffree/en/copenhague-1675.html

Same key as this folder (TKUA "Chiffer" 1670-1709, image 55), transcribed independently as `key_K55.tsv` and
frozen on 26 Oct 2026 before any reading (`K55.sha256`). Values added after freezing are in
`key_K55_additions.tsv`, each justified in the reading notes. Validated 208/213 on held-out passages, about
98.7% of cipher tokens valued.

This folder adds the letters not yet read here: Høeg from 13 February to 5 June 1675 (7 letters) and the
Meyercrone letters from 16 February to 21 June 1675 (19 letters; 23 March is also read in
`../reading_1675-03-23_meyercron.md`, so the two readings can be compared). For each letter, in `texts/`:
transcription, decryption (`python decode.py texts/<file>`), reading in context (French) and English translation.

Our readings of 2 January to 9 February 1675 (11 Høeg letters), made in parallel with this folder's own readings,
are on the write-up; they agree with them in substance and are not duplicated here.

Letters: h0213, h0302, h0306, h0330, h0410, h0515, h0605, m0216, m0223, m0306, m0316, m0323, m0330, m0406, m0413, m0420, m0424, m0427, m0504, m0511, m0522, m0601, m0605, m0608, m0611, m0621.
