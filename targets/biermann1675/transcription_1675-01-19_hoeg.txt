# Doubtful readings
# p23v.07, .14, .16 '31': clearly 31 (as in the 12 Jan letter); reads s each time ('la-31-sistence', 'a-31-sistance', 'Augu-31-te').
# p23v.09 '313': here 313 = be as on the sheet ('deli-313-ration'); elsewhere (9 and 23 Jan) the copyist uses 313 for fe.
# p23v.16 'phi 33 434 31 634': a name after 'le duc' and before 'de Plön' (p24 clear: 'Le Duc Auguste de Plön');
#   phi = the hooked q-like sign of p13.02; 33 and 634 are not on the sheet. Reads A-u-gu-s-te.
# p23v.17 '500': with a diaeresis over the last 0 (lö): 'Plön'.
# p23v.18 'A-': the sign read c elsewhere ('effect').
# p23v.28 '8': clearly 8 (m) in 'se servi-8 de', where r (9) is needed; the same 8 for r in the 12 Jan letter.
# p24.03 'm3': clearly the luy-y sign; 'tant de re-m3-re-t' needs g (regret).
# p24.13 '8 9': 'de Mr' (8 = m, 9 = r) before 'Meyercron'.
# p24v.01 '710': not on the sheet; 'Ran-t-710-7s' = Rantzow, so 710 = zo; '7s' the 7-shaped sign, set1 W (w).
