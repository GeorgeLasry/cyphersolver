# p36.18 '?invT': an inverted T (⊥), not among the key.tsv labels; 'co-?-me' = comme, so m.
# p36.20 'q-': q with a crossbar on the stem (set1 O); 'invasion'.
# p36.21 '366 lam 314': 'pour la da-f-bi-culté' = difficulté; 366 (da) where di (368) is needed, 314 (bi) where
#   fi (414) is needed: the 3/4 confusion seen before (313/413, 330/430).
# p36.22 '7': a plain 7; 'fa-7-te' = faite wants i (17): a dropped 1?
# p36v.01 '593': not on the sheet; 'longueurs 593-o-n' = qu'on, so qu (592) or quo (591) meant.
# p36v.06 'A-': the barred-A-like sign, c ('effects').
# p36v.09 '19': 'se ra-f-19-ro-i-di-r' = se raffroidir (refroidir): 19 (d) where f is needed.
# p36v.24 '8': clearly 8; 'p-o-u-8 augmenter' = pour, so r (as in the 12 and 19 Jan letters).
# p37.16 '?Cn': the C with inner hook (c on p31v); here 'luy-mesme na-?-ant encores exercé' wants y (n'ayant).
