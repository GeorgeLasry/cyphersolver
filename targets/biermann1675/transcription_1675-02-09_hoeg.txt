# Doubtful readings
# p47v.07 '38': a name number, not on the sheet ('ce que 38 vous a escrit'; again p48.06 'la lettre que 38 vous a escrite').
# p47v.07 'A-': the barred-A-like sign, c ('marche').
# p47v.10, p48.15, p48.26, p48v.17 '?Xl': the looped X, r (secours, pour, tirer, songer).
# p47v.13, p47v.24 '702': xe ('e-702-cu-te', 'l'e-702-cu-tion'), as in the 9 Jan letter.
# p47v.15 '33': u ('avoir e-u-33'?): after 'y' (e) the sense 'eu' needs only u; 33 stands there.
# p47v.15 '559': not on the sheet; 'le moindre 559 pour cela' (ordre?).
# p47v.23, .24 '31': s ('de-31-sein', 'em-pe-31-cher').
# p47v.24 'E': written as a plain C here; c ('empescher').
# p48.05 'cf 50': 'que vo-u-50-y' = que vous luy; 50 is clearly two digits (501 lu with the 1 dropped?).
# p48.29 '498 498': 'si elle le le voyoit': dittography.
# p48.29-p48v.01 'm3': 'plus lo-n-m3-te-m-p-s' wants g (longtemps), as in 'regret' on 19 Jan.
# p48v.08 '582': 'avec ce 582' = Prince, as on 9 Jan ('si ce 582').
# p48v.12 '26': n ('dans u-26 pareil cas'); also fits 'resolutio-26' on 26 Jan.
# p48v.20 '#': the double-crossed sign, set2 F (f): 'offrent'.
