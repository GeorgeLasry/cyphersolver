# Sign notes
# 'sq': an open square (□), not on the key sheet; reads l each time here (p9v.16 'il', p10.21 'alliez',
#   p9v.25 'Alsace'), which also fits the open square of the Meyercron transcription (p105.24 'qui[l]').
# '?Xl': an X with a looped top, distinct from the plain X (u); reads r each time (p9v.08 'accommoder',
#   p9v.20 'pour', p10.12 's'equipper', p10v.02 'Mr', p10v.09 'quartiers', p10v.15 'ordre', p10v.16 'avertir').
# 'A-': a sign like a barred A / R with a stroke (set2 C); reads c ('eschec' p9v.24, 'effect' p10v.05).
# 'q-': on p9v.01 a 7-like sign with a top bar; taken as set1 O (q with bar); 'ils ont'.
# 'db': the looped d (set2 B): 'capable' p9v.26, 'obmettre' p10.22, 'publiques' p10v.08.
# 'd-': the barred d (set2 ö), p10v.03 in 'Mr. Gi-ö-u' (= Giøe) and p10v.17 between 401 and 451 ('Hollandois').
# 'K': the k-like sign, set2 E (e).
