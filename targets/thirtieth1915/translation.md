# “Thirtieth” table: English translation

Working translations from the Wenmi site; […] = passage not translated for want of a sure reading.

## 0211 | 26 February 1915 | Shanghai → “Rosamonde”, Tokyo (“Thirtieth”) | JACAR B03050087600, PDF page 49 | sent 3.32 pm

The money for Shanghai has met with some difficulties and probably cannot be sent on the 4th of next month …; so I met Mr Inuzuka; I have already wired Mr Inuzuka's office to entrust the matter to him.

## 0203 | 27 February 1915 | Shanghai → “Rosamonde”, Tokyo (“Thirtieth”) | JACAR B03050087600, PDF page 41 | sent 11.43 am

The money has just been wired to Hong Kong. The family can be ready within a week.

## 0220 | 7 March 1915 | Shanghai → “Rosamonde”, Tokyo (“Thirtieth”) | JACAR B03050087600, PDF page 58 | sent 11.12 am

Inuzuka's telegram received. Liu has done the accounts: there are no funds left for the requests. Whatever you hold on deposit, Sir, please wire at once.

## 0224 | 10 March 1915 | Tokyo (頭山) → “Junzaburo”, Shanghai | JACAR B03050087700, PDF page 4 | sent 11.10 am

What rumours are going round in Shanghai these days about my safety? Reply, and [tell] the comrades not to believe them. — Wen

## 0233a | 12 March 1915 | Shanghai → “Rosamonde”, Tokyo (“Thirtieth”) | JACAR B03050087700, PDF page 13 | sent 1.57 pm

Quickly allot the maintenance funds for Zhejiang and Jiangsu, two thousand yuan. I have telegraphed Inuzuka; please come to terms with him quickly. — Shan (山).

## 0233b | 13 March 1915 | Shanghai → “Rosamonde”, Tokyo (“Thirtieth”) | JACAR B03050087700, PDF page 13 | sent 2.3 pm

[…] there is a rumour of an assassination; I have told the comrades not to worry about it (?). Jiangsu and Zhejiang (淅 for 浙) are waiting urgently for funds: please remit quickly; awaiting your reply. — Shan (山).

## 0243 | 30 March 1915 | Shanghai → “Rosamonde”, Tokyo (“Thirtieth”) | JACAR B03050087700, PDF page 23 | sent 11.30 am

The Zhejiang (?) funds are exhausted; […] it is especially hard […]. I fear something unforeseen that would harm the whole undertaking. Please transfer the navy's funds temporarily for this use. Reply.

## 0246 | 31 March 1915 | Shanghai → “Rosamonde”, Tokyo (“Thirtieth”) | JACAR B03050087700, PDF page 26 | sent 11.55 am

Arrived safely in Shanghai; details by letter. — Ying (英, Chen Qimei?).

## 0255 | 11 April 1915 | Shanghai → “Rosamonde”, Tokyo | JACAR B03050087700, PDF page 35 | sent 3.22 pm

At the end of the lunar month the various offices need five thousand in gold; if it is hard for you to raise, please ask Mr Inuzuka to find it without fail and wire it.

## 0261 | 5 May 1915 | Shanghai → “Rosamonde”, Tokyo (“Thirtieth”) | JACAR B03050087700, PDF page 41 | sent 5.40 pm

Guo refuses to see us; funds are hard to come by. Wang Chonghui (王寵惠) proposes to bring Kang [Youwei] and Liang [Qichao] to join hands with us, united against Yuan [Shikai]; he says they have already placed their forces in the army and in key posts, and have sent a representative to America to get in touch with Ke[qiang] (Huang Xing), etc. Could we put off their oath, look into what they are really worth, and use them as circumstances allow? Please instruct.
