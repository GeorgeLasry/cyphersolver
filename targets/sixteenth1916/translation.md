# “Sixteenth” table: English translation

Working translations from the Wenmi site; […] = passage not translated for want of a sure reading.

## 0590 | 15 April 1916 | Shanghai (0125) → Nakayama 109 Harajuku (15 words) [Sixteenth] | JACAR B03050088500, PDF page 40

Arrived in Shanghai today. Land and naval operations have been agreed with Yingshi and Major Nakajima. Please […] Tong, respectfully.

## 0592 | 18 April 1916 | Shanghai (03) → Nakayama 109 Harajuku (18 words) [Sixteenth] | JACAR B03050088500, PDF page 42

Telegram received. […] Control the sea to secure the country’s foundations. Send future telegrams through Yamanaka Minetarō at the Banzaikan. Tong.

## 0603 | 19 April 1916 | Tokyo (中山) → Yamanaka, Banzaikwan, Shanghai (13 words) | JACAR B03050088500, PDF page 53

When can you act? What is the latest situation? Please send frequent detailed reports so that the whole situation can be coordinated. Essential. Sun Wen, the 19th. “Please” is a proposed reading.

## 0616 | 21 April 1916 | Tokyo (中山) → Yamanaka, Banzaikwan, Shanghai (21 words) | JACAR B03050088600, PDF page 12

Did you receive the telegram to Yamanaka on the 19th? […] Are there obstacles […]? What is the situation on each side since your arrival in Shanghai? […] Detailed reports requested.

## 0629a | 22 April 1916 | Shanghai (015) → Nakayama Harajuku (7 words) [Sixteenth] | JACAR B03050088600, PDF page 25

This does not harm the overall situation; please do not worry.

## 0629b | 22 April 1916 | Shanghai (082) → Nakayama Harajuku (29 words) [Sixteenth] | JACAR B03050088600, PDF page 25

First Squadron […] Guangdong and Zhejiang; Second […] the Yangzi. Tong proposes to take the great river first, then Fujian […]. For Shanghai, Ying leads the plan and Tong assists. Arrangements are being made.
