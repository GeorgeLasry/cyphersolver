## Translation

Most Illustrious Lord and Benefactor,
I would count it a singular happiness to write to Your Grace and benefactor at every moment and at every moment to
receive every command of your kind hand.
As soon as I arrived here [...] I send Your Grace and benefactor a letter [...] [?dispatched] was to have an audience
with the vizier [...] [?unpleasant] respect.
The Turks will not believe the Seraskier, nor the Khan, and so they summon me directly.
The Seraskier wrote to the hospodar [of Wallachia] to persuade me, and urge me, to go to Bender.
So by God's grace I am now past the first trap.
The hospodar is well disposed to us, but timid, [for] he says that [the Khan] denounces him to the Porte.
The hospodar, His Grace, faithful [...]
