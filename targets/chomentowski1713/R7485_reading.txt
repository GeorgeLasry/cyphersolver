## Notes

Cipher passage: 96 tokens, all carried. Slips (R7485_pass2.txt): 191 p written for 181 o in "Han[o]wi"; 120 ku written
for 139 ła in "wo[ła]ia" (the clear head-text has "wołaią"); 329 za glossed "zą" in "pi[e]r[w]sza" (the figures give
pi-r-s-za; the clear head-text "pierwszą"); 59 e after 140 le in "ale" written twice (left out); 161 unglossed, "ny"
from its band and the clear head-text. "[bo]" is a clear word in the glosser's dark ink between the figures, not the
writer's. 284 u glossed "Han" by the glosser, who wrote the subject over it; the figures give "u-da-ie" (udaie); the
subject (the Khan, per the gloss) is not written. "Hospodar" (code 96) stands alone in the cipher; "Wołoski" is only in
the clear head-text.
