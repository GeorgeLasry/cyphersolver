## Translation

f. 103r: I cannot fail to tell Your Highness that it seems to me that [?] has not favoured the Lord Duke's affairs in the way that it seems to me he ought to have, since he is the one who governs as he does in these times; and if I could tell you everything in a short letter, Your Highness would see that he has shown that he holds His Excellency in little account, which has greatly astonished me, because I would never have thought it; a[nd] if…

f. 103v: I were somewhere I could speak to you, I would tell you things that would seem strange to you, as they seemed to me when the event happened. I have just this moment heard from a person worthy of trust and a man of quality…
