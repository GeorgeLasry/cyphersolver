## Notes

* The battle is Calcinato (19 April 1706, near Montichiari), where Reventlow's imperial corps was beaten by Vendôme;
  "Reventau" is printed `[?Reventau]` because the name is faded.
* "6000 teutsche gegen 25 m.": the "25" is overlined with a small m (25,000), not a cipher.
* The code on P2 l.4 is written 131 (the earlier transcription had 181).
* "[?bittet um]" (P2 l.5-6): "belobt 187 und Nachricht" lacks a verb; the bracketed words are supplied in part.
