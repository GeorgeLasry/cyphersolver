# Grid G0 — “文密”: English translation

Working translations from the Wenmi site; […] = passage not translated for want of a sure reading.

## 0348 | 23 June 1916 | Tokyo → Zhaoqing (軍務院, 岑) | JACAR B03050089900 | sent 8.13 pm

Eleven thousand Type 30 rifles and military supplies have arrived at Moji. The price is treated as a temporary loan at ten per cent annual interest, without guarantee. Only Koike is in charge; your telegram is awaited before shipping to Guangdong: press quickly by telegram, and do not refuse under any circumstances, to avoid having to pay compensation. Furthermore, the private cipher telegraph link is now working. Reply directly to Seki Masao. — Shen. The 23rd.

## 0386 | 30 June 1916 | Tokyo → Zhaoqing (軍務院, 岑) | JACAR B03050089900 | sent 2.45 pm

Your telegram has arrived. The weapons and aircraft left yesterday at noon from […]; they can arrive on the […]. It is advisable to return to Shanghai […] will come to Japan again. Shen remains […]. — Shen. The 20th (?).

## 0406 | 12 July 1916 | Tokyo → Shanghai (中華新報, 轉文羣) | JACAR B03050090000 | sent 2.50 pm

Duan [Qirui] has neither the sincere will nor the ability to form the cabinet. The Japanese government now shares this view; the misunderstanding regarding the Military Affairs Council and the South as a whole has […] been dispelled. From now on, as long as the agreed plan is not changed, the final assistance can be hoped for. […] There is no longer any obstacle to action. […] — Shen. The 12th (文, written twice).

## 0415 | 18 July 1916 | Tokyo → Zhaoqing (軍務院) | JACAR B03050090000 | sent 5.30 pm

According to the telegram from Yamagata received by the General Staff, the weapons this time […] Yunnan; its difficulties are beyond words, and the General Staff itself is very disappointed. When Shen was in Yunnan, a division had already been promised; the half delivered this time is only part of it. If it is not given, for the weapons as for the funds, Yunnan is disregarded: what will people say? We urge you to distribute the aid in the agreed quantities, to avoid criticism. What do you say? — Shen. The 18th.

## 0416b | 19 July 1916 | Tokyo → Zhaoqing (軍務院) | JACAR B03050090000 | sent 5.50 pm

A cancellation is reported; the attitude of the Japanese has suddenly changed. Tell me quickly whether this is true, so that I know what to do. — Shen. The 19th (皓; the ciphertext has 4210 through an e → i slip, corrected thanks to the parallel telegram 0416a).

## 0455b | 13 September 1916 | Tokyo → Zhaoqing (岑都司令) | JACAR B03050090100 | sent 7.30 pm

The apparatus is purchased on behalf of Yunnan. If the General Staff enquires by telegram, please reply approving it. — Shen. The 13th.

## 0462 | 30 September 1916 | Tokyo → Zhaoqing (都司令部, 李印泉) | JACAR B03050090100 | sent 11 am

Your telegram is received. For the vehicle supplies arranged earlier and the loan contracted with Xinghua, please proceed as agreed; otherwise our credit with Yunnan will be ruined. The Guangdong affair being over, please hand over to Yunnan the second batch of weapons arranged earlier, so as not to give grounds for criticism. What is the situation? Awaiting your reply. — Shen. The 30th.
