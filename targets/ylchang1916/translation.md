# Huang Xing's table: English translation

Working translations from the Wenmi site; […] = passage not translated for want of a sure reading.

## 0091a | 26 May 1916 | Tokyo → Shanghai (Junzaburo) | JACAR B03050089100, PDF page 32 | sent 6.10 pm

Has the arms matter been telegraphed to the Japanese authorities (?)? They do not wish you to rush to Shandong (魯). Please reply. — Xing (Huang Xing).

## 0095a | 28 May 1916 | Tokyo → Shanghai (Junzaburo) | JACAR B03050089100, PDF page 36 | sent 9.15 pm

Your telegram reached me after I moved. Yesterday they had no time; this evening I shall go and discuss it. Whatever happens, I shall reply again. — Xing.

## 0115b | 30 May 1916 | Shanghai → Tokyo (Ylchang) | JACAR B03050089100, PDF page 56 | sent 3.35 pm

According to news from the Navy and from Mitsui: Mitsui, Mitsubishi and Ōkura […] intend to provide five million to the Southern army […]. Cen [Chunxuan] (岑), Huang [Xing] (黃) and Sun [Yat-sen] (孫) […] would sign the loan contract. Cen replied that […] Yunnan […]. Is Jiangxi (贛) confirmed? […] decide as circumstances require.

## 0130b | 31 May 1916 | Tokyo → Shanghai (Junzaburo) | JACAR B03050089200, PDF page 13 | sent 2.50 pm

Funds: 200,000; arms: a certain quantity. I have asked [Hu] Hanmin (漢民) to go in person and ask Aoki (青木) to telegraph the authorities again; success can be hoped for. — Xing.

## 0131b | 31 May 1916 | Tokyo → Shanghai (Junzaburo) | JACAR B03050089200, PDF page 14

I have not been informed about the loan; the Japanese authorities have not notified anything either; its content is still unknown. […]

## 0144a | 2 June 1916 | Shanghai → Tokyo (Ylchang) | JACAR B03050089200, PDF page 27

Telegram received. I have already informed (?) Aoki (青木); please […] also do all you can (?). — Wen (Sun Yat-sen).

## 0145 | 3 June 1916 | Tokyo → Shanghai (Junzaburo) | JACAR B03050089200, PDF page 28 | sent 10.10 am

This evening I met Tanaka (田中) and discussed: 2,000 rifles and 6 guns can be shipped shortly. Then […], but [they] do not wish you [to rush there; Puquan could be asked,] together with a respected military man, to mediate and unify [the forces]. Their intention is […]: what do you think? Please reply. — Xing. (In brackets, nine codes lost in transmission, restored by the telegram of 6 June.)

## 0152b | 6 June 1916 | Tokyo → Shanghai (Junzaburo) | JACAR B03050089200, PDF page 35 | sent 2.45 pm

(Retransmission of the passage of 3 June) […] but [they] do not wish you, Sir, to rush there; Puquan (溥泉, Zhang Ji) and a respected military man could be asked to mediate.

## 0160a | 6 June 1916 | Shanghai → Tokyo (Ylchang) | JACAR B03050089200, PDF page 43 | sent 4.34 pm

Liewu (烈武, Bai Wenwei) is going […] to unify the armies; all ask for Puquan (溥泉, Zhang Ji). I have sent […], leaving on the […] with Xu Chongzhi (許崇智). On the 200,000 matter, Aoki says he has already telegraphed […]; please press for its conclusion. […] — Sun Wen (孫文). The 6th.
