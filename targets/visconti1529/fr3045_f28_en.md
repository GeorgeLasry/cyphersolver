# Galeazzo Visconti to Angelo Bolano, Alessandria, 15 Oct 1528 (BnF fr. 3045 f. 28): English translation

Translated from `fr3045_f28_v2_reading.md` (Claude Opus 5.5, 23 Oct 2026): the cipher, read with Tomokiyo's Visconti
key as extended in `KEY.md`. Holes as in the reading: `[42]`, `[rr]`, `[qq]`, `[ep]` code groups with no key value;
`[?word]` a doubtful reading; `[?]` unread; letters supplied inside a word in [ ]. The 1528 Lombard is often elliptic;
where the Italian does not build a sentence (the reading marks ll. 8-9, the "quanto ci è" clause and ll. 14-15), the
English follows it word for word and stays broken.

## Translation

Bolano. [42] This [rr] alone gives you: Savona, which has been relieved, for the slowness of the world; I say that he
should provide for everything [qq], otherwise everything will always go badly, of the going of Montigia to enter
Savona; divisions are the cause that this command has been, apart from its reputation, like a lost battle: everyone
wanted to go into winter quarters, so that it was enough to make the allies despair, who are not far from being so,
and yet [?at] all so much ease, I say, finding expedients so that the matter, which [was] desperate, the [?] harm
will be had with Urbino; and one will see a very hard thing mended, at least badly. All the adventurers [have]
left, through their cowardice and the deceits of their captains, [?as] [?there] [?is] one soldo, which is the cause
that the Italians who have [rr] them the advance money are in Puglia. Designated [?here] [rr] was being sent to the
si[?]ege [?] [?had] shown the ruin [qq]; the lances that [?]suit us are few, and will be very late, and [?I] think
who will go paid [when] arrived at [?]; God knows how ill content they are with the service of [ep], which,
notwithstanding all my ill treatment, will be the end of mine, commending myself to your good gra[ce]. From
Alessandria, 15 October 1528.
