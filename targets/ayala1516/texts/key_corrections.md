# Corrections and additions to Tomokiyo's key (KEY.md), copied verbatim from NOTES.md (second and third passes, 21 Sept 2026).

### Key corrections to Tomokiyo's table
- Φ (circle with vertical stroke) = **m**, confirmed on every page (comendador, mucho, amistad, malamente). The hook sign is **j** (carvajal, aconseja, junto consejo).
- The I-shaped sign is a variant of **s** (sobre, se las). A horizontal looped sign (∝) is also **s** (esto se mirara).
- X· = que, and also q inside words (aqui, requerile). 卄 and H both serve for f/h. H· = Xebres (Chièvres): "v. s. dize mal de H·".
- Abbreviations: n[uest]ro señor, su a[lteza], v. s. (vuestra señoría).
