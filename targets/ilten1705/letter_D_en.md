# D. Elector Georg Ludwig to J. H. von Ilten, Hannover, 25 May 1706: English translation

Translation (Claude Opus 5.5, 23 Oct 2026) of `letter_D_reading.md` (the clear German letter with each code run
replaced by the contemporary interlinear gloss). The passage from "the King in Prussia" to "anything of it" was in
code. No holes; the gloss's awkward "bey auch" is rendered as it stands.

## Translation

By the grace of God, Georg Ludwig, Duke of Brunswick and Lüneburg, Elector of the Holy Roman Empire. Our gracious
greeting first, noble, steadfast councillor, and dear faithful subject! We have learned from your report in your own
hand of the 22nd of this month that you think you have reason to believe that the King in Prussia would himself in
person ask for the Princess our daughter for the Crown Prince in Prussia, inasmuch as the Grand Chamberlain gave you
to understand that the King desired to see you. Now, if you can with good manner avert it, so that such a personal
suit does not take place, it will be very welcome to Us, and you would also try your best in it, yet merely as on
your own account, and perhaps through intimations to the Grand Chamberlain, inasmuch as you [are] towards no one
else, or [?with] him either, to let anything of it be noticed. We remain graciously disposed towards you. Hanover, the
25th of May in the year 1706.

Georg Ludwig, Elector. J. Hattorf
