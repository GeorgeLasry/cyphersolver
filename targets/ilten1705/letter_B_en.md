## Translation

Monsieur. The 10th of October 1705. The Chamberlain, Monsieur the Count of Wartensleben, cannot praise enough the
courtesies and honours he received at Hanover; he is confused by the fine present that was added there, and which he
did not expect. He has had it intimated to me by Monsieur de Berlips, the better to hide our dealings, that, having
had occasion to make known to the King the uprightness of His Electoral Highness's sentiments, he had put His Majesty
into rather favourable dispositions; that he had also done justice to [?] Madame the Princess, daughter of His
Electoral Highness, speaking very advantageously of her, without however touching on the point of marriage; but that
he had nevertheless spoken of it to the Grand Chamberlain, making him understand the particular interest he had in
favouring the marriage of the Crown Prince with the said Princess; that, although this Minister was pledged by his
word to another quarter, he hoped nevertheless to win him over in such a way that the aforesaid affair could in time
be put on the table with hope of success, and that then your expedient could be sent here to work on it; that it was
still necessary to temporise a little and to show the King of Prussia every possible complaisance, and above all to
avoid its appearing that either Madame de Bülow or Mademoiselle de Pöllnitz have a hand in the affair. I gave
assurance that people were much mistaken to believe that these ladies were employed in an affair of such consequence,
since one was only too persuaded that it could succeed only through the channel of the Grand Chamberlain. The said
Count will continue to have me warned secretly of what passes on the subject in question, so that measures can be
taken accordingly. Your Excellency will be pleased to give an account of this, judging yourself how necessary it is
that everything be carefully handled. For the rest I refer to my report, and am always, with the same zeal and
respect, Monsieur, Your Excellency's very humble and very obedient servant, Heusch. Berlin, this 10th of October 1705.
