# Sega to Cardinal Caetano, 9 Jan 1591, BnF fr. 3613 f. 148v: the reading of the enciphered passage

Written 23 Oct 2026 (verification 3): George Lasry's reading of the complete enciphered passage of f. 148v, quoted
from his report "BnF cipher research" (10 Oct 2026; `lasry_entry.md`), with Meister's published key 65 (1906). The
cipher's spelling is kept (single consonants, u for v). The ciphertext has not been transcribed in this repository;
one sample was spot-checked against the Gallica image on 13 Oct 2026.

## Reading

Che Lignerac è stato inuiato d'ordine di Umena in Poitu et Bretagna a solecitar la impresa di Tours, per doue ua
anco a questi efeto un religioso, acciò se l'uno fose impedito l'altro pasi.
