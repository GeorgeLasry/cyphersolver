# catinat1702: web research, 28 Oct 2026 (no key, no crib text found; two useful context findings)

## Result in one line
No key material, no clear or deciphered copy, no sibling Catinat letter and no signature were found online. Found: (a) the
place name "Ingleshem" is almost certainly Lingolsheim, the Bruche camp of 14-25 Sept 1702; (b) Vault vol. 2 gives the
events of 9-25 Sept day by day, which is the plaintext context (list below).

## 1. Aargau Kantonsbibliothek / Zurlaubiana
- Catalogue = kbaargau.visual-library.de/ah (Acta Helvetica regests). The search form (`/ah/search/quick?query=`) sits behind a
  JavaScript browser-check, so it could not be queried from here (not bypassed). Individual items are reachable only as
  `https://kbaargau.visual-library.de/ah/download/pdf/<id>` or `.../ah/periodical/titleinfo/<id>`; the web search engine
  indexes some of them but returned none for Catinat or for 15 Sept 1702.
- Neighbouring item found: **AH 167/50, 1702 Mar 30, Marly** (Bl. 85-86, copy in French, pdf id 264855,
  https://kbaargau.visual-library.de/ah/download/pdf/264855). Unknown author (the regest guesses an addressee Tessé), writes
  to Zurlauben about "sans chifre", the Mantua garrison and the Duke of Mantua; text breaks off. Not Catinat, but it shows
  (i) AH 167 is the 1702 Spanish-succession volume, so the Catinat leaf (Bl. 238) is probably **AH 167, Bl. 238-239**, and
  (ii) Beat Jakob II Zurlauben was blockaded in Mantua in 1702 (regest footnote cites Pinard/Chronologie IV, 457f.). So the
  addressee of a Sept 1702 Alsace letter is doubtful; Tomokiyo already notes the 1702 Zurlauben was in Italy.
  A guess for the neighbours of Bl. 238: pdf ids are not in date order, so they cannot be derived; ask the library for
  AH 167 Bl. 230-245 (letters of the same bundle may be copies of other Alsace dispatches, "Dup.ta" = duplicate).
- Blog Klaus Schmeh 2016 (https://scienceblogs.de/klausis-krypto-kolumne/2016/05/01/wer-knackt-diesen-verschluesselten-brief-von-nicolas-de-catinat/):
  names no signature. Links collected from its comments: Bazeries-Appendix.pdf and Bazeries-More-Letters.pdf (Ruth Wust
  scans), jfbouch.fr/crypto/catinat/catinat.html (table that Schmeh later found did not fit; the host has a bad TLS
  certificate for www.jfbouch.fr and jfbouch.fr, so it would not load), e-periodica sgw-002:2008:23::329 (Busser 2008,
  Zurlauben family c. 1700), Google Books ids BUT_hUwNO3UC (Memoires et correspondance du marechal de Catinat, vol. 3) and
  kro5AQAAMAAJ (Vault, Memoires militaires). Thomas cites a Catinat to Louis XIV letter of 25 Aug 1702.
- Searched, nothing: Ruth Wust articles (none found online on this letter), e-manuscripta / e-rara (nothing for Catinat), the
  digithek blog posts on the Acta Helvetica edition (general only).

## 2. Print sources
- **Vault/Pelet, Memoires militaires relatifs a la succession d'Espagne, vol. 2** is on archive.org
  (`mmoiresmilitai02vauluoft`; OCR text at https://archive.org/download/mmoiresmilitai02vauluoft/mmoiresmilitai02vauluoft_djvu.txt,
  2.1 MB; vol. 3 = `mmoiresmilitai03vauluoft`). Campaign of Germany 1702 is at about pp. 335-390 (OCR lines 11060-15200).
  It prints the King's letters and some Catinat replies, but **no letter of 15 Sept and none to or from Zurlauben**
  (grep "Zurlauben" in v02 finds no 1702 hit). It does give the narrative below. Page numbers are from the OCR.
- **Place name.** Vault p. 562-565: Catinat was at Ingenheim (near Hochfelden) 17-30 Aug, then Schwindratzheim, Drusenheim,
  Roeschwoog (4 Sept), left Roeschwoog 11 Sept, Drusenheim, **Mundolsheim 12 Sept**, stayed 13th, **14 Sept camp under
  Strasbourg, right at Schiltigheim, left at Eckbolsheim on the Bruche (headquarters)**; he left it 25 Sept (Villars from
  "camp d'Eckbolzheim"), then Illkirch 28th. A camp at Eckbolsheim is next to **Lingolsheim**: "Ingleshem" 15 Sept is very
  likely Lingolsheim (not Ingenheim, which he had left on 30 Aug). Tomokiyo reads Ingenheim; this should be corrected.
- **Plaintext context for 14-18 Sept (Vault pp. 565-580), usable as cribs:** Landau: Melac capitulated 9th (after an
  assault repelled in the night 8-9), a gate handed over the 10th, garrison out the 12th, escorted to Haguenau, reached
  Strasbourg 17 Sept; 81st day of trench, 140th of the blockade; "honneurs de la guerre", 4 canons, 2 mortiers, "chariots
  couverts". Elector of Bavaria took **Ulm on 8 Sept** (Catinat learned it at Mundolsheim on the 12th "par des voies
  indirectes", and "par des marchands suisses"); Catinat's letter by courier of **14 Sept** to the King is answered by the
  King on 18 Sept (Vault p. 579, "marchands suisses ... duc de Baviere ... Ulm", "marechal de Boufflers", "comte Verita");
  M. de **Ricous** (minister at the Bavarian court), count d'**Arco** (14 bataillons, 26 escadrons, Stuhlingen,
  Pfullendorf, Friedlingen), **comte de Luttens**, Huningue, **Villars** to march on the 25th, "40 bataillons et 50
  escadrons", "roi des Romains", "prince de Bade", Heidelberg, Lauter, Moder, Zorn, Bruche, Molsheim, Locmaria, Sarre.
  Catinat took precautions about "lettres chiffrees" with Ricous (Vault p. 574); the Elector's unciphered papers were
  intercepted. So the 15 Sept letter is a **second** dispatch (the 14th is the one the King answers) and is probably a
  duplicate of one of these Alsace/Bavaria reports. A Swiss link: Vault says Villars sent news to the Elector "par la voie
  de M. le marquis de Puysieulx, ambassadeur du roi pres la republique des Suisses", then at Baden; the Elector's
  officers were stopped by "les Suisses". Unproven hypothesis: the leaf is a duplicate sent through Switzerland to be
  relayed (hence "Dup.ta"), not a letter to Zurlauben personally.
- **Memoires et correspondance du marechal de Catinat (1819), vol. 3**: Google Books id BUT_hUwNO3UC; the plain-text view is
  not retrievable by script (consent wall / API quota exceeded). archive.org has no copy (search of titles and full text for
  "Catinat" + "correspondance" returned 0). Not read. Catinat's 1819 correspondence goes to 1702 on the catalogue record
  (vol. 3: 1693-1702), so it may print the French 15 Sept letter in clear; needs the book itself (Gallica / BnF / HathiTrust
  to try by hand).
- Not available online: SHD Vincennes A1 vol. 1569 (Catinat originals; Vault cites "vol. 1569" for the 12 July reply and
  "vol. 1528" for the King's minutes). If the 15 Sept letter has a clear copy it will be in A1 1569/1570 (Sept 1702).

## 3. Candidate codes
- Tomokiyo, https://cryptiana.web.fc2.com/code/louisxiv.htm: item (9) is this letter ("unbroken", highest figure 464, 253 of
  591 groups under 100, bigrams 41 269 x5; 13 101, 50 84, 50 28 x3-4). 1702 codes listed: (8B) d'Usson April 1702, 540/560
  entries; (10) Guelders-Rheinberg Nov 1702, at least 383; (11) c. 1702-04, numbers up to 100 (BnF Clair. 452 f.200). BnF fr.
  6204 (Recueil de chiffres diplomatiques 1688-1713, Gallica btv1b525094217 for no. 9): nos. 1-16 are all 450-886
  entries addressed to others (Boufflers, d'Usson, Villeroy, Bouchu, Tallard/Marcin (mentions Ricous), Vendome 1696,
  Louvois items; no. 13/14 St-Maurice 1711, 495 entries but made 1711). **None is addressed to Catinat**; d'Usson's table
  lists "M. le Mal de Catinat" as a name code (336). The 495-entry no. 13/14 is the nearest in size but is 1711, so not a
  candidate for 1702 unless the same base table was reused (untested).
- Tomokiyo notes Ricous wrote Catinat in a small cipher that Villars could not read, and an Elector-Catinat cipher existed
  (Vault p. 574); neither key is online. The 15 Sept cipher could be the Catinat-Elector one (highest figure 464) if the
  letter is a duplicate of a Bavarian dispatch. Unverified.
- Searched, nothing: DECODE (de-crypt.org) has no Catinat 1702 record that I could find by search; Bazeries' Catinat 1691
  Grand Chiffre (IC 0.005, 587 entries) differs in statistics as Tomokiyo says; no digitised SHD chiffre of 1701-03 found.
