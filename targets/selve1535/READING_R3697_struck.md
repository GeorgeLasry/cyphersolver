# R3697, the struck runs read from the decipherer's glosses

## Read (glossed runs: A, B, C1 with the head of C2, T2; 169 signs)

**Run A (f. 25r line 3):** ⟨Sire, je feis … pour⟩ au marchant, ⟨duquel je vous ay … escript⟩.
Gloss "au marchant"; the eleven signs under the strike agree with the key (a u | m a r · c h a n t).

**Run B (f. 25r line 8):** [?ung] sansacque cy prochain […].
Gloss "ung sansacque cy prochain" ("a sanjak-bey near here"); "prochain" agrees sign for sign, "cy" and the a, n, e of
"sansacque" agree; the four signs after "prochain" have no gloss.

**Run C1 + head of C2 (foot of f. 25r):** Il a consumé ses facultez et la plus part de ses amys […] et grand du
T[urc].
Gloss above C1 (one line for both struck lines). On the signs: C1 does not separate from the strike; C2 reads "ses amis
et gran[d]" by the key, as the gloss has it.

**Run T2 (f. 25v, second struck line):** [?l'entretiennent] amiablement pour la charge qu'il a de Vesprim de la
[?guerre] en luy.
Marginal decipherment ("amiablement — pour la charge qu'il a de Vesprym de la guerre, en luy …"); "amiablement",
"pour", "la", "qu'il", "de", "de la", "en luy" agree sign for sign (about 50 of 64 signs); the c of charge, the a,
and the g of guerre are lost under the strike or a blot. "l'entretiennent" is the tail of T1 (signs, medium-low).

  "du T[urc]" does not align and the words after "amys" in the gloss are blurred.
- C3 (9 signs): "[g]uerr[.]s(?)", not secured; no gloss.
- T1 before "-tretienent" (25 signs): no gloss line matches; the margin's first two lines ("en Hongrie", "Hiero[nimo]
  …") cannot be placed in the signs.
- T3 (55 signs): "on fait […] la […] contre ses […]"; the margin's "s[..] fond[..]" and "ad[..]" are not legible and
  do not align with any value set.

## In English

Run A: "… to the merchant, of whom I have written to you …". Run B: "a sanjak-bey near here". C: "He has consumed his
means and most of his friends […] and great [man] of the Turk". T2: "[they treat him] amicably on account of the
charge he holds of Veszprém, of the war, in him …".
