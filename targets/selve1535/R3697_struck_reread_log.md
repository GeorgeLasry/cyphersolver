# R3697 struck runs: re-read log, 22 Oct 2026 (Claude Opus 5.5, batch mix1)

Brief: research/reading_audit/REREAD_BRIEF.md. The audit had put all 304 struck signs under "decoded, not read".

## 1. Is there a text already?

Yes, in part: the King's decipherer glossed the struck runs, between the lines on f. 25r (runs A, B, C) and in the
margin of f. 25v (T1-T3). The three earlier passes (work/f25r_pass*.md, f25v_pass*.md, 22 Sept - 5 Oct) transcribed
those glosses and used them as cribs, but counted a struck sign as read only when its shape separated from the
strike. Under the brief a contemporary decipherment carries the reading where it is legible, with the signs checked
where they can be seen. That changes the count, not the evidence.

## 3. Split

- Read from the glosses: A (11 signs), B (27), C1 (55), C2 head (13), T2 (64) = 169 signs (one doubling null in A
  excluded: 169 non-null of the 170 counted from R3697_signs.txt lines).
- Not read: C2 tail (36), C3 (9), T1 (35, of which "-tretienent" 10 doubtful), T3 (55) = 135 signs. No gloss or a

## 4. Where it stops

The 135 signs need either a legible gloss (the margin of f. 25v is legible for about 60% of its letters, and its
illegible parts are exactly the ones these runs need) or an image that separates the lighter strike ink from the
cipher ink (colour or multispectral, or the original). Blocker: illegible / needs-physical-access.
