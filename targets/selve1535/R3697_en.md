# R3697, Georges de Selve to Francis I, Venice, 14 Sept 1535: English translation of the unstruck cipher

Translated from `R3697_reading.md` (Claude Opus 5.5, 23 Oct 2026): the unstruck cipher of BnF fr. 3091 f. 25r-v,
deciphered with Tomokiyo's Selve key as extended in `KEY.md`. The clear text of the letter, the decipherer's glosses
and the struck runs (`READING_R3697_struck.md`, `R3697_struck_en.md`) are not part of this entry. Holes as in the
reading: `[?]` an unread sign or group, `[?word]` a doubtful word, letters supplied inside a word in [ ]. The three
paragraphs are three separate cipher runs; each begins in mid-sentence after clear text.

## Translation

… that he should go by the road of the Swiss, for the other would not be safe for him, because the ambassador who is
[?] [?here] for the Emperor, soon after the aforesaid merchant had arrived, as I was informed from that very hour,
moved by a conjecture that he had come in order to go to France, [?pr] [?] [?Anthone] de [?Le] to keep watch for him,
so as to hold him back if he passes through.

… merchant, [and] so [?] ambassa[dor] had told him that it would be good for him to find a way, under colour of
trade or of some other occasion, to make his journey to France and go to your court, to learn on what terms your
affairs stood with the Emperor, and whether some agreement was not being made between you [?].

… they did not fail to send word, and to have word sent by others, of what happens here, without specifying further;
to learn, and the reports they give to the Turk, and that they have given to him by the aforesaid merchant, [and]
when they have sometimes talked of it as from themselves [?two], [you] [?will know] [?].
