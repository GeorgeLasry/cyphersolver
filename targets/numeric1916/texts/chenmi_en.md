# 辰密 (chenmi), 4 telegrams, June 1916: English

Translation of `chenmi_reading.md` (23 Oct 2026, Claude Opus 5.5), same holes. Names as in NOTES.md and Dudout's notes:
興 = Huang Xing; 烈武 = Li Liejun; 溥泉 = Zhang Ji; 黎 = Li Yuanhong; 岑 = Cen Chunxuan; 趙夷午 = Zhao Hengti;
濟武 = Tang Hualong; 湯督 = Governor Tang (Tang Xiangming).

## Translation

### N05

Forward to Brother Liewu: if the Shandong side wishes you to take charge of unification, you may accept at once. Ask
Puquan to [?plan] it together here; we shall help with all our strength. What do you think? [Awaiting] your reply.
Xing.

### N13

To the three brothers Xiao, Yong and Feng: the telegram to Li, Cen and the provinces is very hard to write. If our
positions [?are not one], there is the risk of seeming to go different ways; if it is written as your telegram says,
people will surely take it for opportunism. Please obtain the agreement of the National Protection Army and [?let]
your office draft and send it. Japan's attitude is very much wait-and-see; the comrades in Shanghai should show a firm
position, and the Military Affairs Council above all must be clear. Please telegraph the changes of the last two days.
Xing.

### N16

Also forward to Messrs Zu, Feng and Xiao: unless Li prepares one or two divisions of reliable troops, his government's
orders will hardly be carried out. Zhao Yiwu has always been trusted by Li and is now training troops under Governor
Tang; could you consult Jiwu and the others and find a way for him to train one division of picked troops quickly, to
be at Li's command? As for weapons, we shall try with all our strength, and it should be possible. Yesterday Ke
telegraphed asking that the National Protection Army's agreement be obtained and the telegram drafted and sent on its
behalf; [?on this point] I hope you act quickly. Zhun, [10th].

### N26

Jiu: give three hundred yuan to Baili. Zhun.
