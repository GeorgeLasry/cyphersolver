## N01 | May 1916 | Shanghai → Tokyo | JACAR B03050089000, PDF page 35

To be passed to Mr Keqiang: the rebel Yuan is about to fall, and the situation grows ever more difficult; may your banner come […]; the whole country looks to you. We hope to receive your guidance, so as to have a line to follow. Signed: Jian, Wei, Yu, Yi, Zi, Lu, Kai, Xiu, Lin, […], Yu, Li, Fu, Run, Sheng.
