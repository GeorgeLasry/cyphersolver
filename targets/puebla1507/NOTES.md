## Content (from the prints)

f. 31: Henry VII's quinsy and recovery; Puebla read him at Richmond the despatch of 15 March, deciphered for him;
Henry agreed to put off the payment of Katharine's marriage portion only to Michaelmas (five and a half months);
plate accepted at valuation, jewels only at London resale prices; on Henry's marriage to Queen Juana of Castile,
Henry will send an ambassador with full powers as soon as Ferdinand writes from Castile, to conclude and marry
per verba de praesenti; the English mind little her insanity; Puebla's money troubles.

f. 32: the princess took Puebla's whole despatch into the bag of the courier Perote; this short copy goes in a
Lucca merchant's bag in case French troops in Italy stop the courier; the dowry payment postponed to Michaelmas;
Henry waits for Ferdinand's arrival in Castile to send his embassy for the marriage with the Queen of Castile.
