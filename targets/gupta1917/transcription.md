## M1085 roll 760, frame 0286 (naId 370916833): *German Plots and Intrigues* (CPI 1918), p. 52

The page is filmed upside down. Its key sentence, quoted:

> The following telegram has not been as yet satisfactorily deciphered. It is possible, however, to see what the
> substance of it is:

The substance follows (money through Batavia, Shanghai and Siam; Lahiri, Mukherjee, Sanyal, Roy alias Martin;
the Maverick). After it, under 16 Nov 1916, comes an "extract from a cipher message sent by Gupta, from New York,
to an acquaintance in Switzerland". No ciphertext is printed. Page 51 (frame 0285) gives the context: "telegrams from
Chakraberty to Zimmermann ... during the year 1916".
