my letters, to [repeat?] this question: what is Hetman Branicki doing. The plans of others [?] I do understand, as

Branicki is one of the most attached to Russia. Good God! This Prince of Nassau, who [...] receiving Your Majesty's
[letter], had no time for Your Majesty's answer at his leave-taking, [the way?] on his return from here to say these
words to the Empress, in Your Majesty's letter of the 28th of last month [...], to which it came so far that Your
