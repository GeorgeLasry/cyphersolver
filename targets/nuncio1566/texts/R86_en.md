Translated from `R86_reading.md`; holes as there.

### f. 236r

His Holiness's design, to tell it to Your Lordship alone without showing that you know it to others, is to proceed
against the authors of [?that] deed, and above all against the Senate, with his spiritual arms; and so he wants [?to
have] that order given as soon as possible to His Excellency [the governor of Milan] that he should, if need be, force
[them] to obey His Holiness's monitories whenever they are served on them; because there would truly be no dignity for
this Holy See unless one proceeded at least against the Capitano di giustizia and the fiscal, who has done the worst of
all, and against some of the Senate too, who, [2020] tainted with some notable vice, make more noise than the others, so
that they may not perhaps be punished for their sins, thinking by this [?way] to win all the more the King's favour; and
the King ought to deprive his two said ministers of their office, to give His Holiness this satisfaction; and Your
Lordship should press for this with skill at the right time.
