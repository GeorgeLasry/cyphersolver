# R85 — English (Cardinal Alessandrino to the nuncio Castagna, cipher of Rome, 8 January 1567)

Translated from `R85_reading.md`; holes as there.

### f. 165r

His Holiness wishes to be made certain whether the King's journey will be for [3050], because, if so, he would wish to
meet the King in whatever place suits him best; which you will let the King know, telling him also that His Holiness
holds it as certain that the Queen [Mother] of France and His [French] Majesty desire that the King should ask them to
come to a parley with him, which they would do if asked. It is true that His Holiness says he does not know how far one
can build on women's words; but he begs and exhorts the King [3020] not to doubt that great fruit for the Catholic
religion may come of such a meeting, as […] is given to understand from several sides that great good will come of it
for religion, and that he should be willing to admit, nay to seek, such a meeting. His Holiness however leaves it all
to the prudence of the King, who has more experience and knowledge of their Majesties, and would think it impossible to
be speaking with and listening to everyone. And if the King did not see fit to ask them, and thought it good that His
Holiness as common father should do it, he will not fail to do […] other thing that is in the service of God and [?of]
the King.
