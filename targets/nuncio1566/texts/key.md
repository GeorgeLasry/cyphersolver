# Key of Cardinal Alessandrino's ciphers to the nuncio in Spain, 1567 (Spagna 1, DECODE R85-R90)

Base: George Lasry's reconstruction on DECODE (DOC_R8x_D32xx, 24 Oct 2020), the same key as R93-R102
(alessandrino1568), with the extension made there from the R94/R95/R100 decipherments
(`../../alessandrino1568/texts/key.md`: 9 also = con, dotted 0 = z, 0^.0 = et, X0Y0 codes often written X0Y, doubled
letters enciphered once, 3020 quando, 3060 -mente, 4020 come, 5090 qui, 209 also "chi").

## Letters (polyphonic: each figure two letters)

1 u (v) / t · 2 i / m · 3 f / g · 4 a / l · 5 o / s · 6 e / n · 7 d / r · 8 b / c · 9 p / z, and the syllable con ·
0 null (dotted 0 = z).
