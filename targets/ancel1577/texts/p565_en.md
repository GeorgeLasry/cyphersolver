# Ancel, p. 565: English translation

…cane the Emperor keeps it waiting as long as he can, but the [manua] of Spain not wishing to declare himself so
promptly on the permission for the levies of soldiers, to solicit which the Marquis of Varambon was chiefly sent here by
Don John of Austria; now that this Marquis has returned from the court of Archduke Charles, where he had lately gone, I
can well assure you [mesme] … has still obtained nothing, nor has any intention even been given them of obtaining more
hereafter, the Emperor deferring to the answer he awaits from the King of Spain on what he wrote to him after the
departure of Archduke Matthias, although it is asserted that the aforesaid [manua] may already have had [as he told
me] word from the said lord … master of what he is to negotiate
