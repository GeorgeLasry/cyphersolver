# BnF Dupuy 468, "Ernestus dux" and "Joachimus" to Francis I, eve of St Paul (c. 1518-1520): English translation of Lasry's excerpt

Translation (Claude Opus 5.5, 23 Oct 2026) of `lasry_excerpt_reading.md`, **an excerpt only** of the letter, the passages George Lasry printed in his BnF cipher research report (f. 28r, the last four cipher lines); the rest of the letter is not in the repository. Same holes: [?] unread, [x] a word he supplied. His own translation is in `lasry_entry.md`.

## Translation

Then it will be best that the Most Christian [King] should ask to be willing to enter this league; in this way, even if at some time they had not wished to be in league wit[h] the Most Christian, in this way they will be bound to be confederates of the Most Christian.
