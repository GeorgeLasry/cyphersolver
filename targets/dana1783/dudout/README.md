# Independent reading of Dana's eight lines (catalogue 485), Brice Dudout

Made on 27 Oct 2026, the same day as WAINUT's reading in this folder and independently of it.
Write-up: https://bdudout.github.io/europe-chiffree/en/documents.html#dana-1783

## Agreement

Our letters agree with WAINUT's reading everywhere. We transcribed the lines separately (`transcription.txt`,
`decryption.txt`: 213 groups, 201 read directly, 5 partly under the binding, 2 wholly hidden, 4 slips of Dana).

## What this adds: the three word codes

WAINUT's reading leaves four numbers as codes: [29], [31], [137], [168]. From the nomenclator of the table in the Adams Papers we read:

- 31 = **Administration**, 137 = **Minister**, 168 = **Minister** (nomenclator words of the table sent "by Mr. Adams's son";
  see the write-up's notes);
- 29 at the head of line 1 is outside the table (1-27 letters, words from 30): "I" is imposed by the sense
  ("‹I›n an Administration like this"); the slip is not explained.

Hence: "In an Administration like this, which is not guided by any settled principles or system, you can be
certain of no measure. What the Minister adopt in this hour may be opposed by an irresistible influence in the
next. In short, properly speaking, there is no Minister here. Hence my present difficulty."

## Method

Method: (1) transcription of the 8 lines from the NARA image of p. 730; (2) blind break: a numerical polyalphabetic cipher of period 3 over 27 signs, the shifts recovered from letter frequencies and English 4-gram statistics; (3) Dana’s table (p. 656 of the same volume) was found afterwards, independently, by leafing through the volume: its three columns coincide with the three shifts of the blind break; applied as it stands, with its keyword-restart rule, it also reproduces its own example (“When the Army of America go”); (4) every correction is listed (none silent). English 4-gram score of the reading: −1.87 per letter, against −9.03 to −6.97 for 200 controls (same numbers, random three-column keys).
