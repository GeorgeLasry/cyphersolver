# Avranches' cipher 1, key as used for fr. 3083 no. 8

Key: "Avranches' cipher 1" (Lasry, table dated 05/11/2023, cryptiana GL.htm; Tomokiyo's partial table
`img/francisAvranches1.png` from fr. 3005 f. 107). Simple substitution with a few homophones. Readings
here refine the table:
* y-shaped sign with a zigzag descender = C (Lasry's C); ɤ (loop-bottomed gamma) = S, as in Lasry's second S.
* 9-shaped sign with a long curved tail = A (Lasry's second A) in RAISON, DEPARTIRA; = V in SECOVRIR, VENVE
  (Lasry's second V). The two are very close on this leaf; context decides.
* ɓ-shaped sign = P (APROCHER, APORTEROIT, OPINION), I (MILLAN, IREPARABLE), N? (CONSENTEMENT). Lasry
  lists it under I and P.
* ₠ = LL (ALLER, MILLAN, ICELLE, QV'ELLE); crossed C = SS (POSSIBLE).
* 8∞ = LE (L + E), distinct from 8o = P.
* Macron over ∞ = en (ARGĒT).
