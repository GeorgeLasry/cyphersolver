# R9665, the Duke of Sessa to Charles V, Rome, 26 April 1523: English translation of the cipher runs

… this so good demonstration was made: ⟦His Holiness, by this bull, is declared against France⟧; seeing taken … and
shown … by Your Majesty …

… for cardinals in his chamber, and especially ⟦[?those] the one of Ancona⟧; in this case he has behaved as a very good
servant of Your Majesty.

… ⟦His Holiness was better yesterday.⟧
