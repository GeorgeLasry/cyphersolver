# R53 (f. 499v), Morosini to Montalto, [Moulins, early May 1589]: English

Translation of `R53_reading.md` (23 Oct 2026, Claude Opus 5.5; b21). Holes as in the reading (none).

Navarre, from what the secretary of the Vice-legate of Avignon told me on Sunday, arrived at Tours on Thursday and
lodged at Plessis with a large company of French lords and gentlemen, who are lodged in the village by the said
Plessis; and the same day he went to see the King, to whom the next morning Navarre himself handed the shirt at his
rising and the napkin at table; but His Majesty had him dine with him. So a mixing of heretics and Catholics has come
about at the court, which is hateful to all honest men. [He also told me] that Navarre was to have all his troops pass
in review before His Majesty on Saturday; and these, once some others of the Viscount of Turenne had joined them, he
meant to push against Mayenne, who was between Le Mans and Vendôme, it not being known what he intends to do. That the
