## Notes

- The text runs on into R54 (f. 502r, decipherment f. 500v): '... al quale si tiene se gli andavano ingrossando ogni
  giorno'. R53 and R54 are two cipher passages of one dispatch's matter, deciphered on one leaf (f. 500r-v).
- 'Umena' is Mayenne (code 1̇24); 'il Navarro' Henri of Navarre (1̇85, written over the word by the decipherer);
  'Plesi' Plessis-lès-Tours; the Visconte di Turena is Henri de La Tour, vicomte de Turenne.
- 'la camisa al letto ... la salvietta alla tavola': Navarre served the King at his lever and at table (the meeting of
  the two kings at Plessis, 30 April 1589).
