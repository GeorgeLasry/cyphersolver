# Austria sweep, Oct 2026 (OeStA / ÖNB)

Brief: `research/archive_sweep_2026-10/BRIEF.md`. Output: `candidates.json` (12 rows, generated by `build_candidates.py`),
`coverage.md` (every query and count). Scripts: `ais_search.py` (AIS full-text postback and result paging),
`run_terms.py` (batch of terms), `details.py` (detail pages), `onb_search.py` (onb.digital). Scratch data is in
`_tmp/` (git-ignored; holds two viewed page images).

**Important overlap.** `research/central_archives/` (same day, different agent) had already run the AIS search with
almost the same terms and produced 32 Austrian rows. This sweep re-ran the search, fetched the image-bearing hits
one by one, and kept only items whose shelfmark is in none of `catalogue.json`, `central_archives/candidates.json`
or the targets' NOTES. The grouped central_archives rows list shelfmarks of 7-8 items; the 150+ other "chiffriert"
items of the same series are what is new here.

## Method and counts

- AIS (scopeArchiv) is an ASP.NET postback: POST `volltextsuche.aspx` with the viewstate fields, then GET
  `resultatliste.aspx` (the list), page with the `nextPage` postback. 665 unique records over 22 terms.
- The result list shows a camera icon where scans exist (`openimage(...)`); only HHStA RK Maximiliana, StAbt Türkei I,
  HausA Familienakten and a few HS items have it. Scans are viewable without login: `bild.aspx?VEID=<id>&DEID=<n>&SQNZNR=<page>`
  and `getimage.aspx?...&SIZE=100`, after a GET of the detail page (session cookie) and with a Referer. I viewed two pages.
- 162 image records fetched in full; 48 say "interlinear dechiffriert" or similar (dropped); the rest were grouped into
  11 rows. `access` is "online" for those 11; the 12th (Lang 1526) is "archive" (location unknown).
- ÖNB: 4 relevant searches on onb.digital and web searches found only treatises and printed books. Dead end.

## Ranked leads

1. **HHStA HausA Familienakten 2-3** (AIS ID 3483953), ca. 1608, three ciphered German letters in the Rudolf II / Matthias
   Bruderzwist, to Archduke Ferdinand. Viewed: the first letter page holds a ciphered block, with a German passage
   below that may be a decipherment (partly struck out). Possible crib on the page. Score 3/4/2, cls A.
2. **StAbt Türkei I 7-4-4, 7-4-5, 7-4-9** (1548): Sigismund August, Martinuzzi and Isabella to Ibrahim Pasha, "2-3 chiffrierte
   Exemplare, 1 lateinisches Exemplar" each. A parallel Latin text makes it a known-plaintext case. Score 3/5/1.
3. **StAbt Türkei I 8-2-22 to 9-1-2** (1550): Ferdinand I's ciphered despatches to Malvezzi and replies, with duplicates; same
   key family as the repo's malvezzi1548 reading (R366/R367). Score 3/4/1.
4. **StAbt Türkei I 7-3-21 ... 7-4-43** (1548): six more Malvezzi despatches with ciphered passages. Score 3/4/2.
5. **RK Maximiliana, de Burgo, 17 further reports 1511-16**: the repo already holds part of his key (`targets/maximiliana1510/key_deburgo.md`).
   Score 3/4/2.
6. **RK Maximiliana, Carpi (Rome 1513-15)**, Colla/Rosinus (Rome 1511, 1517), Lang (Gurk 1513-14): new hands, cls C, no key.
7. Sinckmoser/Petrovics/Isabella 1550; miscellaneous Maximiliana and Türkei I singletons; Lang 1526 Salzburg (needs the zobodat article).

None of these has a DECODE record: DECODE's Vienna holdings are keys (Interiora Chiffrenschlüssel, Cod. Pal. 2398), not
Türkei I or Maximiliana letters.

## Dead ends

- ÖNB: onb.digital returns only treatises and printed cryptography books; no letter ciphertext.
- Chiffrenschlüssel (244 hits): all keys or key folders, no ciphertext.
- Kriegsarchiv WWI: "Chiffrierbüro" 0 hits in AIS; no intercept series is described under cipher terms there.
- Items with "interlinear dechiffriert" or "mit Dechiffrierung" (48 of the 162 image records, mostly Vrančić/Zay 1553-54): decipherment on
  the page, kept only as sibling keys (as central_archives did).
- RK Fridericiana 4-5-12: printed offprint (Mitis), not a cipher.
- Türkei I 2-2-7 (Ella → Cles 1532): "chiffrierte Briefe und Auflösungen", decipherment present.

## Caveats

- `Zugänglichkeit: Ausgewählte Archivmitarbeiter` and `Kein Zugang (Reservatbestand)` appear on every Türkei I and Maximiliana
  record, but scans open in the viewer with no login. I viewed only Familienakten 2-3, fol. 1 and the first letter page.
- "chiffriert" in the AIS can mean a stray ciphered line rather than a full letter; a row's solvability is a guess until the image is seen.
- The decipherment status was taken from the AIS description fields only (Titel, Darin, Inhalt, Unterteilung/Enthält).
