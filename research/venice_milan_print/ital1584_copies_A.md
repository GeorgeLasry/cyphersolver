# BnF italien 1584: are the cipher leaves already deciphered? (agent A)

Checked the cipher leaves Mazzatinti 1887 lists "in cifre" for leads 1315, 1318 and 1319 against the Gallica images
of BnF italien 1584 (ark btv1b100373864), fetched from the IIIF host openapi.bnf.fr (images in
`targets/barbavara1447/img/agentA/`). Each canvas is an opening (verso left, recto right), so "canvas" below means the
opening that shows the recto. For each leaf I read the stamped folio number at top right, the margin date of the late
copy, and the signature, looked at the openings before and after for a late clear copy (18th-c. italic, margin date
like "1447 19 avril"), and zoomed in to count dotted blanks ("......") in the copy. Mazzatinti's folio numbers match
the stamped foliation.

**Summary.** Every leaf checked has a decipherment in the volume. Lead 1315 (Guarna): all 11 leaves have a late clear copy; 7 complete, 3 with 1-3 dotted blanks (ff. 163-64, 235, 238), and ff. 235, 245, 268 also have a 15th-c. clear text of the same letter filed beside them (ff. 236, 246, 269), which fills the late copyist's blanks for f. 235. No leaf is Amidani's: all are signed Guarna ("S. N. G."). Lead 1318 (f. 2) and lead 1319 f. 198: 15th-c. interlinear glosses, no late copy. Lead 1319 ff. 125, 295, 299, 306: late copies, f. 125 complete, the other three with one dotted blank each.
