# Clear copies filed with the cipher leaves of BnF italien 1584 (checked 29 Oct 2026)

Barbavara and Pusterla (leads 1313, 1314; `targets/barbavara1447/`) showed that most cipher leaves in ital. 1584 have a
clear copy filed beside them. The copyist wrote in a regular late italic hand, probably 18th century, with a margin date
on each copy. Mazzatinti (1887) records few of these copies, so this file checks the other ital. 1584-1585 cipher leads
leaf by leaf.

Method: the Gallica microfilm through the openapi.bnf.fr IIIF host (ark btv1b100373864). The canvas-to-folio map comes
from crops of the folio number at the top right of every canvas (folio ≈ canvas + 3 at the start of the volume, + 6 to
+ 9 after f. 150). Each cipher leaf and its neighbours were then viewed at 900 px.

**Volume-wide pattern.** Up to about f. 200 the late copies are complete texts. From about f. 203 onwards, many copies
have dotted blanks ("........") where the copyist could not decipher. Those leaves were read at the time only in part.

**Lessons.**
- Mazzatinti's "del medesimo" chains misattribute: six "Amidani" leaves are Guarna's or Birago's.
- A copyist's dotted run is not always an unread group: on ff. 205 and 370 it stands for clear text he skipped. The
  unread groups are blanks with the sign drawn in the margin.
- Several partial late copies have a complete 15th-c. clear text filed nearby (ff. 236, 246, 269, 386, 390; 1585 f. 16).
  Look for those first.
