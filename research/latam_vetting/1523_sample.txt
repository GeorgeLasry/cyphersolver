Lead 1523 - Luis López Méndez (London) to Simón Bolívar, 29 Jan 1819
AGN Colombia, HISTORIA.SAA-I.17,25,D.24, ff. 226-228 (the López Méndez letters; ff. 222-224 and 230-233 are
Hurry, Powles & Hurry, Herring/Saffray and a printed loan certificate, all in clear).
Images: DataViva record 66b0cd4f151c9d47762a2382, gallery index 11 (f. 227v) and 12 (f. 228r).
Transcribed 28 Oct 2026 from the DataViva "big" images (1684x1999 px). First-pass reading; superscripts uncertain
where marked "?".

System seen: the cipher words are underlined and written in ordinary lowercase letters, some carrying a small
superscript digit (1-7) over the letter. A letter alone and the same letter with different digits stand for
different plaintext letters (e alone = m?, e1 = b, e2 = f, e3 = l, e4 = r, e5 = t, e7 = y). Clear Spanish runs
around the cipher words. No interlinear decipherment, no key and no clear copy in the unit.

Notation: letter followed by ^n = that letter with superscript n.

f. 227v, lines 20-22 ("pongo en la noticia de V.E. con sumo dolor, que ...")
  o^1 r                      -> he
  r t^3 e^5 l i^1 o^2        -> estado
  i^3 e^4 r t^3 o^2          -> preso
  m^1 c a m^1 o^2            -> cinco
  e r m r t  (digits 6 1 3 over r/m, r, t; placement uncertain)  -> meses
  e^7                        -> y
  o^1 r                      -> he
  t^3 l e^3 c i^1 o^2        -> salido
  m^1 o^2 a                  -> con
  e^2 c l a m^3 l            -> fianza
  => "que he estado preso cinco meses y he salido con fianza"

f. 227v, line 27 (before "ha causado un perjuicio indecible á nuestra causa")
  t^1 l m^1                  -> Mac
  d^1 e^4 r d^1 o^2 e^4      -> Gregor
  => "Mac Gregor"

f. 227v last line / f. 228r line 1 ("entré en una expedición, ... Me separé de ella")
  i^3 l e^4 l                -> para
  t^1 l e^4 t | m^1 l c e^1 o^2   -> Mar? ... caibo?  (tentative: Maracaibo; the 4th letter t is unresolved)

f. 228r lines 5-6 ("Está pensando y haciendo esfuerzos para ir á ...")
  t^3 l a e^5 l - e^2 r      -> Santa-fe
  => "ir á Santafé"

e^4=r, e^5=t, e^7=y, t^1=m, t^3=s, m^1=c, m^3=z, d^1=g. Open: e alone, m with 6, r with 1, plain t.
