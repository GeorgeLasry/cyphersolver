SYSTEM (found during vetting)
  Vigenère with the standard alphabet, plaintext = cipher - key, key of 8 letters restarted at the start of EVERY
  word (cipher words keep the plaintext word lengths; two plaintext words are sometimes run together, which breaks
  the restart). Several keys:
    MFTDBSJV  Vespasiano (Minister of War) and Rego Barros, 14 Mar - 17 Apr 1914 (pagfis 1319-1329)
    LETCARIV  close fit for the first telegram (Pamplona, 10 Mar, pagfis 1317); some positions still garbled
    a different key (about 10 letters, not yet settled) for Rego Barros 20 May (pagfis 1330-1332)
    LBQPOHVJ  Vespasiano 7 Oct 1914 (1471, FSC cont 1914.10.07/2), from the pencil glosses
